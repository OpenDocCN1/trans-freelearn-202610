In order to have a many-to-many relationship between the Post and Tag entities, we created a TagPost (a kind of walk down table) entity purely to allow EF to join the Post and Tag entities. It is achieved by having a one-to-many relationship between the Tag and TagPost and Post and TagPost entities.
