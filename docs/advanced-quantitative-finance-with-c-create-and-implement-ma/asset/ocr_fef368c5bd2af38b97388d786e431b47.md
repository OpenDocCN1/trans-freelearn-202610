We consider an example of a European Call option on currency with strike 0.75 EUR/USD with a spot price of 0.75 EUR/USD. The option has six months to maturity. We divide the x axis in N=5 steps and the tau axis in M=6 steps. The premium under these conditions is 4.36 EUR/USD.
