If you don't have a 3D printer at home, you can try to find a hackerspace or fab lab near you.
These are groups of makers and hackers who meet on a regular basis to share their projects
and provide the tools and information to get them done. Many of them have 3D printers
these days.

From step 9 onwards, I also show you how to use an online 3D printing service to order your
object. I used http://www.shapeways.com for this example because they offer a wide
range of materials to choose from and deliver to most countries.
