To play audio, Godot has a special node called AudioStreamPlayer. It is ideal for playing background music as it does not play audio positionally. If you want audio to play from different sources on the screen, then you use the AudioStreamPlayer2D or AudioStreamPlayer3D node.

Since the level will have background music, add a new AudioStreamPlayer node to the level scene, as shown in Figure 10.24:

![](bbox=[0.28, 0.43, 0.71, 0.69])

Figure 10.24 – Adding an AudioSteamPlayer node to the level scene
