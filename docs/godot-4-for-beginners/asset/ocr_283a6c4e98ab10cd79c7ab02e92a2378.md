+   - `time * 0.5` is the **frequency** of the wave, and this slows down the oscillation.
+   - Multiplying the result by 0.5 outside the brackets is the **amplitude** of the wave. Here, we are reducing it, making the movement less extreme.
+   - The entire result is returned for use in updating the position in the transform property.

In essence, this script creates a smooth, oscillating movement for the cloud. The sine wave makes the movement more natural.
