# Spotlight

Just like at the theatre, SpotLight3D is a node that emits light in a single direction in the shape of a cone. This works well for lights such as headlights or streetlights. Disable OmniLight3D and DirectionalLight3D by clicking on the eye visibility toggle to the right of them in the scene tree.

Now add a Spotlight3D node to the scene. You will see the cone wireframe of the light, and again we can use the little orange circles to change the range and the angle of the light.
