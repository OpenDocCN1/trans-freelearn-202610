# Setting up the player animations

To animate a sprite, we use multiple frames that depict the sprite at various stages of motion. These frames are often grouped together in a single image known as a **sprite sheet**. To animate the sprite, we first import the sprite sheet and then divide it into individual frames. To do this, select the **AnimatedSprite2D** node, and in **Inspector**, on the **Sprite Frames** property, click on **New SpriteFrames**. This is shown in Figure 6.12:

![](bbox=[0.32, 0.25, 0.67, 0.47])

Figure 6.12 – New SpriteFrames

Clicking on **SpriteFrames** in **Inspector**, as in Figure 6.12, will open the **SpriteFrames** area at the bottom of the screen (see Figure 6.13). This is the space where we can import frames and create new animations.
