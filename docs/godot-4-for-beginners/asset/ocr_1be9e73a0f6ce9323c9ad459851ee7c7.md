**Velocity** is the speed of something in a specific direction. In game development, it’s used to describe how fast and in what direction an object, such as a player or enemy, is moving. Velocity is often represented by a vector, which shows both the *speed* (magnitude) and *direction* of movement.

If we wanted our player to move up and to the left, we could use a velocity vector of **(-2,3)** to do so. This is represented in *Figure 5.4*:

![](bbox=[0.24, 0.22, 0.71, 0.48])

*Figure 5.4 – A vector depicting the velocity of the player*

In *Figure 5.4*, the *velocity vector* indicates that the player will move 2 units left and 3 units up. Note that the *x* coordinate is always given before the *y* coordinate.
