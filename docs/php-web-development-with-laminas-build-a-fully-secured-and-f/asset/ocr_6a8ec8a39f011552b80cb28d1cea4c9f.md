# Figure 11.5: The content of the product basket

The page of the product basket is produced by the `view/store/product-basket/index.phtml` file. This file receives the output of the `ProductBasket->getProducts` method, which is invoked by the `ProductBasketController->indexAction` method. You can increment or decrement the amount of a product by clicking on the + or - buttons in the **operations** column. To remove a product from the basket, you click on the **remove** button.
