10. Deselect the **Keep body** option because we no longer need to keep the body. Also, deselect the **Reverse thickness** option. This will mean that this new sheet will fit within the first sheet and is a good example of why **Reverse thickness** can be useful for creating multi-sheet *parts* that fit together properly.

11. Press **OK** to create the second sheet. *Figure 12.20* shows both sheets in an exploded view and shows how **Keep body** can be used with a single solid model to create *parts* with multiple sheets.
