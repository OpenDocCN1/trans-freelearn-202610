+   9. A preview of the file that will be exported will now appear. Solid lines indicate the model's geometry (i.e., the outer perimeter), whereas dashed lines are the Bend Lines. The view can be adjusted using the controls at the top. If any additional lines aren't needed, they can be selected by left-clicking and then deleted. Note that the usual Ctrl + Z shortcut for undoing actions doesn't work in this preview, but there are Undo and Redo buttons in the bottom left of the preview window.
