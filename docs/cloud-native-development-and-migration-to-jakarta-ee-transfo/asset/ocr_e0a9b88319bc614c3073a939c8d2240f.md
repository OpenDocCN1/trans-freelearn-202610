When you click on any of the route options, that route will be selected for the cargo. With a route selected, the status of the cargo will change from not routed to routed.

When we go back to the overview in the administrative interface, we’ll see that the cargo is no longer listed in the Not Routed Cargo section but in the Routed Cargo section:
