# About the author

Ahmad Sahar is a trainer, presenter, and consultant at Tomafuw Productions. He is an Apple Certified Trainer for app development in Swift, and he has conducted training courses for over 10 years on iOS mobile application development, Flutter mobile application development, and how to use Apple software and hardware. He also currently holds App Development with Swift Associate and Certified User certifications.

He is a member of the DevCon iOS online community in Malaysia and has conducted presentations and talks for this group. In his spare time, he likes building and programming LEGO MINDSTORMS® robots.

To my mother, Sharifah; my father, Sahar; my two sisters, Ainol Shareha and Ainol Shaharina; and my beloved wife, Oni – thank you for your love and support.

# About the reviewer

**Ian Lockett** is an iOS developer based in the UK. With over 20 years of experience in the software industry, he has spent the last 15 years developing apps for a wide range of clients and industries, from small start-ups to large enterprises. Ian prides himself on his attention to detail and delivering high-quality software. Ian has also been a volunteer co-organizer for the “Hacking with Swift Live” conference.
