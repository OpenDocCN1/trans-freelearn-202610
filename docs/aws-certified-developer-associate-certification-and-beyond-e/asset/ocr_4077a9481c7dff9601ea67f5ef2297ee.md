Amazon EBS also offers another type of block storage called EBS Multi-Attach, which allows the simultaneous attachment of a single EBS volume to multiple instances. However, this feature comes with certain restrictions. For example, Multi-Attach enabled volumes can be attached to a maximum of 16 instances, built on Nitro System, that are in the same Availability Zone.

Amazon EBS presents a diverse range of storage options, such as General Purpose SSDs (gp2 and gp3), Provisioned IOPS SSDs (io1 and io2), Throughput Optimized HDDs (st1), Cold HDDs (sc1), and Magnetic HDDs (MHDS). These options offer different performance levels and costs, allowing you to select the most suitable solution for your application requirements. Although all options are suggested, the ideal choice hinges on the specific demands of the user’s workload.

The storage options provided by Amazone EBS are as listed here:

+   - **Disks used for general purposes (gp2 and gp3) (SSDs):** These are cost-effective yet provide sufficient throughput for many different kinds of transactional workloads. These include things like virtual desktops, single-instance databases of moderate size, interactive programs that suffer greatly from latency, test beds, and boot drives.
