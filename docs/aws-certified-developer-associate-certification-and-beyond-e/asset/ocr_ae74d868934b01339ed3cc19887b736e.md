# Step 4 – Configure Message Delivery

+   1. Under the **Email** section, select **Send email with Cognito**. This option is designed for development and test purposes and is limited to 50 emails a day. For production environments, you would want to send emails using the **Amazon Simple Email Service (Amazon SES)**, which offers high email volumes. Click **Next**.
