This helps you quickly build your application architecture without having to design your own pub/sub service. As per *Figure 15.11*, the ordering service sends notifications of the order to an SNS topic. The other services running across a range of AWS services subscribe to that topic and receive those notifications, which are then used to complete their individual tasks.

Figure 15.11 – Amazon SNS pub/sub model

With Amazon SNS, you can configure two types of subscribers – **Application to Application (A2A)** or **Application to Person (A2P)**:

+   - **A2A** – This type of subscriber is another application or a component of your primary application. For example, the Lambda function that subscribes to the Order Service SNS topic in *Figure 15.11* uses the notification to process the shipping component of the application to ensure the order is correctly dispatched to the customer.
+   - **A2P** – This type of subscriber is usually a human subscriber such as an administrator who needs to be alerted or, in the example illustrated in *Figure 15.11*, the customer, who gets an email confirming that the order has been placed.
