You can also use Route 53 to perform a weighted routing policy as part of your testing where you can continue to use both environments and send only a small percentage of traffic to the green deployment to validate and test the new deployment. Once you are ready to go live, you can disable the weight routing policy and finally perform a URL swap so that all traffic is directed to the new version.
