Figure 16.1: Amazon CloudWatch dashboard

The screenshot in Figure 16.1 depicts a dashboard showcasing metrics for several EC2 instances. These include CPU Utilization, DiskReadBytes, and more. These metrics are built-in metrics provided by CloudWatch. The dashboard provides a quick visual representation of your resources’ performance and whether they are overutilized or underutilized.

You can also use another service, AWS Compute Optimizer, which integrates with Amazon CloudWatch to provide recommendations on optimizing the provisioning of your compute resources. This service helps you avoid overprovisioning or underprovisioning your compute resources across your EC2 instances, EBS volumes, ECS services running on Fargate, Lambda functions, and RDS database instances.

The service will analyze your compute usage patterns, configuration, and utilization data from Amazon CloudWatch and application monitoring tools to generate recommendations. These recommendations can then be exported to Amazon S3 and integrated with AWS Cost Explorer and AWS Systems Manager. Based on those recommendations, you can reconfigure your resources to improve performance and identify cost-saving opportunities.
