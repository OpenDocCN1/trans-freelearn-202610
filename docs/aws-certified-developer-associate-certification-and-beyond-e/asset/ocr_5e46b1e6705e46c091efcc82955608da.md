+   15. Open Secrets Manager in a separate browser tab.
+   16. Click on Secrets from the left-hand menu and then click on the secret you created in the right-hand pane.
+   17. From the Secret details section, copy the secret ARN to your clipboard.
+   18. In the Specify ARN(s) section in the dialog box in your IAM browser tab, paste in the ARN you copied from the Secrets Manager console in the textbox next to Resource ARN. The other fields in the dialog box will get filled automatically.
