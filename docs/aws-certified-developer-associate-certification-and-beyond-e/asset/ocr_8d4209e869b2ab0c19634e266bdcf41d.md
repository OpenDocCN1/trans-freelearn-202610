# All-at-Once Deployment

This is by far the quickest option for rolling out updates. The strategy involves pushing updates to all application nodes in one shot and performing necessary upgrade operations. However, this does result in some downtime until the newer version of the application is installed and configured. There are no additional costs for resources with this deployment approach.

This option is ideal if you regularly roll out updates and don’t mind the outage period, especially if it is short. You can plan such rollouts to occur outside of work hours or when you anticipate very little traffic, such as midnight on a Sunday. To ensure customers are not alarmed, you may wish to send out necessary communications for such planned outages.

# Rolling Deployment

In this deployment type, you roll out updates to a portion of the compute nodes at a time until all nodes have been updated with the latest version of the application. This results in only a subset of nodes being taken out of the fleet at a time, enabling you to avoid a complete outage of service to your end users.

Depending on the number of nodes that need to be updated and the acceptable percentage of nodes that can be taken out of service (while the update rolls out), this process takes some time before all nodes are configured with the latest version of the application.
