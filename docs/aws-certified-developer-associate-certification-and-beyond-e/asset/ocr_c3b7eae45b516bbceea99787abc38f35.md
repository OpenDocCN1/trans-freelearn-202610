# The Different Pricing Models of EC2

Amazon EC2 offers various pricing models to cater to the diverse needs of its users. These pricing options enable businesses to optimize their cloud computing costs while ensuring they have the required resources for their workloads. You will **explore the four different pricing options next.**

# On-Demand Instances

On-Demand Instances allow you to pay for compute capacity by the hour or by the second, depending on the instance type you choose. There are no long-term commitments or upfront payments required, making this option ideal for short-term, irregular workloads or applications with unpredictable usage patterns. On-Demand Instances provide flexibility and help you avoid the cost and complexity of capacity planning.

# Reserved Instances (RIs)

RIs enable you to reserve compute capacity for a specific period – typically, one or three years – at a discounted price compared to On-Demand Instances. RIs are suitable for predictable, steady-state workloads or applications with long-term commitments. There are three payment options for RIs: All Upfront, Partial Upfront, and No Upfront. The more you pay upfront, the greater the discount you receive. By using RIs, you can achieve significant cost savings while ensuring resource availability for your applications.

# Savings Plans

Savings Plans are a flexible pricing model that offers discounts similar to Reserved Instances but with more simplicity and flexibility. With savings plans, you can reduce your bill and get a maximum of 72% off the On-Demand price in exchange for a one-year or three-year spend commitment. There are three types of Savings Plans:

+   - **Compute Savings Plans**: These offer discounts across AWS computing services such as Amazon EC2, AWS ECS Fargate, and AWS Lambda. You can also apply your discounts across any instance, including family, size, Region, or operating system. Compute Savings Plans offer more flexibility in how your discounts are applied.

+   - **EC2 Instance Savings Plans**: These offer discounts specific to a particular EC2 instance family within a Region. You can only apply your discounts across instance size, availability zone, operating system, and tenancy. However, EC2 Instance Savings Plans offer the most significant discounts.

+   - **Amazon SageMaker Savings Plans**: Offers discounts across any ML instance types, sizes, and Regions up to 64% compared to the On-Demand price.

Savings Plans require a one or three-year commitment, with the option to pay either all upfront, partially upfront, or nothing upfront. This model is ideal for businesses seeking cost savings without being tied to specific instance types or families.
