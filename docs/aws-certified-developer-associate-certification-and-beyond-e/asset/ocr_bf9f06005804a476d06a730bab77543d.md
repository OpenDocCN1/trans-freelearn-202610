10. Next, click Add Task.

11. A pop-up dialog box appears where you need to provide your task details. You will need to provide the following pieces of information:

+   I. Task Type: Choose from Finance, Work, Family, Health, and Other.
+   II. Task Description: Space to describe your task.
+   III. Due Date: Here, you can choose a due date for your task.
+   IV. Status: Specify the current state of the task. Choose from Started, In-Progress, Completed, Delayed, and Not Started.
