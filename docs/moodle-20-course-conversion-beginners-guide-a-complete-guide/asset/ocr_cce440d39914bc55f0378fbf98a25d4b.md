**Seeing the course from a student's point of view**

As a teacher, you will see a lot of options on the screen that your students won't. To get a clear idea of how a student will see the course, use the **Switch role to...** option from the course **Settings** block. Choose **Student** from this list, and you will see the course as students see it.

When you're done, click **Return to my normal role** and you'll get your normal view back. You will also need to turn editing on to get the edit controls back.
