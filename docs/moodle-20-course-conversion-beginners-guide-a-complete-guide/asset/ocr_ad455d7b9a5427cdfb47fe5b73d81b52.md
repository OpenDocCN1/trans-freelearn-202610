# Managing groups of students

If I've got a group of students (especially if they are of mixed ability) then I like to split them into groups. Not only does this make them easier to manage from my point of view, but I also put students into groups if they don't know each other that well (a way of getting them to mix socially). Whether or not you are moving fully online, being able to group students, and then to hand out work depending on what group they are in, is something that you will very likely want to do; in this section we learn how.
