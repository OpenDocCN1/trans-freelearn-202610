# What just happened?

A list of resources in Moodle isn't simply a list of files, such as you would find on a shared drive. One obvious difference is that in Moodle you can arrange your resources to be listed in the order you want, and we've just seen how easy it is to achieve this.

> You can't find the move icon?
> Your site may be configured so that you can drag and drop resources. In that case, instead of the move icon you will see crosshairs next to your resource. To move the resource, click on the crosshairs and, keeping your finger pressed down on the left mouse button, drag the resource to its new location. Look for the line in the background, this tells you where your resource is going to be dropped into, then let go of the mouse button when you have found the spot.
