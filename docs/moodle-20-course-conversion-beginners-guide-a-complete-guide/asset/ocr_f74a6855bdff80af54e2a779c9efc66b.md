2. The next page ("Schema settings") allows you to choose what you want to back up. Again, scroll down to the bottom of the page and click Next.

3. The "Confirmation and review" page allows you to check your choices and also to change the name of the backup file Moodle is about to create. If all is well, press the Perform backup button at the bottom of the page. Once the backup has been performed, press the Continue button and Moodle now displays your backup files.
