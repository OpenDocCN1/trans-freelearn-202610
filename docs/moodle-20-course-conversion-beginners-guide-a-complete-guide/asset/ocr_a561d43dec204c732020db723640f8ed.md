# # Using the Moodle gradebook

Let's take another visit to the gradebook we first encountered in *Chapter 6, Managing Student Work*. The gradebook is a very powerful feature of Moodle, and if you are going to be converting your course to be fully online then you will want to know how to use it well. That said, most of the gradebook is self-explanatory. As you add activities to your course you will see them added to the grader report and see student results displayed there. Most educators are more than happy to use the gradebook as it is. However, you can insert custom grade items into your gradebook (you aren't just limited to activities you've added into your course), you can change the way grades are calculated, and you can categorize grades (grouping grades and even calculating an overall grade for that category).

To access the gradebook for your course you will need to return to the course front page and select **Grades** from the **Settings** block, under **Course administration**.
