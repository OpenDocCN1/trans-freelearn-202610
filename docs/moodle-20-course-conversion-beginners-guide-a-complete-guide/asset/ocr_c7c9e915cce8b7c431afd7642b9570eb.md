3. Think of a description for your new course. Try to make it inviting, it's the first thing students will see before they are actually enrolled on your course. Try not to make it too elaborate, you know how to use the Moodle Editor and you know how to include graphics, audio, and video in your course summary if you wanted to. Try to resist going overboard using these techniques in the course summary, it will put students off when they are trying to look through the list of courses in our course category.
