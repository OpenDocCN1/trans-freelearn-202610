## Chat room tips and tricks

Before we leave chat rooms, here are some ideas for using chat rooms on your courses:

- Let students join the chat room to allow them to get to know each other and you before they meet.
- Hold meetings with colleagues about your course using a hidden chat room.
- Invite guests into your chat room, perhaps specialists from your local college or university.
