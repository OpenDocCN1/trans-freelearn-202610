# Time for action – adding a topic and hiding it

+   1. Return to your course front page and choose Edit settings from the Settings block.
+   2. Scroll down to the number of weeks/topics setting and change the number in the drop-down list to add another topic to your course:
+   3. At the bottom of the page, press the Save changes button. That's it, we're done—and now there's a new empty topic at the end of your course.
+   4. For the moment, I want to hide this topic from students. Click on the eye icon on the right-hand side to hide the topic:
+   5. It depends on your theme but, to show that a topic is hidden, two gray bars are shown on the left- and right-hand side of the topic:
