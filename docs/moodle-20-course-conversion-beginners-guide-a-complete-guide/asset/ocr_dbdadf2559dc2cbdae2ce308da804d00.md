# What just happened?

We've just seen how easy it is to include a picture with any text in Moodle. We'll be looking in more detail at how to arrange images in Chapter 5, Moodle Makeover. For now, you could try returning to the Insert/edit image dialog and taking a look at the settings in the Appearance tab. Have a play with the settings and remember: you won't break anything!

# Have a go hero – having fun with images

Return to the Insert/edit image dialog and click on the Advanced tab. You can use the Advanced settings to change the image when the mouse pointer moves over it and change it again when the mouse pointer moves off it. For example, you could reveal the names of characters from a film or play by your students moving the mouse pointer over their picture.
