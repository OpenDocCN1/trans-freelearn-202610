# Featured tables

The concept of featured tables comes from the reusability mindset, where we take the prepared and already polished data across the organization in a secure way. The Power BI admins then have granular control of configuring or monitoring the featured table, who can publish or update the featured tables, or who, within the organization, can access the featured tables.
