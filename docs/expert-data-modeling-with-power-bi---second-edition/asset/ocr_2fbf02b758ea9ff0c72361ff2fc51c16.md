# Figure 10.43: Dataset partitions and data ranges

As the preceding image shows, Power BI created partitions at Year, Quarter, and Month levels. This is done based on the incremental refresh configuration, which can be different on a case-by-case basis. Based on how we define our incremental policy, those partitions will be automatically refreshed (if we schedule automatic data to refresh on the service). Over time, some of those partitions will be dropped, and some will be merged with others. For instance, in our sample, when we move to January 2023, the month-level partitions will be merged into the quarter level, and the quarter-level partitions will be merged into the year level.
