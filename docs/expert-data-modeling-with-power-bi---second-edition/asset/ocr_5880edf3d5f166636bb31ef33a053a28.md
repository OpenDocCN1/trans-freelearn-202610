# What is a Datamart?

A quick search on the Internet provides multiple definitions by technology-leading companies such as IBM, Amazon, and Oracle. They all agree that a Datamart is a subject-oriented subset of a data warehouse focusing on a particular business unit, department, subject area, or business functionality.
