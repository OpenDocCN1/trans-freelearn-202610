In the preceding model, the **Year-Month** column is not a key column in any of the tables participating in the relationship, which means the **Year-Month** column has duplicate values on both sides. As you may have noticed, the relationship between the **Sales for Customers with Yearly Income Greater Than $100,000** table and the **Date** table has a star (*) on both ends of the relationship, indicating the cardinality of the relationship is many-to-many. There is also a broken chain sign on both ends of the relationship, indicating the relationship’s **evaluation type is limited**. Relationship evaluation relates to the cardinality of the relationship and the data sources of the two tables contributing to the relationship; therefore, it is not configurable.
