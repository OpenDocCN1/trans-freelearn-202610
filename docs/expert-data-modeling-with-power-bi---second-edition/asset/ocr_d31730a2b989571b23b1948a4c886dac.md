So, we expect the queries to hit the **Sales_AggYear** table when we are at the **Year** level and hit the **Sales_Agg** when we are at the **Year** and **Month** level. To implement this scenario, we must go through all the previous steps to implement the aggregation with a small difference. This time, we have to set the value of the **Precedence** option when managing the aggregation. The **Precedence** value tells the engine to consider other aggregation tables. The larger the number, the higher the priority. So, when there are two aggregation tables with 0 and 10 for the precedence values, the engine considers the aggregation table with the higher precedence first. In other words, the xVelocity engine tries to solve the underlying aggregation queries based on their **Precedence** value.
