We have seen the best skills under the top five categories offered by the Alexa Skills Store. Now, a question to you: did you notice any common traits among the top-selling skills? Take a few minutes to think this over before moving ahead to read the answer.

Well, we hope you have given it some thought by now. Here is the answer: all the top skills in these categories have one common feature—**dynamism**. This means they are updated with new features very frequently, and some are even updated daily. Take any skill (with the exception of the News skills, since their daily content is already dynamic in itself); whether it is a **Games/Trivia** skill or an educational skill, if it is one of the top spots, then it stays one step ahead of its users by always providing them with something new to discover, which keeps the users hooked to the Skill, and they keep coming back to discover more content. Dynamism of content is the key if you want your skill to be a leader in its category.
