# # Adding charts to a dashboard

Dashboards provide a way to show saved charts and searches together in one place. To create a new dashboard, follow the steps mentioned here:

+   1. Click on Dashboard from the menu on the left and click on New from the menu at the top. Click on Add at the top of the page to add a new panel to the dashboard.
+   2. Click on the saved visualization or search and it will be added to the dashboard:
