| Stage 4 – Tailor to the business |  |  |
| **Determine your product model** | - Organizational senior leaders (including product and business line leaders)
- Technology senior leaders (especially security)
- Security posture management
- Architects (especially security, business, and enterprise architects)
- Managers
- Application and product security | *This step captures the product model of your organization.*

Businesses sell goods and services, which are collectively known as *products*. This product model helps you understand how Zero Trust addresses three key things associated with a product:

- **Risk**: As a source of potential loss.
- **Opportunity**: As a mechanism to increase organizational success. This typically takes the form of growing revenue in existing lines of business or growing new sources of revenue by entering new lines of business or geographies.
- **Ease of doing business** (in terms of agility and cost).

This context helps link Zero Trust to the organization’s revenue and determine which business functions and processes are impacted, how Zero Trust will integrate and impact them, and how best to measure that impact. This impact will be described in terms of people, organizational structure, business process, and capability.

This initiative will be led by security leaders, using security architecture, enterprise architecture, and business leaders. |
