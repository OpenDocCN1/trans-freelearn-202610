This diagram shows how **business and technical senior leaders** are heavily involved in the beginning of Zero Trust adoption, setting direction for strategy and culture. This first stage anchors and guides the rest of the work and establishes its importance to the organization.

**Clarity brings agility**. The strategy and culture established in the beginning act as ongoing durable guidelines that empower people, accelerate progress, and increase agility.
