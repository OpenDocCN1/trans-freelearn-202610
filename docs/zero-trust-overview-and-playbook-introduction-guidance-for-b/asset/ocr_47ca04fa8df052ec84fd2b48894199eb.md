*Each incident that enables attackers to steal, alter, or destroy data and system functionality undermines the organization's revenue, customer trust, and competitive differentiators.*

These key capabilities collectively enable an organization to become agile, resilient, and secure.

In a Zero Trust approach, the organization learns and grows stronger with each event. Whether the organization directly experiences an attack or learns from the experience of others, the learnings are fed into an improved ability to prevent attacks, detect them, respond to them, or recover from them.

In summary, these Zero Trust security capabilities enable organizations to pursue new opportunities and business models with much lower security friction, more security and risk context, and much higher security assurances than classic security approaches.
