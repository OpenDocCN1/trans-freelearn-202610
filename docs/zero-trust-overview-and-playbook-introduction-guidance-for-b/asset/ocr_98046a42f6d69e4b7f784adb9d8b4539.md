**Business capabilities** and **technical capabilities** *inform* the Zero Trust capabilities of the organization. These **Zero Trust capabilities** are *delivered* over the **short, medium, and long term** with a **capability-based roadmap**.

*To be clear, Zero Trust very much starts with what you already have and builds on it to meet today's needs.*

As we deliver these capabilities, they are continuously measured, monitored, and improved in an agile manner. This ensures that we incorporate current top priorities and context, as opposed to sticking exactly to yesterday's plan that was made when we knew less and was based on priorities that often have evolved. This approach allows Zero Trust security to be agile and adaptable and stay aligned with evolving business, technical, security, and Zero Trust capabilities.
