This step builds on your understanding of the business operating model.

This includes the following:
- Assessing your business culture and value system
- Assessing who needs training for technology, business processes, and more
- Developing a plan to implement this change

The resulting plan usually includes elements such as the following:
- Setting OKRs for teams
- Setting goals in the employee performance review process (including senior executives to model the behavior)
- Establishing a training plan (including a continuous process for sustainment)
- Establishing a communications plan (including a continuous process for reinforcement)

This is owned by security leaders and supported by other roles. The entire organization is impacted, and hence business leaders and technology leaders must support this. |
