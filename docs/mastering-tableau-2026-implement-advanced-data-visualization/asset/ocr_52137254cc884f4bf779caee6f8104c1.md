# Auto-save for Web Flows

Remember the days of frantically pressing Ctrl+S every few minutes, terrified that your computer might crash? Those days are mostly behind us with web authoring.

When editing flows in Tableau Prep web authoring, your work is automatically saved every few seconds as a draft. I didn't believe it at first either, but it's true, you can close your browser, come back hours later, and your flow will be right where you left it.

Drafts are visible only to you and marked with a subtle Draft badge in the corner. Your colleagues won't see your unfinished work, so you can experiment freely without fear of publishing something half-baked.

Once you're happy with your flow, click **Publish**. This creates a permanent version. And here's where it gets even better: Tableau keeps a Revision History. You can access previous versions from the Revision History dialog, rolling back if you ever make a mistake.

> > **Note**
> > A word of caution:
> >
> > Your administrator can disable auto-save (though I've rarely seen this done). If auto-save is off, you'll need to save drafts manually. But honestly, in 2026, working without auto-save feels like riding a bike without brakes: Possible, but why would you?
