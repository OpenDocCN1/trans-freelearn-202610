# Cleaning Your Data

Have you ever wondered whether your data is reliable enough to analyze? Almost everyone who works with data faces this question. That’s why this chapter focuses on *data preparation*; the essential process of getting your data ready for meaningful analysis is often called *data cleaning*.

By the end of this chapter, you’ll understand what good data quality looks like, how to identify issues in your datasets, and how to apply practical cleaning techniques using Tableau 2026. The overall goal is to help you build confidence in working with data that truly supports sound conclusions.

This information matters because even the most advanced analyses depend on accurate, well-prepared data. Learning how to evaluate and clean your data will save time, reduce errors, and improve the credibility of your insights.

In this chapter, we will discuss the following topics:

+   - Preparing datasets
+   - Surveying data
+   - Cleaning messy data

Now, let’s dive into this fascinating topic with the goal of enriching our understanding of data quality.

# Preparing datasets

Tableau can be used effectively with various data preparation phases:

+   1. Data collection/ingestion: Do I have access to the right data?
+   2. Data profiling/surveying: What shape is my data in?
+   3. Data cleaning: Is my data reliable enough for analysis?
+   4. Data transformation: Is my data structured for the questions I need to answer?
+   5. Data integration: Am I combining data correctly and consistently?
+   6. Data enrichment: Do I have enough context to get meaningful insights?
+   7. Data validation: Can I trust the outputs?
+   8. Data loading and publishing: Is the data accessible and usable for stakeholders?
