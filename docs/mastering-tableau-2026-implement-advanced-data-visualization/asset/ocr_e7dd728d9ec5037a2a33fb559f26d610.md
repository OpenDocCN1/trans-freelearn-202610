This logic allows you to handle various real-world scenarios, such as ensuring the Finance team only sees their specific project, allowing Analysts to edit workbooks without altering data sources, or isolating external consultants within their own dedicated project. To maintain a clean and manageable system, you should always assign permissions to groups rather than individuals and strive to grant access at the highest possible level rather than overriding every individual file. When you need to verify if your setup is working correctly, you can navigate to a piece of content, click the Permissions tab, and search for a specific user to see their *effective permissions*, which reveals exactly what they can do after all hierarchy rules are applied. By following these principles and testing with a dedicated account, you ensure a secure and documented environment that aligns with your organization’s needs.
