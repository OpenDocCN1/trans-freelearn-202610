# Partitioning and addressing with two dimensions

Two additional options are made available when partitioning and addressing two or more dimensions: At the level and Restarting every. You can compare this scenario to the task of counting all countries per continent. In this scenario, At the level will be countries since you are not counting streets or trees but countries. Restarting every will be continents. After you are done counting countries for one continent, you start at 1 again for the next continent:
