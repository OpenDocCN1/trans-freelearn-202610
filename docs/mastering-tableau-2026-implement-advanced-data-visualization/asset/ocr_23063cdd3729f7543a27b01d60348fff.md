Exceeding 600mg of caffeine, 3x+ Pret single-shot espressos, can lead to health risks such as insomnia and increased blood pressure.

Adding notes within charts invites your reader to learn more. Here I'm putting the 180 into context, it's likely my readers don't know whether 180mg is a large amount of caffeine.
From a quick Google search, I found that exceeding 600mg has some dangerous side effects so I've noted this and put it into context as how many Pret espressos that is.
This then sets the reader up to investigate the data further, i.e. how much weaker Starbucks expressos are, or consider their own purchasing decisions.

# Why it works better

Part of why it works better can be seen next to the horizontal bar chart; the numbers were put into context (rule: telling a story). Other than that, Will changed the table to a very simple bar chart; he focuses on one drink only and marks the highest possible health risk by imposing espresso in a dark red (rules: making a dashboard simple and robust, simple colors). He walks the user through a story, whereas the original table leaves a lot of room for context and answers to unknown questions.
