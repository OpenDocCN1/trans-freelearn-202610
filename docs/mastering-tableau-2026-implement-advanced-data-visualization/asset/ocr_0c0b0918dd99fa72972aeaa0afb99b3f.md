This story is terrifying because it could happen to any of us. We've all had that moment of panic after clicking "Delete" on the wrong file. In the past, recovery meant begging IT to restore from backup, if they had one, and if they could find the right version.

Starting with the 2025.1 release, Tableau Cloud and Tableau Server include a Recycle Bin that works just like the one on your computer. When you delete a workbook, data source, or project, it's not gone forever. It moves to the Recycle Bin, where it stays for up to 30 days (your admin can configure the exact retention period). You find your deleted item, click Restore, and it's back exactly where it belongs.

This is governance that protects you personally, not just the organization. Yes, it helps companies prevent data loss. But more importantly, it saves you from career-limiting mistakes and stress-filled evenings.

A few important details:

+   • You can only restore items you had permission to delete in the first place
+   • The Recycle Bin shows who deleted what and when
+   • Items are permanently deleted after the retention period expires
+   • Your admin can configure whether the Recycle Bin retains items for 7, 14, 30, or 60 days

The Recycle Bin protects the workbook itself—if you delete it entirely, you can bring it back.

# # The Data Orchestration Center

The 2026.1 release introduces a significant new capability for data governance: the Data Orchestration Center, as part of Tableau Next. Think of this as your centralized command hub for managing all data assets across your Tableau environment. While this tool is mainly used by Admins, it will come in handy for Data Stewards as well.

The Data Orchestration Center brings together previously scattered governance tools into one unified interface, allowing you to:

+   • Centrally manage all data assets – from data sources and Prep flows to extracts and semantic models
+   • Centralize oversight of data lineage, quality, and usage patterns
+   • Strengthen data trust through streamlined data preparation monitoring
+   • Monitor data health across your entire Tableau ecosystem from a single dashboard

For data governance practitioners, this is a gamechanger. Instead of jumping between different pages to check data source status, extract refresh health, and lineage information, you now have a single pane of glass. This directly supports the GDPR principle of accountability by making it easier to demonstrate control over your data assets.
