# Adding tracing

There may be some debate as to whether this section is required or not, as this section is specific to WCF but not related to sagas or NSB. However, if your WCF communication is not working between a WCF client and a WCF server, the issue could be related to the message structure, the network, the WSDL, or any other factor. And in order to trace the issues in WCF, the tracing feature needs to be enabled and set up. This also allows us to see the reaction of the communication. However, if your WCF is functional and no troubleshooting is required, feel free to skip this section.
