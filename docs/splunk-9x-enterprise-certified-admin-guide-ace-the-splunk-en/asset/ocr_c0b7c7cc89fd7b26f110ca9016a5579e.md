+   - Apps/add-ons that need to be deployed to forwarders are placed in the `$SPLUNK_HOME/etc/deployment-apps` directory.
+   - Apps that are deployed through the DS are stored in the forwarder client's `$SPLUNK_HOME/etc/apps` directory.

Let’s look at the configuration of `serverclass` in the next section.
