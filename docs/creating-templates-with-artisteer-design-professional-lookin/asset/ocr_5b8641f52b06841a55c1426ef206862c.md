WordPress related export options

After you export a WordPress template, it's then time to install it into the CMS:

+   1. Log into Administration Panel.
+   2. From the menu, navigate to **Appearance** | **Themes**.
+   3. Click on the **Install Themes** tab (on top of the screen).
+   4. Click on the **Upload** link.
+   5. Click on the **Browse** button, point to the exported theme file, and click on **Install Now**.

![](bbox=[0.26, 0.5, 0.74, 0.67])

Installing new template in WordPress

After a while, the template is installed, but if we want it to be displayed, we have to activate it.

+   6. Click on the **Activate** link. If you refresh the site, you will see that it's displayed with our template.
