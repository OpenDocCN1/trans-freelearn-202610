The overall way of installing Artisteer and non-Artisteer templates in a particular CMS is the same; so, it's rather a question of operating with a certain CMS solution than with Artisteer itself.

The most important thing you should know while designing for a particular CMS is the layout of the template areas that Artisteer generates for various platforms.
