# ## Summary

In this chapter, we showed you how to load graphics from a sprite sheet and
extract and play the animations. Then, we showed you how to move the
sprites around and change the animation to match the direction of travel.
Finally, we showed you how to animate multiple sprites independently and
detect when they collide with each other or the screen edges.
