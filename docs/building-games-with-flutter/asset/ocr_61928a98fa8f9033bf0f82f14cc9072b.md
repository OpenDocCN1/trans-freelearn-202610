+   21. Next, we need to add a shape for the `HitBox` mixin or it won't detect that it has collided with anything. There are a few built-in shapes that you can use depending on the shape of your sprite and which points of the sprite you want to use for collision detection. Most commonly, your sprites will be rectangular/square-shaped, so you could use `HitBoxRectangle` as your shape. However, if your sprite were a ball, you would use `HitBoxCircle` instead. You can also make custom shapes, but in most cases, that is unnecessary.
