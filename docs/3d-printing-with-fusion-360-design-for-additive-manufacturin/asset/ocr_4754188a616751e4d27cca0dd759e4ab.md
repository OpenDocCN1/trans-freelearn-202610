# Editing inserted Mesh bodies after converting them into solid bodies

Even though working with mesh bodies in their native state is a quick way to manipulate the entire body and add or remove certain features, we cannot create all the features necessary unless we are working with solid bodies. In such cases, we may need to convert a mesh body into a solid body.

When automatically converting mesh bodies into solid bodies, Fusion 360 offers three options:

+   - Faceted (available to all users)
+   - Prismatic (N/A for Personal Use)
+   - Organic (N/A for Personal Use and Requires Access to the Product Design Extension)
