Using the commands within this panel you can connect Fusion 360 to your HP Jet Fusion printers by entering your printer's IP address. You can also store and manage a list of all your HP printers within Fusion. After connecting to a specific printer, you can inquire about additional details from that printer and get the status of your previously submitted print jobs.
