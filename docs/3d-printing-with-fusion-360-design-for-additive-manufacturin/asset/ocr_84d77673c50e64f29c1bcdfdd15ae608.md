There are certain print settings you definitely want to modify based on your filament or resin, such as the layer height, the temperature with which you will extrude the filament, the temperature of the build plate, how many perimeters and top and bottom layers you wish to print to control the strength of your print, and your print bed adhesion settings.

You can also customize the body presets to your liking and later assign a unique body preset for each body within your additive setup. Using these techniques, you will have full control over the speed and the quality of your 3D prints.
