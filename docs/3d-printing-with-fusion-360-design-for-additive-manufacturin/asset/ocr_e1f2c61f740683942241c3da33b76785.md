This investigation tells us that the diameter of all the small holes is 1 mm, which is smaller than what our FFF printer could possibly print. So, we can simply delete these holes and drill holes once our 3D print is complete if they are absolutely necessary. To delete all 32 holes, we have several options. We can orient our view so we are looking at the model from View cube = TOP, select all the faces with rectangle select, and right-click and delete. This method was demonstrated in the right half of Figure 2.20. Alternatively, as shown in Figure 2.31, we can select the two rectangular patterns from the tree, right-click, and delete them, which will delete all the holes that belong to these patterns.
