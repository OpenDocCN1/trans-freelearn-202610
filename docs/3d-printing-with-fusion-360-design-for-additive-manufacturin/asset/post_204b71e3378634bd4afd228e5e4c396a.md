Even though both the DLP and SLA printers utilize resin as the raw material to 3D print with, they use different technologies to print that resin, and the print settings for such printers also reflect that difference in technology. In this print setting, you can customize the image size, the image quality, and the naming associated with each image file (* .png) during the export process.
