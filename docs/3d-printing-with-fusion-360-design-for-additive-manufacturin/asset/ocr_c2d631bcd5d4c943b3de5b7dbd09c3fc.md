As we covered in the previous subsection, whenever we hollow out a body, it is important to add drain holes so that the trapped resin or powder can be removed after 3D printing. If you are utilizing a volumetric lattice to hollow out a body, you can simply create a sketch, make a circle, and use the HOLE command to create a drain hole. In Figure 4.29, you can see the addition of a 2-millimeter-diameter drain hole.
