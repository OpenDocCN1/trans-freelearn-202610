In certain cases, we may not want to send our models to a 3D print utility but rather export them as a mesh file. Figure 5.16 shows how we can utilize the 3D PRINT dialog to export our models out of Fusion 360 as STL files. To achieve this, we simply need to uncheck the Send to 3D Print Utility checkbox and select OK.
