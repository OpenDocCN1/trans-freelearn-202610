Figure 5.12 shows that same mesh body being transferred as a 3MF file into a third-party 3D print utility called PreForm. Whenever we are exporting a mesh body or transferring it to a 3D print utility, the 3D PRINT dialog will automatically hide the irrelevant options, such as Preview Mesh, Number of Triangles, and Refinement, as those settings are only applicable when exporting a solid body as a mesh object.
