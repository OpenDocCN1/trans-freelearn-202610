The **Geometry** tab has additional functionality around how to select surfaces to avoid generating supports and how much gap there should be between each support.

If you have chosen to support an entire body or a component, by selecting the **Avoid** checkbox, as shown in *Figure 12.13*, you can select one or multiple faces to avoid creating support structures. If you activate the **Advanced Area Filter** checkbox, Fusion 360 gives you additional options around which area within your selected model/face to create support structures.
