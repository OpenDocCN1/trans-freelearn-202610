The final feature I want to highlight, which is unique to the parametric modeling methodology, is **Geometric Pattern**. You can access **Geometric Pattern** from the **CREATE | Pattern** slide-out menu. Figure 2.13 shows the outcome of a geometric pattern applied to a face on our model. Using this feature, you can parametrically create a modeled surface texture with ease. Just like the plastic rule and feature presets, **Geometric Pattern** also requires access to the Product Design extension.
