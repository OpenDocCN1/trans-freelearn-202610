Now that we have covered why we need drainage holes and how big they should be, let’s discuss where we generally should place them. In resin printing, it is best to place drainage holes as close to the build plate of the printer as possible in the orientation chosen to be for the part to be 3D printed. This means we have to orient our model for 3D printing before designing drain holes. We will cover arranging an orienting component in detail in Chapter 8. Once we have our orientation selected, we can place our drain holes on the bottom surface of our model close to the build plate. This will ensure that we don’t have pressure building up in our hollow model during the printing process for the resin printers. We don’t have the same issues about drain hole placement for SLS printers, as cupping and pressure buildup during printing is generally not an issue. This means we can place them anywhere on our model as long as they are away from critical surfaces.
