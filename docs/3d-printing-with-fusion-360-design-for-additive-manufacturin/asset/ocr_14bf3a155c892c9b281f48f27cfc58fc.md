It looks like we have high stress close to where an organic shape meets a prismatic shape close to the bottom of this part. Even though the stress results are low, and the factor of safety results are high, the location where the max stress is being displayed is a cause for concern and could be a potential failure point depending on which material we print this object with and/or the 3D printing method we choose.
