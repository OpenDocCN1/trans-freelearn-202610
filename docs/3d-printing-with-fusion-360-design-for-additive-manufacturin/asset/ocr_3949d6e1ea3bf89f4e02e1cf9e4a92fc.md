In some cases, you may need to do additional work to achieve the desired face group outcome. You can start by combining face groups in parametric mode or accessing the Direct edit mode to create face groups manually. Once you have all the face groups needed, you can utilize the PRISMATIC conversion method, as shown in Figure 2.43, and convert your mesh body into a solid body. At this point, you are ready to add any new features, including complex shapes such as fillets and chamfers.
