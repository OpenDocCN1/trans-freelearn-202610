# About the Author

**Tameem Bahri** is a Salesforce Certified Technical Architect with a demonstrated work history in the information technology and business consultation industry. He has undertaken several roles in his career, including the Global Engineering Director for CRM at A.P. Moller - Maersk and the European Salesforce Chief Technology Officer at Capgemini. He has in-depth experience across business transformation, digital services, innovation, process design and redesign, enterprise system security, Identity and Access Management (IAM), and enterprise solution architecture.

He has worked across several industries, such as supply chain and logistics, manufacturing, CPRD, travel and transportation, energy and utilities, property management, and software development. He led dozens of Salesforce implementations worldwide and mentored several architects to become Salesforce Certified Technical Architects.

He lives in Dubai, UAE, with his wife and son. In his spare time, he enjoys learning about history, walking in nature, reading classic literature, and watching football.
