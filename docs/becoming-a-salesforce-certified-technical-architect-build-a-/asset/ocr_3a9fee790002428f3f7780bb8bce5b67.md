# About the Reviewers

**Mustapha El Hassak** is a Salesforce Certified Technical Architect (CTA) with extensive expertise, holding 17 Salesforce certifications. With over 13 years of experience in the IT industry, he has established himself as a Senior Technical Architect. His main focus lies in designing and delivering SaaS solutions using Salesforce Customer 360 Platform technologies.

He has contributed to the industry as a Technical Reviewer for respected books such as ‘Talend Open Studio Cookbook’ and ‘Creating Actionable Insights Using CRM Analytics’ and has had presented at various Salesforce community events.

Throughout his career, he has taken on significant roles, successfully managing data analysis, data quality, and data migration projects at two Moroccan companies and overseeing the transition from legacy databases to new solutions. Moreover, he has led numerous Salesforce implementations on a global scale and currently serves as a mentor to aspiring architects pursuing the Salesforce Certified Technical Architect certification.

Residing in Casablanca, Morocco, Mustapha values a well-rounded life, cherishing quality time with his wife and two children. During his leisure hours, he enjoys reading productivity books and practices martial arts, including Aikido and Judo.

**Nitin Sharma**, a senior manager at Deloitte UK, is an accomplished Salesforce Certified Technical Architect (CTA) with a wealth of experience spanning 14 years. His expertise encompasses CRM architecture, security and data modeling, enterprise integration, business process design, and transformation. Starting his career as a CRM business analyst, Nitin has steadily progressed to become a Salesforce Technical Architect.

Throughout his career, Nitin has consistently delivered successful multi-cloud digital transformation programs for global clients. He has worked across diverse sectors, including manufacturing, insurance, banking, telecom, and private industries. Alongside his passion for the Salesforce platform and CTA coaching, Nitin harbours a keen interest in automation. During his leisure time, he enjoys building a smart home.
