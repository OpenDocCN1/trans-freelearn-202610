An accessibility-first mindset means putting inclusivity at the core of every decision. It shifts the focus from designing for an assumed “default user” to designing for everyone.

Ask yourself: Who am I creating this for? What is the intended purpose and outcome? Have I considered how this feature will work for users relying on a screen reader, a keyboard, or voice control? These questions lead to better, more thoughtful designs that work for all users.

Neglecting accessibility isn’t just a missed opportunity – it excludes people from life-changing products and services. By making inclusion a priority, you’re building a fair, equitable, and welcoming web.
