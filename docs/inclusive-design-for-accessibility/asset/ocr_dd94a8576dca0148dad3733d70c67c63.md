We must avoid 100% white paired with 100% black. For people with astigmatism, this stark contrast can cause text to appear uncomfortably vibrant, especially with white text on a black background.

# Affordances for magnification tools

People with very low vision may also use magnification software, allowing them to magnify very small portions of their screen. On handheld devices, this often means pinching/zooming in.

Seeing only a small portion of the screen at a time means people with low vision will struggle to find content and controls arbitrarily scattered across the page.

While some controls are unquestionably grouped with their labels, such as checkboxes or radio buttons, toggle switches and expanders are often separated to opposite sides of the content, creating barriers for people with low vision.
