Remember when I told you that the Lumion interface is intuitive and friendly? This section is an example of that. You can create a movie in the simplest and easiest way possible.

At the bottom of the preceding screenshot we can see nine clips. Each clip can store a small animation, or if you want, the entire movie. Lumion also gives you the possibility of importing an image or a video. So, perhaps we want to start our video by showing our logo; we don't need to do this in another application. Now, we can edit the clip again if needed. Click on the clip and we have two buttons inside it: one that allows us to go back and edit the clip, and the other to delete it. This is not the only way to do it. If you look at the top-left corner, we have four new buttons:
