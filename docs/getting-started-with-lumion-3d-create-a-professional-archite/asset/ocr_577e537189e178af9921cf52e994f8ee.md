# The importance of framing

One important thing in composition is using the concept of framing. What is framing? Framing is when we use certain elements in the scene to focus our viewer's attention to certain aspects of the scene. Have a look at the following screenshot and see, for example, how we have used trees on each side to turn the attention of the viewer to the house:

![](bbox=[0.18, 0.266, 0.82, 0.545])

We can use almost everything in the scene to frame the shot, including the trees, rocks, and other buildings just to mention a few.
