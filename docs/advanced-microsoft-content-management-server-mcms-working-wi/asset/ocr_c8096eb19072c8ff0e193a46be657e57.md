For postings to be included in the RSS feed, they must have been created or modified and published in the last 20 days, as this is the timeframe we configured in the Web.config.

# Testing the RSS Feed in an Aggregator

If you have an RSS aggregator on your computer, you can test the RSS feed by pointing your aggregator at `http://localhost/TropicalGreen/UtilityPages/rss.aspx`. Below is a screenshot of the feed being aggregated by RSS Bandit (www.rssbandit.org), which is one of the most popular of the free RSS aggregators.
