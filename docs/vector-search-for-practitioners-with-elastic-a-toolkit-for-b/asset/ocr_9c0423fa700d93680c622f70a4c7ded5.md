Far from being isolated units, these components are intricately linked, ensuring that our chatbot’s insights are not only accurate but also timely and contextually relevant.

As we progress deeper into this chapter, these components will act as our navigational beacons, guiding us through the architecture and flow of the DCL system. Let’s embark on this journey by first understanding each of these foundational pillars.
