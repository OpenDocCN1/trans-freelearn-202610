# EIP life cycle

The life cycle of a proposal life cycle is pretty straightforward. On a typical happy path, a proposal is born with an idea of improvement for Ethereum. Such an idea is formalized by a draft EIP. Once the EIP has gained the confidence of the Ethereum community, the proposal is accepted and assigned an EIP number. Then it is implemented using code, and given a Final status, or if it is a guideline, made active on GitHub. The EIP list is eventually updated with the finalized EIP proposal number. The life cycle of a proposal can dramatically change if it is critically opposed by the general community:

Technically, "life cycle" is a misnomer. We must generally refer to it as a proposal workflow. This is because the proposal does not change its state itself; external human intervention is required to change the status of a proposal. Figure 11.6 illustrates the different statuses of a proposal, starting from a draft, until it is finalized and updated on the EIP list. Also note that EIPs can be superseded, making the older EIP obsolete. For informational EIPs, making them Active is analogous to a final state.
