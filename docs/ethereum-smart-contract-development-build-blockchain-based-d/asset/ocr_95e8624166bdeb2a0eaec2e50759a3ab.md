More secure wallets include paper wallets and hardware wallets, which are often called cold wallets. These basically store the tokens in offline mode. A paper wallet is stored as scanned QR codes representing the receiving address, along with a passcode, which must be remembered. In simple words, you print your keys on a paper so that they do not get hacked. Hardware wallets have hardwired encryption over a USB enabled device, which opens only with a passcode.
