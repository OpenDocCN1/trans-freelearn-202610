As you can see, converting the lead basically turns the lead into a contact and
optionally allows you to simultaneously create an account, an opportunity, or even
an appointment. If you wish, you may also create a note or attachment (an uploaded
file, such as a Microsoft Word or Excel file) to be linked to the **Account** record.

**Opportunity** box to enter data corresponding with the potential sale of replacement
windows. Note that you must provide an amount and expected close date for
the opportunity. When you are done, click on the **Save** button to save all the new
information records at once. You will then see a screen like the following, which
