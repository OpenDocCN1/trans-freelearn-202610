One key field for each lead is the **Status** field. It is automatically set to **New** when  
a lead is first entered and should be set to **Assigned** when the lead is assigned to a  
particular sales person. It automatically becomes **Converted** if the lead is converted  
into an opportunity. Diligent use of this field allows you to effectively measure the  
number of new leads your business is obtaining, as well as gauging the number that  
have been converted into potential sales or opportunities.
