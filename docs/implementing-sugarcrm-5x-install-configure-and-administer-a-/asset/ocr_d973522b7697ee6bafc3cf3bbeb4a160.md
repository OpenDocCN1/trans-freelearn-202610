# ## 2. Opportunities by lead source by outcome chart:

This chart is handy for
giving you a quick visual indicator of which lead sources are producing
the greatest value of opportunities. The color-coded, stacked bars help
demonstrate which lead sources are especially good, or bad, at being
converted into sales. It shows Doc whether his money is better invested
in advertising replacement doors and windows in a home improvement
magazine, or on the TV Guide channel in his local market.
