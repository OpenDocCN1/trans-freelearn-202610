# ## 3. The outcome by month chart

This chart shows projected and closed sales on a per-month basis. The stacked bars break down the opportunities depending on whether their sales stage is closed won, closed lost, or anything else (meaning the opportunity has its expected close date in the month shown, but it has yet to close in favor of our business, or a competitor).
