# A screen similar to the following should appear:

![](bbox=[0.214, 0.092, 0.739, 0.531])

Once you have finished entering your SugarCRM connection details, click on **Connect** and then click on the **OK** button to close the screen. Now return to Outlook and we will take a look at the various features that the connector offers.

Perhaps the most important feature that the connector offers is the ability to archive e-mails from Outlook to SugarCRM. The process of synchronizing e-mails is quite simple. To archive a message to SugarCRM select it from within Outlook and then click on the GrinMark toolbar.
