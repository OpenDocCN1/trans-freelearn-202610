Another key field is the **Lead Source**. When combined with the status field, it
becomes easier to measure the proportion of leads you are generating per lead
source, usually representative of a specific of marketing campaign. Such information
is considered a key business enabler, as it allows you to quickly see where your
marketing dollars are best invested, and which yield the most actual revenue.
