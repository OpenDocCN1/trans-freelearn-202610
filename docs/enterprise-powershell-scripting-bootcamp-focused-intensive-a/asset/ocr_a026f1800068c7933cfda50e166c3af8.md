The result of the drive type is then placed in the $type variable for use in the final output of the script. At the very end of the `Foreach` loop, you run the `write-host` cmdlet to output the result of `Drive $driveletter | Drive Type: $type | Size: $convsize $sunit | Freespace: $convFreeSpc $funit` for that disk iteration. After executing the script, all of the disk drive letters, drive types, sizes, free space, and appropriate units of measure are returned to the screen.
