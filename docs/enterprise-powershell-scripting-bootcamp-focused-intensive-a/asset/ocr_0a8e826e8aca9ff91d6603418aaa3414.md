There are two primary methods to modify a scheduled task:

+   1. You may choose to unregister and recreate a scheduled task.
+   2. You may choose to leverage the set-scheduledtask cmdlet. The set-ScheduledTask cmdlet allows you to call a specific scheduled task by the -TaskName parameter, and specify new or additional triggers, actions, and scheduled task settings.
