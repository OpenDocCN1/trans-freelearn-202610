If you plan to develop interactive reports in complex use cases, always remember to consider other solutions more related to dashboard frameworks or OLAP clients. The suggestion is to always remember that the reporting solutions are great, but their main goal is to show detailed or aggregated data in a static or parameterized content. If you force the use of the reporting solutions to develop dashboards or something really dynamic and interactive, the risk of a failure is high. Be aware!
