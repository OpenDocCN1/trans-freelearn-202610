If you want to see how a data stream looks, click on the Preview button. Once done, click the OK button to confirm the Data Grid setup.

Back to the Design tab in the left panel, expand the Output set, and drag the Pentaho Reporting Output step and drop it into the right panel, on the right of the Data Grid step. To instruct the transformation to execute the two steps in the right sequence, click on the Data Grid step, and drag on the right connection icon and drop it to the Pentaho Reporting Output step.
