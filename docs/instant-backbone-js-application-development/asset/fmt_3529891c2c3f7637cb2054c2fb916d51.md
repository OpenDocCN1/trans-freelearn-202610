## Introduction

Welcome to *Instant Backbone.js Application Development*. This book will guide you through the steps of downloading and installing Backbone.js and its dependent libraries, getting a simple Hello World app running to confirm your installation, and then finally going through each of the different classifications of code so that you can build a simple contact manager. You'll even build a quick server app to persist your data.
