We define three properties—post, authUser, and commentsPerPost—and decorate them with the @Input decorator to enable the post component to receive their values from its parent component(s) (the profile and feed's posts components).

We use the non-null assertion operator (!) to assert that the post input will not be null. Check out https://www.typescriptlang.org/docs/handbook/release-notes/typescript-2-0.html#non-null-assertion-operator for more on this.
