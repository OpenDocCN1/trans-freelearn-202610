For example, if we analyze the https://www.packtpub.com/ site, we can see that on the date of the test it got a **B** grade. This is due to its support for weak Diffie-Hellman parameters and the obsolete and insecure **RC4** cipher. However, it is not always as simple as removing old ciphers. You can have a very secure site, but you might exclude some of your customers, who use older clients that don’t support the latest standards. This will, of course, vary depending on the nature of your client base, and you should measure your traffic and consider your options carefully.
