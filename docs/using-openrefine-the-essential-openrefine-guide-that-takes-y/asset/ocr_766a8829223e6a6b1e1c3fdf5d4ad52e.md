Now back to our record IDs. Since the duplicates facet is of no use on integers, we will use a workaround to detect duplicates in that column. To achieve that, we will first sort the data on their IDs by navigating to Record ID | Sort... and toggling numbers and smallest first. As you may recall from Chapter 1, Diving Into OpenRefine, sorting is but a visual aid, so the reordering needs to be made permanent by going to the Sort menu that has just appeared (right of Show: 5 10 25 50 rows), and clicking on Reorder rows permanently. If you forget to reorder the rows, further operations will ignore the sorting and yield unexpected results.
