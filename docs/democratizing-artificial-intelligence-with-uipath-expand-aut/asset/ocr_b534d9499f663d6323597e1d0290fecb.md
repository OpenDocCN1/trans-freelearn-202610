# How to test RPA development

Testing RPA development in a comprehensive manner is pivotal to gaining the confidence of the business and building a successful automation program. However, there are many components of RPA that could lead to exceptions, such as the following:

+   - **Application issues**: Technical changes; customizations
+   - **Automation issues**: Business logic changes
+   - **Environment issues**: System updates; desktop changes
