The UiPath chatbot with Druid has six key benefits:

+   - 500+ pre-built conversation skills:

The UiPath chatbot with Druid has over 500 pre-built skills for business scenarios across multiple industries and roles. These conversation skills include replying to questions, sending notifications, delivering reports, tracking smart tasks, filling in forms, and routing to humans. Druid is a no-code authoring platform that allows RPA developers to create and deploy chatbots quickly without an in-depth technical knowledge of AI.

+   - Configurable training model:

The UiPath chatbot with Druid is equipped with comprehensive training tools and learning mechanisms to improve the knowledge and performance of the chatbot.
