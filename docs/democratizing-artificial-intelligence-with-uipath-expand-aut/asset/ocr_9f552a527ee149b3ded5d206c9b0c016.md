**The UiPath platform** covers the full cycle of the automation life cycle: Discover, Build, Manage, Engage, and Run. Each pillar of the automation life cycle follows the guiding principles of a versatile platform:

+   - **Discover, Build, and Engage pillars:** UiPath products are designed to allow for a variety of skill levels to be able to use UiPath to start engaging, discovering, and building automation. The products always keep in mind pre-built templates, reusable components, low-code/no-code options, and flexible configuration.
+   - **Maintain and Run pillars:** UiPath products are also designed for enterprise scaling. The platform is secured and managed and has a governance hub to ensure things are secured from data to applications, can sustain constant changes internally and externally, can optimize resources on a large scale, and can monitor and proactively detect issues.
