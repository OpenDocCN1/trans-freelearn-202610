
8. With our pistons working, let's add some rotation to the gear. Slide the time slider to frame `5` and rotate the bone that controls it `45` degrees by pressing `R`, typing in `45`, and pressing `Enter`. Press `I` and select the `Rotation` option from the menu. Slide the time slider to frame `10` and repeat. Repeat this process until you have recorded new rotation keyframes for the gear bone and five frame intervals.

9. Slide the time slider back and forth over the whole animation and test how it looks.
