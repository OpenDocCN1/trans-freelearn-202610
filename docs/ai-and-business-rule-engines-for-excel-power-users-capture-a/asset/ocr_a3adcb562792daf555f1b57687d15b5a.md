Think of a space like a solution – in our chocolate examples in the previous chapters we included all of the decision models (for sales and marketing, and for production) in one folder. But in reality, using Business Central, each of those different departments would have its own space.

Clicking into a space shows that it can contain multiple projects, as shown in Figure 10.12, each comparable to a folder in KIE Sandbox. While these levels or nested folders for organizing our decision models and other assets might be confusing when we first open Business Central, for large projects, you’ll appreciate the structure that they give.
