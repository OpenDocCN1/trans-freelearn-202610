> **Not a great prediction? Rubbish in, rubbish out**
> Since some people view ML as a magical black box, it's worth reminding ourselves that the quality of the model is only as good as the training data used.
> In this case, we need to be wary of **overfitting** since the sample data size is so small—the model works very well with customers who are exactly like the small batch we used in training. But the model has no experience to deal with anything outside of this and will easily get confused.
