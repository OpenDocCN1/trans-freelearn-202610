This book is about aligning AI agents with human intent. Capabilities of AI have improved exponentially over the period of last two decades, and some milestones in disciplines such as machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing, deep learning, and **reinforcement learning** (`RL`) played a prominent role in this rise. Some of these capabilities and applications demonstrate how well AI scales and impacts almost everything. This makes it very important that the behavior or actions taken by AI align with human intent, and this very necessity is also the path towards safety of using this technology. In this book, we will delve into **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback** (`RLHF`), which played a key role in the success of generated pretrained models and brought it to mainstream technology usage. The first part of the book introduces `RLHF` from a general perspective and develops the readers understanding of core technological components necessary for applying `RLHF` to any application. The second part dives into applying `RLHF` for language modeling especially large language modeling. The final part provides the readers with current trends and what lies ahead for this technology. In this chapter, we introduce `RL` to the readers by first covering the essentials of `RL` and key concepts for understanding `RL` algorithms by explaining their categories at a high level. Then we introduce a specific simple classic environment called the grid world, and show how `Q-learning`, a fundamental `RL` algorithm be implemented. Using the basic implementation for comparison and context, we then detail how reward functions can be designed, and how transfer learning can be implemented to accelerate `RL` training. Finally, we show how **Human Feedback** can help the `RL` based training, with a hands-on implementation on the same example, so that readers can clearly compare improvements over classic `Q-learning` implementation. This gives readers an idea of how `RLHF` works in practice and sets the stage for rest of the chapters to delve into more involved algorithms and architectures. We will cover the following main headings in this chapter:

- Essentials of Reinforcement Learning
- Understanding RL Algorithms
- Navigating RL Environments: A Grid World Environment
- Designing Reward Functions and Transfer Learning
- Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback
