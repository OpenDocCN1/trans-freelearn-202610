# Compound wound DC motors

This type of motor has the field winding (housed in the stator) in series with the armature winding and a shunt field that is excited separately. The shunt field winding is connected across the field supply and the series field winding is connected in series with the armature. When DC is supplied, both the shunt field winding and the series field winding produce the required magnetic field that interacts with the armature to produce the necessary turning force that creates rotation. Compound wound DC motor has the characteristics of both the series and shunt types of DC motor.
