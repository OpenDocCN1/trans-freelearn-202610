When you simulate the program, you will find out that when I0.0 is not marked (low), the value of MW0 is 0, and since 0 is less than 5, the output Q0.0 is de-energized. When I0.0 is marked (high), MW0 becomes 20, and since 20 is greater than 5, the output Q0.0 becomes energized.
