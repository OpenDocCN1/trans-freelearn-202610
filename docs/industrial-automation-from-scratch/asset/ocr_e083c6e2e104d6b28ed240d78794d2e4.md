This section explains electrical diagrams and the various types available. You should now be able to differentiate between schematic diagrams, pictorial diagrams, ladder diagrams, and wiring diagrams, which most people find difficult to differentiate between. Many people think they all mean the same thing, but they actually have different meanings.
