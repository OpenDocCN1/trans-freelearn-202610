# Common industrial network protocol – PROFINET

PROFINET is an industrial Ethernet protocol designed to exchange data between devices and controllers in industrial automation. It is the most well-adopted industrial Ethernet protocol. It can operate at a higher speed than PROFIBUS because it is Ethernet-based. The connector that’s used for PROFINET is a standard RJ45 connector (Figure 13.13) and the network cable can be a standard Ethernet cable such as those you find in offices, such as twisted pair CAT 6 cables (Figure 13.9).
