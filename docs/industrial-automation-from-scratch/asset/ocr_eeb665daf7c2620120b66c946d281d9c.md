# Shunt DC motors

This type of motor has the field winding (housed in the stator) in parallel with the armature winding. It is powered by DC and the total current splits into two parallel paths to supply the field winding and the armature winding. As the current flows through the stator and armature winding, a magnetic field is produced. The interaction between the magnetic fields of the stator and rotor produce the turning force that creates rotation.
