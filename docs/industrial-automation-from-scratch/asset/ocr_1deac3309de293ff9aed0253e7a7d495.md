Figure 4.14 – Wiring diagram of a DOL starter

The DOL wiring diagram can be divided into two parts: the power circuit (left) and control circuit (right).

In the power circuit (left), the circuit breaker connects to a three-phase power supply (L1, L2, and L3). The other end of the circuit breaker connects to a contactor at terminals 1, 3, and 5. Terminals 2, 4, and 6 of the contactor then connect to an overload relay while the other end of the overload relay (T1, T2, and T3) connects to a three-phase motor as shown in the figure.
