# Contactor

A **contactor** is an electrically operated switch just like a relay. It is a switch that can be operated electrically unlike manual and mechanically operated switches. It usually consists of at least one coil, and up to three NO contacts. In some instances, an auxiliary contact that operates with the main contact is included. The terminals of the coil are usually labeled A1 and A2. When a current is supplied to the coil through terminals A1 and A2, it becomes energized and the magnetic field forces the contacts to close simultaneously. When the power is removed, the contacts become open.
