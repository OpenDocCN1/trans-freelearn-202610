# Photoelectric

A **photoelectric** sensor consists of a light emitter (*sender*), photoreceiver, and supporting circuitry. They are used to detect the presence or absence of objects by using a light emitter and a receiver.

The emitter transmits a beam of either visible or invisible light to the detecting receiver.

There are three types of photoelectric sensors, as follows:

+   1. **Through beam**: With this sensor, the emitter (sender) and the receiver are in a separate housing and positioned to face each other.
