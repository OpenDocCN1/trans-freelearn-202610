+   • **Revolutions per Minute (RPM)**: This refers to the speed of the shaft at the rated frequency, the rated load, and the rated voltage. It is the number of complete rotations the rotor’s shaft will make in 1 minute.
+   • **Frequency**: This is the frequency of the supply that the motor is designed to operate.
