Note

An open protocol is a protocol that can be used by any company or organization. The manufacturer of an open protocol provides the protocol and allows anyone to use it freely without any charge or conditions of use, while a proprietary protocol is a protocol developed and owned by a specific manufacturer and used by the devices that they manufacture.
