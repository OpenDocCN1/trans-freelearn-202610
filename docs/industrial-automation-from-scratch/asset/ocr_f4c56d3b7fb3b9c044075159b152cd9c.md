This is most common among several PLC programming languages. It is a graphical programming language that's easy to understand by most plant technicians because it's similar to the relay diagrams that they are familiar with. **Normally Open (NO)** or **Normally Closed (NC)** contacts are used as input. Specific outputs are activated depending on the logic performed on the input.
