The difference between the rotor's speed and the speed of the rotating magnetic field of the stator is referred to as slip. One major fact about induction motors is that the rotor current is created by induction instead of being separately excited as in synchronous motors or being self-magnetized using a permanent magnet.

Depending upon the power supply input, there are basically two types of induction motor, which include single-phase and three-phase. Three-phase induction motors are more commonly used in manufacturing and other industries. They have a high starting torque, low costs, low maintenance costs, and are durable. They can be used in conveyors, pumps, blowers, mixers, lifts, crushers, and so on.
