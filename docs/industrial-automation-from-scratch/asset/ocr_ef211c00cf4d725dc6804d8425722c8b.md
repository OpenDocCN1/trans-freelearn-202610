When the switch at I0.0 in the wiring is not ON, channel I0.0 is not ON, the timer will not start, and Q0.1 will be energized (ON) since a normally closed contact is used at the input side of the network (network 2) in the program. When I0.0 is ON, the timer will start and, after 25 seconds, Q0.0 will be ON while Q0.1 will be OFF because the timer’s output is energized (high) and that will open the normally closed contact used in network 2.
