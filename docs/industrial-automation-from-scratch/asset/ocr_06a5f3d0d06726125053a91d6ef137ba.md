# Note

A computer system that performs some kind of control function is represented by a hexagon.

The ISA symbols in *Table 12.1* to *Table 12.4* can be summarized here for easier understanding:

+   - A shape with no line running through its center represents an instrument or device located in the field.
+   - A shape with a single horizontal line running through its center means the instrument or device is located at the front of the main panel (visible at the front of the panel).
+   - A shape with a dotted line running through its center indicates that the instrument or function is located at the rear of the main panel (not visible).
