+   - **Competitive edge:** Why should anyone choose your app over others? It’s essential to pinpoint what you’re doing better than anyone else. Ideally, there should be a unique problem that your product addresses better than any other solution in the market.
+   - **Features and benefits:** Beyond just functionality, what are the key features and benefits that your app offers? These could be technical aspects, user-friendly designs, or even cost-effectiveness.
+   - **Differentiation:** What is the distinct value that sets your product apart from similar applications in the market? This differentiation is the crux of your value proposition and should be prominently highlighted.

In conclusion, a well-defined value proposition is the narrative that will guide your product’s journey. It’s the story you tell potential customers, partners, and even your team. As you progress, this narrative will shape your marketing and sales strategies, ensuring your product not only finds its audience but also thrives in a competitive environment.
