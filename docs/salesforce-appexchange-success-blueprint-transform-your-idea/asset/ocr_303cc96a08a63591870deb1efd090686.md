# Key features

AppExchange Marketplace Analytics allows Salesforce partners to delve into various facets of customer interaction with their listings. These include the following:

+   - The frequency of customers viewing your listing tile on AppExchange’s home and search results pages
+   - The contribution of traffic sources, such as Google Ads, to customer activity on your listing
+   - The top AppExchange search terms that lead customers to your listing
+   - The listing resources, such as screenshots and white papers, that customers engage with
+   - The performance of your **AppExchange Marketing Program (AMP)** promotions, which we are going to discuss in detail in [Chapter 12](Chapter 12)
