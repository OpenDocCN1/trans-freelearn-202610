# Encouraging virality

Word-of-mouth referrals are an incredibly powerful growth driver. Encourage users to invite others to use your product by building sharing features directly into your product. For B2B products, this could be as simple as allowing users to collaborate with their colleagues within the app. Incentives, such as additional features or storage space, can also encourage users to make referrals.
