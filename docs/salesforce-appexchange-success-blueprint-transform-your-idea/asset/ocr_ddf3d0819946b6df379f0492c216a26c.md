This formula field is designed to display a warning image and text when the renewal date is within 45 days of the current date. The issue with this formula field is that it does not account for the possibility of the renewal date being in the past. To fix this, the formula should be modified to check if the renewal date is in the past and display a different warning image and text if it is.
