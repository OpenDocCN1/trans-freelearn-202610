# Building exceptional applications

Creating an outstanding UX isn’t a one-person job, nor does it end with deployment—it’s a collaborative, continuous process that echoes the voice of the user from inception to implementation and beyond. UI and UX designers, along with researchers, ensure that this voice resonates throughout all stages of product development, harmonizing user needs with business goals and technical requirements.

Dr. Ralf Speth, Chief Executive Officer of Jaguar Land Rover, famously said, “If you think good design is expensive, you should look at the cost of bad design.” This wisdom resonates strongly with our journey. Ignoring UX can have substantial costs, from increased customer support to additional development work (and rework), and even risks product failure. It’s akin to launching a ship without checking for leaks—risky and, quite likely, disastrous.
