# The importance of trusted reviews on AppExchange

Trusted reviews on AppExchange enable customers to understand which reviews are written by select Trailblazers, making them more impactful. They allow customers to easily identify feedback from the community’s most active, knowledgeable, and helpful members, making it easier for them to make informed decisions for their business.

Having a healthy collection of reviews on your AppExchange listing is crucial for not only the success of your customers but also the success of your business as a partner. Trusted reviews can influence and potentially drive even greater demand for your app, and having more recent positive reviews can positively impact your performance in the AppExchange Partner Program.
