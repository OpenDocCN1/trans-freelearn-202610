+   2. **Selection of a sign-up form hosting option:** Evaluate and opt for a suitable hosting solution for your sign-up form. +   3. **Creating sign-ups through the API:** Implement API calls to the `SignupRequest` object for the creation of sign-ups aimed at potential customers, allowing you to customize the sign-up journey.

+   5. **Provisioning trial orgs:** Leverage Trialforce to provision a no-cost trial of your solution for potential customers.
