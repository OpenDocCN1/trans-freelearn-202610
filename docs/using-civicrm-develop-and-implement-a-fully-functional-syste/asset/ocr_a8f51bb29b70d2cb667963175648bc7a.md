At this point, the mailing enters the CiviMail queue. It won't actually be sent until the mailing script (`civicmail.cronjob.php`) is run. Normally, this will be set up as a cron job on your server and scheduled to poll the queue at regular intervals and start mailings. Understand that it will take time for the mailing to be processed. Initially, there will be a delay as the mailing won't begin until the next cron job is run. Thereafter CiviCRM will process the mailings as fast as your hosting environment and resources permit.
