# Implementing an appeal

As we have seen, fundraising plans generally include several different fundraising programs, such as converting one-time givers into pre-authorized monthly donors, holding a fundraising dinner, or prospecting for new donors. In this section, we will use CiviCRM to deliver an appeal across many channels: online, direct mail, telemarketing, and direct contact.

# Planning

An appeal is a targeted message to constituents encouraging them to donate for a specific purpose – such as a project or new initiative. Standard elements of a simple fundraising appeal plan may include:

+   - **Segment**: Who is to be asked to donate?
+   - **Goal**: What total amount of giving is the appeal aiming to raise, and what will the funds be used for?
+   - **Case**: What reasons are to be provided to donate? This should be a refinement of the general case for giving in the overall fundraising plan. It is usually best if there is an emotional component and a sense of urgency in the communications.
+   - **Ask**: What is the specific thing to be asked for? (For example, a one-time gift larger than last year's, any gift, an increase in the monthly pledge, and so on.)
+   - **Face**: Who is the face of the campaign? What name will the letters and e-mails come from?
