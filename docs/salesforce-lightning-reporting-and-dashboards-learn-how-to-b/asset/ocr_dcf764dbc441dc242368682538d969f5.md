There are multiple ways to open a report in Salesforce Lightning. First, click on the **Reports** tab.

# The Reports tab

You will see two main panels here: the left-hand side panel and the right-hand side panel. The left-hand side panel contains categories of **Reports** and **Folders**, and the right-hand side panel will show a list of reports or folders related to the menu selected in the left-hand side panel.

# The Reports menu

Once you click on the **Reports** tab, you will see all things related to reports. There are two main categories on the left panel, which are **Reports** and **Folders**.
