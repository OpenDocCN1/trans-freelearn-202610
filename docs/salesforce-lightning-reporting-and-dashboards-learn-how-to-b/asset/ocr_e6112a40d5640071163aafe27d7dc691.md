# Stacked Bar charts

Stacked bar charts are great to compare absolute values between bars. To use a stacked bar chart, the report must have at least two groupings, and one of the groupings must be a number.

You can configure the stacked bar chart to 100%; this enables you to compare relative values for each bar. For example, the stacked bar lets you compare the proportion of opportunity among sales representatives.
