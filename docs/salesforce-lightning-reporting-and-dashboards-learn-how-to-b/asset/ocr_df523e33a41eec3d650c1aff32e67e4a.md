Once you apply the filter, the data will be filtered to show only opportunities owned by users in that role, including all users under **Users** in that role hierarchy.

But if you would like to filter the opportunities owned by a specific user, narrow your **Role Hierarchy** filter by selecting the role and then select a user. Check the following screenshot for filtering opportunity report with **Role Hierarchy**:
