# ## Reports / Dashboards tab and menu

When you click on the **Reports** tab, by default, you will see a list of reports recently opened. In the left panel, you will find a menu with the following items:

+   - **Created by Me**
+   - **Private Reports**
+   - **Public Reports**
+   - **All Reports**
