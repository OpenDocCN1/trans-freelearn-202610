+   - **Narrow Band Width** is the thickness of the band of particles near the top of the fluid. Turning this value up can result in the entire simulation being filled with particles. This can be useful if that is what you are trying to create! Otherwise, it’s recommended to not turn up this value because it will slow down the simulation.
