# Setting up the camera

Let’s set up the camera and position it where we want:

+   1. Press 0 on your numpad to go into **Camera View** or go up to **View | Cameras | Active Camera**.
+   2. To move the camera around easily, go into the **Properties** panel (*N*) section and, in the **View** tab, check the **Camera to View** checkbox. Now, you can move the camera around just like how you would move around the 3D space:
+   3. Move the camera to where you can see the entire simulation. Then, make sure to uncheck **Camera to View**; otherwise, you may accidentally move the camera when you don’t mean to.
