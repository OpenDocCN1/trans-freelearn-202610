To use **Property Weights**, you first create a vertex group and assign vertices to it. In this example, we have assigned half of the vertices on this plane a weight of 1 (left):

Then, in the **Property Weights** tab, we set **Max Tension** and **Max Compression** to 100. We also set **Max Bending** to 15 and assigned that vertex group to each of the four **Property Weight** groups.
