In this case, we are adding transparency to the middle of **Cylinder 1**, but the edges will still have that glass shader.

+   10. Finally, to control how much transparency there is in the glass, add a **Color Ramp** node and place it between the **Fresnel** node and the **Mix Shader** node.
+   11. The left side of the **Color Ramp** node controls the transparency in the middle of the glass and the right side controls the edges. Since the left side is completely black at default, this means the glass is 100% transparent. We do want a little bit of reflection, so let's set the left handle to be more of a gray color.
