# Part 3:
Diving into Rigid Bodies

The Rigid Body simulation is all about creating motion with solid objects in Blender. Over the next two chapters, we will learn all about rigid bodies, what they are, and how they work. We will go through all the settings and values and see how to use them effectively in Blender. By the end, we will have created a really cool and interesting rigid body obstacle course animation!
