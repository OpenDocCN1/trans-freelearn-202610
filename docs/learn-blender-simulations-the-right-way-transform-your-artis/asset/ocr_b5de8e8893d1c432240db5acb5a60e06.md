IV. Currently, the texture is a bit too big for our object, so let's set Size to 0.1.

V. When using textures for inflows, they act as a mask for where the fire will emit. The white parts of the texture will emit fire and the black parts won't. To get a bit more definition between the white and black parts of the texture, let's set Contrast in the Color tab to 2. This will give us sharper edges on the texture.
