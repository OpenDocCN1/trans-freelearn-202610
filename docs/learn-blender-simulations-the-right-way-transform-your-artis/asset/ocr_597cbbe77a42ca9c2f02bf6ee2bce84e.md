+   3. **F-Stop** controls how strong the depth of field will be. Lower values will make the background blurrier. Let’s set this to 1.0.
+   4. From here, skip to **Frame 1**, position the camera looking at the scene, press *I*, and select **Location & Rotation** to add a new keyframe.
+   5. The next step is to play the animation for about 100–200 frames and position the camera where you like to add a new keyframe. Repeat this step until you complete the animation. Remember that you can always fine-tune the location of keyframes in the timeline if you don’t like how the animation looks.
