If you haven't saved your blend file, all these settings will be grayed out. Make sure you save your project – only then will you be able to bake the simulation!

When you bake the simulation, the cache will be locked in and if you want to make changes, you will need to free the bake. It's generally recommended to bake the simulation before rendering. This will ensure that there are no issues and that the rendering process goes smoothly.
