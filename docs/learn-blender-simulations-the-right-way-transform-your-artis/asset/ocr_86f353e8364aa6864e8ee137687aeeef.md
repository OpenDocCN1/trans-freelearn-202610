Another use would be to create a fluid simulation splattering on a wall. Then, using the fluid as a brush, you can easily create a paint splash effect! You can also combine multiple dynamic paint simulations. With the Displace and Paint surface types, you could create a wood carving animation! The possibilities are endless!
