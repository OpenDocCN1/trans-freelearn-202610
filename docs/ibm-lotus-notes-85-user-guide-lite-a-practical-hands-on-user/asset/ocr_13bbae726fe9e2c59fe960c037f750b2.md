# Preface

Lotus Notes is quite a number of "things", including e-mail, calendar, To Dos (tasks), chat, RSS Feeder, an applications platform, and more. Many of us will use Lotus Notes for most of our working day; and being able to use it more efficiently is a gain in our hectic work life. Many of us want to use tools and features to save time, but often we are unsure of how to master those tools and features.

This short, fast-paced book enables business users to master the Client Interface and Mail features of Lotus Notes simply and effectively. Using clear explanations and instructions with explicit screenshots, this book gives business users a deeper and clearer understanding of basic functionality, providing the answers to many questions that are encountered while sending mails and working with Lotus Notes applications.

This book is a LITE edition of a longer book, IBM Lotus Notes 8.5 User Guide. The full edition provides comprehensive coverage of Lotus Notes.

# What this book covers

Chapter 1, First Impressions (The Client Interface), explores and explains what we first see and how we work with what we see when we open Lotus Notes.

Chapter 2, Mastering Lotus Notes Mail, helps us understand mail in more detail.

Chapter 3, Lotus Notes Mail Tools, focuses on the advanced features available in Mail and how we can best utilize them to assist us in being efficient with mail management.
