# Out of Office

Whenever we take annual leave, or we are away from the office, it is important to enable our Out of Office. When a colleague or customer sends us an e-mail, they get notified that we are not available with dates of when we are away, and more importantly, when we will be back in the office. In Lotus Notes 8.5, the notification of our absence is sent immediately.
