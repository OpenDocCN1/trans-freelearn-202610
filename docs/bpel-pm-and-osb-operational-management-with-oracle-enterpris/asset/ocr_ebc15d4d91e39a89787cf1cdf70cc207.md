+   10. Add another performance metric. Click on the **Add** button.

+   12. Pick a relevant infrastructure metric, that is, **CPU Utilization (%)**.

This is similar to the expectation that was set for the other services.

+   1. From the **Related Links** section at the bottom, click on **Edit Service Level Rule**.
+   2. Change the **Expected Service Level** from the default value to the value that is required for this service, for instance, **99.0**.
