# Viewing the BPEL infrastructure service

The BPEL infrastructure service encapsulates the infrastructure components that are required for BPEL processes to run.

+   1. Click on the **Target Services** tab.
+   2. Locate the infrastructure service (generic service) created in a previous chapter.
+   3. Click on the infrastructure service to go to the service home page.

# Adding infrastructure performance metrics

Not only are service test metrics important, but infrastructure metrics can also be used to represent service disruptions.

+   1. Click on the **Monitoring Configuration** tab.
+   2. Click on the **Performance Metrics** link.
