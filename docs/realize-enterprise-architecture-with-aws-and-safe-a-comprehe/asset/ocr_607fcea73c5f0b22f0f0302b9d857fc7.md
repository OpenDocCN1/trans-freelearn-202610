+   4. Organize implementation oversight meetings by establishing communications and reporting protocols.
+   5. Align, enable, and mobilize resources and teams during sprints. Start slowly, create early successes, and work up to your full migration capacity.
+   6. Build your migration at scale around outputs, best practices, and lessons learned.
+   7. Use Wave 1 to create agile blueprints for scaling migrations. This will provide a foundation to prepare for and plan migrations.

As you work through Wave 1 migrations, you gain experience and confidence. Additionally, you must choose a migration plan based on your organization’s needs, along with the patterns and tools that will be used. This ensures that operational and security procedures are tested. A list of application groupings based on common patterns is also created, using patterns (such as architectures and technology stacks) within the portfolio. Group migrations are then handled in a standardized way.
