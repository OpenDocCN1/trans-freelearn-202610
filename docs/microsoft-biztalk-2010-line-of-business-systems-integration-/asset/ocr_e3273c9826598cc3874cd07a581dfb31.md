If we wanted to support a callback pattern, we could choose to send **SessionID** in the message payload. Choose to send the following fields: **AccountNumber**, **BillingStreet**, **BillingCity**, **BillingState**, **BillingPostalCode**, **BillingCountry**, **AzureValidationToken__c**, **Name**, and **LastModifiedDate**. The **LastModifiedDate** field is useful if you suspect that you could have backlog in your on-premises processing and need a way to find the most recent update.
