No matter whether it is a desktop applications or web-applications, the Basic recording generates full selectors, which means there are no separate containers generated, such as **Attach Browser** or **Attach Window**. Therefore, this recording is the _slowest_ and most suitable for single activities within containers or for linear workflows.
