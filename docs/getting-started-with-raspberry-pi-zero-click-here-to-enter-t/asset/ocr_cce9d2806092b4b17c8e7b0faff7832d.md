The two rows on each side of the board are generally designed to provide power, so they are connected up and down. Connect pin 2, 3 volts, to the + connection and pin 6, GND, to the + and - rows of the breadboard, as shown:

![](bbox=[0.28, 0.2, 0.71, 0.56])

Now you can place the electronic parts on the breadboard. Place the LEDs so that one wire is on one side of the middle split of the breadboard. The direction on the LED is important; make sure that the longer of the two wires is on the left-hand side of the hole.
