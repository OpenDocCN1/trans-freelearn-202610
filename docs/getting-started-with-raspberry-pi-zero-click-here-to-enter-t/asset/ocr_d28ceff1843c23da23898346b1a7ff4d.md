To connect the device, you need to connect the I2C interface pins on Raspberry Pi Zero. Here is the detail of the GPIO pins:

![](bbox=[0.257, 0.19, 0.742, 0.535])

You'll connect the device to pins 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9. For these connections, you'll want female-to-female jumper wires:

+   1. Connect the VCC pin on the module to Pin 1 3.3 V on Raspberry Pi Zero and GND to Pin 9 GND.
