# Getting ready

To follow along with this recipe you must have completed the previous recipe:

+   - *Downloading and installing a language pack*

# How to do it...

+   1. To have a bilingual portal you must first have downloaded and installed the desired language packs as described in the recipe *Downloading and installing a language pack*.

+   2. Select **Languages** from the **Admin** menu. The installed language packs for your portal are shown:

![](bbox=[0.17, 0.352, 0.83, 0.556])

+   3. To pick the language or languages for your portal click on the **Enabled** checkbox next to the language. After a pause the **Update** and **Cancel** links will display.

+   4. Click **Update** to save your changes.

+   5. If you enable more than one language, the Language Selector control will appear on the portal pages (depending on the skin your portal uses). Clicking on the different national flag icons will change the current language of the portal. Try clicking on the Canadian flag.

![](bbox=[0.264, 0.737, 0.736, 0.854])

+   6. Notice how the label on the logout link has changed from **Logout** to **Déconnexion**.
