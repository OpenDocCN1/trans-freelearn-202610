# Getting ready

To follow along with this recipe you must have the following:

+   - A Gmail account (you can get a free one by going to `http://mail.google.com` and clicking on the **Create an Account** button)
+   - The username and password to your Gmail account

# How to do it...

+   1. Begin by logging in as the host (`syschost` in our examples).
+   2. Look under the **Host** menu and select **Host Settings**.
+   3. Under **Basic Settings**, provide the following information:
+      - **Host Email:** (put your Gmail address here, like `dnnsyschost@gmail.com`)
+   4. Scroll down and expand **Advanced Settings**.
+   5. Find **SMTP Server Settings** and expand it.
