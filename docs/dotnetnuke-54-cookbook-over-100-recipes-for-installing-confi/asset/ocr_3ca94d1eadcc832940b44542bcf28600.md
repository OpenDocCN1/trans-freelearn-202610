# Belt and Road (一带一路)

CHINA'S belt and road initiative is a plan to boost trade and infrastructure

The plan, announced in 2013 with then-president Hu Jintao attending the annual meeting of the Boao Forum for Asia, was named after the ancient Silk Road network. It now involves more than 60 countries and vast infrastructure projects in areas ranging from roads and ports to oil and gas fields.

Beijing says the initiative will boost trade and infrastructure with its neighbours and open markets abroad for Chinese goods.
