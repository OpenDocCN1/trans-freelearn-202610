# How it works...

Google Analytics works by inserting a small piece of JavaScript containing a unique tracking ID into each page you want to track. As visitors view your DNN pages, Google records the ID and offers the results on your Analytics overview page. In addition, if you use a Google Webmaster account it will give you access to other Google options.
