# How to do it...

+   1. The first step is to get an Analytics tracking ID from Google. If you already have a Tracking ID, skip to step 13, otherwise start by opening your favorite web browser and going to the Google Analytics home page (`http://www.google.com/analytics`).

![](bbox=[0.156, 0.215, 0.844, 0.397])

+   2. If you have already signed up Analytics click on the **Access Analytics** button. Otherwise click on the **Sign Up Now** link.
+   
+   3. You can sign in with your Gmail address and password.
+   
+   4. If you are new to Analytics, you will see the Google Analytics **Sign Up** button.

![](bbox=[0.323, 0.525, 0.676, 0.615])

+   5. Click on the **Sign Up** button.

![](bbox=[0.294, 0.675, 0.706, 0.906])
