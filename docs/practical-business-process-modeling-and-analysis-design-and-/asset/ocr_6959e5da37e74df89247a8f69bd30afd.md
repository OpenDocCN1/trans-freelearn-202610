Business processes are critical because they help improve operational efficiency, create value, translate strategy into action, are adaptable, and allow for continuous organizational improvement. BPM also introduces a process-centric view of organizations, providing a holistic perspective that considers interactions between processes, people, technology, culture, and strategy. It is beneficial to model processes to properly understand those inter-dependencies and organization in some formal way using standards.
