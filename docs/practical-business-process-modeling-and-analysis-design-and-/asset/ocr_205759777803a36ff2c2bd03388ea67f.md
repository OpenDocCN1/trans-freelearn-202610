For the whole company to get comfortable with these concepts, the wheel must spin a couple of times, each creating the inflection point that leads to accelerated adoption of process-led solutions, a culture of continuous improvement, and genuine excitement created by successful projects. When BPM creates results, you will hear and feel the positive buzz in all parts of the company as many project teams start announcing their successes. It is a gratifying and fun part of the program as the success of others brings other use cases out of the woodwork, reenergizing everyone about the possibilities of improving operations. Success breeds more success, so you should prepare for it upfront and consider the potential scale of your program.
