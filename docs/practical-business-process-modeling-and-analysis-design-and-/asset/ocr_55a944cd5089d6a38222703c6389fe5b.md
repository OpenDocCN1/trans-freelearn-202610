# Figure 8.31 – Possible approaches to naming start events

A start event without a type is used often. It’s particularly useful in processes when the decision of the employee involved in the process causes the process to begin, that is, it doesn’t depend on external requests or isn’t executed at specific times. Another possibility is a situation where some other process triggers our process through the mechanism of sub-processes. We’ll discuss this case in more detail in the next section.
