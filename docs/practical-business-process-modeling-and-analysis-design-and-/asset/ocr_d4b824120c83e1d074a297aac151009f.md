# # Collaboration between processes and bots

As AI bots get smarter, they will replace certain decisions and actions in the process, but they will
still collaborate. Processes will concentrate on sequential actions and likely become smaller snippets
of what was an overall process of the past. Over time, this collaboration and sharing of decisions and
work will become more fluid.

AI will be essential to link and assist businesses in gleaning higher levels of change agility while
increasing business profitability and satisfying constituents’ goals. See Figure 1.8 for the goals businesses
are looking to achieve with processes. We can see the role of more intelligent processes created,
assembled, and managed by AI.
