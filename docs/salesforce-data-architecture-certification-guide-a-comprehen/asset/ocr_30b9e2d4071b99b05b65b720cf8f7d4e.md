We touched upon indexing when exploring LDV, so let’s recap that here:

# Salesforce Query Optimizer – Standard Indexes

30% of up to 1 Million Records + 15% of Additional Records over 1 Million, up to a total of 1 Million Records

Examples:
2 million records – 300,000 + 150,000 = 450,000 records
6 million records – 300,000 + 700,000 = 1 Million records

# Salesforce Query Optimizer – Custom Indexes

10% of up to 1 Million Records + 5% of Additional Records over 1 Million, up to a total of 333,333 records

Examples:
2 million records – 100,000 + 50,000 = 150,000 records
6 million records – 100,000 + 233,333 = 333,333 Million records
