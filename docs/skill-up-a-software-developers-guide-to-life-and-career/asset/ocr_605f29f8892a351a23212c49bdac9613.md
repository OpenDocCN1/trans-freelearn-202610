# Visiting the DailyProgrammer subreddit on Reddit

Reddit is a community full of people who share ideas and help one another. You can get in on the action with the DailyProgrammer subreddit ([https://www.reddit.com/r/dailyprogrammer/](https://www.reddit.com/r/dailyprogrammer/)). This subreddit posts three programming challenges each week. The first challenge is relatively easy, and then they increase in difficulty. The community reviews the solutions and provides feedback. Use this subreddit to improve your skills while having some fun.
