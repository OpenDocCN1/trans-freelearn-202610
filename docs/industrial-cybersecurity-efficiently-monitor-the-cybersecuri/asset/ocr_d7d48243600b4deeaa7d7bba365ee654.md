Tuning of IDS rules is a crucial part of creating a usable security monitoring environment. The more benign or bogus alerts our analysts must sift through to get to the real stuff, the more likely it is that they will miss actual alerts. The bulk of any type of signature-based tool deployment is in fine-tuning the rules.
