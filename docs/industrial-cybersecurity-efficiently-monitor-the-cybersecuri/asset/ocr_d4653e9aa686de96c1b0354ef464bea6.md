When we click on the **Show button for Known vulnerabilities**, we can see a listing of the discovered vulnerabilities and details on each of them, as illustrated in the following screenshot:

The details on each vulnerability include solutions to address it.

Another method to view vulnerabilities is to filter the **Network Map** view to highlight assets with known vulnerabilities. In order to achieve this, click on the **Threats** drop-down menu at the top of the **Network Map** screen and select **Vulnerabilities**.
