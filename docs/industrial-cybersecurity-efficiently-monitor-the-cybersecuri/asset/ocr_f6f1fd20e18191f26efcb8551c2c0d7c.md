# Pentest engagement step 2 – pivoting into the industrial environment

At this point, we've got our initial foothold into the target network. Now, it's time to work toward the objective of the first phase of the ICS attack: getting into the industrial network. Again, we will not be spending too much time covering this step in this chapter as we dedicated quite a few pages to the subject in Chapter 16, Red Team/Blue Team Exercises.

# Scanning and probing for information and vulnerabilities

The first thing we want to do after getting a (persistent) backdoor into the enterprise network is start scanning and probing the enterprise network for information (clues) on where the best pivot point into the industrial network could be. We are interested in computer names, logged in users, and other bits of information that can tell us a potential next target to compromise. This will put us in the position to pivot into the industrial environment. Tools that can be useful at this point include the following:

+   - Nmap: https://nmap.org/
+   - Nessus: https://www.tenable.com/products/nessus
+   - PowerShell:
+     - Empire (no longer actively being developed): https://www.powershell empire.com/
+     - PoshC2: https://poshc2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/
+     - PowerSploit (no longer actively being developed): https://github.com/PowerShellMafia/PowerSploit
+     - Nishang: https://github.com/samratashok/nishang
+   - Windapsearch: https://github.com/ropnop/windapsearch
