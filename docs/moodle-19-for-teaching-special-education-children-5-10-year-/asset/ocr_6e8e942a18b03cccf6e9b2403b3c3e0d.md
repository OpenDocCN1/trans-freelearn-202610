Why did we use the digital pen? This device offers a great variety of resources to perform artistic activities. Furthermore, it is another resource for those children who have manual dyspraxia.

> The child usually has to work with the help of a therapist or a family member in order to solve this exercise using Inkscape, Moodle, and the digital pen. The child solves the exercise uploading his/her drawing to the upload a single file activity.
