8. Double-click on each part of the pyramid and erase its default text.

9. Select the pyramid. Click on Shape format | Rotate & Flip | -90 on the ribbon. The pyramid will now appear drawn from left to right.

10. Press F3 and the Shape format dialog box will appear. Click on Fill | Gradient fill and select the desired colors to fill each part with a linear gradient. You can also add other effects to each part of the pyramid.

11. Click on Shape format | Grouping | Ungroup on the ribbon. Each part of the pyramid will now be an independent shape. Therefore, you can move each part as desired to separate them as needed.
