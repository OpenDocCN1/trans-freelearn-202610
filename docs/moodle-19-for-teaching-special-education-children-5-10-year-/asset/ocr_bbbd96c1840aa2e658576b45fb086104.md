+   - **Attention stimulation.** The child has to focus on memorizing each word as soon as possible. As the exercise uses animations instead of static words, they have to pay more attention and the exercise is more fun.

This exercise could be too difficult to solve for certain children, especially those who have difficulty writing or have reading comprehension problems. In these cases, it is very important to work with the help of a therapist or a family member.
