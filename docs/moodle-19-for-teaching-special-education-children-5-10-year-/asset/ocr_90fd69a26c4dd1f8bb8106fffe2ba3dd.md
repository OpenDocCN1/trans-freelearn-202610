# Moodle 1.9 for Teaching Special Education Children (5-10 Year Olds):
Beginner's Guide

Develop interactive and feature-rich online content for Special Education Needs children using this friendly, non-technical guide for teachers and therapists

This book offers solutions to developing interactive courses and therapies for children with special education needs between 5 and 10 years old. It teaches how to combine Moodle 1.9 with the opportunities offered by Web 2.0, free and commercial software, and general purpose hardware devices.

# What this book will do for you...

+   - Create activities to match composite pictures with sentences and rich exercises to stimulate both attention and concentration
+   - Search for and organize 3D models to create a 3D scene and work with colors to define skill levels
+   - Combine videos, sounds, photos, letters and words to improve long-term memory management
+   - Learn to combine pictures and symbols to improve the concentration when working with mathematical operations
+   - Create exercises to help the SEN child's parents to interact with them
