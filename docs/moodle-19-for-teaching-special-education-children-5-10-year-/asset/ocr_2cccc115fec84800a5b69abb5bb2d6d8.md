We added an upload a single file activity to a Moodle course that will allow a student to read, understand, and listen to six questions. Then he/she will be able to record the answers for these questions by using a Webcam or a microphone, and upload the resulting video or audio file to Moodle.

We added a picture of a simple situation about a chef cooking eggs and we took advantage of the embedded audio players provided by the Moodle multimedia plugin. This way, it is going to be simple for the students to listen to each question.
