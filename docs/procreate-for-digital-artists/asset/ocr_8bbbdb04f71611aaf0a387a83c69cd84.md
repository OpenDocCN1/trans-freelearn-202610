# Two-point perspective

A two-point perspective in art is another method for creating depth and dimension in a drawing. Unlike the one-point perspective, which uses a single vanishing point, the two-point perspective relies on two vanishing points placed on the horizon line. These two points are used to draw objects at an angle, showing two sides of an object as they recede into the distance.
