# Tip

To create a more atmospheric perspective in your background, try using **Gaussian Blur** or gently lowering the saturation using the **Hue, Saturation, Brightness** adjustment in Procreate. This helps distant elements look softer and less vivid, making them feel further away and adding a sense of space to your artwork.
