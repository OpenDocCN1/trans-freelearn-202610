The light source is on the left side thus the light is created on the surface of the sphere, and then a shadow is created on the area that the light does not hit. The shadow area that is close to the surrounding object or ground will create reflected light. Reflected light appears when a light meets an object and bounces back to another object near it. In this case, you can see that the reflected light is bright yellow because the surroundings are yellow. If we change the surroundings to purple, the reflected light will appear as bright purple:
