Let us learn how to apply texture effectively to our art:

+   1. We can start by preparing an illustration as a base layer, Base Layer (illustration).
+   2. Then, add an additional layer on top of it as a textured material. Textured materials such as paper, canvas, fabric, or a material of your own preference can be added to this layer.
+   3. Once added, change the blending mode based on your preference. For texture layer placement, you can refer to Figure 1.17.
+   4. Blending modes that are often used for texture are Overlay, Multiply, Soft Light, and Color Burn. A mixture of these blending modes can create a stunning effect in your illustration as well.
