# Poko

Little Monster From The Star

Poko is a little monster that fell from a star. Poko received his name from a human friend who took care of him. Poko didn't get to know his friend's name and only remembers his silhouette. One day, Poko's human friend disappeared. Poko decides to go on a journey to find the truth about his human friend's disappearance. Poko is a kind, hopeful, and joyful soul. He has an ability to see people's auras and is able to solve their problems. While on his journey, he meets a lot of new people that he helps while searching for the truth. Poko hopes that he will be able to meet his human friend again.
