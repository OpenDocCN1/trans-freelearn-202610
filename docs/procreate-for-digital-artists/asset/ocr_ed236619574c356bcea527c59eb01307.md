# Size perspective

**Size perspective** is how we see or think about the size of something compared to other things. It helps us understand how big or small something is in relation to its surroundings. For example, when you look at a picture of a tiny animal next to a huge building, the size perspective helps you realize just how small that animal really is. It also applies to the connection between the distance of an object with another object.

Imagine that you are standing and facing a long road. If you pay attention to the road, you will notice that the farther the road is from you, the smaller it gets. By drawing distant objects smaller than the ones nearest to you, you can add a sense of depth to your background scenery. The advice here is to trust your eyes and paint them how you see them. If the distant trees look smaller, draw them smaller, even though you know that all the trees are roughly the same size. Size perspective helps us better understand the scale of things to make our background drawing look more realistic.
