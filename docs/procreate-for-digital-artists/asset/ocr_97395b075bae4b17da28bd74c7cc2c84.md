# Understanding the technique

Contrast relates to the differences in background art composition, such as color, value, size, and texture. To enhance contrast in your artwork, you can use varying light and dark color values to create depth, change the sizes of elements for a sense of scale, and mix different textures to draw attention to your art.

In Figure 5.27, our eyes are immediately drawn to the center of the illustration because that area is highlighted. The darker colors at the front and back give the illustration depth, making it seem as if the room is longer and divided into three sections.
