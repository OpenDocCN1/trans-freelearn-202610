Figure 10.25 – Detailing process for a peaceful night

Before we finish the illustration, let's softly add highlights where light sources such as the moon or stars shine from above. To do this, change the layer's blending mode from **Normal** to **Color Dodge**. This will gently brighten parts of the scene and give a subtle, warm glowing effect that fits the quiet and magical feeling of a night setting. Use Procreate's **Soft Brush** with low opacity, so the glow stays soft and doesn't overpower the calm nighttime atmosphere.
