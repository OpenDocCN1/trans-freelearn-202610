> **Coloring tips**
> **ColorDrop** in Procreate lets you quickly fill an area with a color. Just pick a color, then drag it from the top-right color circle onto the spot you want to fill. If the color doesn’t spread how you want, hold down the drop to adjust the **Threshold** slider – this controls how much of the space gets filled. It’s a super-quick way to color large sections without painting everything manually.

# Learning which colors create a sad atmosphere

To design a color palette for a sad atmosphere, focus on muted and cool tones, such as deep blues, desaturated greens, and grays. To simplify the process, you can choose from any desaturated shades to create a sense of heaviness and a somber mood in your artwork. There is often the tendency to stick to blue when creating a sad theme. In this walkthrough, we’ll explore how to include other colors to craft a striking and somber illustration.
