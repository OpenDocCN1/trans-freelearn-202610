# Finalizing character designs

Finalizing a character design involves creating a polished design that represents the character's role, background, and personality traits. It is important to create physical appearance, clothing, and color schemes that reflect their story and personality. Developing a detailed character sheet with various angles and expressions helps to maintain consistency in design elements. We will understand this better as we progress through this chapter. Once satisfied, we can finalize the character design while remaining open to adjustments as ideas evolve throughout the storytelling process.
