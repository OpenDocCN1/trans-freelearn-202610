Before we finish the illustration, let's gently add highlights where the sunlight hits the scene, which is from above since it's afternoon. To do this, switch the layer's blending mode from **Normal** to **Add**. This brightens those areas and gives a warm, glowing effect that matches the vibrant afternoon light. Use Procreate's **Soft Brush** with low opacity, so the glow stays natural and doesn't overpower the cheerful, sunny atmosphere.
