# Color emotions and how to apply them to our art

**Color emotion** refers to the emotional responses that colors can evoke and how they connect with individuals. Certain colors can trigger psychological reactions, which can influence the color schemes we choose for our art. By mastering the use of color emotions, we can effectively engage the right audience. For example, to create a calming and therapeutic illustration, blue and green can be used. Each color has a unique purpose, but deciding on the right colors to convey specific emotions in art can sometimes be challenging. With a deep understanding of color emotions, we can enhance both the visual impact and storytelling of our art. Let us look at an example of color emotion next.
