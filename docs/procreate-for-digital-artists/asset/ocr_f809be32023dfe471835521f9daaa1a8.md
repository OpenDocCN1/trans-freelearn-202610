# How and when to use warm and cool colors

Warm and cool color tones play a significant role in art. Warm colors, such as reds, oranges, and yellows, are often associated with energy, passion, and warmth. They tend to stand out as vibrant, making them ideal for drawing attention to certain areas. On the other hand, cool colors, such as blues, greens, and purples, evoke calmness, tranquility, and distance. They provide a sense of depth and balance. Understanding how and when to use warm and cool colors can enhance the mood and impact of your artwork.
