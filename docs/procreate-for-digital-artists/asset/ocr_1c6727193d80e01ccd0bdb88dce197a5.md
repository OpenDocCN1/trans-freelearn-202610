# Following the steps

Identifying lighting and shadow from a soft light source through a real-life image can be done as follows:

+   1. Choose any real-life image that you prefer for this walkthrough. You can use this image as your reference:
+   2. From Figure 4.5, circle the lighting created on the oranges and mark it down as a lighting area.
+   3. Then, circle the shadow cast from the oranges and mark it down as a shadow area.
+   4. Take a look at the lighting and shadow cast from the oranges and write down a description and characteristics of the lighting and shadow.
