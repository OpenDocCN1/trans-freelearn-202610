# Understanding the technique

When you start simple, it’s much easier to see the overall composition of your background and identify areas that might need adjustments or improvements. This clarity makes it simpler to refine your work as you go, whether you’re adding details or reworking certain elements. Additionally, because the composition is already straightforward, you’ll find it easier to incorporate any creative touches or changes you might think of along the way.

Once the base of your background is in place, you can gradually layer in more details and focus on enhancing it through techniques such as detailing and rendering. This step-by-step approach not only keeps your work organized but also allows you to make thoughtful adjustments without feeling overwhelmed. Overall, starting simple gives you the flexibility and control to create visually appealing art while staying open to creativity throughout the process.
