When coloring, you have choices: you can combine all layers into one to improve brush strokes and create smoother textures, or you can keep the layers separate if that works better for you. Adding textures, as mentioned in Chapter 1, is another great way to give the illustration a unique, finished touch. Wrapping up your illustration and blending everything together can feel a bit intimidating at first, but do not let that worry you. Just trust the process, take it step by step, and you will be amazed at how it all comes together in the end. Sometimes, magic happens when you least expect it!
