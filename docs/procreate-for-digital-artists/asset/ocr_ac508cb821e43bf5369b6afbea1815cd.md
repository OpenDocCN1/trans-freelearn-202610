# Summary

You’ve done such a wonderful job learning how to make your drawings more thoughtful and visually pleasing. In this chapter, we explored how placing objects in just the right way can help tell your story clearly and beautifully.

We practiced using shapes such as a C, V, circle, and spiral to gently guide the viewer’s eyes through your scene. We also discovered how balance, depth, and contrast can change how your artwork feels, whether calm and peaceful or full of energy and excitement.

The best part is that you don’t need to follow every rule. These techniques are here to guide you, but your imagination always comes first. With every sketch, you’re learning to trust your eyes and your ideas more and more.
