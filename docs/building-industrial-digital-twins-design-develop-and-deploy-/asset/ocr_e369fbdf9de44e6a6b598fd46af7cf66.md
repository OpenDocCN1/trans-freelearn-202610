Often, the petrochemical industry is challenged to control its environmental impact due  
to emissions. Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd, in India, is working with AspenTech on  
a Digital Twin for the Refinery-Wide Emission Model, to help control the impact on the  
environment and stay within the regulatory guidelines when operating a refinery. (Please  
refer to https://www.worldofchemicals.com/media/digital-twin-for-  
refinery-wide-emission-and-efficiency-monitoring/4697.html.)
