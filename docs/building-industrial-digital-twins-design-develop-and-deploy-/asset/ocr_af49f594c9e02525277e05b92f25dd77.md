# # New innovations in renewable power generation

Let’s discuss wind-catching technology in the context of wind energy. Wind catching has
great potential to reduce the **Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE)**, which is a measure of
the lifetime costs of an energy generation facility divided by the total amount of energy
produced. Figure 8.17 shows the wind-catching design for offshore wind energy. It is a
floating installation. This technology takes up less space and can produce up to five times
more energy than traditional offshore wind turbines. For more information,
refer to https://windcatching.com/.
