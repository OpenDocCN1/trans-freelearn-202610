In a phased approach, the Digital Twin is rolled out to one-third of the wind turbines in a single farm. This way, the performance of the Digital Twin and the outcomes can be evaluated against the other wind turbines in the same farm. This provides a good baseline and, generally, the age and the model of the wind turbines in the same wind farm are expected to be similar. They are all subjected to the same environment, such as similar wind speeds and temperatures.
