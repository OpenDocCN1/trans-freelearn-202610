# # The Digital Twin of a solar farm

We often see rooftop solar panels on homes, as shown in Figure 8.12. The state of California in the US has introduced the California solar mandate, which requires solar panels on all single-family and multi-family residences that are less than four stories high from January 1, 2020. This mandate intends to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by the equivalent of 115,000 gas-powered cars being taken off the road. This mandate was later relaxed and allowed building developers to augment rooftop solar panels with off-site solar power plants.
