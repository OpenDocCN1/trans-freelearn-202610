The final step of the infrastructure functional test in _Figure 7.1_ is to check that the relationships are correctly configured. ADX provides a visual, graph-based interface that makes it easy to see the various relationships defined in the multiple DTDL models. The relationships between these models are critical to the success of the Digital Twin instances, and testing this as part of the infrastructure functional test ensures that the underlying graph functionality is correctly deployed.
