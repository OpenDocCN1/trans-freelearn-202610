# What is audio?

**Audio** is "sound" which is detectable to the human ear. Sounds are pressure waves of air that we hear because our ears are designed to be sensitive to these waves.

For humans, hearing is usually detected between the approximate range of 20 Hz and 20 kHz (20,000 Hz). 20 Hz is the lowest pitch (bassiest sound) we can interpret, 20 kHz being the highest. It is recognized that as humans get older, the higher range of our hearing perception can often deteriorate.
