# # Using Video in Your Joomla! Website

Video on the Internet is booming! Relatively recent improvements to factors such as superior video compression, increased connection speeds, and better hardware have all contributed to making the Internet a more reliable and accessible environment for the delivery of video content.

This chapter highlights the use of video media within your Joomla! website, and includes the following topics:

+   - Video on the Internet
+   - Video formats
+   - Embedding and displaying video in Joomla!
+   - Creating video podcasts for Joomla!
+   - Third-party video extensions
+   - Browser support and accessibility

# ## Video on the Internet

The terminology, video, refers to the recording, manipulating, and display of a sequence of still images, representing scenes in motion.
