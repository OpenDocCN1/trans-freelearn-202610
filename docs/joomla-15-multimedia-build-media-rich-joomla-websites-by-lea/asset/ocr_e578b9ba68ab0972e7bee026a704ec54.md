# *Using Audio in Your Joomla! Website*

The FeedBurner service offers you additional features to customize your feed and even submit it automatically to iTunes. We need to utilize one of these, so click on **Next**.

At the top of this page, make sure the **Create podcast enclosures from links to: any rich media file** is selected. It should be the default setting:

![](bbox=[0.29, 0.22, 0.7, 0.27])

You can give your podcast a description, some keywords, and even add it to iTunes and the Yahoo! search engine. Then click on **Next**.

In this last page, FeedBurner offers you some powerful statistics, which lets you know who is subscribing to your podcasts and how often they have clicked on the content in your feed:

![](bbox=[0.25, 0.41, 0.77, 0.65])

Click on **Next** and we are all done here!
