BK-Thumbs contains a number of parameters, all creating quite a different look and feel to the presentation of your images. I suggest you adjust these as you feel the need to, but for now let's make sure the plugin is enabled and click on Save at the top-right of the screen.

In order for BK-Thumbs to do its work, we need a new content article called "BK-Thumbs" and to make this reside within the section labeled "Images" and the Category labeled "General Image". Within your content item, insert some text and upload a good-size image by using the "Image Upload" button at the bottom of the content article. For a suitable size, aim for a pixel size between 600px and 700px width or height, depending on the image layout.
