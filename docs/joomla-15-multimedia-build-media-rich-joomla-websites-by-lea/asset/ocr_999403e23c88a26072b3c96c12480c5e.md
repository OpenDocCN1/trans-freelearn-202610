# # Third-party audio extensions

Joomla! Extensions provide us with a range of powerful audio solutions which can be installed and configured by administrators, with nearly no developer's knowledge required.

There is a huge number of extensions that are now available for Joomla!. The most difficult decision for a Joomla! site administrator is usually not finding an extension to do what you want it to, but finding the most suitable one out of the available options. The following section highlights some of the most popular, and non-commercial third-party audio extensions.
