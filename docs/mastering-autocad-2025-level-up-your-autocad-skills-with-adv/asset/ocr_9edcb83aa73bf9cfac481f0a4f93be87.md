11. The viewport will display a zoomed extents view of what is in the **Model** tab as soon as the viewport is placed. Double-click inside the boundary of the new viewport to activate it. You will see the viewport boundary highlight and you can now work within the viewport as if you were drafting in the **Model** tab. This is colloquially known as *floating modelspace*. *Figure 8.12* shows the viewport activated. When a viewport is active, navigation tools such as the **Navigation Bar**, **ViewCube**, and the **UCS icon** are displayed inside the active viewport.
