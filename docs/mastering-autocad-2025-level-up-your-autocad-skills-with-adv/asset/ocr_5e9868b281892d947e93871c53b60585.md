# AutoCAD for Mac

To customize the status bar in AutoCAD for Mac, you would need to click on the gearwheel icon at the bottom left of the AutoCAD for Mac application window. This displays a similar menu to the one in *Figure 3.20*. The list of drafting settings is shorter, and each has a checkbox next to it to switch settings on and off, unlike the Windows version of AutoCAD, where you click on the setting name to toggle it on or off and display a check mark. To close the menu in AutoCAD for Mac, you will need to click on the gearwheel icon again.
