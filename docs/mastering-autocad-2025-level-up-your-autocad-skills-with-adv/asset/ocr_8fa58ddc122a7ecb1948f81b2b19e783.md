> **Tips and tricks #9**
> 
> When working with the ViewCube, you can use the compass display by clicking on the arrows to rotate any view you are using. You can also go back to the **Home** view by clicking on the *house* symbol. Right-click on the ViewCube and use **Set Current View as Home** on the shortcut menu to set the required home view. Also, test the other settings on the shortcut menu to investigate their effects on the visual display of the drawing. Use the AutoCAD system variables *DISPLAYVIEWCUBEIN2D* and *DISPLAYVIEWCUBEIN3D* to turn the display of the ViewCube on and off in either of the 2D or 3D workspaces.
