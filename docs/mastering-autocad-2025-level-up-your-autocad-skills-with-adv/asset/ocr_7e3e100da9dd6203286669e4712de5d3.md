# # Part 4:
# Communicating and Collaborating

AutoCAD has been around for over 40 years and has adopted new technology with each new version. AutoCAD 2025 is no different. Cloud-based technology and file storage now allow your AutoCAD DWG files to go mobile and travel with you. Using the AutoCAD mobile application and the browser-based AutoCAD Web, combined with cloud-based file storage, you can now take your drawings everywhere, allowing for easy, seamless, real-time communication and collaboration on your AutoCAD designs.
