Your block is now placed on the tool palette and can be dragged and dropped from the palette into any drawing from its central location. The palette remembers that location so make sure that if you do change the block library drawing location, you update the properties of the icon on the palette. You can do that by right-clicking over the palette icon and selecting **Properties** on the shortcut menu.
