Why do you need **Polar Tracking**? It allows you to work with preset angle increments and add individual angle increments on an as-needed basis.

Now, let's explore some properties of this feature. You'll notice that the **Polar Tracking** icon on the status bar has a flyout menu arrow to the right of it. If you click on this arrow, the flyout menu will display the different preset angle increments that can be used. *Figure 5.2 shows this flyout menu:*

![](bbox=[0.248, 0.217, 0.746, 0.564])

This flyout menu displays the preset angle increments that can be used. These include increments of 90 degrees (*default*), 45 degrees, and 30 degrees, all the way down to 5-degree increments.
