Let's begin this exercise:

+   1. Select the **Circle** flyout on the **Draw** panel on the **Home** tab on the ribbon to draw the new circles.
+   2. Then, select **Center, Radius**. AutoCAD will prompt you for the center of the circle to be drawn. It's at this point in the workflow that we'll be using **Object Snap Tracking** (*AUTOSNAP*).
+   3. Using the settings described previously, hover over the **Midpoint** Osnap of the left-hand outside edge of the bearing case in the drawing.
+   However, *don't click* on it; simply hover over it for a few seconds. You'll see a small cross symbol appear *inside* the snap symbol. That's **Object Snap Tracking** memorizing the **Midpoint** Osnap.
+   4. Then, drag your mouse to the right. You'll see the green, dashed tracking line. *Figure 5.10* shows what this will look like onscreen:
