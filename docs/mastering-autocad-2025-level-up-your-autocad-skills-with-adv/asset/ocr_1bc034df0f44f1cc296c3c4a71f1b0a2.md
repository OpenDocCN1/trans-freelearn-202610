Tolerances tend to be used in machining and manufacturing to display lateral tolerances in a material when it is machined for manufacture. These tolerances apply to an aspect of a part, such as a point, line, axis, or surface. Tolerances are often used in mechanical and production drawings, where the drawn objects are cut, planed, or milled. These production processes require a tolerance as the tools and machinery used are (normally) cut into a material such as metal. These processes will never be absolute, so the appropriate tolerances are needed. In the case of the exercise file, you do not need any tolerance values.
