**ShowMotion** has three main parts: control bar, shot sequence thumbnails, and shot thumbnails. Shots are named locations showing specific parts of the model, with related shots being able to be organized into a shot sequence. **Figure 3.33** shows **ShowMotion** in a 3D modeling environment, with two saved shots and the **ShowMotion** control bar displayed.
