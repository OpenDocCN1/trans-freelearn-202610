+   1. Open **Site Manager** and navigate to the **SiteMap** channel.
+   2. Right-click on the **SiteMap** channel and select **Properties**.
+   3. In the **Properties** dialog, click the **Publishing** tab.
+   4. In the **Channel Rendering** section of the dialog, click **Select**.
+   5. In the **Select Channel Rendering** dialog, set the **Script URL** field to `/tropicalgreen/templates/sitemap.aspx` and click **OK**.

6. Close all open dialogs.

Now navigate to any page on the website and click on the **Site Map** link on the top menu bar. The entire tree is loaded and displayed. Expand or contract any of the nodes to reveal the sub-items.
