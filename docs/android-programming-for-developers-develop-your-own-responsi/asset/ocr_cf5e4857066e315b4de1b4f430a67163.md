# "Pages" scroll in and out of view

![](bbox=[0.148, 0.162, 0.676, 0.414])

The Android API, as you would have come to expect by now, has some solutions through which you can achieve paging in a quite simple manner.

In this chapter, you will learn to:

+   - Achieve paging and swiping with images like you might do in a photo gallery app.

+   - Implement paging and swiping with Fragments, which will give our app the potential to offer our users the ability to swipe their way through a selection of user interfaces. This will give our apps enormous potential.

We will not be adding paging/swiping to the Where it's snap app, but let's build two paging mini apps: one with images and one with Fragments.
