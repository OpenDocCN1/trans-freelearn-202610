We will spend most of our time, in the next few chapters, in the design view of the editor window. If the previous screenshot looks slightly different than your layout, then you can take the following steps to make your layout more design friendly:

+   1. Make the editor window larger by making sure that the Android window is closed. Click on the Android tab if the window is open, and this will make it disappear.
+   2. Make the project explorer window smaller by dragging the area between it and the editor to the left.
+   3. Make the Palette area (E2) larger by dragging its right-hand side edge to the right.
+   4. Tweak all the window sizes to suit your preferences.
