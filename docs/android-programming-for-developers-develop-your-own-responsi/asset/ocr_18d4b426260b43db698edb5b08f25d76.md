When the app is running you can left-click and drag the left-hand pane of the app to scroll the contents up and down. Notice that the Android image in the right-hand pane stays still:

![](bbox=[0.115, 0.33, 0.885, 0.7])

We achieved this by adding two `LinearLayout`s to our root `LinearLayout`. We made one cover 40% and the other 60% of the parent by setting their `layout_weight` properties to 40 and 60 respectively. Then we put a `ScrollView` into the left-hand `LinearLayout` and put another vertical `LinearLayout` into the `ScrollView`. We did this step because `ScrollView` can only have one child (very wise). However, once we have the `LinearLayout` inside the `ScrollView` we can add as many widgets as we like and they will all scroll nicely, as we saw.
