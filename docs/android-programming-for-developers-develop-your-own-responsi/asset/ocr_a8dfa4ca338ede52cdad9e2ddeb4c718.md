# Running the Where in the world mini app

If GPS is enabled and has a connection, you will see the precise details of where
you are; otherwise, the source for the location could be your wireless network or
your mobile network. The former will be disconnected when you move too far
away, and the latter could be a very long way off. Mine was around 60 miles off
with the GPS switched off.

![](bbox=[0.112, 0.26, 0.465, 0.72])

If you have a connection and walk around a bit, you will see the values of
latitude and longitude change.
