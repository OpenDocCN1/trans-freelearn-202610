This event indicates that multimedia is playing on the device. The ZVALUESTRING column will display information on the app that is playing it, such as YouTube or Apple Music.

By now, you should have a rough idea of what kind of events are logged in the KnowledgeC.db database and how to analyze such records. We will now look into more advanced topics, such as analyzing application usage in detail.
