+   • Hot-potato routing: With this routing method, the traffic leaves the Microsoft global network at the earliest opportunity and travels to its destination via many hops across the internet, hence the term *hot potato*, as the traffic is not held on to for long before being handed off to the *next hop* or *hand-off* for the packet.

The preceding diagram visualizes the cold-potato versus hot-potato egress routing methods; the benefits and disadvantages are as follows:
