# Setting markers

**Markers** are useful for giving you a visual reference for moments you need your music to hit. Markers are visual label indicators you can manually set in your playlist timeline. For example, perhaps you need your music to come in or fade out at a certain timed position. Markers can help you to identify where the position is.

Let's learn how to create markers:

+   1. Load an instance of Fruity Video Player into the Channel rack. Load a video into Fruity Video Player that you want to score music for.
+   2. Make sure that Fruity Video Player is detached so it remains open when you click outside of the player window.
+   3. Set the in and out points of the video using the **as start time** and **as end time** controls.
