If you find that most of your instruments are occupying the same frequency range, you may find your instruments drowning each other out. In this case, it's worth swapping an instrument for one in a different frequency range.

While you're listening to your compositions, you can use plugins to visually see the frequency ranges in your mixer channels. If you can, you may want to look into spectrogram plugins. Spectrogram plugins provide you with a fine-grained insight into your song's frequencies.
