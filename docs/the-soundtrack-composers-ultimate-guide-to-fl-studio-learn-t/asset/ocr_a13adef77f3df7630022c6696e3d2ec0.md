This red light indicates that recorded audio will be sent to that selected mixer track. Any effects on the track will also be applied to the audio while recording. You can record on multiple mixer tracks at the same time if desired. In general, it's a good practice to record raw audio and then apply effects later on, but you do have the option of applying effects while recording, which is useful if you want to see how pitch-correction effects such as autotune might sound.
