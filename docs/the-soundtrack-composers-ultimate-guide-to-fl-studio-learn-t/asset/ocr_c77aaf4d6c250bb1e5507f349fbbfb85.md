In the following steps, I assume that you have an orchestral plugin. If not, you will need to acquire one before you can follow the remaining steps. In my example, I am using an EastWest orchestral instrument; FL Studio does not come with this instrument by default:

+   1. Go to the plugin options at the top left of your orchestral instrument plugin and select the **Browse parameters** option:

![](bbox=[0.13, 0.23, 0.855, 0.725])

After selecting **Browse parameters**, a list of all the plugin effects that are available for your instrument will appear in the Browser. Hopefully, your plugin will have an *expression* effect.
