If you want to make your sounds even creepier, you can consider slowing down the time and pitch of the audio as well.

# Rendering audio to a wave form

If you have an instrument playing MIDI notes and you want to reverse the sound, you won’t be able to simply double-click on the sample in the playlist. There’s an additional step you need to do before you can reverse your sound. You need to convert the MIDI music notes to an audio waveform first. Once that is done, the sound becomes a regular audio sample, which you can reverse using the previously described method.

You can convert the instrument's MIDI note music to an audio sample using a technique called **rendering to audio**, more commonly known as **freezing audio**.
