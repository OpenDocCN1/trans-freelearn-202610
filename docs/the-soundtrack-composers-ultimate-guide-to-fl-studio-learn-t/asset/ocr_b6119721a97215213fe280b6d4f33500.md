# Igniter

Krotos's **Igniter** plugin allows you to create the sounds of vehicles, sports cars, motorbikes, planes, helicopters, spacecraft, drones, and many other vehicles.

![](bbox=[0.148, 0.191, 0.869, 0.512])

Figure 5.24 – Krotos Igniter

It includes the sounds of cars, planes, helicopters, motorcycles, trucks, and tractors. Here’s a list of some of the vehicle sounds included:

Agusta Westland 119x, Aston Martin, Rapide, Audi R8, BMW i3, Bucker BU 131, Cessna 560XL, CH-47D Chinook, Dacia 1310, De Havilland Tiger Moth, Ferrari 348, Ford F150, Harley Davidson 99 Hog, Honda Civic, Huey UH-1H, Land Rover Defender, Mercedes Actros, Porsche Carrera, Subaru Crosstrek SUV, Agusta Westland, Tesla Model S, Toyota Chaser, U650M Tractor, and Subaru Impreza

You can learn more about Igniter at https://www.krotosaudio.com/igniter/.
