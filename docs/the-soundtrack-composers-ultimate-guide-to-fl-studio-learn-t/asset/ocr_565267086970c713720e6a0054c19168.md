If you don’t have notes already, play notes with your MIDI keyboard while recording. Alternatively, you can have the notes already inputted in the Piano roll, and just record your mod wheel automation on top of your existing notes.

While the notes are playing, move the mod wheel to control the instrument's expression. Moving the mod wheel up will increase the expression's intensity, while moving it down will decrease the expression's intensity.

If you want to use both hands while playing the MIDI keyboard instead of having one hand on a mod wheel, you could connect an expression pedal to your MIDI keyboard.
