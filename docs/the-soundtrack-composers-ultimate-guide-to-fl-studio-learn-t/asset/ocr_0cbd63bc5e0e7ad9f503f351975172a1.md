# Considerations for mixing orchestral compositions

When mixing, you strive to achieve a nice balance between the low, mid, and high frequencies. This means you need to be careful when you're choosing instruments. Your note choice will also dictate whether you can achieve a nice balance. For example, the bass should rarely lie more than an octave from the part above it. Anything much more and you'll find that you're lacking in the mid frequencies. This is the reason why you should usually focus on five voices in the string section (one for each section – contrabass, cello, viola, first violin, and second violin). Achieving a good composition of these five voices will give you a nice balance of lows, mids, and highs.
