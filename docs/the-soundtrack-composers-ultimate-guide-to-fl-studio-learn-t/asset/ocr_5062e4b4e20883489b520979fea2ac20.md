The music plays **Combat-A** and **Combat-B** sequentially. Then, there's a 50% probability it will play **Combat-Bridge**, and then a 50% probability that it plays the **Combat-TransToBridge** and **Combat-Bridge** tracks.

The game is playing a loop, but a loop that varies according to set probabilities. Sometimes it plays just the **Combat-A** and **Combat-B** tracks. Sometimes it plays **Combat-Bridge**, and sometimes it plays the **Combat-TransToBridge** and **Combat-Bridge** tracks. Playing music in this manner helps to create some random variation in audio for the listener.

Music segments can be dragged from the **Audio** tab into **Music Playlist Editor**. You can then designate the order and the probability of the song playing that you want.
