## How it works

Content should always be distributed from workspaces and not My Workspace. Even in relatively informal scenarios such as sharing a dashboard with one user, sharing a dashboard from My Workspace creates a dependency on a single user to maintain the content. Sharing the content from a workspace with multiple members of the BI team addresses the risk of a single user not being available to maintain the content and benefits from Microsoft’s ongoing investments in administration and governance features for workspaces.

Members of the workspace with edit rights can disable reshares of the dashboard and stop sharing altogether. To edit rights, open the dashboard, click on Share, click the ellipsis (...), and then click Manage permissions to identify and optionally revise the current access.

## How it works

Per the *Preparing for Content Creation and Collaboration* recipe earlier in this chapter, workspaces have a one-to-one mapping to apps and can have a wide scope (for example, sales), a narrow scope such as a specific dashboard, or a balance between these two extremes, such as European sales. If broad workspace scopes are used, then it may not be necessary to create a new workspace for a particular project or dashboard as new content can be added to an existing workspace. However, as an increasing volume of reports and dashboards are added to workspaces, it may be beneficial to consider new, more focused workspaces and revisions to the policy.

As covered in the recipe, users and groups can be added to one of four different workspace roles: **Admin**, **Member**, **Contributor**, or **Viewer**. Care should be given in assigning these roles as each role conveys certain privileges that may have unintended consequences.

The **Admin** role provides the ability to manage all aspects of the workspace, including adding and removing users and configuring all workspace settings.

The **Member** role provides the same level of access as the Admin role except for the ability to update or delete the workspace, add or remove any user in the workspace (can only add members or others with lower permissions), allow contributors to update the app for the workspace, and manage subscriptions of others.
