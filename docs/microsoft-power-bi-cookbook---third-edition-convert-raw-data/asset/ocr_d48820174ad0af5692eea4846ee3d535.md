# About the authors

**Greg Deckler** is a seven-time Microsoft MVP for Data Platform. He is an active blogger and Power BI community member, having authored over 7,000 solutions to community questions. Greg has authored numerous books on Power BI, including *Learn Power BI First and Second Editions*, *DAX Cookbook*, *Power BI Cookbook Second Edition*, and *Mastering Power BI Second Edition*. Greg has also created several external tools for Power BI and regularly posts video content to his YouTube channels, *Microsoft Hates Greg* and *DAX For Humans*.

*I would like to acknowledge the wonderful Power BI community that continues to inspire and educate me every day. Also, I want to acknowledge my wonderful son, Rocket, who motivates and supports me in all my creative endeavors.*

**Brett Powell** is a Microsoft Business Intelligence consultant and author. Brett has over 15 years of Business Intelligence experience across many industries as a developer, architect, and administrator. Although he’s most known for authoring the *Microsoft Power BI Cookbook* and *Mastering Microsoft Power BI* as well as his *Insights Quest* blog, Brett primarily focuses on delivering successful project engagements for clients via Frontline Analytics LLC, a Microsoft BI consultancy and partner.

*I give all glory and praise to my Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.*
