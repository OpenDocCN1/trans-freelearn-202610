# Connecting Dynamics 365 and Power Platform

Power BI is part of Microsoft’s Power Platform family of products. Power Platform includes Power BI, Power Apps, Power Automate, Power Pages, and Power Virtual Agents. However, Power Platform is also part of a larger family of products, Microsoft Business Applications. Microsoft Business Applications is a set of applications built around Dynamics 365 for Sales, Dynamics 365 Business Central, and Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain Management (F&SCM). Microsoft actually breaks down Business Applications into the following:

+   - Microsoft Power Platform
+   - Customer data platform
+   - Sales
+   - Service
+   - Marketing
+   - Commerce
+   - Supply chain
+   - Small and medium business
+   - HR
+   - Finance
+   - Project management
