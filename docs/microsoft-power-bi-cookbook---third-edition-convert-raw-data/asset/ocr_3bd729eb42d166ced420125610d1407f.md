+   4. In the **New environment** pane, enter a **Name** for the environment, choose a **Type** (either **Production, Sandbox, or Trial**), and choose a **Region**. Additionally, toggle **Add a Dataverse data store** to **Yes** and click the **Next** button at the bottom of the pane.
+   5. Choose a **Language** and **Currency**. Provide a **URL** if desired.
+   6. Select a **Security group** by clicking **Select**. You can choose **None** as the security group.
+   7. Toggle **Enable Dynamics 365 apps** to **Yes**.
+   8. Click the dropdown for **Automatically deploy these apps** and check all the boxes, except **Project Operations** and **Guides**.
