This recipe demonstrates how to utilize Teams to distribute and collaborate on Power BI content.

# Getting ready

To prepare for this recipe, follow these steps:

+   1. Check with your Microsoft Teams administrator and ensure that the Power BI app is unblocked and available to you within the Microsoft Teams admin center. The defaults from Microsoft are that the Power BI app is unblocked and available to everyone. However, organizational security teams and administrators may restrict the use of any or all apps within Teams.
+   2. Ensure that you have published a report, dashboard, or app to the Power BI service.
+   3. Open Microsoft Teams and join or create a team.

You are now prepared to complete this recipe.

# How to distribute content with Teams

To implement this recipe, use the following steps:

+   1. Within Teams, select Teams in the navigation pane and then select a channel within a team.
+   2. In the Teams’ header area (tabs), select the plus icon (+) to add a tab.
