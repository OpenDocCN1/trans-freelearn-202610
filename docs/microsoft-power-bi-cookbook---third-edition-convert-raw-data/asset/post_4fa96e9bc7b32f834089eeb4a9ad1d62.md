# Contributors

# About the authors

**Greg Deckler** is a seven-time Microsoft MVP for Data Platform. He is an active blogger and Power BI community member, having authored over 7,000 solutions to community questions. Greg has authored numerous books on Power BI, including *Learn Power BI First and Second Editions*, *DAX Cookbook*, *Power BI Cookbook Second Edition*, and *Mastering Power BI Second Edition*. Greg has also created several external tools for Power BI and regularly posts video content to his YouTube channels, *Microsoft Hates Greg* and *DAX For Humans*.

*I would like to acknowledge the wonderful Power BI community that continues to inspire and educate me every day. Also, I want to acknowledge my wonderful son, Rocket, who motivates and supports me in all my creative endeavors.*

**Brett Powell** is a Microsoft Business Intelligence consultant and author. Brett has over 15 years of Business Intelligence experience across many industries as a developer, architect, and administrator. Although he’s most known for authoring the *Microsoft Power BI Cookbook* and *Mastering Microsoft Power BI* as well as his *Insights Quest* blog, Brett primarily focuses on delivering successful project engagements for clients via Frontline Analytics LLC, a Microsoft BI consultancy and partner.

*I give all glory and praise to my Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.*

**Andrei Khaidarov** is an accomplished expert in the fields of data management, data analytics, machine learning, and information technology. He holds a PhD, having focused his dissertation related to mathematical modeling.

Throughout his career, Andrei has made significant contributions to both academia and industry. He has authored and co-authored over 70 scientific papers, patents, books, software packages, and educational guides. His professional experience includes holding senior and lead positions in various organizations, where he has driven numerous innovative projects to success.

Andrei has been a Microsoft Most Valuable Professional (MVP) since 2023, receiving accolades for his contributions to the technology community. He is certified in multiple Microsoft and Google technologies, including Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, Power Apps, Azure, and Google Analytics, and holds many others professional certifications. He is also a Microsoft Certified Trainer, sharing his extensive knowledge with the next generation of professionals.

In addition to his technical prowess, Andrei is deeply involved in mentorship and community initiatives. He has guided numerous undergraduate and graduate students, organized conferences, and served as a jury member and expert for international competitions and grant allocation funds.

Andrei has worked on several technical books as a reviewer, providing detailed feedback and technical insights to ensure the highest-quality content. His expertise spans data management, data visualization, machine learning, AI, and more, making him a valuable asset in any technical endeavor.
