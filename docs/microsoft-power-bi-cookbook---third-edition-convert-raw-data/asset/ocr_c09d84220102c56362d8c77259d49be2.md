# How to create scorecards

To complete this recipe, perform the following steps:

+   1. Navigate to the workspace where North American Sales is located in the service.
+   2. Under the workspace name, select New, and then Scorecard.
+   3. Double-click on Untitled Scorecard and rename the scorecard North American Sales Scorecard.
+   4. For Metric name, enter North American Total Net Sales.
+   5. Under Current value, click the dropdown for Set up, choose Use submetrics, and then SUM.

+   6. Repeat this for Final target.
+   7. Click the Save button.
+   8. Hover your mouse over the new metric, click the three vertical dots, and choose New submetric.
