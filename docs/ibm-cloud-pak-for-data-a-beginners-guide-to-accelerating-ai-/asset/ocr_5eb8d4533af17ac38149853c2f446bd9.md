# Planning Analytics

Easily create more accurate plans, budgets, and forecasts using data from across your business. A good plan starts with good data. Ensure that your plans are based on data from across your business with IBM Planning Analytics, powered by TM1®. Planning Analytics is an AI-infused solution that pulls data from multiple sources and automates the creation of plans, budgets, and forecasts. Planning Analytics integrates with Microsoft Excel so that you can continue to use a familiar interface while moving beyond the traditional limits of a spreadsheet.

Infuse your spreadsheets with more analytical power to build sophisticated, multi-dimensional models that help you to create more reliable plans and forecasts. The Planning Analytics service includes the following:

+   - Easy-to-use visualization tools

+   - Built-in data analytics and reporting capabilities

+   - What-if scenario modeling that helps you understand the impact of your decisions:
