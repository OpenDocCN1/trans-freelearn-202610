## Industry accelerators

The industry accelerators that are provided by IBM are a set of artifacts that help you address common business issues. Each industry accelerator is designed to help you solve a specific business problem, whether it's preventing credit card fraud in the banking industry or optimizing the efficiency of your contact center.
