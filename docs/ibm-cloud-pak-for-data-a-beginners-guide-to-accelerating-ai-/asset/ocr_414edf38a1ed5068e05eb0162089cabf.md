# Cognos Analytics

Self-service analytics, infused with AI and machine learning, enable you to create stunning visualizations and share your findings through dashboards and reports.

The Cognos Analytics service makes it easier for you to extract meaning from your data, with features such as the following:

+   - Automated data preparation
+   - Automated modeling
+   - Automated creation of visualizations and dashboards
+   - Data exploration
