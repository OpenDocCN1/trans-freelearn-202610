# Voltage regulator

You maybe wondering why we don't just use 5V batteries to power the Raspberry Pi and reduce the cost of extra batteries and voltage regulators.

It all depends on the same principle as electricity in your home. The power station transports power in extremely high voltages exceeding tens of thousands of volts, but your computer only requires 12V to run.
