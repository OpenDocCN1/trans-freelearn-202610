Were this epidemic to happen in a real population of herons, it may be possible to quarantine the infected heron showing a long infection period to avoid further spread in the population. In conserved populations such as those in wildlife preserves or rehabilitation centers and populations bred for human consumption, this strategy is common to avoid disease spread across animals. In the wild, it is difficult to identify infected animals or even detect an outbreak until many animals turn up sick in areas that are monitored.
