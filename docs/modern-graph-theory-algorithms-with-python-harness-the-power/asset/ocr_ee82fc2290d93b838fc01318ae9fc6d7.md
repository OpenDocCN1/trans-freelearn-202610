*Figure 2.12* shows the network of AIMS students we created from our survey data. Note that some students only have the five closest friends as connections while others have many more close friends. This is typical of a social network, even one created from survey data. Some individuals have more connections than others. Some connect to different parts of a network, linking separated communities. Some highly connected individuals connect to other highly connected individuals, forming dense regions within the larger network. These properties play an important role in network analytics, and we’ll dive deeper into their meanings and computation in later chapters.
