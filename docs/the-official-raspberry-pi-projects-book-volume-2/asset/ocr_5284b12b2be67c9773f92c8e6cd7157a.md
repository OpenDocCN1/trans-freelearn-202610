# >STEP-01
Beacon

A Estimote beacon is attached to a collar and placed around the dog’s neck. Powered by a single cell battery, it’ll give away her position for up to a year.

![](bbox=[0.56, 0.41, 0.93, 0.57])

# >STEP-02
Three Raspberry Pis

Three Raspberry Pis are set up, with WiFi and Bluetooth dongles to act as antennas. They measure the distance of Bean to each Raspberry Pi, which is used to determine her position.
