11. Perform deterministic encryption.

To perform deterministic encryption, follow these steps:

+   I. Click on the **SENSITIVE** icon and navigate to the **Encryption** section.
+   II. Select **Deterministic encryption**.

![](bbox=[0.125, 0.195, 0.855, 0.504])

+   III. Choose the columns you want to encrypt, such as Employee_Full_Name, E Mail, and Date of Birth.
+   IV. Select the secret option and choose the default DataBrew secret key.
+   V. Click on Apply and you will be able to see the encrypted data.
