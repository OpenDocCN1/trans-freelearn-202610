# Copying an entire story

Here are the steps to make a duplicate of an entire story:

+   82. Switch back to the main list of stories by clicking on the Stories breadcrumb.
+   83. Select the story you want to copy.

+   84. Click on the Copy Selected icon.
+   85. Enter a name in the Copy to Story dialog box, and click on OK. The duplicate story will appear on your screen:
