# Present mode

After completing the page, you can preview it by clicking on the Present option under the More toolbar section to check how it will appear to other users. In this mode, the story page is displayed full-screen, without toolbars.

42. In the toolbar, click on the ellipsis icon under the More toolbar section, and select Present:

![](bbox=[0.345, 0.263, 0.655, 0.367])

When you click on the Present option, the page should look like the following screenshot. To switch out of Present view, hover at the top of the page selector bar, and select the Present icon (A) again.
