# Copying objects to the same page

Now you will see the other side of the picture by creating another chart to display the bottom five sales managers.

+   17. Select the Top 5 sales managers chart, and click on the Copy icon.
+   18. In the sub-menu, click on Copy. The selected chart will be copied to clipboard, and a corresponding message (A) will appear on your screen:
+   19. Click on an empty area in the page, and press Ctrl+V to paste the chart from the clipboard onto the page. Alternatively, you can use the Paste option from the Copy and Paste menu on the File toolbar. A copy of the Top 5 sales managers chart appears on the page.
+   20. Select the new chart, and replace its title with Bottom 5 sales managers.
+   21. Click on the Top N options on the chart’s menu.
+   22. Select Top N Options from the sub-menu.
