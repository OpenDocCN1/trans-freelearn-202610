As you can see, I have captured the user's email address and password for this social media site. This is such an easy process compared to trying to crack their password or guessing it.

The nice thing on the user side is that, when they fail to log in, they are actually redirected to the original site. They can then log in, and it will be successful. The user will just think they entered the wrong email and password, but in reality, they gave me all their information.
