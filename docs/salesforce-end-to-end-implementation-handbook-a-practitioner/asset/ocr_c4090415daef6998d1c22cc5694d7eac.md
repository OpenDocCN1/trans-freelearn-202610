# Important note!

If your workshops and analysis at this stage have revealed *significant* variations in business processes, reporting requirements, or data segregation requirements, you need to carry out an important analysis to determine your **org strategy**. Determining an org strategy is beyond the scope of this book. You will need to engage with an experienced Salesforce technical architect to assist you in this analysis.
