# Creating a roadmap for your Salesforce program

The term *program* is used here intentionally as the program may consist of many projects and streams related to Salesforce – but not necessarily included in the scope of your initial Salesforce project, or at least not in the initial release of your Salesforce solution.

Let’s see the **Salesforce program roadmap** our scenario company, PME, has put together, illustrated in a classic **transformation map (T-Map)**.

+   - **MVP Release**: The MVP release contains capabilities currently supported by PME’s existing email marketing and CRM systems, in addition to a few select capabilities that PME believes will bring great value to the business.
