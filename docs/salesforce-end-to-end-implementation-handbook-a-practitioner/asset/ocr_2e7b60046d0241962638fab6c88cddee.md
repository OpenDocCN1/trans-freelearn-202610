As a *rough* rule of thumb, if your solution was – in the pre-development phase – initially estimated to require less than 5 sprints (10 weeks) for a small development team to build, you may consider breaking down all the business processes to user stories, and have solutions proposed and estimated prior to beginning development. If you choose to do so, you should still be mindful that some things may change as you start building your solution – your PO may be inspired or receive new input from end users.
