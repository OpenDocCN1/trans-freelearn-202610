# Defining

Your **data steward** should lead the definition of your organization’s goals for data quality across all the domains supported by your Salesforce platform. Your data steward should not be alone in this exercise, but rather work together with **data owners** – consumers of the data and representatives from the domains of your platform.

You can operationalize and make your goals concrete by using a data quality goal template:

*The [Field_Object_RecordType] to be [Quality Definition] in order for [Stakeholder] to be able to [achieve business value]*

An example of a practical data goal is as follows:

*The “billing address on the Account object of Record Type Bill-To” to be “verified at least annually” in order for the “finance team” to be able to “invoice with minimum risk”*
