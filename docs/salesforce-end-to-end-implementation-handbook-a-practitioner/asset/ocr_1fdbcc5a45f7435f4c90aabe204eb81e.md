Data being of high quality all the time is the ambition. But when you have to prioritize your efforts to improve it, consider the timing of when the data needs to be accurate and complete. A Lead, for example, may not need to be 100% complete on creation – just enough to be able to contact it and confirm information and interest. At Lead conversion though, the quality does need to be high.
