In loving memory of my beloved father, my greatest inspiration, my strength, and the guiding light who first encouraged me to write this book.

Not a day passes without feeling the quiet absence you left behind, yet in every moment of doubt and every step forward, I still feel your presence—steady, reassuring, and everlasting. Your love shaped who I am, your belief in me gave me courage, and your strength became the foundation on which I continue to build my journey.

Everything I have achieved, and everything I continue to strive for, carries a part of you. You were not only my inspiration but the silent force behind these pages—the reason this work exists. Though you are no longer physically here, your spirit lives on in my thoughts, my efforts, and every line of this book. This book is, and always will be, for you.

With deepest love and gratitude, I also dedicate this work to my beloved mother, whose unconditional love, strength, and blessings have been my constant support and the foundation of my life.

— Sandipan Dey
