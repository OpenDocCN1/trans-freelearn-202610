*Chapter 6, Frequency Domain Filtering*, details frequency-domain filtering techniques including low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, and notch filters using Ideal, Butterworth, and Gaussian formulations, with applications in denoising, periodic noise removal, and Fourier feature representations.

*Chapter 7, Image Enhancement*, covers point-wise intensity transformations, histogram processing, contrast enhancement, dithering, fuzzy logic-based enhancement, color correction, denoising techniques, wavelet methods, and modern deep learning-based enhancement models including Zero-DCE, BM3D, and EDSR.

*Chapter 8, Image Enhancements Using Derivatives*, explains derivative-based image processing including gradients, Laplacian operators, edge detection (Sobel, Canny, LoG, DoG, compass filters), anisotropic diffusion, structured and ridge-based detection, multiscale pyramids, and deep learning-based edge detection methods.

*Chapter 9, Image Restoration: Inverse Problems in Imaging*, provides a rigorous treatment of image restoration, including deconvolution, Wiener filtering, Tikhonov regularization, Bayesian formulations, CLEAN algorithms, sparse representations, and modern deep learning-based restoration and inpainting methods such as NAFNet, LaMa, and diffusion models.

*Chapter 10, Image Segmentation: From Classical Methods to Deep Learning*, explains segmentation techniques ranging from thresholding, watershed, superpixels, and variational methods to deep learning approaches, including FCNs, DeepLab, Mask R-CNN, YOLO-based segmentation, and transformer-based models such as SegFormer, Mask2Former, and DETR.

*Chapter 11, More Deep Learning Methods for Image Segmentation*, explores promptable and multimodal segmentation models such as SAM and CLIP-based methods, vision–language integration, monocular depth estimation, 3D reconstruction, medical and remote sensing segmentation, and evaluation metrics like IoU, Dice, and mAP.

*Chapter 12, Image Classification and Object Detection*, details image classification and detection using CNNs, transfer learning, Vision Transformers, few-shot and zero-shot learning, self-supervised learning, adversarial robustness, OCR pipelines, face recognition, and real-world applications including AR filters, deepfakes, and industrial inspection systems.

*Chapter 13, Generative AI in Image Processing and Computer Vision*, explores generative modeling from GANs and VAEs to diffusion models and large-scale foundation models, including Stable Diffusion, ControlNet, DALL·E, and GPT-based image systems, along with applications in image synthesis, editing, inpainting, restoration, multimodal reasoning, and domain-specific generation across science, medicine, and industry.
