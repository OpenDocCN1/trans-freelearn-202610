**Visualization of Temporal Morphing:** The generated GIF demonstrates identity transition, smooth facial structure deformation and preservation of high-frequency details.

This confirms that the *StyleGANEX* latent space is **semantically consistent and geodesically smooth** (i.e., interpolations along the *shortest path in latent space* produce gradual, continuous changes in the generated images without abrupt visual artifacts, confirming semantic consistency in the StyleGANEX latent space).

**StyleGAN3** learns a *pure generative manifold*: $z \to x$, whereas **StyleGANEX** learns an *invertible semantic mapping*: $x \leftrightarrow w \leftrightarrow x'$. Together, they represent two complementary paradigms in modern generative vision: synthesis-first (StyleGAN3) and reconstruction-and-editing-first (StyleGANEX).

Building on these two complementary paradigms of generation and editing, the next method introduces how pretrained generative priors can further be leveraged for image restoration tasks.

# # GFPGAN: Blind face restoration using Generative Facial Priors

**Generative Facial Prior GAN (GFPGAN)** is a face restoration framework that combines a restoration network with a pretrained StyleGAN prior to recover realistic and identity-preserving facial details from degraded images. Unlike conventional enhancement methods that operate purely at the pixel level, GFPGAN leverages a learned facial manifold to reconstruct plausible facial structures.

Given a degraded image $y$, $y = D(x) + n$, where $x$ is the unknown high-quality face image, $D(\cdot)$ denotes an unknown degradation process, and $n$ represents noise, the goal is to estimate the restored image $\hat{x} = R(y)$. GFPGAN constrains the reconstruction using a pretrained StyleGAN generator, $x_G = G(w)$, where $w$ is a latent representation in the StyleGAN latent space. The model combines reconstruction, perceptual, adversarial, and identity-preservation losses to generate visually realistic faces while maintaining facial identity.
