# Mathematical explanation of ringing

The Gibbs phenomenon occurs when there is a sharp discontinuity in a signal (in our case, the edges of the circle). The Fourier series representation of such a signal converges to the correct function value everywhere except near the discontinuity, where it overshoots and oscillates, leading to ringing artifacts.

When we apply a Gaussian filter, the smoothing reduces the sharpness of the edge, decreasing the amount of high-frequency content in the Fourier transform, which helps mitigate the ringing.

# Linearity and distributivity

**Linearity** means the DFT of a combined image is the same as the sum of the DFTs of the individual images, while **distributivity** means you can apply the DFT to parts of the image separately and then combine the results, yielding the same final outcome.
