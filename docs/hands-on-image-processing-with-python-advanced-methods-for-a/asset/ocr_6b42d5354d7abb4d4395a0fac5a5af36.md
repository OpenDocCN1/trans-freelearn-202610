# Applications of the chromaticity diagram

The CIE chromaticity diagram has a wide range of practical applications in color science and technology, including the following:

+   - **Color display calibration:** The diagram helps calibrate devices (e.g., monitors) by comparing their gamut to human perception.
+   - **Illuminant and light source comparisons:** The diagram can show the chromaticity coordinates of different illuminants or light sources.
+   - **Color research:** Useful for studying how colors are perceived and how different light conditions affect color appearance.
