Beyond GAN-based frameworks—including cGANs, CVAEs, CycleGAN, StyleGAN, and GFPGAN—a complementary and equally seminal paradigm in modern generative AI is *diffusion models*, which have emerged as a foundational approach for high-fidelity image synthesis through iterative denoising.

# ## Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPMs)

Diffusion models have emerged as one of the most successful generative modeling paradigms, powering modern image synthesis systems such as Stable Diffusion, Imagen, and DALL-E. Unlike GANs, which learn to generate images through adversarial training, **Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPMs)** learn to gradually reverse a stochastic noise process. The central idea is remarkably simple: an image is progressively corrupted by adding Gaussian noise until it becomes pure noise, and a neural network is trained to invert this process and reconstruct the original image.

DDPMs consist of two complementary processes:

+   - **Forward Diffusion** (noise addition)
+   - **Reverse Diffusion** (noise removal)

Together, these processes define a Markov chain that transforms images into noise and then reconstructs images from noise.
