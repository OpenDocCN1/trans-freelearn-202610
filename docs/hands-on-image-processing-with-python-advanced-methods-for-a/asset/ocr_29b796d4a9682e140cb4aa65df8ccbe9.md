# Part 3

# Computer Vision and Generative AI

Part 3 covers modern computer vision systems and state-of-the-art deep learning and generative AI methods that enable machines to interpret, understand, and create visual content. It begins with image segmentation, progressing from classical approaches such as thresholding, clustering, and watershed methods to deep learning architectures including fully convolutional networks, encoder–decoder models, and transformer-based segmentation systems.

The part then expands into higher-level visual understanding tasks, including image classification and object detection using CNNs, attention mechanisms, Vision Transformers, and modern detection frameworks such as YOLO and Mask R-CNN. It also introduces advanced learning paradigms such as transfer learning, few-shot learning, self-supervised learning, and adversarial robustness, providing a comprehensive view of modern vision systems.

The final chapter focuses on generative AI for vision, covering GANs, VAEs, and diffusion models, along with large-scale foundation models such as Stable Diffusion, ControlNet, DALL·E, and GPT-based multimodal systems. These models enable image synthesis, editing, inpainting, super-resolution, and text-guided generation. Emphasis is placed on real-world applications across domains such as medical imaging, autonomous driving, remote sensing, industrial inspection, and creative AI systems.

By the end of this part, readers will be equipped to build and understand end-to-end computer vision pipelines and modern generative AI systems capable of both perception and creation.
