+   * `{tropical fruit} => {bottled water}`

+   * We can see that tropical fruit occurs in three separate transaction

+   fruit is purchased as well and you can easily verify that by inspecting
+   and counting the elements by hand

# ## Lift

Lift is determined by dividing the confidence just calculated by the
independent probability of the consequent. Lift can be a better measure than
either support or confidence, since it incorporates features of both.

To calculate the lift for the second rule (index #64), we only need to
determine the unconditional probability of the consequence we just
calculated. Since `{bottled water}` appears two out of 10 times (20%) as the
consequence, we divide .67 by .20 to yield 3.4, which is the lift for rule 64.
