**Undo** is a helpful safety net in Excel, but bear in mind that certain actions in Excel circumvent your ability to undo any previous actions:

+   - Deleting a worksheet
+   - Running a macro in Excel
+   - Saving and closing a document, and other actions that involve Excel’s **File** menu

Therefore, it is important to have secondary measures in place that can give you fallback positions, such as Excel’s **AutoRecover** feature.
