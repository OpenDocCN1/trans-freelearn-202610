Let’s say that your workbook is named Budget.xlsx and is saved in your **Documents** folder. Every time you save your budget, the previous version of the workbook will be renamed Copy of years. You Budget .xlk and will also be saved inside your **Documents** folder. Each time you save the Budget .xlsx file, Excel updates the back-up copy of your workbook. I can attest that this feature has saved me from losing critical workbooks numerous times over the years. You never know when a file will get corrupted while you’re saving.
