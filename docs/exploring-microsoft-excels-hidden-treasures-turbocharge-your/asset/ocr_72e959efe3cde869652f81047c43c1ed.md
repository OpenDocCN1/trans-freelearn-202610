# ## Wingdings font

You can create your own types of cell icons by using the **Wingdings** font in conjunction with an `IF` function. This allows you to go beyond **Conditional Formatting** to create any sort of symbology that you choose. I’ll share a brief example:

+   1. Click on a blank cell in an Excel worksheet, such as cell G1 in Figure 4.24.

![](bbox=[0.149, 0.469, 0.871, 0.575])

+   2. Choose the **Symbol** command from the **Insert** menu in Excel.

+   3. Choose **Wingdings** from the **Font** list.

+   4. Choose a symbol, such as the **checkmark** (b) icon shown in Figure 4.24, click **Insert**, and then click **Close**.

+   5. Move your cursor to another blank cell, such as cell G2, and then repeat _steps 1–4_ to pick a second symbol, such as the **x** (ý) icon shown in Figure 4.24.
