> > **Tip**
> > You can also press _Ctrl + Z_ (_⌘ + Z_) to undo a **Table** expansion. If you change your mind, press _Ctrl + Y_ (_⌘ + Y_) to restore the expansion.

If you choose **Stop Automatically Expanding Tables** from the menu, you’ll prevent all **Tables** from expanding to incorporate new data that you add below or to the right of the **Table**.

> > **Nuance**
> > The **Control AutoCorrect Options** command is a shortcut to the setting that will enable auto-expansion for **Tables** again, but you won’t be able to get there from here. The **AutoCorrect Options** button ceases to appear on the screen after you choose **Stop Automatically Expanding Tables**.
