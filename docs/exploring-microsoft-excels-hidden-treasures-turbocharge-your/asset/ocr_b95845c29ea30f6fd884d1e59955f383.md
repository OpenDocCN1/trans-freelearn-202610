Let’s look at a situation where it will be harder for **Goal Seek** to find a solution. Enter the formula  
=RANDBETWEEN ( 50 , 100 ) / 1000 into cell **C11** of the **Goal Seek** worksheet to create a variable  
interest rate.

The RANDBETWEEN function has two arguments:

+   • **Bottom** – This is the smallest possible random number to be generated

+   • **Top** – This is the largest possible random number to be generated

In this case, dividing the result of RANDBETWEEN by 1,000 gives us a percentage between 5% and 10%.  
RANDBETWEEN is a volatile worksheet function, which means it recalculates every time a change is  
made anywhere in the workbook. Most worksheet functions in Excel are non-volatile, meaning that  
Excel only recalculates the formula when any cell that the formula references changes. In this case,  
we’re creating a moving target that will be much harder for **Goal Seek** to hit as a way of emulating  
what you might experience in a complex workbook.
