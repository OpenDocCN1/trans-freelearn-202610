IV. Activate the **PivotTable Report** worksheet, click on any cell within the **PivotTable**, and then choose **PivotTable Analyze | Refresh.**

V. **India** should now appear on your **PivotTable**.

**Tip**
The **Table Design | Summarize with PivotTable** command makes it easy to create a **PivotTable** based on a list without needing to activate the **Insert** tab.

As you can see, **Tables** eliminate reselecting expanded datasets with the **Change Data Source** option, but you must still refresh your **PivotTables** any time you change the contents of a **Table** in any way— the same as with **PivotTables** that reference a normal range of cells.
