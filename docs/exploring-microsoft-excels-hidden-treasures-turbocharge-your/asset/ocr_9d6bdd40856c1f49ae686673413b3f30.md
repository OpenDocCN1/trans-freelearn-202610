**Tip**

The **Custom View** we created to display all columns and rows on the **Summary** worksheet
is capturing the settings of all other worksheets in the workbook as well. **Custom Views** are
always applied at a workbook level, even when your focus is on a single worksheet.

Now, let’s test the view:

+   1. Select one or more columns, such as C:R.

+   2. Right-click on any column in the selection and choose **Hide** (or choose **Home | Format | Hide & Unhide | Hide Columns**).

+   3. Choose **View | Custom Views** and then click **OK** since we only have a single view.
