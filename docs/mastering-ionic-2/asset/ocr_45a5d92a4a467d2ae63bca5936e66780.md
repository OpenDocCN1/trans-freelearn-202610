# # Disadvantages of PWA

The following are the disadvantages of PWA:

+   - **Traffic through store**: PWA miss significant traffic who use Play Store or application store for their primary search. Users can be redirected to Play Store to download the native application using mobile web, but it doesn't work in case of PWA.
+   - **Plugin integration**: Plugins such as Facebook and Google login cannot fetch data from their applications, we need to fetch it separately on the Web.
+   - **Promotion**: It is getting difficult to promote PWA experience on social media as companies are making their own in-application browsers.
+   - **Browser support**: Full support for PWAs are not available in some browsers varying on devices.
