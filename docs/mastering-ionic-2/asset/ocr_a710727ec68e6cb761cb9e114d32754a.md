We have been talking a lot about beacons, let's look into what are iBeacons are an iBeacon is a protocol developed by Apple, which sets a layout about how to transmit data signals. Generally, there are two protocols for BLE broadcasting iBeacon and eddystone. Standard iBeacon advertisement consists of UUID, major and minor:

```
UUID: f7826da6-4fa2-4e98-8024-bc5b71e0893e

Major ID: 65279

Minor ID: 63150
```
