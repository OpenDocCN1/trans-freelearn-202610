# Zoom IN and OUT of a Schedule

In the latest Revit versions, we can zoom IN and OUT of schedules.

If you open any Revit schedule, you can use Ctrl + “+” to zoom in and Ctrl + “-” to zoom out the schedule. However, the most practical process is to hold the Ctrl key and rotate the mouse wheel to zoom into the schedule.

> Tip
> To return to 100% zoom, just press Ctrl + 0.
