# The hunting queries list

Below the header is a listing of all the hunting queries. At the top of the listing is the MITRE ATT&CK® breakdown. Clicking on any of the icons in the MITRE ATT&CK® breakdown will show you only those queries that have that specific attack type associated with them. So, if you click on the Initial Access icon (the first icon that looks like a monitor), it will only show those hunting queries that have Initial Access as one of their entries in the Tactics field.

The summary bar gives you a good overview of how your hunting queries are broken down. This can make it easier to find those queries that you need in your investigations.
