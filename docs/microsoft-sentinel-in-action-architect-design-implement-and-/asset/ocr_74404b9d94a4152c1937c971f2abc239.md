One other useful entry is **Hidden**. Selecting this will cause the column to not display in the grid. There may be times when you will need to have the column around, but do not want to show it. Set the column's renderer to **Hidden** for this to happen. It's outside the scope of the book to go into more detail on how to use these different renderers but look at the **Azure AD Sign-in logs** workbook to get an idea of how to use **Heatmap** and **Spark line**.
