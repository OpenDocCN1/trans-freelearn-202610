Now that we know how to look at an incident, its related incidents, and its exploration queries, let's discuss the incident buttons located on the far-right side of the screen in detail.

# The Timeline button

The Timeline button shows the timeline of all the incidents being shown on screen. If this incident was just opened, then there will probably only be one incident shown, but if you show a lot of related alerts, then the timeline will show a history of when the alerts occurred, as shown in the following screenshot:

Figure 9.24 – Investigation timeline

In the preceding screenshot, notice that the Suspicious granting of permissions incident is selected in the Timeline list. When you select a single incident, only those entities related to that incident will be highlighted in the view; the rest will be grayed out, as shown. This makes it easy to progress through the timeline and see what entities are related to which incidents.
