## The Entities tab

This tab will show all the entities associated with this incident, as shown in the following screenshot. There will be one row for each of the entities associated with the incident:

On the right side of the screen are two columns of buttons. The column on the left contains the screen control buttons. The top button will fit your diagram to the screen. While being able to see the incident graphically is nice, the real benefit comes when you look at the related alerts to get a bigger picture of the incident.
