Remember that you are uploading an *interactive* module. This means that the learner viewing this module will be asked to perform actions within the module. These actions can be as simple as clicking buttons, but they can also be drag-and-drop interactions, click-and-reveal interactions, games, quizzes, and more. The author of the module can also grade these interactions, awarding (or removing) points to (or from) the learners as they progress into the module. Also, remember that the SCORM (or AICC or xAPI) standard allows for communication between your training module and the LMS. This communication capability is used to report interactions and scores to the LMS.

This interactivity layer makes it possible to differentiate the *completion criteria* from the *success criteria*. The completion criteria determines when this course module can be considered *complete* for a particular learner, whereas the success criteria determines if the learner has *passed* or *failed* this particular course module.

Normally, these criteria are set by the module author within the authoring tool when creating the SCORM package. Captivate Prime only reads this data from the uploaded .zip file. You can, however, modify these criteria within Prime.

- 8. Click the **Edit** button located in the top-right corner of the page.
- 9. Modify the completion criteria and/or the success criteria as you require.
- 10. Click the **Save** button in the top-right corner of the page to confirm the changes and go back to the Content Library.

You should now have one more module available in your Content Library. Note that, in this section, you uploaded a SCORM 2004-compliant package, but the process is the same when uploading a SCORM 1.2 package, an AICC package, or an xAPI-compliant package.

## Uploading interactive content from Adobe Captivate

Captivate Prime supports interactive content created by *any* authoring tool, provided that the authoring tool is capable of generating either an AICC-, a SCORM-, or an xAPI-compliant package. Adobe Captivate is one such authoring tool, but there are plenty of other excellent applications available, such as Articulate Storyline, TechSmith Camtasia, iSpring, and others.

That being said, if you are using Adobe Captivate as your authoring tool of choice, you do have access to one extra feature that allows you to upload your content to Captivate Prime directly from within Adobe Captivate.

**Note**
The steps of this exercise refer to Adobe Captivate 2019. So, you must have Adobe Captivate 2019 (or higher) installed on your computer to perform this exercise. If you use a newer version of Adobe Captivate, the steps outlined here will, most likely, differ slightly, but the general idea remains the same. Adobe Captivate is available on both Mac and Windows. A free 30-day trial can be downloaded at https://www.adobe.com/products/captivate/download-trial/try.html. The trial version has the same capabilities as the permanent version, with one exception: the content produced by the trial version expires at the same time as the trial. If you do not have access to Adobe Captivate, simply read the following steps to get a general understanding of the process.

Let's review the process of uploading content from Captivate to Captivate Prime hands-on using the following steps:

- 1. Open Adobe Captivate 2019 (or higher).
- 2. Use the **File – Open** menu item to open the `courseContent/extensions_Quiz.cptx` file, which is available in the download associated with this book.

A file should now be open within the Adobe Captivate application. Note that the file extension of a Captivate file is `.cptx`.

Let's review the content of this file and some of its settings before uploading it to Prime.

- 3. On the left-hand side of the Captivate interface, scroll the **Filmstrip** up and down to review the content of this file. Note that a three-question quiz has been inserted at the end of this Captivate module.
- 4. Use the **Project – Quiz Preference** menu item to open the **Quiz Preferences** dialog box.

Even if you haven't seen Captivate before, take some time to review the **Quiz Preferences** dialog box. At the top of the box, notice that *reporting* is enabled for this particular project (see 1 in the following screenshot). Also, notice that this project uses the *SCORM 2004* standard (see 2 in the following screenshot). Finally, the other options that are available in the **Quiz Preferences** dialog (see 3 in the following screenshot) roughly match the **Completion Criteria** and the **Success Criteria** options available in Captivate Prime:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_98_0.png)

Figure 3.9 – The Quiz Preferences dialog box of Adobe Captivate 2019

Now, let’s close the **Quiz Preferences** dialog and upload this course module to your Captivate Prime Content Library.

- 5. Click the **Cancel** button in the lower-right corner of the **Quiz Preferences** dialog to close the box without saving any of the changes you may have made.
- 6. Click the **Publish** icon located in the main toolbar of Adobe Captivate. Choose **Publish to Adobe Captivate Prime** from the menu that opens.
- 7. Review the information contained in the **Publish to Adobe Captivate Prime** dialog. Then, click the **Publish** button in the lower-right corner of the box.
- 8. When Captivate has finished publishing the content, click the **Continue to Publish** button.
- 9. Enter the email address associated with your Captivate Prime account and click the **Submit** button. If multiple Prime accounts are associated with your email address, choose the account you want to upload this module to.
- 10. Enter your password and click the **Continue** button.

After logging into your Prime account, a form very similar to the one used within Prime to upload interactive content will appear on the screen. Note that the **Completion Criteria** and **Success Criteria** options are already available, and that they have been set to the same values as those that were supplied in the **Quiz Preferences** dialog of Adobe Captivate 2019.

- 11. Enter the name of your new Prime module in the **Content Name** field. Note that this is the only required information in this form.
- 12. As a best practice, take some time to enter a description in the **Short Description** field.
- 13. When ready, click the **Publish** button located in the top-right corner of the form.

After clicking the **Publish** button, your course module will be uploaded directly from Adobe Captivate 2019 to the Content Library of your Captivate Prime account. When the upload is finished, an **Upload Complete** message will appear on the screen.

- 14. Click the **Visit Captivate Prime** button located at the end of the **Upload Complete** message.

This action takes you directly to the **Content Library** page of your Prime account, where your new interactive module is already being processed, as shown in the following screenshot:

In this section, you learned how to upload an interactive course module to your Content Library directly from the Adobe Captivate authoring tool. There are some additional benefits in using Adobe Captivate together with Adobe Captivate Prime:

- - The Adobe Captivate Table of Contents integrates nicely with the Prime **Fluidic Player** (The Fluidic Player is the component of Prime that delivers the course. It will be discussed in more detail in *Chapter 7, Reviewing the Learner Experience*).
- - Captivate Prime honors Captivate's controls for showing/hiding the Table of Contents and the Playbar.
- - Closed Captions created in Adobe Captivate integrate with the Closed Caption controls of Captivate Prime.
- - The ability to configure the completion and success criteria within Captivate Prime without republishing the content in Captivate.

Besides these additional benefits, remember that Captivate Prime can host content created by *any* tool and that the content generated by Adobe Captivate can be uploaded to *any* LMS.
