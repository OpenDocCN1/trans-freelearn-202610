## Automatic enrollment using Learning Plans

**Learning Plans** is yet another way of enrolling users in courses. What makes it special is that, once a learning plan has been defined, learner enrollment is made automatic and does not require any more action from an administrator or manager.

You can use learning plans to automatically enroll learners in *courses*, *learning programs*, and *certifications*. Automatic enrollment of learners is triggered by an event that's chosen when the learning plan is created. Learning plans are very useful in many situations, including the following:

- You want to automatically enroll new learners in onboarding courses.
- You want to automatically enroll learners in courses when they join or leave a group. For example, when someone becomes a manager (that is, when he/she joins the self-generated **All Managers** group), that person is automatically enrolled in a course on *Management Best Practices*. You can use the same technique to automatically enroll learners when they change position, move to a new department, and so on.
- You want to ensure that users are only enrolled in a given course once they have completed a previous course. This is one of the ways to ensure that prerequisites are met.
- You want to automatically enroll learners in courses on a certain date. For example, you need to train people when a new product is released, after an organization acquisition has been made official, or when a new branch opens.

These are only some of the situations where learning plans are very useful. In this section, you will use a learning plan to automatically enroll every new learner in the **Certified Prime Learner** certification you created in the *Creating certifications* section, earlier in this chapter.

Remember that a certification has a time limit for completion. In this example, you have given learners 30 days to complete this particular certification. Adding the training plan to the mix allows you to ensure that each new learner completes a basic course on how to use Captivate Prime within 30 days of registration. The idea is to maximize the learners' use of the platform and provide them with the most compelling learning experiences possible.

Now, let's create a learning plan by performing the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Learning Plans** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar. This takes you to the **Learning Plans** page, where a list of all available learning plans, if any, is displayed.
- 3. Click the **Add** button in the top-right corner of the page to start creating a new learning plan.
- 4. Type `New Learner Prime Certification` in the **Learning Plan Name** field.
- 5. Open the **Occurs When** drop-down and inspect the list of available events.

Notice that the list of available options depends on the chosen event. Take some time to review all the available events and their associated options.

- 6. When you're ready, choose the **New Learner is added** event.
- 7. Start typing `Certified Prime Learner` in the **Training** field. Click the name of the certification when it appears in the list.
- 8. Click the **Save** button in the top-right corner of the page when it looks as follows.

This action closes the **Add Learning Plan** page and takes you back to the **Learning Plans** page:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_265_0.png)

Figure 6.19 – Creating a new learning plan

- 9. The last step is to enable the new learning plan.
- Back on the **Learning Plans** page, use the checkbox to select the learning plan you just created.
- 10. Click the **Actions** button in the top-right corner of the screen.

From now on, each new user that's added to the platform is automatically enrolled in the *Certified Prime User* certification and is given 30 days to complete the *Learner Experience in Adobe Captivate Prime* course.

## Viewing Enrolled Learners

In the *Enrollment by managers* section, earlier in this chapter, you saw how managers have access to the list of learners enrolled in a course. But managers can only see the data about their team members. Also, they do not have access to the waitlist. Only administrators and instructors have a comprehensive overview of all the learners enrolled in a given course.

In this section, you will review the list of enrolled learners from the administrator's perspective. The instructor role will be covered in more detail in *Chapter 8, Exploring the Instructor Role*.

Perform the following steps to log in as an administrator and review the list of learners that have enrolled in the custom course you created in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Find the custom course you created earlier in the list of **Published** courses.
- 4. Hover over the lower area of the course card and click the **View Course** option.
- 5. Click the **learners** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
- 6. Use the **Select Instance** dropdown to select the instance you've enrolled learners in throughout this chapter.
- 7. Expand the **Individuals** and **Self Enrolled** sections to reveal the list of learners enrolled in the course.

This gives you access to the list of learners currently enrolled for the selected instance. This is the same list that *Manager 01* has access to, except that *Learner 03* is visible in the **Self Enrolled** category.

- 8. Take some time to inspect the available tools.

Notice the **Search learners** field in the top-right corner of the list, the **Actions** link just next to it, and the **Enroll** link, which allows you to enroll even more learners in the selected course instance.

These tools are depicted in the following screenshot:
