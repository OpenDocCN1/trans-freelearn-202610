In this section, you learned how to modify the content of the email messages that are sent by Captivate Prime. Remember that the following:

+   - The modifications that are made to the email templates of each course, learning program, or certification override the modifications made to the main **Email Templates** section of the Prime account.
+   - The text you type with your keyboard is considered *static* text and is the same every time the email template is used.
+   - The pieces of text you add by clicking the icons available in the **Template Preview** dialog box are considered *dynamic* text elements. These text elements are different each time the email template is used, depending on the relevant user, course, and so on.
