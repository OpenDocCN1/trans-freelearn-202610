# 7
Reviewing the Learner Experience

In this chapter, learning will finally take place on your Prime account. Remember, this is the primary purpose of Captivate Prime and the main reason your organization implements an LMS in the first place. For you as a Prime administrator, it is also the reward for all the work you have done in the previous chapters.

As a learner-centric LMS, Captivate Prime pays particular attention to providing an exceptional learning experience to anyone taking courses on the platform. As a matter of fact, the learner experience is one of the landmark features of Adobe Captivate Prime. It is centered around a critical component known as the fluidic player. The fluidic player is the component that actually delivers the learning content to learners. It has been designed to support a wide range of content types, including video, PDF and Word documents, PowerPoint presentations, and, of course, interactive learning content in AICC, SCORM, or xAPI format.

Learners can also download the Adobe Captivate Prime mobile app for iOS and Android in order to take courses using their mobile device. One of the key features of the mobile app is the ability to download the course content and review it offline. The course tracking data is uploaded to the server the next time the mobile device connects to the internet.
