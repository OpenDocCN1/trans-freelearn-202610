The course effectiveness score can be useful for getting a rough indication of the effectiveness of a course, but as discussed at the end of the previous paragraph, it can be somewhat misleading in many situations.

That being said, it is possible to display the course effectiveness score to learners instead of displaying the star rating score using the following steps:

+   6. Close any **Course Effectiveness** dialog currently open.
+   7. From the administrator home page, click the **Settings** link located in the **Configure** section at the end of the left sidebar.
+   8. Next, click the **General** link located in the **Basics** section of the left sidebar.
+   9. At the bottom of the page, select the **Course Effectiveness** radio button.

With this configuration, the course effectiveness score replaces the star rating information in the learner experience. Note that this does not affect the other roles of Captivate Prime. For instance, administrators still see the course effectiveness score when browsing the course section of the administrator application.

You will now revert back to the default situation and enable star rating.

+   10. Switch the **Show Ratings** option back to **Star rating**.

In this section, you discussed the **Ratings** feature of Captivate Prime. Remember that it is enabled and set to the **Star rating** mode by default, which means that learners are able to rate courses and learning programs on a scale of 1 to 5 stars and that this rating is visible to learners browsing the course catalog in the learning experience.

Captivate Prime also calculates the course effectiveness score for each course based on the L1, L2, and L3 feedback received. This score is visible to all the other roles of Captivate Prime that have access to this information. The course effectiveness score can be useful to get a rough indication of the effectiveness of a course but be careful not to judge a course on this sole basis, as the course effectiveness score can be misleading in many situations. This is why you probably want to hide it from learners and display the star rating instead.
