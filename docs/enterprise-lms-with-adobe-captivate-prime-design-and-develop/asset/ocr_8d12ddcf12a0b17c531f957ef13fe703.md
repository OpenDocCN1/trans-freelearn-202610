Regardless of the area of application, game design elements are the basic building blocks of gamification. Some of the typical game design elements used include points, badges, leaderboards, scenario-based training, and avatars. Many of these game design elements are available in Captivate Prime and are used by organizations to increase learner engagement and the retention rate.

In this chapter, we will discuss **badges** and how they are used within Captivate Prime. You will then explore the **gamification** features that you can use to award points to your learners when they interact with learning content on your Prime account. You will start by exploring these from the administrator's perspective, before reviewing how they affect the learner experience.

This chapter contains the following main sections:

+   - Creating and using badges
+   - Enabling and configuring gamification
+   - Gamification from the learner's perspective

By the end of this chapter, you will have reviewed the features that will help you make learning more fun on your Prime account. By applying these techniques, you should see a significant increase in learner engagement and the number of learning activities carried out on your Prime account. This will further help your organization meet its training goals and promote a learning culture among employees.

# Technical requirements

To perform the exercises of this chapter, you need to meet the following technical requirements:

+   - You must have administrator and learner access to a working Captivate Prime account (it can be a trial account).
+   - You must have at least one skill, one course, one learning program, and one certification available on your Prime account. This can be custom training content that you have defined yourself or content that is available by default on your trial Prime account.
