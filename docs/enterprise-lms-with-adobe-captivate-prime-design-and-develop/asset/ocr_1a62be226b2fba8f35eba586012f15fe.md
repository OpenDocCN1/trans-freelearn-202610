# Technical requirements

To follow along with the exercises of this chapter, you need to meet the following requirements:

+   - You must have administrator access to a working Captivate Prime account (it can be a trial account).
+   - You must have a custom course with at least three learners enrolled. At least one of these learners should have completed the course.
+   - You must have manager access to your Prime account using the profile of the manager of the learners enrolled in the custom course mentioned in the previous item.

If you have completed the exercises of the previous chapters, you are almost ready to begin this chapter. Just make sure that one of your learners has completed the *Fundamentals of cybersecurity* course you created in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*. To do this, you may need to log in as an instructor to mark that learner as present in the synchronous (live) class, as well as accepting the learner's submissions using the tools and techniques covered in *Chapter 8, Exploring the Instructor Role*.
