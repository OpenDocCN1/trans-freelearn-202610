# Enrollment by Managers

The other role that can enroll learners in courses is the *manager* role. The only difference is that a manager can only enroll team members in courses, while administrators can enroll any learner registered in your Prime account.

When you created users back in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*, you defined *Manager 01* as the manager of both *Learner 01* and *Learner 02*. You will now log in to your Prime account as *Manager 01* and go through the process of enrolling *Learner 02* to the same course instance you used in the previous two sections when enrolling *Learner 01* and *Learner 03*. Perform the following steps to make this happen:

+   1. Open a new browser or another private browser window and use it to log in to your Prime account as *Manager 01*.
+   2. By default, *Manager 01* is logged in as a regular learner, so you must click the user icon in the top-right corner of the screen to switch to the **Manager** role.

This is your first visit to the manager home page, so take the time to inspect the various components of the page and the links available in the left column.

+   3. When you're ready, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
+   4. Locate the course you created in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, in the list of available courses.
+   5. Hover over the lower area of the course card and choose the **Enroll Learners** option. This opens the same **Enroll Learners** dialog as the one for an administrator.
+   6. Use the **Select Instance** dropdown to select the same course instance as in the previous two sections.
