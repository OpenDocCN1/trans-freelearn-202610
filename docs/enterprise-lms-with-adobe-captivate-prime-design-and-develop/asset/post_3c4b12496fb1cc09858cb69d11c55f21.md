## Modifying the Sender's Name and Address

Until now, the sender of the emails received by the users of your Prime account has been admin@captivateprime.com. This is the default sender email address for Captivate Prime. That said, many organizations prefer to use an email address from their email domain (that is, an email address that looks like <name>@company.com>) as the sender address.

This is perfectly possible but cannot be done within the administrator application of Captivate Prime. To configure a custom sender address, it is necessary to contact Captivate Prime support at CaptivatePrimeSupport@adobe.com.

Changing the sender's name and address is an important step that helps learners trust messages sent by Captivate Prime, especially if they are very wary of phishing emails they may receive.

But this is just the first step in customizing the email messages sent by your Prime account. In the next section, you will learn how to customize the email banner and the email signature of every message that's sent to your learners.

## Disabling Optional Manager Emails

By default, Captivate Prime sends a fairly large number of messages to your learners, but managers receive even more emails than others.

First, managers are also learners so they receive all the emails about their activities on the Prime account as any other learner. But managers also receive additional emails about the actions that have been performed by their team members.

In fact, most of the events happening on the Prime account trigger two emails to be sent: one email to the relevant learner and a second email to the learner's manager.

Hopefully, you can turn off these **Optional Manager Emails** using the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are on the **Settings** tab of the **Email Templates** section of the administrator.
- 2. Deselect the **Optional Manager Emails** checkbox.

Note that, by default, this checkbox is selected, and managers receive lots of additional emails.

In this section, you've helped busy managers by keeping Captivate Prime from sending too many emails to their already overloaded inboxes. They'll certainly thank you for that, but on the other hand, don't forget that managers play a critical role in your Captivate Prime account. For example, they must review the enrollment requests of their team members in a timely fashion, they must submit their L3 feedback after their team members have completed training, and more. Now that they're getting fewer reminder emails, make sure you have other strategies in place to remind them of their duties. Targeted announcements and mastheads can help you with this prospect. (L3 feedback was discussed in Chapter 9, Configuring and Using Feedback.)

In addition to mid-level managers and senior managers, other top executives of the organization also need to be protected from the high number of emails sent by Captivate Prime. We'll look at this issue in the next section.

## Configuring the Do Not Disturb feature

For most learners, the high number of messages sent by Captivate Prime is not an issue as these emails are directly related to the actions they take themselves on the platform. However, senior managers and other top executives who are already overwhelmed with tons of emails may not be happy with the additional messages that are automatically sent by the corporate LMS.

This is where the Do Not Disturb feature of Captivate Prime comes into play. You can use it to limit the number of messages that are sent to selected individuals, typically members of the senior management team and other top executives in the organization.

Follow these steps to configure the Do Not Disturb list of your Prime account:

- 1. Make sure you are on the Settings tab of the Email Templates section of the administrator.
- 2. Click the Edit List link associated with the Do Not Disturb option.
- 3. Type the name of a user in the Search users to add or update field.
- 4. Use the Search user to add or update field to select the users that must be on the Do Not Disturb list.
- 5. Open the Direct Emails drop-down menu associated with the users you just added.

Note that you can only enter users, not user groups. In other words, you can only select one user at a time, which emphasizes the fact that this feature is designed to affect only a small number of select individuals.

In this exercise, add any available user to the Do Not Disturb list.
