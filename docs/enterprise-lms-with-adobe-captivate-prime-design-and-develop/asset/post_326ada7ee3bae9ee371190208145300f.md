## Sharing content on Discussion Boards

Now that you have a discussion board available, learners will want to use it to share different types of content with their peers. In the previous section, you added some simple text messages to the discussion board, you saw how learners can respond to these messages, and also how they can upvote (like) or downvote (dislike) them.

In this section, you will post more content of different types on the newly created discussion board. This includes sharing web pages, documents, images, videos, and more. Let's start by creating polls and questions.

## Creating Polls and Questions

In addition to the simple text-based posts discussed in the previous section, learners can also generate simple polls and questions. These polls and questions are a great way to get learners to engage with the social learning feature, especially if they have never participated in these types of discussions before. It is also a great way for instructors to extend their class discussions or prepare their audience for the next class.

In this section, *Learner 02* will start a poll in the newly created **Cybersecurity in the organization** board. Perform the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are using the browser window where you are logged in as *Learner 02*, and that you are on the default **Social Learning** page.
- 2. Click the **New Post** button located at the top of the page.
- 3. Type **Which security threat are you most afraid of?** in the **Write or paste something here...** field.
- 4. Click the **Add Poll** button located below the text field.
- 5. Enter the following four options for the poll responses: **Phishing**, **Viruses**, **Ransomware**, and **Other**.
- 6. Type the first few letters of **Cybersecurity** in the **Type here to search a board** field and select the **Cybersecurity in the organization** board.
- 7. When the form looks as follows, click the Post button:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_456_0.png)

Figure 12.16 – Creating a poll in a discussion board

- 8. Finally, provide your response to the poll by selecting one of the four available answers. Don't forget to click the Submit Choice link to validate your choice.
- 9. Return to the browser window where you are logged in as Learner 01 and browse to (or refresh) the Social Learning page.
- 10. Confirm that the poll created by Learner 02 is visible on the Social Learning page of Learner 01.
- 11. Answer the poll as Learner 01.

You can use the same technique to create questions. In such a case, you would use the Mark Question button instead of the Add Poll button. This allows you to mark one of the comments that's been submitted by other learners as the right answer to the question.

Creating polls is a simple way to engage learners in an ongoing conversation, while creating questions and marking the correct answer help learners quickly find relevant information in the discussion thread.

## Sharing Web pages

While browsing the internet, Learner 01 comes across the Wikipedia page on computer security. He/she finds it so insightful that he/she wants to share that page with the other learners participating in the Cybersecurity in the organization board.

This is such a common situation that Captivate Prime provides a unique mechanism that allows users to quickly post any URL they find interesting in any available discussion board.

In this section, you will put this mechanism in place and use it to post a web page on the **Cybersecurity in the organization** board. Perform the following steps to do so:

- 1. Make sure you are still on the default **Social Learning** page of *Learner 01*.
- 2. Also, make sure that the bookmark bar of your browser is currently visible.
- 3. On the right-hand side of the **Social Learning** page, locate the **Share to Social** widget at the top of the right column.
- 4. Click and drag the **CP** icon to the bookmark bar of your browser. After this process, you will have one additional bookmark available in your bookmark bar, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_458_0.png)

Figure 12.18 – The Captivate Prime bookmark is available in the bookmark bar of your browser

- 5. Now that the URL sharing mechanism is in place, browse the Wikipedia page on computer security at `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security`.
- 6. Click the new **Captivate Prime** bookmark to share the page you are currently viewing in one of the available discussion boards.

If necessary, follow the on-screen instructions to log in as *Learner 01* on your Prime account.
- 7. Fill in the form that appears in the right pane and click the **Post** button. This action is shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_459_0.png)

Figure 12.19 – A Captivate Prime pane appears on the right-hand side of the browser window

Sharing web pages this way awards *Learner 01* five additional points!

- 8. Click the **Go to post** button to return to the **Social Learning** page of *Learner 01* on Captivate Prime and see the URL that has just been posted.

The widget you place in the bookmark bar of your browser is called a **bookmarklet**. This special bookmarklet makes it very easy to share any web page to any discussion boards available in Captivate Prime. Ultimately, this bookmarklet makes the process of sharing web pages so simple that learners have no excuse not to start sharing!

> **Tip**
>
> This bookmarklet requires that you allow pop-ups in your browser. Also, be aware that some browsers (such as Firefox and Brave) block lots of JavaScript trackers by default. You may have to adjust the security settings of your browser to allow the bookmarklet to work. More information can be found in the official Captivate Prime user guide at https://helpx.adobe.com/in/captivate-prime/learners/feature-summary/share-to-social.html.

Sharing web pages on discussion boards is a very common use case. Sharing files is another very common situation and is what you will examine in the next section.
