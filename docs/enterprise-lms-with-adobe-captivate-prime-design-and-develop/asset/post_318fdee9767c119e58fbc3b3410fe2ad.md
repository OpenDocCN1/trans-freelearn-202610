
## Using industry-aligned skills

The current learner experience you reviewed in the previous section works very well when you have a somewhat limited course catalog containing mostly courses defined internally by in-house authors or contractors. However, most large organizations also include third-party off-the-shelf content in the course catalog of their Prime accounts.

There are a lot of learning content providers in the marketplace. Many larger organizations subscribe to third-party learning content libraries in order to provide their workforce access to a wealth of learning resources. Some of the most famous providers include LinkedIn Learning (previously known as Lynda.com), Udemy, Open Classrooms, and Pluralsight.

> **Note**
> The August 2021 update of Captivate Prime introduced the Content Marketplace, which is Adobe's own offering of off-the-shelf content. This feature will be discussed in Chapter 14, Working with Catalogs and Peer Accounts.

Captivate Prime is able to integrate this external content into its own course catalogs either through dedicated integrations (we will discuss that in Chapter 16, Exploring the Integration Admin Role) or through some kind of custom development.

Using this capability, you greatly expand the list of skills covered by the courses available on your Prime account. This is great news for your learners and the organization as a whole, but it makes administering and tracking these skills a lot more complicated!

- How do you maintain the list of skills available on your Prime account when most courses come from these external libraries? Not to mention that these libraries are constantly being updated by their respective owners.
- How do you map the skills covered by these massive course catalogs to the skills your company and employees need the most?
- How do you mine these huge course catalogs to make relevant course recommendations in an ever-changing world where industries and regulations are in constant motion?

These are some of the questions you need to address to ensure that both the learners and the organization get the most out of this massive course offering.

Hopefully, Captivate Prime has you covered! The built-in AI does the heavy lifting for you based on a few simple options that are available to the administrator. Let's review these options, hands-on, by following these steps:

- 1. Use the browser window in which you are logged in as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Branding** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Scroll down the **Branding** page until you can see the **Homepage Experience** section.
- 4. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Homepage Experience** option.
- 5. Switch the **Training Type** option to **Industry Aligned**, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_500_0.png)

Figure 13.3 – Switching Training Type to Industry Aligned

This is how you tell Captivate Prime that your course catalog includes off-the-shelf content provided by third-party providers such as LinkedIn Learning, Get Abstract, and Pluralsight, or that you have purchased such content directly from Adobe using Captivate Prime's Content Marketplace. As a result, when generating individual recommendations for learners, the AI must take into account thousands of industry-aligned skills, in addition to the custom skills you defined in your Prime account.

These industry-aligned skills are curated by Adobe by constantly monitoring hundreds of industries, thousands of courses, millions of resumes and job descriptions, and more. This data is used by the Captivate Prime engineering team to train Captivate Prime's built-in AI algorithm.

Before saving the changes and examining how this affects the learner experience, there is one more important step to perform in the administrator. This step must be carried out now in order for Captivate Prime to allow you to save the changes. However, it will be discussed further in the next section:

- 6. Open the **Internal** drop-down menu associated with the **Identify Learners as Peers based on these fields** option and select the **profile** checkbox.
- 7. Now, open the **External** drop-down menu associated with the **Identify Learners as Peers based on these fields** option and also select the **profile** checkbox.
- 8. Finally, click the **Save** button located in the lower-right corner of the page.

These simple steps are all it takes for the administrator to enable the use of industry-aligned skills. From now on, over 50,000 new skills are available in your Prime account! Best of all, the AI will automatically map these skills to the thousands of courses available in your course catalog, thus helping your learners browse the catalog and enroll in the courses that are most relevant to them.

Speaking of learners, let’s return to the learner experience and explore how it is affected by the change you just made.

## Industry-aligned skills in the learner experience

In this section, you will return to the browser window in which you are logged in as *Learner 01* and examine how activating **Industry Aligned** skills affects the learner experience.

> **Note**
> 
> As you read through this section, bear in mind that industry-aligned skills are typically used with larger course catalogs, which are primarily comprised of ready-to-use third-party training content. This means that you will not see the immediate benefits of this feature with the very small course catalog currently available on your Prime trial account.

Use the following steps to browse industry-aligned skills as a learner:

- 1. Use the browser window in which you are logged in as Learner 01.
- 2. From the learner home page, click the Skills icon of the left sidebar to open that My Areas of Interest page.

If you are already on that page, make sure you refresh the browser window so that the changes made in the previous section are applied.

Now that you have activated the industry-aligned skills in the administrator, notice that a new Industry Aligned Skills tab is available on the My Areas of Interest page:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_502_0.png)

Figure 13.4 – The Industry Aligned Skills tab is available from the My Areas of Interest page

On this page, the Admin Defined Skills tab includes the custom skills that you define using the Skills page of the administrator application. These skills are specific to your organization, and you must link them manually to your custom courses. The Industry Aligned Skills are provided by Captivate Prime’s AI. They are automatically mapped to any third-party content available in your course catalog:

- 3. Switch to the Industry Aligned Skills tab of the My Areas of Interest page.

A message should tell you that You have not expressed interest in any Skill yet.

- 4. Click the Add Interest link located at the bottom of the page. This opens the Add to my Areas of Interest page.

- 5. Switch to the Industry Aligned Skills tab of the Add to my Areas of Interest page.

And that's where the magic of AI comes in! As shown in the following screenshot, you are presented with a giant **Skill Map** containing thousands of skills that are known to the AI:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_503_0.png)

Figure 13.5 – You are presented with a giant Skill Map

Each dot on the picture represents a skill. The lines are used to connect related skills together. You can explore the skill map using your mouse cursor. Additionally, you can use the **Search Skills** field atop the **Skill Map** as well as the **Show Skills for which trainings are present in my account** checkbox located below the **Skill Map**:

- 6. Use your mouse to move the **Skill Map** around. Additionally, you can use the mouse wheel to zoom in and out of the **Skill Map**.
- 7. When ready, use the **Search Skill** field in the upper-left corner of the **Skill Map** to search for a skill of your choosing (with more than 50,000 skills available on the map, you can, quite literally, search for *any* skill you want).
- 8. Upon choosing a skill in the **Search Skill** field, the **Skill Map** zooms into the selected skill and the skills it is connected to.
