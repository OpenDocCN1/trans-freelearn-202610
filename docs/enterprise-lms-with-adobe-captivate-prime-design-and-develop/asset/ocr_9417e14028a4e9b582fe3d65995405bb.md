+   9. Then, click the **Back** link at the top of the left sidebar to return to the main **Catalogs** page of the administrator.
+   10. Repeat the preceding operations to add some training content to the **External Catalog**. Remember that the same content can be included in multiple catalogs.

To test all possible situations, make sure that you add courses to both catalogs, that other courses are only available in one of the two catalogs, and, finally, that some courses are not added to either catalog.

> **Note**
> When we say *courses*, we are referring to any type of learning content that can be added to a catalog: courses, learning programs, certifications, and job aids.

In this section, you added content to the two custom catalogs that you created in the *Creating custom catalogs* section earlier in this chapter. The main takeaways of this section are as follows:

+   - A catalog is a collection of learning items that can contain courses, learning programs, certifications, and job aids.
+   - The same learning object can be part of multiple catalogs.

# Sharing Catalogs

In this section, you will decide which learners will have access to which catalog. Within the Captivate Prime administrator, this is called *Sharing* catalogs. Captivate Prime allows you to share catalogs either internally or externally:

+   - Internal sharing means that you give access to your catalog to learners defined within your Prime account. These can be *both internal and external learners*. Remember that both of these types of learners are indeed defined within your Prime account, as discussed in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*.
+   - External sharing means that your catalog is accessible to learners defined *in other Captivate Prime accounts*. This means that the learners (internal or external) defined in that external account (called the receiving account) are those who will ultimately be able to access your content.
