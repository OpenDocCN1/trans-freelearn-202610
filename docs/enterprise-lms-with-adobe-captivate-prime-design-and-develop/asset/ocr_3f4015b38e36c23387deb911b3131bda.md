In this section, you will explore the **Learning Summary** dashboard, which is the third default dashboard available in Captivate Prime. This third dashboard focuses on the interactions between learners and the learning content. From that perspective, this dashboard contains reports that measure the *liveliness* of your Prime account.

The reports contained on this dashboard allow you to answer questions such as:

+   - How many enrollments, views, and course completions have taken place on the Prime account? How many learners are actually involved in these statistics?
+   - What are the skills acquired by the learners in a given period of time? This helps you figure out whether the skills available in the workforce match the needs and targets of the organization.
+   - How are learners doing with the deadlines? Are they taking their assigned courses on time, so they remain compliant? This is especially useful in organizations using Captivate Prime for compliance training.

Let's now take a look at the **Learning Summary** dashboard and at the reports it contains using the following steps:

+   1. Log into your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Reports** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. Next, click **Learning Summary** located in the left sidebar.

This last action takes you to the **Learning Summary** dashboard. From top to bottom, this dashboard contains three main reports: the **Summary** report, the **Skills Status** report, and the **Compliance Dashboard**.

This dashboard is a little different than those discussed in the previous two sections. First, notice that there is no **Export** button visible on this dashboard.
