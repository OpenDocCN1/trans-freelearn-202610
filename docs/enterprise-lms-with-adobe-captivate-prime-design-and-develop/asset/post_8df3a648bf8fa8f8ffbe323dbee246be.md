## Modifying the Board Creation Permission

By default, any learner can create any number of discussion boards on your Prime account. This default is in line with the information sharing and informal discussion behavior that social learning aims to promote. But it also causes problems, especially on larger accounts with several thousands of learners or if hot topics (for example, those related to large training initiatives) arise within the organization. In such circumstances, you may end up with so many discussion boards that social learning quickly becomes a nightmare to administer. In addition, relevant discussions are distilled to so many boards that it becomes difficult for learners to access this information, which defeats the very purpose of social learning.

These are the main reasons why you want to restrict the number of people who are allowed to create new discussion boards. You can do so by performing the following steps:

1. Make sure you are on the **Settings** tab of the **Social Learning** page of the administrator application.
2. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Board Creation Permissions** option.
3. Select the **A group of learners** radio button.
4. Finally, use the search field to search for the group(s) of users that are allowed to create new discussion boards.

You can choose any built-in or custom group. Users who do not belong to one of the selected groups will not be able to create new discussion boards. That said, they retain all their abilities to participate in discussions taking place on any available boards.

> **Note**
> Built-in and custom groups were discussed in *Chapter 5, Managing Users.*

In this example, you will stick to the default and allow **All Learners** to create new discussion boards.

5. Switch back to the **All Learners** radio button and click the **Save** button.

## Defining Special Social Learning Users

The next available option is the **Special Users** option. This one is very useful when you're using scopes, as discussed in the *Using Social Learning Scopes* section, earlier in this chapter.

As a reminder, social learning scopes allow you to segment your learners by a chosen user characteristic so that they can only interact with the other learners in the same scope.

This means that nobody in the organization has a comprehensive overview of all the social learning activities taking place on your Prime account across the different scopes. Hopefully, administrators can grant the *Special User* privilege to a user group. Captivate Prime then bypasses all scope restrictions and grants these users access to all the available discussion boards.

Let's take a look at this feature by performing the following steps:

1. Make sure you are on the **Settings** tab of the **Social Learning** page of the administrator application.
2. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Special Users** option.
3. Use the search field to search for the appropriate group of users.
   Note that you can only select a single user group. Hopefully, this can be either a built-in or a custom group.
4. In this example, though, since you have not enabled any social learning scope, the **Special Users** option is not relevant.
   Click the **Cancel** button to close the **Special Users** option without saving any changes.

## Summary

Already in ancient times, Aristotle said that,

> *Man is, by nature, a social animal.*

This is why, despite all the technological tools we have to communicate remotely, we still like to travel, attend conferences, and take live face-to-face classes conducted by instructors made of flesh and blood. The added value of these live events is the countless informal conversations we have with our classmates and instructors, or with other conference attendees. In fact, a lot happens during these informal conversations: people get to know each other, business cards are exchanged, use cases are discussed, and more. Profitable business partnerships and even true friendships are often born out of these conversations. Believe me, this is a true story! The very book you are reading is a perfect illustration of this phenomenon (Hi, Sean!).

Some say that distance learning complicates or even prevents these social interactions. While it is true that LMSes and distance learning will never completely replace our dire need for social contact, learning platforms have developed a wide variety of tools to facilitate discussion, information sharing, positive competition, collaboration, engagement, and more. These tools not only complement formal (live or online) learning. Used properly, they can even become an integral part of an organization's learning and development strategy by fostering a true culture of learning, sharing, and collaboration to everyone's profit.

Captivate Prime is no exception! First, it allows you to enable an informal discussion board in any of the courses available in the LMS. This allows any learner enrolled in that course to participate in a discussion with other learners. This feature is disabled by default, but you can easily enable it either separately for each course or at the account level.

Captivate Prime also incorporates a full-fledged social learning platform. It is based on discussion boards, where learners can post a wide variety of content, ranging from simple text-based messages to sophisticated videos that include both a screen recording and a webcam feed. The URL sharing bookmarklet and Adobe Captivate Prime Desktop application make it so easy to create and share content that learners have no excuse not to participate in these discussion boards. In addition, advanced content moderation and curation capabilities, including an integrated AI-powered curation engine, makes the day-to-day administration of these discussion boards a breeze!

But the AI-powered curation engine is not the only feature of Captivate Prime that relies on artificial intelligence. Remember that Captivate Prime is a learner-centric LMS where anyone can self-register for courses made available in the course catalog. Finding your way through a huge catalog of courses is not always easy. Fortunately, Captivate Prime's artificial intelligence can be used to provide personalized recommendations to each learner. That's what you will explore in the next chapter.
