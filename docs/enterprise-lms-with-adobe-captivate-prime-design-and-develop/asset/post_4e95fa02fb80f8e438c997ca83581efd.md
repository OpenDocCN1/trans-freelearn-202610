## Understanding the Course Effectiveness Score

In addition to the star rating discussed in the previous section, Captivate Prime also calculates a course effectiveness score for each course.

The course effectiveness score is automatically calculated by Captivate Prime and is obtained by combining the results from the L1, L2, and L3 feedback. Captivate Prime calculates the effectiveness as a score out of 100 points for each course and learning program available on your Prime account. Let’s take a look at it hands-on using the following steps:

-   1. Log in to your Captivate Prime account as an administrator.
-   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar to access the list of courses currently available on your Prime account.

-   3. Open the Sort By drop-down menu and choose to sort the list by Course Effectiveness (see the circled item in the following screenshot).

This last action is not mandatory, but it makes it easier to review the course effectiveness scores. As shown in the following screenshot, the course effectiveness score is displayed near the upper-right corner of each course card:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_357_0.png)

Figure 9.19 – The course effectiveness score as seen by an administrator

Notice that administrators do not see the star rating submitted by learners. Actually, the star rating, if enabled, is only visible to learners in the learner experience.

If Captivate Prime does not have enough data to calculate the course effectiveness, the corresponding course is not rated, as indicated by the NR label (see the third arrow in the preceding screenshot).

It is possible to view the breakdown of each course effectiveness score by clicking the score on the course card. This gives you a better understanding of how Captivate Prime calculates the score for each course:

-   4. Click the score of the first course displayed in the list to open the Course Effectiveness box of this course.
-   5. Also, click the How Course Effectiveness is calculated link to get additional insight into how Captivate Prime calculates the course effectiveness score.

As shown in the following screenshot, Captivate Prime takes the L1, L2, and L3 feedback data into account when calculating the course effectiveness score:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_358_0.png)

Figure 9.20 – The Course Effectiveness dialog box

Note that the preceding screenshot has been taken on an older Captivate Prime account with more courses and more learners, so more data has been generated for Captivate Prime to calculate the course effectiveness score more accurately.

Take some time to read the lower portion of the Course Effectiveness dialog box. Note that 2/3 of the score is based on L3 feedback from managers. Keep in mind that managers typically enter L3 feedback at least 45 days after a learner completes the course. During that time frame, Captivate Prime cannot take L3 feedback into account when calculating the course effectiveness score, which explains the somewhat low scores for courses currently hosted on your Prime trial account.

The course effectiveness score can be useful for getting a rough indication of the effectiveness of a course, but as discussed at the end of the previous paragraph, it can be somewhat misleading in many situations.

That being said, it is possible to display the course effectiveness score to learners instead of displaying the star rating score using the following steps:

-   6. Close any **Course Effectiveness** dialog currently open.
-   7. From the administrator home page, click the **Settings** link located in the **Configure** section at the end of the left sidebar.
-   8. Next, click the **General** link located in the **Basics** section of the left sidebar.
-   9. At the bottom of the page, select the **Course Effectiveness** radio button.

With this configuration, the course effectiveness score replaces the star rating information in the learner experience. Note that this does not affect the other roles of Captivate Prime. For instance, administrators still see the course effectiveness score when browsing the course section of the administrator application.

You will now revert back to the default situation and enable star rating.

-   10. Switch the **Show Ratings** option back to **Star rating**.

In this section, you discussed the **Ratings** feature of Captivate Prime. Remember that it is enabled and set to the **Star rating** mode by default, which means that learners are able to rate courses and learning programs on a scale of 1 to 5 stars and that this rating is visible to learners browsing the course catalog in the learning experience.

Captivate Prime also calculates the course effectiveness score for each course based on the L1, L2, and L3 feedback received. This score is visible to all the other roles of Captivate Prime that have access to this information. The course effectiveness score can be useful to get a rough indication of the effectiveness of a course but be careful not to judge a course on this sole basis, as the course effectiveness score can be misleading in many situations. This is why you probably want to hide it from learners and display the star rating instead.
