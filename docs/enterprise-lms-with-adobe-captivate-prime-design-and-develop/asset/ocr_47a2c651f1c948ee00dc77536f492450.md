Besides allowing you to define who can access which courses, creating catalogs also allows you to share your courses with other Prime accounts, which is very useful in many situations, such as when you sell courses to other organizations or when the headquarters of a multinational corporation wants to share courses with local offices from around the world.

In addition to sharing course content, Captivate Prime also allows you to share user seats with other accounts using the Peer Accounts feature. As discussed in the *Getting Captivate Prime* section of *Chapter 1, Introduction to Adobe Captivate Prime*, Captivate Prime is billed on a per-seat basis. With peer accounts, an organization can purchase a large number of seats and distribute them among the Prime accounts of its regional subsidiaries. Additionally, organizations can use peer accounts to fund training for their partners or franchisees. In this case, Prime is purchased by the organization and the user seats are distributed among the peer accounts used by these partners and franchisees. This allows large organizations to centralize the cost of using Captivate Prime (not to mention that purchasing a large number of seats usually results in better rates) while keeping an eye on the overall training and development strategy of the entire organization.

These are the features you will explore in this chapter, which includes the following main sections:

+   - Working with multiple catalogs
+   - Sharing catalogs
+   - Enabling and disabling catalogs
+   - Catalogs in the learner experience
+   - Exploring the Content Marketplace
+   - Working with peer accounts

With these features, you will be able to tailor your Captivate Prime deployment to exactly match the needs of your organization, regardless of its size, industry, strategy, or internal operation.
