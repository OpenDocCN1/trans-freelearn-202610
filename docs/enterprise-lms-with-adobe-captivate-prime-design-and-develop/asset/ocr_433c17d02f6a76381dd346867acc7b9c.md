# Enabling and Disabling Messages

There are lots of events in Adobe Captivate Prime that trigger an email being sent. These messages can be customized in various sections of the Captivate Prime administrator:

+   - The **Email Templates** section of the Captivate Prime administrator is the primary place to customize all email messages sent by Captivate Prime. Any customization that's made in this section of the administrator affects your entire Prime account, regardless of the course, learning program, or certification that the message was sent from.
+   - You can override some of the messages defined in the **Email Templates** section of the Captivate Prime administrator at the individual course, learning program, and certification levels. This allows you to customize the content of each message based on the learning activity that the email originated from.

Let's start by reviewing the list of email messages available in the main **Email Templates** section of the Captivate Prime administrator.
