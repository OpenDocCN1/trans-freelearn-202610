
## Exporting the Notification Announcement Report

You now have several announcements of different types in your Prime account. They have all been published and are currently being broadcast, so they are all visible in the learner experience. But does this mean that learners will actually view them? What about learners who do not log in to their Prime account often? Will they see important announcements in time? These are some of the reasons why, as an administrator, it can be important to have an overview of who *actually* viewed which announcement.

This is the very purpose of the *Notification Announcement Report*, which can be exported from the **Announcement** page of the administrator. Let's take a look at it by performing the following steps:

-   1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
-   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Announcements** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
-   3. In the top-right corner of the **Announcement** page, click the **Actions** button, and then click **Export Notification Announcements Report**.
-   4. Take some time to read the **Generating Report Request** message. When done, click the **OK** button to dismiss it.

Depending on the size of your account and on the number of announcements currently being broadcast, it may take a little bit of time for Captivate Prime to generate your report. When the report is ready, a notification will appear in the notification area of the administrator, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_559_0.png)

Figure 15.8 – A notification appears when the report is ready

Just click the link available in the notification to download **Notification Announcements Report** in CSV format.

> **The CSV file format**
> CSV stands for *comma-separated value*. Remember that such a file can be opened in virtually any spreadsheet application, including Microsoft Excel, Apple Numbers, and Open Office.

Take the necessary time to open the report in the spreadsheet application available on your system and inspect its content. For your convenience, a sample Notification Announcements Report in `.csv` format is available in the `announcements` folder of the download associated with this book.

## Viewing, Modifying, and Deleting Announcements

One thing you didn't do when creating the masthead announcement earlier in this section was provide a start and end date for your announcement. As a result, the masthead is broadcast immediately and remains visible until you manually withdraw it. Now, let's take a closer look at this process using the following steps:

-   1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator.
-   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Announcements** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.

By default, the **Announcements** page of the administrator opens on the **Draft** tab, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_560_0.png)

Figure 15.9 – The Announcements page opens on the Draft tab

This is where the announcements that are still in a draft state are visible.
An announcement is in a draft state when it has been saved but not published yet.

Notice that there are two additional tabs available at the top of the **Announcement** page:

-   - The **Scheduled** tab displays a list of published announcements with a start date in the future. Captivate Prime automatically broadcasts these announcements and moves them to the **Published** tab when their respective start dates have been reached.
-   - The **Published** tab lists all the announcements that are currently visible to learners. If you have completed all the exercises in this book, then this is where the various announcements you have created should be displayed.

Now, go to the **Published** tab and manually withdraw the masthead announcement you created in the previous section.

-   3. Switch to the **Published** tab of the **Announcements** page.
-   4. Click the *gear* icon associated with your masthead announcement.
-   5. Click the **Edit** option in the menu that opens to open the **Edit Announcement** dialog.
-   6. Click the **Advanced Settings** link located in the lower-left corner of the **Edit Announcement** dialog.
-   7. Try to modify the **Start Date** and/or the **End Date** property of your masthead announcement.

Note that Captivate Prime does not let you change this information. You can't change either the start date nor the end date of a published announcement. In other words, the only thing you can do to stop broadcasting the masthead you created earlier in this section is to completely withdraw that announcement.

-   8. Click the **Cancel** button located in the lower-right corner of the **Edit Announcement** dialog to return to the **Published** tab of the **Announcement** page.
-   9. Click the *gear* icon associated with your masthead announcement one more time.
-   10. This time, click the **Withdraw** option in the menu that opens.

From now on, this masthead is no longer displayed to any learners. Note that this does not remove the announcement from the list of published announcements. You can always modify the announcement and/or broadcast it again later. You can also decide to completely delete the announcement, which is what you will do next.

-   11. Click the *gear* icon associated with your masthead announcement one last time.
-   12. Click the **Delete** link available in the menu that opens. Note that the **Delete** option is only available for announcements that have been previously withdrawn.

This concludes your overview of the announcements of Adobe Captivate Prime.
Remember that Captivate Prime provides three types of announcements:

-   - **Recommendations** were discussed in *Chapter 13, AI-Powered Recommendations for Learners*. You can use them to manually recommend courses, learning programs, certifications, and job aids to targeted user groups. These manual recommendations are displayed on the learner home page, next to the recommendations that have been generated by the built-in artificial intelligence of Captivate Prime.
-   - **Notifications** are displayed as dialog boxes in the learner experience whenever a learner logs in. They are also available in the notification icon (the bell icon), located in the upper-right corner of the learner experience. In addition to that, Captivate Prime also sends an email message to all the users who have been targeted by the notification when the notification gets published.
-   - **Mastheads** are displayed at the top of the learner experience. What makes them special is the fact that they can be used as call to actions to redirect the learners to another web page.

## Configuring the Messaging System

If you have completed the various exercises that have been provided so far in this book, then the users you have defined in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*, should have received a significant number of email messages from Captivate Prime. In fact, almost every event that takes place in Captivate Prime triggers an email. Managers have received even more messages because in addition to messages about their activities as learners (remember that managers are, first and foremost, learners), they also receive messages about the activities of their team members.

This is an intentional approach to get learners to come back to Captivate Prime regularly, despite the large number of online platforms we all use every day. As such, these emails extend the reach of the Captivate Prime experience and can be considered the beginning of many Prime workflows.

This is the reason why many organizations pay particular attention to both the visual design and the content of these email communications.

In this section, you will review the main options that allow the administrator to configure the Prime account email system. Then, in the next section, you will focus on customizing the content of each email message.
