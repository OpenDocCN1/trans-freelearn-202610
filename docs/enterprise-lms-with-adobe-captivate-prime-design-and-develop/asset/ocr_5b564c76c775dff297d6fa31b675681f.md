# Assigning badges to certifications

As discussed in the *Creating certifications* section of *Chapter 6, Enrolling Users to Courses*, certifications are similar to learning programs in that they also allow you to group related courses together. What makes them different from learning programs is the fact that they have a time limit to complete and that they can expire after a certain amount of time.

When it comes to badges, Captivate Prime allows you to assign a badge to a certification so that every learner that completes the certification on time is awarded the corresponding badge. Let's review this feature using the following steps:

+   1. From the administrator home page, click the **Certifications** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
+   2. Hover over the **Certified Prime Learner** certification you created back in *Chapter 6, Enrolling Users to Courses*.
+      (If the **Certified Prime Learner** certification does not exist in your Prime account, feel free to use any certification available to you.)
+   3. Click the **View Certification** link to open the **Overview** page of the chosen certification.
+   4. Click the **Edit** button located in the upper-right corner of the screen.
