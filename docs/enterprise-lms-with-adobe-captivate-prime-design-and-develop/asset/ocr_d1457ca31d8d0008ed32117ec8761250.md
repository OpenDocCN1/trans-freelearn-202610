3. Click the **Create Catalog** link or the **Create** button located in the upper-right corner of the page to open the **Create Catalog** page.

Note that the left column of the **Create Catalog** page only contains two links: the **Back** link and the **Overview** link.

4. Type `Internal Catalog` into the **Catalog Name** field. Note that this is the only information that is required when creating a new catalog.

> **Tip**
> It's a good idea to establish clear naming conventions in the early stages of your Prime deployment. This is true not only for catalogs but also for any other elements of Captivate Prime that you can name. This will ensure consistency and, therefore, ease of use for learners and administrators alike, right from day one.

5. As a best practice, take some time to provide a **Cover Image** and type a small description into the **Description** field.
