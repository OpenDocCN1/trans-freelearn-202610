# Summary

Now that you've completed this chapter, learners can finally enroll in courses and your Prime account has started to come alive.

In this chapter, you have given the coup de grace to the popular belief that learners enroll in courses, which is never the case in Captivate Prime. Learners enroll in *course instances*, *learning programs*, and *certifications*, never in courses, per se.

You have created course instances to address the time-bound nature of the synchronous activities included in many courses, grouped courses into learning programs, and created an internal certification that learners must obtain within 30 days of enrollment.

With these learning objects available in the course catalog, you have reviewed many workflows that allow users to enroll themselves. In Captivate Prime, learner self-enrollment is the preferred way of ending up taking a course, but administrators and managers can also enroll learners in courses without them asking. The platform itself can also automatically enroll learners when a trigger event occurs, as defined in a learning plan.
