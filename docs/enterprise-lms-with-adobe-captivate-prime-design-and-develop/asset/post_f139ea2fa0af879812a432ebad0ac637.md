
## Email templates for courses, learning programs, and certifications

Each course, learning program, and certification also has its own list of **Email Templates**. By default, these learning objects inherit from the email templates defined at the account level, but you can also decide to customize the email templates of each individual course, learning program, or certification. In such a case, the edits that are made at the learning object level take precedence over the options defined at the account level. Perform the following steps to modify the email templates of a course:

- 1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. Click the **Courses** link located in the learning section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Hover your mouse over the course card that you want to change the email templates of and click the **View Course** link.
- 4. Finally, click the **Email Templates** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar.

This opens the list of email templates for that specific course. By default, **Email Templates** are inherited from the Prime account. Notice the switch in the last column of the table, which allows you to enable/disable each message. Keep in mind that the changes you make here take precedence over changes made at the account level. In other words, you can disable a message in the main **Email Templates** section of the Prime account and reenable that same message for that course only (and vice versa).

- 5. Take some time to review the list of available messages.
- 6. When done, click the logo image located in the upper-left corner of the screen to return to the administrator home page.
- 7. You can use the same process to review the email templates of the learning programs and certifications that are available on your Prime account.

By the end of this section, you should have a much better idea of the number of email messages sent by Captivate Prime. It is important to remember that you cannot add your own messages to this list. However, you can modify the content of each of these messages, which is what will be discussed in the next section.

## Modifying the Content of Email Messages

In the *Configuring the Messaging System* section, earlier in this chapter, you modified **Email Banner** and **Email Signature** of all the messages sent by Captivate Prime. It is important to remember that these modifications affect all the messages in the same way.

In this section, you will learn how to modify the content of each email message. You will review this process using one of the messages available in the main **Email Templates** section of the Captivate Prime administrator, but the process is the same for every other email template available in the various sections of the **Captivate Prime** administrator.

In this example, you will customize the **Course – Enrolled by Admin/Manager** message at the platform level, so this change will affect all the courses that are available on the platform by default (unless you also modify the same message at the course level, in which case the course-level message takes precedence). When you're done, you'll enroll *Learner 02* in any of the courses available on the Prime account to trigger your customized email message being sent by Captivate Prime. Finally, you will log in to the mailbox of *Learner 02* and review your customized email message.

Let's start by customizing the email template by performing the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Email Templates** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Click the **Learning Activity** tab.
- 4. Finally, click the **Course – Enrolled by Admin/Manager** message.

As shown in the following screenshot, this action opens the **Template Preview** dialog, which contains all the tools you need to customize the content of the email template:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_574_0.png)

Figure 15.16 – The Template Preview dialog is used to customize the content of email templates

- 5. At the very top of the **Template Preview** dialog, make sure you are on the **Learner** tab.

The **Learner** tab displays the message that are sent to the learner when the corresponding event occurs. The **Manager** tab displays the message that are sent to the learner's manager when the event occurs. (Remember that you can disable all manager messages using the **Optional Managers Email** option, which is available on the **Settings** tab.)

- 6. Use the **Language** drop-down menu to choose the language version you want to customize.

- 7. If needed, you can modify the subject of the message using the **Subject** field.

- 8. Finally, you can modify the body of the message, including the message title in the banner.

When modifying the content of such a message, there are two types of text elements you can use:

- **Static text** is the text you type using your keyboard. These portions of the text are always the same for each message based on this template, regardless of the learner or course involved.
- **Dynamic text** is the pieces of the text that are different each time a message based on this template is sent. For example, the learner's name or the course name are pieces of dynamic text.

To enter pieces of dynamic text, you must use the icons that appear in the upper-right corner of the text you are editing. For example, in the following screenshot, we are customizing the body of this email message. The icons that allow us to include pieces of dynamic text in this area of the message are highlighted by the arrow:

In the preceding screenshot, the **Learner**, **CourseName**, and **CourseCompletionDeadline** elements are dynamic pieces of content.

- 9. If needed, you can switch to the **Manager** tab to review and customize the message that was sent to the learner's manager.
- You can also select another language from the **Language** drop-down menu and customize the same message in several different languages.
- 10. When done, click the **Save** button located in the lower-right corner of the **Template Preview** dialog.
- 11. Finally, make sure that the **Course – Enrolled by Admin/Manager** message you just customized is enabled (the corresponding switch is set to **Yes**).

Now that you have customized the content of the **Course – Enrolled by Admin/Manager** message, you will use your administrator superpowers to enroll *Learner 02* in one of the courses available on your Prime account.

- 12. Click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 13. Hover your mouse over any of the available course cards and click the **View Course** link to open the course details.
- 14. Click the **Learners** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
- 15. Click the **Enroll** link to open the **Enroll Learners** dialog. If the chosen course has multiple instances, use the **Select Instance** drop-down menu to select the desired course instance.
- 16. Add *Learner 02* to the list of learners to enroll.
- 17. When the **Enroll Learners** dialog looks as follows, click the **Proceed** button in the lower-right corner:

- 18. Confirm your intention by clicking the **Enroll** button in the **Enroll Learners** message that appears on the screen.
- 19. Finally, check the mailbox of *Learner 02* and confirm that he/she received an email from Captivate Prime about the new course enrollment, and that the message matches what you have configured earlier in this section.

In this section, you learned how to modify the content of the email messages that are sent by Captivate Prime. Remember that the following:

- The modifications that are made to the email templates of each course, learning program, or certification override the modifications made to the main **Email Templates** section of the Prime account.
- The text you type with your keyboard is considered *static* text and is the same every time the email template is used.
- The pieces of text you add by clicking the icons available in the **Template Preview** dialog box are considered *dynamic* text elements. These text elements are different each time the email template is used, depending on the relevant user, course, and so on.
