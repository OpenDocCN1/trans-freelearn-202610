External profiles were discussed in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*. As a reminder, external profiles allow you to define external users. External users are individuals who have access to your Prime account without being part of your organization. Typical external users are partners, suppliers, subcontractors, consultants, and so on. In the preceding screenshot, the **xTernal Company profile** option corresponds to the external profile that was defined in the *Creating external users* section of *Chapter 5, Managing Users*.

*Root users* are all users of your Prime account that meet the following two requirements:

+   - First, root users must be managers.
+   - Second, root users do not report to any manager themselves.

In other words, root users are at the top of the user hierarchy. By default, the primary administrator of your Prime account is the only available root user. In the preceding screenshot, my user profile (**Damien Bruyndonckx**) is the only root user available. Typical root users are the top executives of your organization or someone in your organization that manages external users/partners.

**Listing root users**
Captivate Prime maintains a list of all root users in the **Root Level Learners** user group. This is one of the many user groups that Captivate Prime automatically creates and maintains. From the administrator home page, go to **Users -> User Groups** and look for the **Root Level Learners** group.
