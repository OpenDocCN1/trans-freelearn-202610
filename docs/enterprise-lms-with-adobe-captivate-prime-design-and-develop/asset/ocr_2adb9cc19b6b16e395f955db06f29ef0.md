# Mastheads in the learner experience

In this section, you will return to the learning experience in your Captivate Prime account by logging in as an internal user to review the masthead you created in the previous section:

+   1. Log in to your Prime account as *Learner 01* or as any other internal user.
+   2. As soon as you are logged in, you should see your new masthead in the top section of the learner home page:

![](bbox=[0.13, 0.28, 0.86, 0.51])

Figure 15.7 – The new masthead is visible at the top of the learner home page

3. Click the **Learn More** link and confirm that you are being redirected to the Internal **Catalog** page of the learner experience.

> **Multiple mastheads**
> If there are several mastheads for the currently logged-in user, they will be displayed one by one in an image carousel.

In this section, you have reviewed the masthead announcement type. You started by creating a masthead using an image and a link that redirects the learner to one of the custom catalogs you created in the previous chapter. Remember that the ability to use a **Masthead** as a call to action is what makes mastheads different from the other announcement types of Adobe Captivate Prime. When done, you headed to the learner experience to see how the new masthead is presented to the internal users of your Prime account.

In the next section, you will learn how to get an overview of which users saw which announcement by downloading the **Notification Announcements Report**.
