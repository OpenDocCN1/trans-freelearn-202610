A *retired* badge can no longer be linked to any skill, course, learning program, or certification, but it remains available in the skills, courses, learning programs, and certifications to which it is already linked. In addition, learners who have been awarded retired badges retain them and can continue to use them as usual.

+   5. Click the **Retired** tab located in the upper-left corner of the page to open the list of retired badges.
+   6. Again, use the checkboxes to select one or more of the retired badges.
+   7. Open the **Actions** dropdown located in the upper-right corner of the screen and inspect the available options.
+   8. In this example, select all the retired badges and republish them to return to the initial situation.

Clicking the **Republish** option puts the selected badges back in the *active* state, so they can be assigned to skills, courses, learning programs, and certifications again.

Clicking the **Delete** button definitively deletes the selected badges from the system.

In this section, you learned how to *retire* and *delete* existing badges. This concludes your exploration of badges in Captivate Prime. As a reminder, badges are a visual representation of a learner's achievement. Learners are awarded badges by acquiring new skills, taking courses and learning programs, and getting certified. By default, Captivate Prime contains a handful of predefined badges but it is very easy to define your own custom badges. You can also *retire* and then *delete* existing badges (including those provided by Captivate Prime out of the box) very easily.

> **Note**
> 
> There is absolutely no obligation to use badges in Captivate Prime. In fact, organizations that are new to badges are not always sure how they will be perceived by their staff, or even whether they will incentivize learners at all. Badges can be something for later on in the Captivate Prime deployment, once learners are up and running with the platform.
