
## Branding your Captivate Prime account

Other important aspects of a company's corporate image are the logo, color theme, fonts, and so on used in all of the company's communications. In this section, you will learn how to upload your logo and modify the color scheme of Captivate Prime LMS:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an administrator and that you are on the administrator homepage.
- 2. Click the Branding link located in the Configure section at the bottom of the left column.

This takes you to the Branding page of the administrator application. From here, you can upload your logo and modify the color theme of your Prime account. Let's review these two options in more details.

### Uploading your logo

First, you will upload your company logo using the following procedure:

> Note
> If you do not have access to your company logo, you can use the sample logo provided in the files that accompany this book. Look for the branding/sampleLogo.png or branding/sampleLogo_negative.png files.

- 1. Make sure you are on the Branding page of the administrator area.
- 2. Click the Edit link associated with the Logo Styling option.
- 3. Drag and drop your logo image or click the upload area to select the logo image you want to use. For best results, it is recommended to use an image file in .jpg or .png format.
- 4. Choose your favorite Header Style among the three available options (Only Logo Image (default), Logo Image + CompanyName, or Only Company Name).

The header style defines the way your logo and/or your company name is displayed in the top left corner of the Captivate Prime interface.

- 5. Click the Save button located in the bottom right corner of the page to validate.

Your logo should now be visible in the top left corner of the Captivate Prime interface (unless you chose the Only Company Name header style) as depicted in the following screenshot.

### Customizing the color theme

With your logo displayed in the top left corner of the header, your Captivate Prime account already conveys a much different identity than a few minutes ago. That being said, there is still some work to do before it looks similar to other online platforms of your organization. In this section, you will introduce yet another critical aspect of your brand identity to your Captivate Prime account: your corporate color scheme.

To help you out, Captivate Prime comes with six professionally designed color themes. You will first choose the color theme that takes you the closest to the final look and feel you want to achieve. Then, you will further customize the chosen theme to perfectly align it to your corporate colors:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an administrator and that you are on the administrator homepage.
- 2. Click the Branding link located in the Configure section at the bottom of the left column. This takes you to the Branding page of the administrator application.
- 3. Click the Edit link associated with the Themes option.

When you scroll down the page a little bit, you see the list of the six themes included with Captivate Prime. By default, the Prime Default theme is applied to any new account.

- 4. Select any other available theme. You can preview the selected theme in the theme preview section located just above the theme chooser.

Note the left and the right arrows located on either side of the theme preview section. These allow you to preview the selected theme in different areas of Captivate Prime. In the following screenshot, the Pebbles theme is applied, and the theme preview area displays the third available preview image.

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_56_0.png)

Continue browsing and previewing the available themes until you find one that you like. The next step is to further customize the chosen theme so it perfectly aligns with your organization's color guide.

> **Accessing the color guide**
> If you do not have access to your organization's color guide, or if your organization does not have a color guide, you can use the sample color guide provided in the accompanying files. Look for the branding/colorGuide.pdf file.

Adobe Captivate Prime lets you customize four different aspects of each theme: **Top Bar Color**, **Accent Color**, **Primary Color**, and **Sidebar Brightness.**

- 5. To identify which area of the interface is controlled by each of these options, click the **Show Hints** link at the bottom of the theme options area.

This toggles on hints on the theme preview as shown in the following screenshot.

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_57_0.png)

Figure 2.5 – Hints toggled on in the theme preview

Now that you have a better idea of what aspect of the **user interface (UI)** each option controls, let’s start customizing the top bar color.

- 6. In the options for the chosen theme, click the **Top Bar Color** field. By default, the top bar color is #FFFFFF in every theme. (#FFFFFF is the hex code for *white*.)

- 7. Type in the desired color code or choose the color in the color picker. Click the **Select** button when done.

The **Top Bar Color** is the background color of the header area that spans across the top of the Captivate Prime interface. Your change is immediately reflected in the theme preview area. If you don’t like the new color, just repeat the above actions with another color code until you find a color that you like.

Now, let's modify the **Accent Color**. The accent color is the color of the icons displayed in the left sidebar.

- 8. In the options for the chosen theme, open the **Accent Color** dropdown.
- 9. Choose any color in the list. Your choice is immediately reflected in the theme preview area.
- 10. If you want to use a custom **Accent Color**, you must first choose **Custom** at the end of the **Accent Color** dropdown, then, you must enter the color code of your custom color or choose it with the color picker.

The next option available is **Primary Color**. Just like for the **Accent Color**, you can choose another color in the drop-down list. If you want a custom color, you first need to choose **Custom** at the end of the drop-down list and then, you must enter the exact color code you want to use as the primary color.

Unlike the other theme customizations, changing **Primary Color** will not reflect in the theme preview area. Actually, **Primary Color** is a brand-new option in Captivate Prime. It controls some UI elements of the new *immersive* user experience. For now, take good notes of the chosen **Primary Color**. You will refer to it in the next section when learning about the *immersive* and the *classic* user experiences.

The last customization option available for themes is **Sidebar Brightness**. This controls how dark or how light the background of the left sidebar is.

- 11. Move the **Sidebar Brightness** slider to the left and to the right. As you do it, take a look at the left sidebar in the theme preview area.

Notice that the font color automatically toggles between white and black depending on the chosen **Sidebar Brightness**.

- 12. Keep adjusting the **Sidebar Brightness** until the left sidebar looks the way you want in the theme preview.
- 13. Finally, click the **Save** button in the bottom right corner of the screen to save your changes.

After Captivate Prime reloads, you should see your new customized theme applied.

- 14. Return to the administrator homepage before moving on to the next section.

At the end of this section, the graphical aspect of Captivate Prime is already much closer to your company's brand image. Your logo is visible in the upper left corner and the colors used are the brand colors of your company.

This is how far the built-in options of Captivate Prime take you when it comes to customizing the general look and feel of your account. If this feels somewhat limited, you can take your customization to the next level by using Captivate Prime as a **headless LMS**. This requires some custom development from your internal dev team, or from a specialized contractor. Using Captivate Prime as a headless LMS is discussed in more detail at the end of this chapter.

## Other visual customizations

Adobe Captivate Prime allows you to customize other graphical aspects of the platform, such as custom progress icons, custom mouse pointer images, custom fonts, and so on. Unfortunately, at the time of this writing, these additional customizations cannot be done within the admin interface.

If you need to make these changes, you need to make contact with the Captivate Prime support team.

## Customizing the learner's experience

Only a small fraction of your users will ever need to access the administrative area of your Prime account. The bulk of your account users will be learners. These learners will log in to your account in order to browse the course catalogs, enroll themselves into courses, take courses, interact with other learners, and so on.

That's why getting the learner experience right is a critical aspect of setting up your Captivate Prime account. In this section, you will take your first steps into getting control of the learner experience.

### Understanding the Classic and the Immersive experiences

For years, Captivate Prime has provided a certain user experience for learners. This user experience has played a key role in making Captivate Prime an award-winning LMS. But times change. Whether for listening to music, watching movies or series, online shopping, or accessing public services, the number of platforms has exploded in recent years and users are now much more accustomed than before to consuming digital experiences. Their expectations have changed.
