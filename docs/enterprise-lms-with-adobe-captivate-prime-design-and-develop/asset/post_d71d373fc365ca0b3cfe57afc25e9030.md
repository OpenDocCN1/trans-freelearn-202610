## Discovering the fluidic player

The **fluidic player** is a key component and a landmark feature of Adobe Captivate Prime. What makes it unique is its ability to deliver a wide variety of learning content. This allows learners to remain in the same learning environment regardless of the reviewed module being a video, a document, a SCORM package, or any other content supported by Captivate Prime.

This is different from many other LMS solutions where the learner is systematically taken back to the course home page when completing a module and where each type of content is displayed in an environment specific to the type of content being reviewed.

You will now review the features of the fluidic player in more detail using the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are still logged in as *Learner 01*.
- 2. From the learner home page, click the **Fundamentals of cybersecurity** course card (make sure you do *not* click the **Continue** link). This action takes you to the course overview page.
- 3. Click the **Welcome to the course!** link in the **Prework** section of the course.

This action opens the fluidic player to display this course module. If you have used the files included in the download associated with this book, this module is a PowerPoint presentation. Notice the icons located in the center section of the fluidic player, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_285_0.png)

Figure 7.9 – The fluidic player when displaying a PowerPoint presentation

These icons allow you to change the zoom level and to move from one slide to the next within the PowerPoint presentation. These controls *are specific to PowerPoint presentations and documents*.

- 4. Use these controls to review the PowerPoint presentation.
- 5. When done, click the close icon located in the upper-right corner of the screen.

This action takes you back to the course overview page, where a green checkmark appears next to the **Prework** module *Learner 01* has just visited. Remember that it is *not mandatory* to consult prework activities. This is the reason why the progress bar is not yet displayed in the course banner and the button in the right column still indicates **Start** and not **Continue.**

- 14. While the video is playing, take a closer look at the items available in the lower center section of the fluidic player. These are specific to a video file and are different from the icons available when reviewing a PowerPoint presentation or an interactive SCORM package.

This demonstrates the real magic of the fluidic player! When a module is finished, the next module is automatically loaded into the fluidic player. The fluidic player then examines the new module, adapts its icons to the type of content (video, SCORM, document, and so on), and starts playing the module, all without leaving the learning environment. This allows the learner to stay focused on learning while moving seamlessly from one module to the next, regardless of the type of content being served.
