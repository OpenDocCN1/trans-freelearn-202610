# Summary

In this chapter, you have discovered the tools and features available to the integration admin to connect your Captivate Prime account to the other enterprise tools and services used in your organization. All these tools and features are geared toward a single objective: enabling the exchange of data between Adobe Captivate Prime and the rest of your IT infrastructure.

To do so, Captivate Prime ships with a handful of connectors that you can use to integrate your Prime account with some of the most popular applications used by organizations all over the world. If there is no built-in connector for the solution used within your organization, or if your project requires additional custom development, you can use the Captivate Prime API to programmatically access virtually every aspect of your Prime account.

In this case, remember that Captivate Prime uses the OAuth 2.0 security framework to authenticate and authorize external applications that request access to your Prime account via the API. Generating secure requests to your API is the responsibility of your developers. Your only responsibility as an integration admin is to register these applications with your Captivate Prime account and to communicate the necessary security tokens to the development team.

Finally, you discovered the migration wizard included in Adobe Captivate Prime. This feature is designed to help organizations transition from their current LMS solution over to Captivate Prime. Keep in mind that the migration assistant is not enabled by default. You need to contact the Adobe Captivate Prime support team to activate it and to assist you with your migration project.
