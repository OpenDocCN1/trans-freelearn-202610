In this section, you will return to the administrator application and review the options that allow you to enable content creation on your Prime account. Perform the following steps:

+   1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Social Learning** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. Switch to the **Settings** tab by clicking the **Settings** link at the top of the page.
+   4. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Content Curation** option.

As shown in the following screenshot, **No Curation** is the default value:

![](bbox=[0.12, 0.31, 0.82, 0.47])

Figure 12.30 – By default, No Curation is enabled on your Prime account

Take some time to read the descriptions associated with the three values:

+   - When **Manual-Only Curation** is enabled, only the designated SMEs can curate content that's uploaded by learners to the discussion boards.
+   - When **Auto-assisted Manual Curation** is enabled, the built-in AI-powered curation engine tries to automatically check the quality of the uploaded content. If it fails, the content is then passed on to designated SMEs for manual curation.

Note that, when **Auto-assisted Manual Curation** is enabled, you must map the skills defined on your Prime account to specific **skill domains** to help the AI-powered curation engine do its job. This is what you will do in the next section, but first, you must enable content curation on your Prime account.

+   5. Select the **Auto-assisted Manual Curation** option.
+   6. Click the **Save** button to confirm this.

**Auto-assisted Manual Curation** is now enabled on your Prime account. Remember that for it to be efficient, you need to provide the AI-powered curation engine with a mapping between the custom skills defined on your Prime account and the skill domains known to the artificial intelligence. We'll explore that process in the next section.
