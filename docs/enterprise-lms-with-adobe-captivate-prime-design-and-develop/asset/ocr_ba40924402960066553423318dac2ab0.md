+   - As for *Learner 02*, remember that, when creating the course instance, you specified a **Seat Limit** of two. With the enrollment of *Learner 01* and *Learner 03*, the seat limit has been reached and the course instance is currently full!

# Manager approval

Back in the *Reviewing the course options that affect enrollment* section, earlier in this chapter, you inspected the **Enrollment Type** option for courses, and you discovered that one of the options that's available is **Manager Approved.**

Using this option, learners cannot self-enroll in courses. Instead, they send an approval request to their manager who must approve or reject the enrollment request. Although the course you are using in these examples does not make use of this workflow, let's take advantage of the fact you are currently logged in as *Manager 01* to take a quick look at how managers can approve or reject such enrollment requests:

+   1. From the manager home page, click the **Notifications** link located in the **View** section of the left sidebar.
+   
+   This takes you to the **Notifications** page, where all recent notifications are displayed by default. This includes pending enrollment requests sent by team members.
+   2. Click the **Pending Tasks** tab located in the top-left corner of the **Notifications** page.
