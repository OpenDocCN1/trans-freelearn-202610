# Marking learner attendance

Obviously, the primary task of an instructor is to conduct teaching activities (face-to-face or virtual). But, in addition to their teaching role, instructors must also spend some time reporting learner attendance back to the LMS. As an instructor, I have to admit that this is the part I almost always forget to do, even though it is a very important task from a training management perspective. Fortunately, Captivate Prime makes it very easy to mark learners' attendance.

First, remember that learners can self-report their attendance using the Captivate Prime mobile app and the QR code located in the top-right corner of the attendance sheet generated by the system. This has already been discussed in the *Reviewing the participants list* section earlier in this chapter.

But not everyone has access to the Captivate Prime mobile application. In such cases, the instructor must manually report learners' attendance to the LMS using the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in to your Captivate Prime account as an instructor.
+   2. From the instructor home page, click the session you want to mark attendance for. This opens the **Session Overview** page.
+   
+   Remember that, if the session has already been delivered, you need to click the **Past Sessions** link in the left sidebar to access the desired session.
+   3. Click the **Learners** link located in the left sidebar of the **Session Overview** page to open the **Learners** page of the chosen session.
+   4. Use the checkboxes to select the desired learners.
+   5. Click the **Actions** button located in the upper-right corner of the screen and select either the **Mark Attended** or the **Mark Not Attended** action.

Instructors would typically perform these actions either during or after the session.

> **Tip**
> If you are an instructor, make sure you connect to the Prime account and print the attendee list prior to the session. First, this allows learners to scan the QR code and report their attendance themselves (which reduces your administrative workload even more). It also gives you the ability to take attendance in writing. You can then report attendance on the LMS *after* the session, so you don't have to deal with such administrative requirements while teaching. Some organizations also require a written record of learner attendance to be kept.

*Attending* a session is one thing, *successfully* completing the session is another. As instructors, you can therefore grade each learner's attendance and add comments about it in the LMS. This is what you will do in the next section.
