# Customizing the color theme

With your logo displayed in the top left corner of the header, your Captivate Prime account already conveys a much different identity than a few minutes ago. That being said, there is still some work to do before it looks similar to other online platforms of your organization. In this section, you will introduce yet another critical aspect of your brand identity to your Captivate Prime account: your corporate color scheme.

To help you out, Captivate Prime comes with six professionally designed color themes. You will first choose the color theme that takes you the closest to the final look and feel you want to achieve. Then, you will further customize the chosen theme to perfectly align it to your corporate colors:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as an administrator and that you are on the administrator homepage.
+   2. Click the Branding link located in the Configure section at the bottom of the left column. This takes you to the Branding page of the administrator application.
+   3. Click the Edit link associated with the Themes option.
