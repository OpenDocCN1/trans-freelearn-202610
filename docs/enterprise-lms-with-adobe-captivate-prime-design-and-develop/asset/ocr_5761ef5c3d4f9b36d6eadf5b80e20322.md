# Technical requirements

In order to perform the exercises described in this chapter, you need to meet the following requirements:

+   - You must be able to access your Prime account with at least two different learner profiles.
+   - These learners must both be enrolled in the same course.
+   - The course must contain at least one live or virtual classroom activity, one file submission activity, and one checklist activity. These activities should all have the same instructor.
+   - You should have access to a mobile device (tablet or smartphone) with the Adobe Captivate Prime app installed. The app is available for iOS from the Apple App Store and for Android from the Google Play Store.
