# Reviewing the express interest report

You now have many learners expressing interest in the content available on the **Content Marketplace**. This generates a lot of interesting data to help organizations decide whether to purchase access to some or all of this third-party off-the-shelf content.

In this section, you'll discover how administrators can download the **Express interest report** before making a purchase decision. To do this, perform the following steps:

+   1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Content Marketplace** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. From the **Content Marketplace** page, click the **Enterprise Training** card.
+   4. Scroll down the **Enterprise Training** page until you can see the Invite Users section.
