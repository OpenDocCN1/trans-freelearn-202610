The ability to export this data from Adobe Captivate allows it to be integrated with much larger datasets produced by a wide variety of systems within the organization. Organizations can then use data mining and visualization technologies to cross-reference Prime-generated learning data with a host of other data from a variety of sources for integration into their overall business intelligence efforts.

# Data exchange with virtual classroom solutions

Back in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, you learned that a Captivate Prime course can comprise both **synchronous** and **asynchronous** modules:

+   - *Asynchronous* modules are training content uploaded to the LMS that learners access individually at their own convenience. Typical asynchronous modules are instructional videos, PDF documents, PowerPoint presentations, and SCORM-compliant interactive learning modules.
+   - *Synchronous* modules are live or virtual classes where multiple participants are together in the same physical or virtual space. Traditional instructor-led training, which takes place in a classroom or lecture hall, and virtual classes delivered using any type of video conferencing solution are typical examples of synchronous modules.

Synchronous modules are hosted directly on your Prime account using your account's content library, as described in *Chapter 3, Uploading Learning Content and Managing the Content Library*. But Captivate Prime has no built-in solution for conducting virtual classrooms.

In other words, when adding synchronous modules to your courses, you must rely on an external video conferencing system or virtual classroom solution such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Adobe Connect.

Hopefully, Captivate Prime is able to exchange data with these popular virtual classroom solutions. For example, attendance data, interaction data, and completion statuses can be automatically synced between most of these solutions and your Prime account.

# Integration of external content libraries

Captivate Prime can exchange data with virtually any third-party instructional content library, either through a dedicated connector or through custom development using the Captivate Prime API. This allows you to display courses provided by these external libraries in your Prime account and track your learners' interactions with this external content.
