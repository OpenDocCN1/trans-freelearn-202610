Notice that only two of the fields in the **Add Peer Information** dialog are required:

+   - The **Account Subdomain** field is the URL of the Prime account to which you want to allocate seats.
+   - The **Admin Email ID** field is the email address of an administrator of the other Prime account. After completing this form, Captivate Prime will send an email to this address with instructions on how to set up this peer account on the receiver end.

Of course, you should also specify the number of user seats that you want to allocate to the other account using the **Seats** field.
