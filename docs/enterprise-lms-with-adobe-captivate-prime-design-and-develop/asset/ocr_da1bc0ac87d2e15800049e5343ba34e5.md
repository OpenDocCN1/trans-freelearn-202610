After saving the new self-registration profile, Captivate Prime generates a unique URL that you must share with potential Prime users. This unique URL appears on the screen and is also sent to you in an email, confirming the creation of the new self-registration profile.

You can create as many self-registration profiles as needed (keep an eye on the number of user licenses you have purchased!). For example, each department of the organization can have its own self-registration profile with a different manager assigned to each profile. This allows you to add users to your Prime account without any action from an administrator, all while respecting the hierarchical structure of your organization.

# Using a self-registration profile to auto-register as a Prime learner

Now that a self-registration profile is active, you will switch hats again and put yourself in the shoes of a user who wants to register to your Prime account.

You will use the self-registration link to create *Manager 01*. Later in this chapter, you will define this new user as the manager of other learners. Also, remember that *you* will be defined as the manager of any user using this self-registration link to register to the platform. This will allow you to create a three-level hierarchy where you will be the general manager, while *Manager 01* will manage a small team of users.

Finally, keep in mind that, by default, all internal users must have an Adobe ID. So, one of the steps in the self-registration process is for users to create their own Adobe IDs. Let’s experiment with this by following these steps:

+   1. Open a new private window in your web browser (or another browser altogether).
+   2. Paste the auto-registration URL Captivate Prime generated in the previous section in the address bar. This will take you to the Adobe **Sign in** page. The link is also available in the email you received from Captivate Prime.
+   3. Click the **Create an account** link to create a new Adobe ID for *Manager 01*.
+   4. Fill in the **Create an account** form and click the **Create account** button when you’re done.
+   5. You should be automatically redirected to the Learner experience of your Prime account and logged in as *Manager 01*.
