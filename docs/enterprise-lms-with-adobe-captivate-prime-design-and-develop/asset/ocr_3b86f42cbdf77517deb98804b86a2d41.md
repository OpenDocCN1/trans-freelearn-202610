In Captivate Prime, these features are based on a well-known theoretical model named the *Four Levels of Learning Evaluation* that was developed by *Dr. Donald Kirkpatrick* in the *1950s*.

Level 1 is the *Reaction* level. In Captivate Prime, this is a form that each learner fills in whenever a course or learning program is complete. Level 2 is the *Learning* level. In Captivate Prime, these are the scores acquired by learners as they review the modules of the courses they take. It includes the quiz scores of the interactive modules, the grades obtained by submitting assignments, and any other type of activity that can be graded. Level 3 is the *Behavior* level. In Captivate Prime, this is a form that the learner's manager must complete to assess how attending a training has modified the learner's behavior when back in the workplace. Typically, managers would fill in the L3 feedback form 45 days after training completion.

L1 and L3 feedback are disabled by default in Captivate Prime. You can enable them either at the course instance level, the course level, or the account level. Also remember that these feedback features are not limited to courses and also apply to learning programs.

But don't forget that there is a fourth level to Kirkpatrick's Learning Evaluation model. It is the *Results* level and it is where organizations take advantage of the data collected to measure the impact of learning on the organization as a whole. This is the subject of the next chapter, where you will review the extensive reporting capabilities available in Captivate Prime.
