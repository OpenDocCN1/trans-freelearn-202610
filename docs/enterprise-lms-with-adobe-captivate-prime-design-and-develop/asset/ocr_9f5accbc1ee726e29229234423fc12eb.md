In this section, we discussed sharing catalogs either internally or externally. Remember that internal sharing allows you to grant access to your catalogs to the user groups defined within your Prime account. This includes both the internal and the external users available on your account. External sharing allows you to share your custom course catalogs with other Captivate Prime accounts. These can belong to providers, suppliers, partners, and customers that are also using Adobe Captivate Prime as their LMS or to local subsidiaries of your own international organization.

You now have two custom catalogs available in your Prime account. Each contains a number of learning items, and you have defined the users who have access to these catalogs. That said, the learner experience is not yet affected by any of the changes you have made so far in this chapter. This is because you haven't yet enabled your newly created catalogs. We will explore this in the next section.

# Enabling and Disabling Catalogs

In the preceding sections, you created two custom catalogs in your Prime account, added learning content to them, and decided which user groups have access to these catalogs. Now you are ready to activate them so that they become available in the learner experience.

But before doing that, remember that there is a third catalog available in your Prime account: the default catalog that was automatically created along with your account. This default catalog has some special characteristics of its own. Let's explore that in the next section.

# Modifying the Default Catalog

Remember that the default catalog contains all of the learning objects that are available in your Prime account and is accessible to all users. Let's take a look at how you can modify this default catalog using the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Catalogs** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. Click the **Default Catalog** that is visible at the top of the list to open the **Default Catalog** page.
