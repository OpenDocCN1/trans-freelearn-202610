# Content moderation and curation

Allowing users to post content online and participate in discussions can certainly be a rich, intense, and rewarding learning experience. But it also opens the door to all sorts of arguments, disputes, and even verbal assaults from learners, not to mention the varying quality of the content being shared. This is not specific to Captivate Prime, though – just take a quick look at most social networks; you will realize that these issues arise as soon as content is shared and when people engage in discussions.

That's why Captivate Prime includes powerful content moderation and curation features:

+   - **Content moderation** refers to the ability to edit and/or delete any content that is deemed inappropriate. This can be done directly by moderators, or after a learner has reported a post as inappropriate.
+   - **Content curation** is the process of checking the quality and relevance of content that's been shared by learners. For example, when someone posts information that is outdated, irrelevant, or even false, it must be removed from Captivate Prime, even if the person posting it did so in a perfectly honest and polite manner.
