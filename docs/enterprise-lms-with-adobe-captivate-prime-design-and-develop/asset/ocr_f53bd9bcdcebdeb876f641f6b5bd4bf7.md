# Browsing the Content Marketplace as a learner

In the previous section, you invited all of your internal learners to explore the courses available in the Content Marketplace so that they could express their interest in the courses being offered. This is a good way for organizations to gauge interest in this additional content before purchasing access to all or part of it. In this section, you will log in to your Prime account as an internal learner, browse the Content Marketplace, and express interest in some of the courses it contains.
