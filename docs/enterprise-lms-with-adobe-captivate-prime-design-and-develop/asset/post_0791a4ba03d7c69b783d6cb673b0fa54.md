
- **Level 3: Behavior**: This level is about determining the extent to which participants are able to apply what they have learned during the training to their work. In other words, it is about measuring the change in learners' behavior (such as increased productivity or better leadership in the office) after taking a course.
- **Level 4: Results**: This level measures whether or not desired outcomes were achieved through training and accompanying support programs. This is where we start measuring the impact that the training has on the organization as a whole, rather than focusing on each individual learner.

In practice, most teachers and trainers get stuck at levels 1 and 2, while levels 3 and 4 are where the most useful data exists from the organization's perspective. As an enterprise LMS, Captivate Prime attempts to bridge the gap between these four levels to bring up as much meaningful data as possible to levels 3 and 4, which in turn provides information about the real impact of learning on the organization and therefore also on the return on investment. Thinking backward (from L4 to L1), Kirkpatrick's model can also be a way to help evaluate the quality of the training content itself. Success at L1 and L2 better prepares for success at L3 and even L4.

Now that you know a bit more about the theoretical aspects of Kirkpatrick's Four Levels model, let's look at how it is implemented in Adobe Captivate Prime over the next few sections.

## Implementing L1 and L3 feedback

In this section, you will review the tools and options that allow you to enable and manage L1 and L3 feedback on your Captivate Prime account.

> **What about L2 feedback?**
> L2 feedback corresponds to the *Learning* level of Kirkpatrick's model. In Captivate Prime, L2 feedback is entirely automatic and does not require any action from an administrator to enable or customize it. This is the reason why L2 feedback is not discussed in this section. It will be discussed the *Retrieving L1, L2, and L3 Feedback* section later in this chapter.

L1 feedback refers to the first level of Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Learning Evaluation. As a reminder, level 1 is known as the *Reaction* level. It tries to measure the overall learner satisfaction. In Captivate Prime, L1 feedback is a form that each learner must complete at the end of a course or learning program.

L3 feedback refers to the third level of Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Learning Evaluation. This is the *Behavior* level, which attempts to measure whether a student's behavior has changed after taking a course. In Captivate Prime, it is a form that the learner's manager fills in, usually a few days or weeks after the learner has completed a training.

> **Courses and learning programs**
> Keep in mind that although the next few sections discuss L1 and L3 feedback for courses, most of the features discussed can also be applied to learning programs. The specifics of L1 and L3 feedback for learning programs are addressed in the *L1 and L3 feedback for learning programs* section later in this chapter.

By default, L1 and L3 feedback are disabled on any new Prime account, but you can override this setting and enable feedback for each individual course (or learning program) instance. This is what you will examine in the next section.

## Enabling L1 and L3 Feedback for a Course Instance

By default, L1 and L3 feedback are disabled at the account level, because this might be a new approach for many trainers and HR managers who are not too familiar with Kirkpatrick's model or who have never used an LMS that offers such a feedback workflow. If it were enabled by default, there would be a large number of email interactions, which most organizations do not want in the early stages of their Captivate Prime deployment.

Administrators can override this account default by enabling L1 and/or L3 feedback for each individual course. In other words, when creating a new course, feedback is *disabled* by default unless an administrator explicitly enables feedback for that particular course.

To be more exact, feedback is enabled or disabled for each individual *course instance*, not for the course as a whole. It means that you can, for the same course, enable feedback for some instances and leave feedback disabled for other instances. You can also customize L1 and L3 feedback for each individual course instance.

Also keep in mind that L1 and L3 feedback are triggered after a learner completes a course. Therefore, changing the feedback options only affects future learners as well as current learners who have not yet completed the course. Learners who have already completed the course when the feedback options are modified are not affected by the change.

You will now turn L1 and L3 feedback on for one of the default courses available in your Prime trial account using the following steps:

1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator.
2. From the admin home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
3. Hover over the course cards of the course you want to enable feedback for.

In this example, I chose the **Color Masterclass: Simple Steps to Create Vivid Art** course. It is one of the courses that is automatically available on any new Prime trial account. If this particular course is not available on your account, choose any (short) course available.
4. Click the **View Course** link to access the course overview page.
5. From there, click the **Instances** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.

There should only be a single default instance available for that course. As discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, a default course instance is automatically created whenever you create a new course. Most of the time, this default instance is all you need.
