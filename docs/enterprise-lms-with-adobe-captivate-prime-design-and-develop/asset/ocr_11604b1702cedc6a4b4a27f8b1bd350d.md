# Creating a self-registration link

In the last two sections, you, the Prime administrator, have created new users either one by one or in batch form by uploading a CSV file. In both cases, creating new users requires action on your part. In this section, you will allow users to register themselves to your Prime account, a technique that requires (almost) no intervention from you.

The idea is to generate a self-registration link and share that link with users who might be interested in joining your Captivate Prime account. Users can then click the link and fill out a form to register themselves on the platform. Let's get started:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as an administrator and that you are on the **Internal Users** page.
+   2. Click the **Add** button located in the top-right corner.
+   3. Click the **Self-Registration** option in the menu that opens. This opens the **Add Self-Registration** profile form.
+   4. Enter a profile name in the **Profile Name** field.
