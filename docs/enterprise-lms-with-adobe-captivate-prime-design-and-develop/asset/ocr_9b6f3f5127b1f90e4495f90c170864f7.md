Another key aspect that sets Captivate Prime apart is the fact that it is designed as an enterprise LMS. As such, it includes unique features designed specifically for midsize to large organizations, such as a dedicated manager role, extensive reporting capabilities, the ability to share content among various Prime accounts, the ability to include external third-party content in your Prime catalog, an AI-powered course recommendation engine, and more. Captivate Prime can also be integrated with a wealth of other enterprise solutions used by a wide range of organizations. For example, you can integrate Prime with your company’s LDAP directory for user authentication, with your CMS, with your marketing automation tool, with your e-commerce platform, and more. These integrations are possible with any such third-party platform, but if you decide to use the solutions of the Adobe Experienced Cloud (AEC), you will be able to leverage the built-in connectors available out of the box in Captivate Prime. Thanks to these integrations, you can deliver customized learning experiences to your employees, partners, and customers alike.
