# Working with ratings

With the massive development of e-commerce in recent years, we have become accustomed to purchasing items online using an ever-increasing number of e-commerce sites and services. Virtually all of these e-commerce sites have at least one thing in common: a rating and review system. In fact, these ratings and reviews are an essential part of the process of converting a mere interest into an actual purchase. Indeed, it is perhaps these comments and reviews that convinced you to buy the very book you are reading!

What holds true on Amazon, Booking.com, Netflix, and other such sites and services also holds true in the Captivate Prime course catalog. This is why learners have the ability to rate the courses they are taking using a five-star rating scale.

> **New feature**
> Star rating is a new feature introduced with the August 2021 update to Captivate Prime. If you are working on an older account (created before mid-August 2021), it is possible that star rating is completely disabled or replaced by the old course effectiveness score. The course effectiveness score will be discussed later in this section.

By default, the star rating feature is enabled on any new Prime account. Let's check this out using the following steps:

+   1. Log in to your Captivate Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the Settings link located in the Configure section of the left sidebar.
+   3. Then, click the General link located in the Basics section of the left sidebar.
+   4. Scroll down the page until you see the Show Ratings section.
