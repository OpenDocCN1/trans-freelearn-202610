# Sharing a screenshot

In this first example, you will start by taking a screenshot of your computer screen and uploading it to the **Cybersecurity in the organization** discussion board:

+   1. Click the **Take Screenshot** icon on the Adobe Captivate Prime desktop application welcome screen.
+   2. Follow the on-screen instructions to capture either the full screen of your computer or just a portion of your screen.
+   3. When done, the Adobe Captivate Prime desktop application displays a preview of your screen capture. If you are happy with it, click the **Next** button; otherwise, click the **Discard** link and start again until you are happy with the preview.
+   4. Next, name your screen capture and click the **Save** button.
+   5. Now, you can add annotations to your screen capture using the tools available in the left column of the Adobe Captivate Prime desktop application.
+   6. When you're ready, click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the screen.
+   7. Choose the discussion board where you want to share your screenshot (in this example, choose the **Cybersecurity in the organization** board), add a small message, and click the **Post** button.
+   8. When the upload is complete, click the **Go to Board** button to open your web browser. Confirm that your screenshot has been posted correctly, as shown in the following screenshot:
