# Using Manual Recommendations

So far, in this chapter, the recommendation types that you've explored are powered by the built-in AI technology of Captivate Prime. The administrator can certainly influence how recommendations are generated, but the process is, for the most part, entirely automatic. These recommendations serve learners well and deliver on Captivate Prime's promise of being a learner-centric LMS.

But Captivate Prime is also an enterprise LMS, and organizations have a say in which courses the platform should recommend to their employees, partners, and customers. In many cases, it is very useful for organizations to ensure that a carefully selected set of courses are given a prominent place on the learner home page. For example:

+   - You want your employees to have an opportunity to be trained on a new product before an official product launch.
+   - You need to train your workforce on a new tool your organization is implementing or on a new version of a business-critical enterprise solution used in your company.
+   - You want to advertise courses related to skills that are not sufficiently available in your organization.
+   - Etc.

These are just a few of the many circumstances in which manually recommending courses to a specific target audience within your organization can be very helpful. Hopefully, Captivate Prime has you covered! Let's review this feature, hands-on, in this section using the following steps:

+   1. Use the browser window in which you are logged in as an administrator.
+   2. From the admin home page, click the Announcements link located in the Manage section of the left sidebar to open the Announcements page of the administrator.
