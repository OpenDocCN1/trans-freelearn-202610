## Reviewing the Badges page

The next icon available in the left sidebar is the **Badges** icon, which takes you to the **Badges** page.

By completing courses, learning programs, certifications, and so on, learners can earn **badges**. The **Badges** page displays all the badges earned by the logged-in learner. From here, learners can download their badges in PDF and/or image format. Note that currently, *Learner 02* should not have any badges on display.

The August 2021 update of Captivate Prime has introduced the ability for learners to connect Captivate Prime with their Badgr account. In such a case, an additional icon appears on the **Badge** page to allow users to upload the badges they have acquired on Captivate Prime to their Badgr account. Note that this new option is disabled by default. Administrators can enable this feature using the new **Badgr Integration** option located on the **Settings | General** page of the administrator application.

**Badgr**
Badgr is an open online service that implements the Open Badge standard. The idea is to allow learners to centrally store all the digital badges they have earned on various compliant learning platforms (including Captivate Prime) and share them on social networks and other compliant platforms. You can find more information on Badgr at https://badgr.org/ and on digital badges in general at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_badge.

Badges will be further discussed in *Chapter 11, Badges and Gamification.*

## Reviewing the Leaderboard page

The last icon of the left sidebar takes you to the **Leaderboard** page. Note that this page can also be accessed by clicking the **Levels** card on the learner home page.

This page is related to the **gamification** features of Adobe Captivate Prime, which will be discussed in more detail in *Chapter 11, Badges and Gamification.* Note that an administrator can decide to completely disable gamification in the Prime account. In such a case, the **Leaderboard** icon does *not* appear in the left sidebar and the corresponding page is made unavailable to learners.

In the previous few sections, you went through all the pages of the learner experience, one by one. Here is a summary:

- The learner home page serves both as a personal summary and an area for personalized recommendations.
- The **My Learning** page is where learners have access to a complete overview of their own interactions with the learning resources hosted on the platform.
- The **Catalog** page is a very important element of the learner experience. This is where learners can browse the available learning resources and enroll in courses (or send enrollment requests to their manager).
- The **Skills** page allows learners to add or remove their own areas of interest. These influence the personal recommendations displayed on the home page.
- Users can also see their badges, participate in social learning interactions with their peers, and use the leaderboard to see how they compare to their colleagues.

Hopefully, this has given you a better understanding of the features available to learners and the corresponding administrative options.
