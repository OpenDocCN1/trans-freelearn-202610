
## Editing scores and comments

Sometimes attending a class is enough, but other times, attending a class requires participating in discussions, formulating an opinion, presenting on a topic, and so on. In such cases, instructors can evaluate each learner's participation in the course and add comments about it in the LMS. Let's review this workflow hands-on using the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an instructor to your Captivate Prime account.
- 2. From the instructor home page, click the session you want to mark attendance for, to open the Session Overview page. Keep in mind that if the session has already been delivered, you click the Past Sessions link in the left sidebar to access the desired session.
- 3. Click the Learners link in the left sidebar.
- 4. Next, click the Actions button located in the upper-right corner of the screen.
- 5. Click the Edit Scores & Comments action to switch the page to score editing mode.

As shown in the following screenshot, you can only enter scores and/or comments for learners that you already marked as Attended.

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_318_0.png)

Figure 8.11 – Entering scores and/or comments

In the example illustrated in the preceding screenshot, it is not possible to enter a score. This is because, when creating the course back in Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses, you did not select the Do you want to add quiz score for this Module checkbox.

- 6. Enter comments for each learner as per your requirements.
- 7. When done, click the Save button in the upper-right corner of the screen.

There is one more action that instructors may be required to perform after a session has been delivered. If you have enabled submissions for that particular session, learners can upload documents such as assignments, reports, assessments, and so on to the session. Let’s take a quick look at how instructors can review and grade these submissions:

- 8. Click the Submissions link in the left sidebar to open the Submissions page of the session.
- 9. If you have enabled submissions, you can download the documents uploaded by the class attendees and approve the submissions using the Approve buttons.

This concludes the overview of the tools and features available to the instructor in order to manage live training activities. Keep in mind that these synchronous training activities can be face-to-face or virtual classes. In this section, you have reviewed the following:

- How to access and edit your sessions' details
- How to manage the attendee list and how to generate a printout of the list before the class
- How to manage the waitlist of your sessions
- How to upload additional resources, such as additional class material or practical details, to your sessions
- How to mark learners' attendance at sessions and how to grade and comment on that attendance
- How to review the material submitted by learners for sessions that have submissions enabled

This represents the bulk of the Instructor role in Captivate Prime. That being said, there are some additional tasks instructors are responsible for. These will be reviewed in the next two sections.

## Reviewing File Submission Assignments

In addition to conducting face-to-face or virtual classes, instructors are also responsible for reviewing the material uploaded by learners using the file submission activity. As discussed in Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses, the file submission activity is one of many types of modules that can be added to a course. When using this activity in a course, you must define the instructor(s) responsible for reviewing the uploaded content. Also remember that the designated instructor(s) can be different for each course instance, as covered in Chapter 6, Enrolling Users to Courses.

It is now time to review the workflow used by instructors to download the learner's assignment, review it, and grade it:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an instructor to your Captivate Prime account.
- 2. From the instructor home page, click the Submissions/Checklists link located in the Modules section of the left sidebar.

This takes you to the Modules page of the instructor experience. From here, instructors can review their assigned File Submission and Checklist activities.

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_320_0.png)

- 3. Click any **Submission** activity available in the **Modules** list to open the **Submissions** page.

From there, you have a quick view of all the documents submitted by learners. Just click a document to download it to your computer so you can review it.

- 4. Click the **Edit** button in the upper-right corner of the screen.

## Filling out checklist activities

As discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, the Checklist activity is a type of module that can be added to a course.

The checklist activity is one of the assessment methodologies available in Captivate Prime. It allows organizations to evaluate the effectiveness of a course by assessing the change in learners' behavior after they have completed a course.

The idea is that instructors visit learners at their workplaces and fill out a form to evaluate how a given course *actually* translates into behavior change or increased productivity. Typically, this type of activity should be conducted a few days or weeks after the course has been delivered.

Let's now hit the road and visit your past learners at their workplace in order to fill out the checklist activities using the following steps:

- 1. Meet your former learner at his/her work location, pat him/her on the back, and take time to have a coffee together! While not a mandatory step, this type of interpersonal interaction is always very helpful and enjoyable!
- 2. When ready, log into your Prime account as an instructor.
- 3. From the instructor home page, click the **Submissions/Checklists** link located in the **Modules** section of the left sidebar.
- 4. Then, click the appropriate **Checklist** activity.
- 5. When on the **Checklist** page, click the **Evaluate** link corresponding to the learner you are currently visiting.
- 6. Answer the questions in the checklist and click the **Submit** button located in the lower-right corner of the dialog box. This action is illustrated in the following screenshot.
