3. Click the **Internal Catalog** you created in the previous section. This opens the **Internal Catalog** page where you can edit the **Catalog Name**, the **Cover Image**, and the **Description** of the catalog if needed.

4. Click the **Content** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.

5. Click the **Add Content** link located in the middle of the page or the **Add Content** button in the upper-right corner.

This takes you to a page where all of the courses available on your Prime account are listed. This page features a **Skills** filter, a **Sort By** drop-down menu on the left, and a **Search** field on the right. Use them to search for the courses you want to add to the currently opened catalog.

6. To add a course to the catalog, hover over the corresponding course card and click the **Add** link.

If the course you add to the catalog has prerequisites, these are automatically added to the catalog in addition to the selected course.

Use this technique to add a few courses of your choosing to the **Internal Catalog**.

7. Use the tabs at the top of the page to add the required **Learning Programs**, **Certifications**, and **Job Aids** to the catalog.

8. When done, click the **Save** button located in the upper-right corner of the page.
