Each learner can add their own areas of interest to the list using the following steps:

+   1. Click the **ADD INTEREST** link. This opens the **Add to my Areas of Interest** page, which lists all the skills available in your Prime account. +   2. Click a few skills to add them to the selection. You can also click a selected skill to remove it from the selection.
+   3. When ready, click the **Add** button to return to the previous page.

Remember that this influences the courses recommended by Captivate Prime on the learner home page, so next time you log in as *Learner 01*, you should see additional courses in the **Recommended based on your areas of interest** section of the home page.

Also remember that administrators can assign skills to learners, as discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*. Skills assigned to learners that way also appear on the **My Areas of Interest** page.

# Asking users to handpick areas of interests

With skills management and home page personalization being such important concepts in Captivate Prime, administrators can ask learners to handpick their areas of interest the first time they log in to the Prime account. Let's review this feature using the following steps:

+   1. Click the user icon in the upper-right corner of the screen and click the **Sign Out** button to log out as *Learner 01*.
+   2. Next, click the **Login** button and log back in as an administrator.
+   3. From the administrator home page, click the **Branding** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar. This takes you to the **Branding** page of the administrator account.
