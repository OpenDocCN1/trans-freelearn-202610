## Modify the Confidentiality Settings

Say you have enabled gamification on your Prime account and learners are competing against each other. It works so well that you do see a significant increase in learner engagements and in the number of learning events taking place on your account.

But not all learners are made equal, and employees are beginning to notice that their managers (or the company’s top executives) don’t have as many points as they do. So, when the said manager tries to motivate their team members to take courses, their credibility may be at stake!

Fortunately, the confidentiality settings of Captivate Prime allow you to hide the scores of specific learners from others. Let's review this feature hands-on by following these steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in to your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the **Settings** tab of the **Gamification** page, click the **Edit** link associated with the **Confidentiality Settings** option.
- 3. Use the **Find Learners by Name, Profile etc.** field to search for learners whose score you want to hide.
- In this example, search for the **All Managers** group to filter the list and display only the learners that are part of the selected group.
- 4. Select all the managers available on your Prime account using the checkboxes in front of their names.
- 5. When your screen looks as in the following screenshot, click the **Hide** button (identified by the arrow):

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_424_0.png)

Figure 11.11 – Hiding managers from gamification

With this option in place, the selected managers continue to take part in gamification. They are still awarded points whenever they complete training or acquire a new skill, but their score is no longer displayed to the other learners on the leaderboard.

You will now unhide the managers and return to the initial situation by following these steps:

- 6. From the **Settings** tab of the **Gamification** page, click the **Edit** link associated with the **Confidentiality Settings** option.
- 7. Switch to the **Confidential Learners** tab to reveal the list of confidential learners whose score is currently hidden from other learners.
- 8. Use the checkboxes to select all the available confidential learners.
- 9. Click the **Unhide** button in the lower-right corner of the page.

You are now back to the initial situation where all learners are part of a single gamification scope.

## Resetting gamification

John, a new employee, joins your organization in June 2021. This date also marks the time when John gains access to Captivate Prime LMS. John begins taking courses and quickly acquires a couple of certifications. John is a competitor and enjoys the gamification features of Captivate Prime. But John started earning points in June 2021! Most of the colleagues he competes against had been on the LMS longer, so they had a headstart in acquiring points, and that’s not fair!

This is one of those situations where it’s a good idea to reset gamification from time to time. In most organizations, gamification is reset at the beginning of each new year, to prevent older employees from accumulating so many points that new employees are discouraged from competing in the first place, which would defeat the very purpose of gamification.

Follow these steps to reset gamification in your Prime account:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in to your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the **Settings** tab of the **Gamification** page, click the **Reset Settings** link associated with the **Reset Gamification** option.
- 3. Take some time to review the available options. When ready, select the **Reset Learners Scores only** option.
- 4. Click the **Reset** button in the lower-right corner of the screen.
- 5. Read the **Resetting Gamification** warning message and click the **yes** button to reset the learner scores to 0!

In this section, you learned how to reset gamification in your Prime account. This concludes your review of the advanced gamification settings of Captivate Prime. Hopefully, you now have a much better understanding of what gamification is and how it is implemented in Captivate Prime LMS.
