But the mobile situation raises a whole new set of challenges:

+   - The screen of a mobile device is typically much smaller than the screen of a laptop or desktop computer. Therefore, the learning experience must be redesigned to fit the mobile situation.
+   - Interacting with fingers on a touchscreen is very different from using a mouse pointer on a desktop or laptop computer. This means that interactions must be redesigned for touch.
+   - The quality and stability of the network connection and the available bandwidth are not guaranteed on a mobile device. Users can be connected on a fast and reliable Wi-Fi network as well as on a slow and unpredictable 3G connection.
+   - How does your HR or IT department support mobile devices for learning?

To help overcome these challenges, the Adobe Captivate Prime team has developed a dedicated mobile app available for iOS and Android. It is even possible to start taking a course using a desktop or laptop computer and to continue the same course on a device using the mobile app and vice versa. The mobile app is available for smartphones and tablets alike. Use the Apple App Store or the Google Play Store to download and install the Captivate Prime app on your mobile device.

Note that, at the time of writing, the Captivate Prime mobile app is only available for the learner role. The other roles (administrator, instructor, manager, author, and integration admin) must use a traditional web browser to access Captivate Prime.

In this section, you will use the Captivate Prime mobile app to log in to your Prime account as Learner 03, review the learner experience provided by the mobile app, and take the Fundamentals of cybersecurity course using your mobile device.

# Connecting to Prime using the mobile app

First, you will use the Captivate Prime mobile app to log in to your Prime account using the following steps:

+   1. Use your mobile device (smartphone or tablet) to install the Adobe Captivate Prime application using either the Apple App Store (iOS) or the Google Play Store (Android).
+   2. Once installed, tap the Captivate Prime icon to execute the application.
+   3. Enter the email address of Learner 03 and click the Proceed button.
+   
+   If the email address is used on multiple Prime accounts, you must choose the account you want to connect to using the Associated Accounts page that appears on the screen.
