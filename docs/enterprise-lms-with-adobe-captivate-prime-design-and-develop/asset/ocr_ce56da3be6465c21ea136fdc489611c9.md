# Sharing files

*Learner 01* has received such good feedback from the instructor for the assignment that he/she submitted in the **Fundamentals of cybersecurity** course that he/she wants to share it with the other learners participating in the **Cybersecurity in the organization** discussion board.

This is just one of the many circumstances in which users may want to upload a document to one of the available discussion boards. In this section, you will review how that can be done by performing the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are still on the default **Social Learning** page of *Learner 01*.

As discussed in the previous sections, you can start a new post by clicking the **New Post** button available at the top of the **Social Learning** page. When using that technique, you must choose the discussion board where you want to post the new message. In this section, you will explore another technique by going to the chosen discussion board and then starting a new post from there.

+   2. Click the **Cybersecurity in the organization** link located at the top of the list of recent posts. You can also use the **Go to Board** link located further down the page.
