+   - You should have at least a dozen or so courses available in your Prime account (the default courses available in each new Prime account will do just fine).
+   - Optionally, administrator access to a second Prime account will allow you to further experiment with some of the features discussed in this chapter.

# Working With Multiple Catalogs

In Captivate Prime, a catalog is a collection of learning objects. So far, in this book, all the content available on your account is part of the default catalog that was automatically created along with your Prime account. This default catalog has the following characteristics:

+   - It cannot be deleted (but it can be disabled instead; more on that in the Enabling and disabling catalogs section of in this chapter).
+   - All the courses, learning programs, certifications, and job aids that you create on your Prime account are automatically included in the default catalog.
+   - By default, all the learners defined on your account (including internal and external learners) have access to the default catalog and, therefore, to all the content available on your Prime account.

This default situation is very simple to understand and administer, and it may well correspond to your needs. There is nothing wrong with using it in production. However, there are also many situations where not all learners should have access to the same set of courses:

+   - For example, courses on enterprise solutions used internally should only be available to your organization's employees, but not to external partners, vendors, or customers who have access to your Prime account.
+   - Courses on future products being developed within your company should only be accessible to the R&D department. Indeed, these courses could contain confidential information that is currently under embargo. This information must not leave the organization before the release of these new products.
