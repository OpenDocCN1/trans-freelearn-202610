From now on, new courses and learning programs you create on your Prime account will have L1 feedback enabled by default. It is important to keep in mind that this only affects *future* courses and learning programs. Existing courses and learning programs are not affected by this change.

> **Note**
> Like any other important change you make to such a workflow, it is important to communicate it properly to learners. After all, this additional feedback step is new to them as well. So, it is very important to tell learners that this feedback step is mandatory because many learners will ignore it if they have not been properly informed of the change.

It is also worth noting that L1 feedback is also available for learning programs. This will be discussed in more detail in a future section.

In the next section, let's review how you can customize the content of the L1 feedback form.

# Modifying the content of L1 and L3 feedback forms

When you experienced filling out the L1 feedback form as a learner in the *Providing L1 feedback as a learner* section earlier in this chapter, there were six predefined questions in addition to the custom questions you defined at the course instance level. In this section, you will learn how you can modify these predefined questions.

Note that this action is done at the account level, so the questions you define in this section are used by all the course instances on your Prime account.

Use the following steps to customize the content of the L1 feedback form:

+   1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Settings** link located at the end of the **Configure** section in the left sidebar.
+   3. Then, click the **Feedback** link located in the **Basics** section of the left sidebar.
+   4. Click the **Edit** link located in the upper-right corner of the screen.
+   5. Scroll down the page until you see the list of questions.
