# 3
Uploading Learning Content and Managing the Content Library

In the previous chapter, you took ownership of your Captivate Prime account by modifying its graphical aspect so that it looks just like any other system that’s used in your organization. This was an important first step, but your LMS still lacks two of its most critical ingredients: content and learners.

In this chapter, you will bring in the first of these two missing ingredients: the content. First, we will discuss where the content hosted in your Prime account comes from. You will discover that you have two options for adding content to your course catalog: you can subscribe to third-party, off-the-shelf content libraries, or you can upload your own custom course content to the platform.
