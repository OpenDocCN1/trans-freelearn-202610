
## Accessing L2 Feedback Data

L2 feedback refers to the second level of Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Learning Evaluation model. As a reminder, this is the *Learning* level, which is the level that involves grading and individual assessment in order to measure the acquisition of new skills or knowledge by learners. L2 feedback examines learners' performance on quizzes, as well as their results on their various assignments.

In Captivate Prime, L2 feedback refers to quiz scores achieved by learners as they take courses and learning programs. These come from all the graded activities included in courses, as well as from quizzes included in interactive course modules, whose results are reported to the LMS via the AICC, SCORM, or xAPI standards.

As such, L2 feedback is mostly automatic in Captivate Prime. There is nothing to do on your end to enable L2 feedback for a course or learning program and have Captivate Prime LMS collect this data. The only requirement for a course or learning program to have L2 feedback enabled is to contain at least one graded activity or quiz. Note that for some activities (such as file submissions and checklists), the grades must be manually entered into the system by an instructor, as discussed in *Chapter 8, Exploring the Instructor Role.*

> **AICC, SCORM, and xAPI**
> AICC, SCORM, and xAPI are the three standards used in the eLearning industry to transmit data between interactive course modules and the LMS. Captivate Prime supports all three standards, so Captivate Prime can host the content created by virtually any eLearning authoring tool. For more information on AICC, SCORM, and xAPI, refer to *Chapter 3, Uploading Learning Content and Managing the Content Library.*

When you created the *Fundamentals of cybersecurity* course back in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, you included a SCORM-compliant activity containing a quiz. This activity was built using the Adobe Captivate authoring tool but remember that Captivate Prime can host content created by *any* AICC-, SCORM-, or xAPI-compliant authoring tools.

You will now review how administrators and managers can access quiz data using the following steps:

- 1. Log in to your Captivate Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Hover over the **Fundamentals of cybersecurity** course card, or over the card of any course containing at least one graded activity.
- 4. Click the **View Course** link to access the course overview page.
- 5. Click the **L2 Quiz Score** link located in the **Reports** section of the left sidebar.
- 6. If the chosen course contains more than one instance, open the **Select Instance** drop-down menu to choose the course instance for which you want to review the data.

> **Note**
> When it comes to AICC, SCORM, or xAPI modules, keep in mind that Captivate Prime is only one of two parties involved, the other party being each individual module that can be generated by a wide variety of authoring tools. For instance, the detailed data displayed in the **By Question** tab is only available if the corresponding interaction data is transmitted by the individual course modules to the LMS. This has to be defined in the authoring tools used to generate the AICC, SCORM, or xAPI packages. Some authoring tools support this functionality, while others do not. Also, this detailed reporting may be enabled for some course modules and not for others. In other words, don't be too quick to blame Captivate Prime for the lack of data and make sure that reporting is properly configured in whatever authoring tools are used to build the course modules.

Take some time to review the available data and to relate what you see on the screen with the experience you've been through while taking the course as a learner in *Chapter 7, Reviewing the Learner Experience.*

Also, you might be puzzled by the lack of data available for the *essay* or *checklist* activities. Remember that these activities have been reviewed by an instructor in *Chapter 8, Exploring the Instructor Role.* But the instructor only had to *approve* or *reject* these activities without actually grading them, which explains why no L2 data is available for these activities. For these activities to be graded, you must select the **Enable scoring for this Module** checkbox when creating the course while logged in as an author. ## Working with ratings

With the massive development of e-commerce in recent years, we have become accustomed to purchasing items online using an ever-increasing number of e-commerce sites and services. Virtually all of these e-commerce sites have at least one thing in common: a rating and review system. In fact, these ratings and reviews are an essential part of the process of converting a mere interest into an actual purchase. Indeed, it is perhaps these comments and reviews that convinced you to buy the very book you are reading!

What holds true on Amazon, Booking.com, Netflix, and other such sites and services also holds true in the Captivate Prime course catalog. This is why learners have the ability to rate the courses they are taking using a five-star rating scale.

> **New feature**
> Star rating is a new feature introduced with the August 2021 update to Captivate Prime. If you are working on an older account (created before mid-August 2021), it is possible that star rating is completely disabled or replaced by the old course effectiveness score. The course effectiveness score will be discussed later in this section.

By default, the star rating feature is enabled on any new Prime account. Let's check this out using the following steps:

- 1. Log in to your Captivate Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the Settings link located in the Configure section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Then, click the General link located in the Basics section of the left sidebar.
- 4. Scroll down the page until you see the Show Ratings section.

By default, the Show Ratings option should be enabled and set to the Star rating mode, as shown in the following screenshot:
