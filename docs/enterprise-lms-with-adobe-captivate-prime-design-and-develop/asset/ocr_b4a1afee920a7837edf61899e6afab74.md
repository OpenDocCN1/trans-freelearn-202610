The first report available on the Training Content dashboard is the Trainings Report illustrated in the following screenshot.

![](bbox=[0.1, 0.15, 0.85, 0.5])

Figure 10.4 – The Trainings Report

This report shows the evolution of the number of trainings available on your platform in the last 12 months on a month-to-month basis.

Note that this particular graph has two vertical axes (also called y axes). The vertical axis on the left is used for the Total trainings available and generates the blue line of the graph. The vertical axis on the right is used for the Trainings newly added and generates the green line of the graph. It is possible that these two axes do not use the same scale. For example, in the preceding screenshot, the total trainings axis on the left goes from 0 to 16, while the newly added axis on the right goes from 0 to 14, which explains why the two lines do not reach the same height. The idea behind this report is to allow administrators and managers to see the behavior of learners from the standpoint of new training versus old training, and what's being consumed. Perhaps new content is more popular?

Also note the Export button in the upper-right corner of the graph, which allows you to generate a CSV file of the represented data.
