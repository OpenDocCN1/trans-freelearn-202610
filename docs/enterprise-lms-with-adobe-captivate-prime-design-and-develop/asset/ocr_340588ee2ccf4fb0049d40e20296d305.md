In this section, you reviewed the default learner enrollment experience. Remember that this is the experience that Captivate Prime is primarily designed for, as this self-service learning approach is at the core of the thinking that led to the creation of Captivate Prime in the first place.

That being said, there are other ways users can end up taking courses in Captivate Prime. In the next section, you will learn how managers and administrators can enroll learners in courses.

# Enrolling users for courses

Allowing users to self-enroll in courses is certainly a great idea, based on the premise that many learners will take advantage of this to *actually* take courses and continually upgrade their skills.

Unfortunately, we all know that even though some users enjoy self-training and enrolling in courses, this principle is sometimes more of a utopia than a reality. Furthermore, there is no guarantee that the courses learners choose to enroll on will magically match the needs of the organization.

That's why administrators and managers are also able to enroll learners on courses. This is what you will review in this section.

# Enrollment by Administrators

The first role that can enroll learners in courses is the *administrator* role. In this section, you will log in as the administrator and enroll *Learner 01* on the course you created in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*. You will use the same course instance you enrolled *Learner 03* on in the previous section. Let's get started:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as an Administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. Hover over the bottom section of the course card representing the course you created in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*.
+   4. Click the **Enroll Learners** option to open the **Enroll Learners** dialog.
+   5. Use the **Select Instance** dropdown to select the course instance you want to enroll users on.
