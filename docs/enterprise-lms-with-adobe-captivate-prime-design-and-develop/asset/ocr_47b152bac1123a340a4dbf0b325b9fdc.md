In addition to skills, the artificial intelligence also generates recommendations based on peer activity. That is where the number of learners using your Prime account needs to be taken into account. Remember that Captivate Prime is an enterprise LMS designed for large deployments of several thousand users. In larger organizations, all of these users have very different roles and functions within the company. They have so many different expectations and interests that it doesn't make much sense to consider them all as a single peer group. That is why Captivate Prime allows you to choose five user characteristics that can then be used to split your users into various peer groups, which allows the artificial intelligence to generate more accurate recommendations based on peers' activity.

Finally, organizations also have a say in what courses are recommended to their employees. Therefore, administrators can manually assign skills to learners and manually recommend courses to specific target audiences.

Remember that these powerful features rely on a gigantic and constant flow of data to deliver their promise, so give your LMS enough time to grow before you take full advantage of this technology on your own Prime account. This is especially true for small and mid-sized organizations using Captivate Prime, as these accounts generate less data than larger deployments.

So far, in this book, you have worked with the default training catalog that is available in any Prime account. This catalog is visible to all internal and external users. So, currently, all users have access to the same courses, training programs, certifications, and job aids. In the next chapter, you will create additional course catalogs and learn how to make them available to certain learners only. This will allow you to tailor your course offerings to the different user groups that are available in your Prime account.
