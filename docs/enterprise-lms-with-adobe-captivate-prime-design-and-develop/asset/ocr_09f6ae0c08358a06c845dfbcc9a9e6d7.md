8. Finally, you can modify the body of the message, including the message title in the banner.

When modifying the content of such a message, there are two types of text elements you can use:

+   - **Static text** is the text you type using your keyboard. These portions of the text are always the same for each message based on this template, regardless of the learner or course involved.
+   - **Dynamic text** is the pieces of the text that are different each time a message based on this template is sent. For example, the learner's name or the course name are pieces of dynamic text.

To enter pieces of dynamic text, you must use the icons that appear in the upper-right corner of the text you are editing.
