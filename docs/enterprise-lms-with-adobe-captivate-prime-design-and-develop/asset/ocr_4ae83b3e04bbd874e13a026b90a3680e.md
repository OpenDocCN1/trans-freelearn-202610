Now that the link is available on the clipboard, your next task is to configure your masthead announcement.

+   5. Click the **Announcements** link, located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar, to return to the main **Announcements** page of the administrator.
+   6. Click the **Add** button located in the top-right corner of the screen to open the **Create Announcement** dialog.
+   7. At the top of the **Create Announcement** dialog, open the **Type** drop-down menu and choose **As Masthead**.
+   8. For an announcement of the *masthead* type, you must supply either an image or a video file in the **Attachment** field.

In this example, you can use the `announcements/masthead.jpg` file available in the download associated with this book.

Notice the **Language** drop-down menu in the top-right corner of the **Attachment** section, which allows you to upload a different image for each language supported by your platform.

> **Preferred image sizes**
> The preferred size for a masthead image or video is 1,280 x 360 pixels.
> You can review the recommended size of images, as well as other system requirements, on the following page of the official Captivate Prime user guide:
> `https://helpx.adobe.com/captivate-prime/system-requirements.html`.
