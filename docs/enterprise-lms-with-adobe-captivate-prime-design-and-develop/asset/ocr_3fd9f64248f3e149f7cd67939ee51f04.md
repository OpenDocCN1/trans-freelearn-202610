In this example, you uploaded a Microsoft Word document with a .docx file extension, but Captivate Prime supports a wide range of video, audio, document, and image files. Refer to the official Captivate Prime documentation for an up-to-date list of supported contents and formats: https://helpx.adobe.com/captivate-prime/user-guide.html/captivate-prime/learners/feature-summary/social-learning-web-user.ug.html#Supportedcontentformats.

The ability to share such a wide variety of files with the other learners participating in a discussion board is a very powerful and engaging way of sharing knowledge. For example, the instructor can use it to drive learners to the social learning tools of Captivate Prime by posting training slides, course handouts, and more.
