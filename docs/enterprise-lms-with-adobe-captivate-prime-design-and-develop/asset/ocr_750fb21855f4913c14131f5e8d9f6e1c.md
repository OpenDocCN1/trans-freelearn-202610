# Touring the Default Learner Experience

The learner experience is probably the most carefully crafted area of Captivate Prime. The reason for this is that only a few users will ever access one of the administrative areas of your Prime account, while most users accessing your account are learners. That is why, when adding new features, the Prime team will always evaluate them based on how they will enhance the learner experience. This is just another way of putting the learner at the heart of the platform.

In this section, you will log in to your Prime account as Learner 01. This is one of the internal learners you created in Chapter 5, Managing Users. In the previous chapter, while you were logged in as an administrator, you enrolled this learner in the course you created in Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses.

Use the following steps to log in as Learner 01 and tour the default learner experience:

+   1. Log in to your Prime account as Learner 01. Since you did not assign a specific role to this user, you should be automatically redirected to the learner experience.

+   2. Click the notification icon located in the upper-right corner of the screen. As shown in the following screenshot, you should see a notification telling you that an administrator has enrolled you in a course:
