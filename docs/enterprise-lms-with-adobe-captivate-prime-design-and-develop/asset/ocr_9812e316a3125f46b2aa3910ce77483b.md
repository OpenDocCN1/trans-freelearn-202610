The gamification feature included in Captivate Prime allows you to award points to learners when certain events occur. These points are visible in the learner experience on the **Leaderboard** page, where learners can review their own score and compare it to the score of their colleagues. The goal is to promote a learning culture within the organization by creating positive competition among learners.

Also keep in mind that the features discussed in this chapter are implemented at the LMS level. In other words, they complement the instructional design and authoring techniques used by authors who build individual course modules using authoring tools such as Adobe Captivate. A successful learning experience relies on many factors, the LMS being only one of them.

But making learning fun is not the only way to increase learning engagement and efficiency. Numerous research has also shown that people learn better when they learn *together*. So, in the next chapter, you'll review the social learning features of Adobe Captivate Prime. These will help you make the learner experience in your Prime account even more appealing.
