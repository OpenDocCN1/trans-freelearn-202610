
## 4 Creating Skills and Courses

In the previous chapter, you uploaded custom content modules to the Content Library of your Prime account. But this content is not available to learners yet. Remember that content modules are only the fundamental building blocks used to create Courses.

Courses are what learners enroll in. So, for the content that we uploaded in the previous chapter to start *actually* being useful, you need to start building courses with this content. This is what you will do in this chapter.

But before creating courses, let's think about why learners would take courses in the first place and why organizations would put so much time, effort, and money into making courses available to their workforce. The answer to these two questions can be found in one word: **Skills**.

Learners take courses because it allows them to acquire new skills or update existing skills. As for organizations, they know that, in a competitive environment, skilled employees are one of the keys to success and profitability. Therefore, for both learners and organizations, it is critical to align courses to skills. This allows learners to choose the courses they want to take. It also helps organizations track the skills acquired by each of their employees to ensure that their workforce stays up to date, and that the skills available in the organization remain relevant to an ever-changing marketplace. Skills may also refer to specific compliance or mandatory training in organizations (such as government entities), where taking courses is considered a function of the position.

In this chapter, you will take your first look at Captivate Prime's Skills Manager by creating a new custom skill. Then, you will assemble content modules into courses and review the wide range of options available for creating various types of courses.

By the end of this chapter, you will have a custom course that's ready for enrollment, and your Prime account will be ready to welcome your first learners. To achieve this goal, this chapter will cover the following topics:

- Creating custom skills with Skills Manager
- Creating Courses
- Adding different types of modules and content to courses
- Linking courses to skills
- Updating, retiring, and deleting courses

These topics make for a chapter full of great features and exciting discoveries

### Technical requirements

In order to perform the manipulations and exercises proposed in this chapter, you need to meet the following technical requirements:

- You need Administrator and Author access to a working Captivate Prime account (which can be a free trial account).
- You need to have some content modules available in your Content Library.

Just keep in mind that the provided instructions are based on the exercises from the previous chapters, so feel free to adapt them to your situation.

### Managing skills

Achieving skills is the expected outcome of any (online or live) learning activity.
Acquiring new skills is the primary reason why learners take courses in the first place. It is also the reason why organizations decide to invest time and money in providing training material to their employees.

But organizations do not provide these courses just for the sake of letting people acquire new skills. For them, it is critical to ensure that the skills available in the organization match both the business goals of the organization and the requirements of an ever-changing marketplace.

As the enterprise LMS of choice, Captivate Prime is equipped with a full-featured skills manager. The primary purpose of this feature is to provide organizations with a tool to help them align learning with business goals.

Let's look at an example to understand the main idea behind the skills manager.

With the sudden increase of employees working from home, an organization has observed a significant rise in cyber attacks and other malicious activities that threaten the security of the organization's IT infrastructure. After analysis, it has been determined that this increase is partially due to the lack of employee awareness about cybersecurity, which results in many best practices not being followed. Because a single successful attack (such as a ransomware attack) could potentially be disastrous for the organization's image, as well as for the costs such an attack could incur, it has been decided to upskill all employees in cybersecurity. It was also decided that members of the IT department should undergo additional training so that they can make the IT infrastructure more secure and adapted to remote work.

One way to implement this strategy with Captivate Prime is to define a custom *Cybersecurity* skill using the Skills Manager. This new skill shall have the following properties:

- You will define two levels for this skill. Level 1 represents basic cybersecurity awareness and best practice training, while Level 2 represents more advanced technical knowledge on the topic.
- You will assign courses to these two levels of the *Cybersecurity* skill. Employees taking these courses will receive credits and when they reach a certain number of credits, they will be awarded the Level 1 or Level 2 Cybersecurity skill. These can be courses that are already available in your learning catalog and/or courses that you have developed for the occasion.
- Finally, you will assign the *Cybersecurity – Level 1* skill to every employee and the *Cybersecurity – Level 2* skill to the members of the IT department.

This approach allows you to define exactly what course material falls into Cybersecurity – Level 1 and Cybersecurity – Level 2. It also allows you to track each learner's progress in acquiring the skill. This information allows you to document the effectiveness of the learning (did IT security actually increased after a given number of employees have acquired the Cybersecurity skill?), send reminders to the learners that are yet to acquire the skill, and more.

### Creating a custom skill

Now that you have a high-level understanding of what skills are and what they allow you to do in the Prime LMS, it is time to log in as an administrator and define the new Cybersecurity skill by performing the following steps:

- 1. Log in to your Captivate Prime account as an Administrator and make sure you are on the Administrator home page.
- 2. Click the skills button located in the Manage section of the left sidebar.

This gives you access to the list of existing skills. Note that, by default, your Captivate Prime trial account comes with a handful of skills already defined, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_128_0.png)

Figure 4.1 – A handful of skills are available in your Prime trial account

You will now add one more skill to the list by performing the following steps:

- 3. Click the **Add** button located in the top-right corner of the screen. This opens the **Add Skill** form.
- 4. Type the name of the new skill in the **Skill Name** field (`Cybersecurity`, in this example). Note that **Skill Name** is required.
- 5. As a best practice, type a short description into the **Description** field.

Now, turn your attention to the bottom of the form. This is where you can define up to three levels for each skill.

- 6. Increase the number of credits needed to reach level 1 to 50 credits.
- 7. Click the + Add link located on the right-hand side of the **Levels** heading to add a second level for this skill.
- 8. Increase the number of credits needed to reach level 2 to 50 credits.
- 9. Leave all the other fields (**Badges**, **Skill Domain**, and so on) at their default values.
- 10. When done, click the **Save** button located in the bottom-right corner of the **Add Skill** form.

After clicking the **Save** button, Captivate Prime will display a short message, saying that the skill has been successfully created. This success can be confirmed by the presence of the new **Cybersecurity** skill in the list, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_129_0.png)

Figure 4.2 – The new Cybersecurity scale has been added to the list of available skills

With the current settings, learners will be awarded the *Cybersecurity – Level 1* skill when they accumulate 50 credits by taking courses linked to that particular skill and level. They will be awarded *Cybersecurity – Level 2* when they accumulate 50 additional credits taking courses linked to level 2 of the Cybersecurity skill. You will learn how to link courses to skills in the *Linking courses to skills* section, later in this chapter.
