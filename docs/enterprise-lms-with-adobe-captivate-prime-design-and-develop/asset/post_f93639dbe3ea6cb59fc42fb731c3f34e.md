
> **Caution**
> We insist on the fact that enabling discussion by default on your Prime account does affect all the courses, *even those that were available before enabling the Discussion Board checkbox*. Make sure that everyone is made aware of the change so that appropriate action can be taken in a timely fashion. To avoid any confusion, this setting is probably something you want to turn on or leave off when you first implement Captivate Prime in your organization.

At the individual course level, if the discussion board is not explicitly enabled for a specific course (see the previous section), the account-wide setting is applied. Since the account-wide setting has been enabled, it is no longer possible to disable the discussion board at the course level. This is probably why most organizations want to keep the account-wide discussion board setting disabled: to allow administrators to enable discussion from course to course based on the wishes of the instructors and administrators in each course.

This concludes your exploration of course discussions in Captivate Prime. This feature attempts to mimic the informal conversations that occur naturally alongside any face-to-face training. It is very simple to implement and use, but its capabilities are limited. Let's quickly recap on what you have learned in this section:

- Course discussion is disabled by default at the account level, but administrators can override this general account setting and enable discussions on a course-to-course basis.
- When course discussion is enabled for a course, any enrolled learner can post messages. However, there is no built-in mechanism to answer someone else's post or to like/dislike a message.
- Users can delete their own messages, but not the messages that have been posted by other users. Administrators can delete any posted message, even if they are not enrolled in the course.
- When you enable **Discussion Board** at the account level, this immediately enables the discussion board of every course currently available on your Prime account, with no way of disabling it on a course-to-course basis.

In the next section, you will review the Social Learning engine of Captivate Prime, which will allow you to activate a social media-like platform within your Captivate Prime account.

## Working with social learning

In addition to the course discussion boards discussed in the previous section, Captivate Prime also contains a full-fledged social learning engine. When activated, it provides an experience that bears many similarities with the most popular social networks:

- Learners can follow each other so that they get notifications when someone they follow publishes something.
- Social learning posts are published on **boards**. The concept of *board* in Captivate Prime is very similar to the concept of *wall* or *story* used by most mainstream social networks.
- Users can upvote (like) or downvote (dislike) any content posted on the social learning boards of Captivate Prime.
- In addition to text-based posts, users can also publish multimedia content (such as audio files, videos, and images), files of different types, and URLs.

One of the main differences between the course discussion boards we covered in the previous section and the social learning boards you'll soon discover is that the latter is skill-related rather than course-related. This means that anyone interested in a skill is welcome to join the discussion boards related to that particular skill, regardless of which courses that person is enrolled in, and even if the learner is not enrolled in any course related to that skill. This aspect of social learning is perfectly in line with the emphasis Captivate Prime puts on skills.

> **Skills**
> Skills were discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*. Please refer to that chapter for more information about creating and managing skills in Captivate Prime.

Organizations can leverage Captivate Prime's social learning feature to encourage discussion among learners, allow learners to upload their own training materials (increasing the amount of knowledge available in the Prime account without any additional expense), evaluate learner response to current course offerings, discuss ideas for future courses, and so on. Social networks have made this type of ongoing conversation a part of everyday life and by seeing the number of organizations active on these networks, the value of such conversations is undeniable. So why not try it out on your corporate LMS and let a truly collaborative learning culture flourish in your organization?

> **Note**
> The social learning feature of Captivate Prime is powered by Adobe Social, a proven and mature product that is now part of Adobe Marketing Cloud.

## Enabling Social Learning

The first decision you must make as an administrator is whether to enable or disable social learning in your Prime account. Like any social network, Captivate Prime's social learning feature involves direct interactions between individual learners. This means that learners have access to each other's profiles, which raises privacy concerns for many organizations. This is especially true if your organization uses Captivate Prime to deliver training to mostly external users.

> **Internal and external users**
> The differences between internal and external users were discussed in *Chapter 5, Managing Users.* In a nutshell, internal users are employees of your organization, while external users are employees of other organizations (such as customers, suppliers, partners, franchisees, and so on).

Some organizations have rules about using social media during business hours. Some Prime customers decide to not use social learning because it might send a conflicting message to their staff.
