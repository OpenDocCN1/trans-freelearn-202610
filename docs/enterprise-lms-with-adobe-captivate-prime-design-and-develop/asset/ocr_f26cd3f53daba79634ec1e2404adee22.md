# Activating, using, and managing course discussions for a single course

By default, course discussions are disabled across your entire Prime account. In other words, none of the courses currently available on your account have discussions enabled. In this section, you will override this general account setting and enable discussions for a single course.

First, you will quickly review the current learner experience by performing the following steps:

+   1. Use the browser window where you are logged in as *Learner 01*.
+   2. From the learner home page, click the **My Learning List** link or the **My Learning** icon on the left sidebar to access the **My Learning** page.
+   3. Click any of the course cards available on the **My Learning** page to open the overview page for that course.

Remember that the **My Learning** page of a learner lists all the courses the logged-in learner is enrolled in.

In this example, I have chosen the **Fundamentals of Cybersecurity** course, which we created back in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*.
