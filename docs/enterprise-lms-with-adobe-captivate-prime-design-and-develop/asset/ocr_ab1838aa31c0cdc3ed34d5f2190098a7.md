In this section, you learned how to upload an interactive course module to your Content Library directly from the Adobe Captivate authoring tool. There are some additional benefits in using Adobe Captivate together with Adobe Captivate Prime:

+   - The Adobe Captivate Table of Contents integrates nicely with the Prime **Fluidic Player** (The Fluidic Player is the component of Prime that delivers the course. It will be discussed in more detail in *Chapter 7, Reviewing the Learner Experience*).
+   - Captivate Prime honors Captivate's controls for showing/hiding the Table of Contents and the Playbar.
+   - Closed Captions created in Adobe Captivate integrate with the Closed Caption controls of Captivate Prime.
+   - The ability to configure the completion and success criteria within Captivate Prime without republishing the content in Captivate.

Besides these additional benefits, remember that Captivate Prime can host content created by *any* tool and that the content generated by Adobe Captivate can be uploaded to *any* LMS.
