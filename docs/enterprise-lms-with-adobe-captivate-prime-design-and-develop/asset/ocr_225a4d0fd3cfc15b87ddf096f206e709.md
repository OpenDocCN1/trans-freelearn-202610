# Creating a new board

Since you've just enabled social learning on your Prime account, there is no board available for you to post your first message yet. Therefore, you must create a new board before you can post a message. Follow these steps to do so:

+   1. Use the browser window where you are logged in as Learner 01.
+   2. From the learner home page, click the Social Learning icon of the left sidebar to open the Social Learning page.

As shown in the following screenshot, the Social Learning page should currently be empty:
