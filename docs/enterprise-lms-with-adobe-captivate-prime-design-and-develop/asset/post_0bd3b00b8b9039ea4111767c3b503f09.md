
## Enabling and configuring Gamification

Remember that gamification is defined as *the use of game design elements in non-game contexts*. In the previous section, we discussed **badges**, which are one of the most popular game design elements used in eLearning. In this section, we will discuss the use of **points**, which is another widely used game design element in online learning. The idea is quite simple: Captivate Prime awards points when various events occur, such as completing a specific number of courses per month, completing self-enrolled courses, or achieving a skill.

These points are visible on the leaderboard. Remember that the leaderboard is one of the pages available to learners. On this page, learners can review their own score and compare it to the scores of their colleagues. The goal is to promote positive competition among colleagues, which, in turn, increases learning and engagement.

> **Avoid confusion**
> 
> Many Prime users are not familiar with the term *gamification* and refer to these features as *the leaderboard*. Since communication is an important part of implementing these features, be sure to use terms that everyone understands when communicating about these gamification features.

First, let's inspect the events that award points using the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in to your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **gamification** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. At the top of the **Gamification** page, notice the different levels available. When a learner reaches a certain point threshold, they are awarded the corresponding level.
- 4. Click the **Edit** link located in the upper-right corner of the **Levels** section.

As shown in the following screenshot, this allows you to modify the number of points needed to acquire each level. Also notice the **Revert to Original** link, which allows you to go back to the original default values:

## Configuring Advanced Gamification Options

In the previous section, you discovered the basic configuration options for gamification in Captivate Prime. In this section, you will dig a little deeper into the feature and discover the advanced gamification options. Use the following steps to access these options:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in to your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the gamification link located in the Configure section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Click the Settings tab located just below the Levels section, as shown in the following screenshot:

### Enabling and disabling gamification

By default, gamification is enabled for internal users only on any new Prime account. This is what most organizations want. With this default, there is nothing for administrators to do to enable gamification for internal users.

That being said, this default is not adequate in all situations:

- Gamification raises concerns about privacy as learners can see each other's scores on the leaderboard. This is the main reason why some organizations want to turn off gamification altogether.
- If your organization trains a lot of external users, you may want to turn gamification on for external users as well. Remember that gamification is turned off for external users by default.

These are some of the reasons why you may want to enable or disable gamification on your Prime account. Let's review how this can be done using the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in to your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the Settings tab of the Gamification page, click the Edit link associated with the Gamification Feature option.
- 3. As shown in the following screenshot, gamification is enabled for internal learners and disabled for external learners by default:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_421_0.png)

Figure 11.9 – Enabling and disabling gamification for internal and external learners

- 4. Make sure you don't make any changes to the current settings and click the Cancel button in the lower-right corner of the Gamification Feature section.

In this section, you learned how to enable or disable gamification for internal learners and external learners. Remember that, by default, gamification is enabled for internal learners and disabled for external learners.

### Modifying the Gamification Scope

When gamification is enabled, all learners compete against each other by default. This is great when you have a small number of learners (up to a few hundred), but for larger organizations with several thousand learners, this default situation may not be optimal:

- You may have some profiles (job titles) for whom training is a critical part of their job description, while other profiles have fewer trainings to take in order to stay relevant and compliant. Does it really make sense to have these very different profiles compete against each other in gamification?
- Multinational companies operating in different countries need to take cultural variations into account. In some cultures, gamification is taken very seriously, while in other cultures it is not as much of a concern. In this case, it makes sense to group people by location and let them compete only against other individuals in the same group.
- Some special profiles, such as top-level executives, should be removed from gamification as they probably have no interest in competing against their own employees for points in the corporate LMS.

These are some of the reasons why you may want to restrict the scope of gamification, which can be done using the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in to your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the Settings tab of the Gamification page, click the Edit link associated with the Scope Settings option.
- 3. Select the Limit Gamification to certain Learners and Learner groups checkbox.
- 4. Open the User characteristic drop-down menu and review the available options. In this example, choose the profile option.
  Remember that in Captivate Prime, the profile of a learner is a synonym of their job title or department.
- 5. Open the Value dropdown and inspect the available options. In this example, deselect the Profile (ceo) option to remove users with this profile from gamification.
