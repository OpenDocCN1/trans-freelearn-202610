## Creating and following discussion boards

Just like any social network, the social learning feature of Captivate Prime is all about allowing users to discuss and share content.

In Captivate Prime, content posted by users using the social learning feature is organized into **discussion boards**. Captivate Prime **boards** are similar to the *walls* or *stories* that are found in mainstream social networks.

What makes Captivate Prime boards particularly interesting from an instructional design perspective is that they are connected to skills. As discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, a single skill can be used in several different courses. Since boards are related to skills, this means that you can find learners enrolled in (or interested in) many different courses on the same board, and at different proficiency levels in that skill. In fact, any learner interested in a given skill is welcome to join a board on that skill, even if he/she has not enrolled in a course related to that skill yet.

To experiment with these features, you will, once again, log in twice to your Prime account. But this time, you will log in as two different internal users. Perform the following steps:

- 1. Open a browser window and log in to your Prime account as *Learner 01*.
- 2. Open a *private* browser window (or another browser altogether) and log in to the same Prime account as *Learner 02*.

*Learner 01* and *Learner 02* are two internal learners that you created in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*. Since they are both internal users, they both have access to the **Social Learning** page of the Captivate Prime learner experience.
