# Summary

In this chapter, you have reviewed the instructor role. The primary task of an instructor is to deliver the (face-to-face or virtual) classroom activities. Remember that, unlike the *asynchronous* self-paced modules (such as video files, PowerPoint presentations, PDF documents, SCORM activities, and so on), classroom activities are time-bound *synchronous* activities that require learners and instructors to be together either in an actual physical location or online.

This implies a whole new level of complexity and logistical constraints such as scheduling courses, managing the waitlist, arranging the venue, sending practical details to learners in a timely fashion, sending reminders before the session, ensuring that everyone has the required video conferencing software installed, reporting attendance to the LMS, and so on. Although Captivate Prime cannot accomplish all of these tasks on its own, it provides instructors with a multitude of features that greatly assist in managing the logistical, administrative, and pedagogical aspects of the instructor-led synchronous activities included in your courses.

Instructors are also responsible for reviewing the material submitted by learners when using the file submission activity and for completing the checklist activities sometime after the course has been delivered.

Thanks to these tools, you can use Captivate Prime to deliver all kinds of training, including courses that are entirely composed of self-paced asynchronous modules, courses that are entirely delivered by an instructor (either live or online), and courses that are composed of both synchronous and asynchronous activities. The latter type is particularly powerful because it combines the advantages of both synchronous and asynchronous techniques. This blended learning format is very popular among learners, instructors, and organizations alike.

Your efforts to implement Captivate Prime LMS, publish learning content, create courses, and deliver them online are starting to pay off. Your Captivate Prime account has come to life as learners and instructors are now able to interact in many ways.

But there is still an essential missing piece to go full circle and have a solution that can truly be considered as an enterprise solution. All these learning activities and interactions are starting to generate a lot of data. In addition, organizations want to ensure that learners are satisfied with the learning experience they provide and that their investment in training is meaningful and effective.

In the next two chapters, you will explore how Captivate Prime can collect feedback from learners and managers and how organizations can leverage all this information to generate meaningful reports.
