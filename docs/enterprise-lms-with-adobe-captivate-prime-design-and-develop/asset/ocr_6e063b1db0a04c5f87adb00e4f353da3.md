You will now add one more skill to the list by performing the following steps:

+   3. Click the **Add** button located in the top-right corner of the screen. This opens the **Add Skill** form.
+   4. Type the name of the new skill in the **Skill Name** field (`Cybersecurity`, in this example). Note that **Skill Name** is required.
+   5. As a best practice, type a short description into the **Description** field.

Now, turn your attention to the bottom of the form. This is where you can define up to three levels for each skill.

+   6. Increase the number of credits needed to reach level 1 to 50 credits.
+   7. Click the + Add link located on the right-hand side of the **Levels** heading to add a second level for this skill.
+   8. Increase the number of credits needed to reach level 2 to 50 credits.
+   9. Leave all the other fields (**Badges**, **Skill Domain**, and so on) at their default values.
+   10. When done, click the **Save** button located in the bottom-right corner of the **Add Skill** form.

After clicking the **Save** button, Captivate Prime will display a short message, saying that the skill has been successfully created. This success can be confirmed by the presence of the new **Cybersecurity** skill in the list, as shown in the following screenshot:
