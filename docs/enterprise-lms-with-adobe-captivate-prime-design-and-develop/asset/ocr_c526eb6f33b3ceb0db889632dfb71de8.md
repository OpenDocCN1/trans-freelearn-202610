At this point, your Prime account should only contain autogenerated groups. Taking a look at the list of autogenerated groups, you should see the following:

+   - Some groups, such as **All Admins**, **All Authors**, **All Learners**, and so on are based on the user role within the Prime account.
+   - The **All External Users** group lists all the external users, regardless of the external registration profile that's used to register to your Prime account. In addition to that, other groups are automatically generated for each of the external registration profiles defined on your account.
+   - Groups such as **Manager 01's Team** are automatically generated based on the user hierarchy that the **Manager Email** field of the user profile defines.
+   - One group is created for each profile. Remember that **Profile** is another field that's present in the user creation form. Most of the time, **Profile** corresponds to the individual's job title or department within the organization. This type of autogenerated group is very useful as it more or less represents the way your organization is organized.
+   - By default, groups are automatically generated for each of the custom fields that you define on the **Active Field** page. That's why you should find groups based on **Location**, **Department**, and **Hobbies** of the users. These are the three custom fields that you have created in the *Customizing the user form* section, earlier in this chapter.
