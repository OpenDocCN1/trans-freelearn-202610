# Other visual customizations

Adobe Captivate Prime allows you to customize other graphical aspects of the platform, such as custom progress icons, custom mouse pointer images, custom fonts, and so on. Unfortunately, at the time of this writing, these additional customizations cannot be done within the admin interface.

If you need to make these changes, you need to make contact with the Captivate Prime support team.

# Customizing the learner's experience

Only a small fraction of your users will ever need to access the administrative area of your Prime account. The bulk of your account users will be learners. These learners will log in to your account in order to browse the course catalogs, enroll themselves into courses, take courses, interact with other learners, and so on.
