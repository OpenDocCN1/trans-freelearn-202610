## Creating Masthead Announcements

The Masthead is the big rectangular area that spans across the top of the learner experience. This is the area where you can currently see the message **Let's learn something new today** when logged in as a learner:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_555_0.png)

Figure 15.5 – The default Masthead of Adobe Captivate Prime

This is, of course, one of the preferred areas to display all kinds of announcements to learners. Typically, you'll use the Masthead to promote new courses, learning programs, certifications, or catalogs, but there's nothing to stop you from using it for other purposes, such as promoting a corporate event, announcing the deadline for company-wide training, or announcing the learner who has accumulated the most gamification points! In addition to its prominent position in the user interface, the Masthead is unique in that it can be used as a call-to-action that redirects the learner to another web page.

In this exercise, you will use the masthead to announce the immediate availability of the internal catalog you created in *Chapter 14, Working with Catalogs and Peer Accounts*, and to provide your internal users with a direct link to this new catalog. To do so, your first task is to grab the link to the internal catalog by following these steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Catalogs** link available in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Hover your mouse over the internal catalog you created in the previous chapter.
- 4. Click the *chain* icon that appears on the screen to copy the direct link to the corresponding catalog to the clipboard.

**Note**
If **Internal Catalog** is not available in your Prime account, choose any of the available catalogs.

Now that the link is available on the clipboard, your next task is to configure your masthead announcement.

- 5. Click the **Announcements** link, located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar, to return to the main **Announcements** page of the administrator.
- 6. Click the **Add** button located in the top-right corner of the screen to open the **Create Announcement** dialog.
- 7. At the top of the **Create Announcement** dialog, open the **Type** drop-down menu and choose **As Masthead**.
- 8. For an announcement of the *masthead* type, you must supply either an image or a video file in the **Attachment** field.

In this example, you can use the `announcements/masthead.jpg` file available in the download associated with this book.

Notice the **Language** drop-down menu in the top-right corner of the **Attachment** section, which allows you to upload a different image for each language supported by your platform.

> **Preferred image sizes**
> The preferred size for a masthead image or video is 1,280 x 360 pixels.
> You can review the recommended size of images, as well as other system requirements, on the following page of the official Captivate Prime user guide:
> `https://helpx.adobe.com/captivate-prime/system-requirements.html`.

- 9. Click the **Learn More** field located in the **Action Button** section of the **Create Announcement** dialog and paste the link to the internal catalog that you copied earlier in this section.
- 10. Finally, add the **All Internal Users** user group to the **Target** section of the form.
- 11. When the **Create Announcement** dialog looks as follows, click the **Save** button located in the lower-right corner:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_557_0.png)

Figure 15.6 – The Create Announcement form when creating a Masthead

12. Click the **OK** button to dismiss the **Announcement created successfully** message.

When creating a masthead, Captivate Prime needs a little bit of time to process the content being uploaded. Usually, it doesn't take more than a few minutes. When Captivate Prime has finished processing the content, your new masthead announcement becomes available in the **Draft** tab of the **Announcements** page. As usual, you must now *publish* the new masthead so that it becomes visible to learners logging into the Prime account.

13. Click the *gear* icon associated with your new masthead and click **Publish**.

14. Switch to the **Published** tab of the **Announcements** page to confirm that your notification is now in a published state.

Once this process is complete, a new masthead should be visible to all the internal learners logging into the Prime account. We'll take a look at this in the next section.
