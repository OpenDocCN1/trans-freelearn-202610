## Browsing the Content Marketplace as a learner

In the previous section, you invited all of your internal learners to explore the courses available in the Content Marketplace so that they could express their interest in the courses being offered. This is a good way for organizations to gauge interest in this additional content before purchasing access to all or part of it. In this section, you will log in to your Prime account as an internal learner, browse the Content Marketplace, and express interest in some of the courses it contains. To do this, perform the following steps:

- 1. Log in to your Prime account as Learner 01. Remember that Learner 01 is one of the internal learners you defined in Chapter 5, Managing Users.
- 2. On the learner home page, notice a new Content Marketplace icon at the bottom of the left sidebar, as shown in the following screenshot.
- 3. Click the Content Marketplace link at the bottom of the left sidebar.

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_537_0.png)

Figure 14.12 – The new Content Marketplace link of the learner experience

This takes Learner 01 to a page that is very similar to the Catalog page of the learner experience. From here, Learner 01 can use the filters available in the left column, the Search field, and the Sort by drop-down menu located at the top of the page to browse through the courses available in the Content Marketplace:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_538_0.png)

Figure 14.13 – The search tools available to browse the Content Marketplace

- 4. Take the necessary time to browse through the Content Marketplace page using the available search tools.
- 5. If you find this course offering interesting and would like your organization to purchase unlimited access to it, click the Express Interest to Catalog button located in the upper-right corner of the page.
- 6. Click the Curated Playlist tab that is available at the top of the page, just below the Search field.
- 7. From here, you can explore the available playlists and express interest in any of them separately.

Note that a two-minute preview is available for some of the courses.

Make sure that you express your interest in some of these playlists before moving on to the next section.

In this section, you reviewed how selected learners can browse the **Content Marketplace** and express their interest either for the entire catalog or for any of the available playlists. In the next section, you will return to the administrator application and download the **Express interest report** before making a purchase in the **Content Marketplace**.

## Reviewing the express interest report

You now have many learners expressing interest in the content available on the **Content Marketplace**. This generates a lot of interesting data to help organizations decide whether to purchase access to some or all of this third-party off-the-shelf content.

In this section, you'll discover how administrators can download the **Express interest report** before making a purchase decision. To do this, perform the following steps:

- 1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Content Marketplace** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. From the **Content Marketplace** page, click the **Enterprise Training** card.
- 4. Scroll down the **Enterprise Training** page until you can see the Invite Users section.
- 5. Click the **Download the Express interest report here** link, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_539_0.png)

Figure 14.14 – Download the Express interest report

- 6. Take some time to read the **Download Express Interest Report** message, then click the **OK** button.
- 7. When the report is ready, a notification appears in the notification icon (the bell icon), which is located in the upper-right corner of the screen.

The express interest report is a .CSV file that can be opened with any spreadsheet application, such as Microsoft Excel, Apple Numbers, or Open Office. For your convenience, a sample express interest report is available in the marketplace/ folder of the download associated with this book.

If your organization decides to purchase some or all of the available contents, you must return to the **Enterprise Training** page of the administrator application and click the link located in the **Purchase** section of the page to initiate the purchase process with the Adobe sales team, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_540_0.png)

Figure 14.15 – Initiating a purchase process

In this section, you reviewed how administrators can download the **Express interest report** and how they can initiate a purchase process with the Adobe sales team.

Once the purchase has been finalized, the new courses can be pushed either in an existing catalog or via a new catalog. More information regarding this process can be found in the official Adobe Captivate Prime user guide at https://helpx.adobe.com/captivate-prime/user-guide.html/captivate-prime/administrators/feature-summary/content-marketplace.ug.html.
