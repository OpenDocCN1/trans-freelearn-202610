# Accessing session resources as learners

Learners can access session resources uploaded by instructors using the following steps:

+   1. Open a new private window of your web browser and log in to your Prime account as *Learner 01*. This is one of the confirmed learners of the session to which you uploaded a resource in the previous section.

+   2. Once logged in, click the **Fundamentals of cybersecurity** course card located in the **My Learning List** section of the home page (just make sure you do *not* click the **Continue** button).

This action opens the course overview page.

+   3. As shown in the following screenshot, the course **Resources** are available at the bottom of the right-hand column:

![](bbox=[0.27, 0.36, 0.73, 0.73])

Figure 8.8 – The Resources are available in the right column of the Course Overview page

**Note**
The course sidebar contains very important information for learners to have the best possible learning experience. Unfortunately, it is easily missed by many learners. To get around this, be sure to highlight the sidebar in the onboarding experience you provide to new learners. It's also a good idea to send this information via email in addition to displaying it on the sidebar.
