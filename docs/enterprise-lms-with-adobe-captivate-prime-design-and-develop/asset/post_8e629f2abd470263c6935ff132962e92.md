## Using Social Learning Scopes

In the trial Prime account you use to do these exercises, only a few users interact with each other; only a few discussion boards are available, and these are visible to everyone. This is a pretty simple situation to administer, and it works well for small organizations with up to a few hundred learners.

But if you have a larger organization with thousands of users creating a large number of discussion boards and participating in many discussions, you might want to segment social learning into different scopes. This allows you to maintain cohorts of users based on a characteristic of your choosing. You can, for example, do the following:

- - Create social learning scopes based on language, so that learners can only access the discussion boards created in their own language.
- - Base social learning scopes on the department of learners, so that learners in the marketing department have a different social learning scope than learners in other departments of the organization.
- - Base social learning scopes on the learner's location, so that learners located in North America share a different social learning scope than learners located in Europe.
- - and much more

Being able to segment social learning activities in this way is even more critical when dealing with external users. As a reminder, external users do not belong to your organization. These are individuals working for partners, vendors, customers, and others who have access to your Prime account. You probably want to confine these users to their own social learning spaces, depending on the organization they are from, or, as you did in these examples, disable social learning for external users altogether.

Perform the following steps to define social learning scopes:

- 1. Make sure you are on the **Settings** tab of the **Social Learning** page of the administrator application.
- 2. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Scope Settings** option. As a reminder, you already used this option in the *Enabling social learning* section to disable social learning for external users, earlier in this chapter.
- 3. Open the **User characteristic** drop-down menu associated with internal learners.

As shown in the following screenshot, the default value for this drop-down menu is **All Internal Users**:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_484_0.png)

Figure 12.36 – The default social learning scope is All Internal Users

This means that all the users share the same social learning space by default. When choosing another option in this list – say, for example, the **Department** option – you define different social learning scopes based on the learner's department so that learners can only interact with other learners from their own department. It is a bit like having a different social learning platform for each department.

In this example, though, since you have very few learners defined on your trial Prime account, you will leave this option as the default **All Internal Users** value.

- 4. Click the **Cancel** button to close the **Scope Settings** options without saving any changes.

Thanks to social learning scopes, administrators can segment learners so that they can only interact in social learning activities with other learners in the same scope.

## Modifying the download Configuration

Moving down the list, the next option is **Download Configuration**. This refers to where users download the Captivate Prime Desktop application from. By default, the application is downloaded from the Adobe servers, and that may be just right in many cases.

However, for obvious security reasons, many organizations restrict where users can download from. In such a case, you can host the Captivate Prime desktop app installers on your corporate network, and then have Captivate Prime redirect learners to that custom location when downloading or updating the Captivate Prime desktop app.

- 2. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Download Configuration** option.

- - **Adobe servers**. This is the default value; the Captivate Prime desktop application is downloaded from the Adobe servers.
