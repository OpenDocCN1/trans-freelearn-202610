Internal and external sharing has nothing to do with the concept of internal and external users, as described in Chapter 5, Managing Users. It's about the fact that you can share the contents of your catalogs either with users defined within your account (internal sharing) or with other Captivate Prime accounts (external sharing) that have their own internal and external users.

In the next section, let's begin by reviewing the internal sharing mechanism.

# Internal Sharing

In our example, we want the Internal Catalog defined in the previous section to only be accessible by the internal users of your Prime account. The External Catalog, also defined in the previous section, should only be accessible by the external users of your Prime account. Since all the users in this example are defined within your Prime account, both of these situations fall under the internal sharing mechanism of Captivate Prime.

Let's review this using the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the Catalogs link located in the Learning section of the left sidebar.
+   3. Click the Internal Catalog that you created in the previous section to open the Internal Catalog page.
+   4. Click the Share Internally link located in the left sidebar.
+   5. Click the Add User Groups link located in the middle of the page or the Add button in the upper-right corner.
+   6. Start typing All Internal Users into the Search field. When the group appears in the list, click it to add it to the selected user groups.

Note that you can repeat this operation to add multiple user groups as needed.
