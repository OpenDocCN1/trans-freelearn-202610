# 5
Managing Users

After reading the previous chapter, there is learning content hosted on your Prime account. This content is ready to be delivered to your learners. Except... you don't have any learners yet! In this chapter, you will review the tools and features of Captivate Prime that allow you to manage the users that will be granted access to your Prime account.

When it comes to user management, there are two important aspects to keep in mind: **authentication** and **authorization**. *Authentication* answers the question, "Who are you?", while *authorization* answers the question, "What can you do?"

For authentication, you can, of course, define users directly within Captivate Prime. But as the enterprise LMS of choice, Captivate Prime understands that users may already be defined in a corporate directory, an **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)** or a **Customer Relationship Management (CRM)** system, and so on. That's why Captivate Prime can retrieve user data from virtually any third-party system, either through a dedicated connector or by automatically importing CSV files via an FTP connection.

You can also decide to integrate Captivate Prime within your **single sign-on (SSO)** infrastructure, providing that your organization has a SAML 2.0-compliant SSO provider. This allows your users to authenticate only once and gain access to multiple enterprise applications, including Captivate Prime.

When it comes to authorization, Captivate Prime adopts a role-based approach. You have already visited the main default roles of the system (*Administrator, Author, Integration Admin, and Learner*), but you can also define custom roles to fit the specific needs of your organization.
