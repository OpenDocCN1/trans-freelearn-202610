Use the following steps to upload an assignment to Captivate Prime:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as *Learner 01* to your Prime account.
+   2. From the learner home page, click the **Fundamentals of cybersecurity** course card in the **My Learning List** section. This takes you to the course overview page.
+   3. Scroll down the page until you see the **Your cybersecurity essay** activity in the **Core Content** section, as depicted in the following screenshot:

![](bbox=[0.115, 0.305, 0.855, 0.69])

+   4. Click the **Upload File** link identified by the arrow in the preceding screenshot.
+   5. Browse to where the file you want to upload is located on your computer. In this example, you can use the `courseContent/myAssignement.docx` file of the download associated with this book.
