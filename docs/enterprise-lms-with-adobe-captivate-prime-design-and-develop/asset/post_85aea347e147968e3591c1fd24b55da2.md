
In this section, we discussed sharing catalogs either internally or externally. Remember that internal sharing allows you to grant access to your catalogs to the user groups defined within your Prime account. This includes both the internal and the external users available on your account. External sharing allows you to share your custom course catalogs with other Captivate Prime accounts. These can belong to providers, suppliers, partners, and customers that are also using Adobe Captivate Prime as their LMS or to local subsidiaries of your own international organization.

You now have two custom catalogs available in your Prime account. Each contains a number of learning items, and you have defined the users who have access to these catalogs. That said, the learner experience is not yet affected by any of the changes you have made so far in this chapter. This is because you haven't yet enabled your newly created catalogs. We will explore this in the next section.

## Enabling and Disabling Catalogs

In the preceding sections, you created two custom catalogs in your Prime account, added learning content to them, and decided which user groups have access to these catalogs. Now you are ready to activate them so that they become available in the learner experience.

But before doing that, remember that there is a third catalog available in your Prime account: the default catalog that was automatically created along with your account. This default catalog has some special characteristics of its own. Let's explore that in the next section.

## Modifying the Default Catalog

Remember that the default catalog contains all of the learning objects that are available in your Prime account and is accessible to all users. Let's take a look at how you can modify this default catalog using the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Catalogs** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Click the **Default Catalog** that is visible at the top of the list to open the **Default Catalog** page.

As shown in the following screenshot, the left sidebar only contains a short subset of its usual capabilities when the **Default Catalog** is open:

Notice that there is no **Content** link available in the left sidebar when the default catalog is open. This means that you cannot change the content of the default catalog. In other words, every course, learning program, certification, and job aid you create in your Prime account will *always* be part of the default catalog, no matter what!

Also, notice that there is no **Share Externally** link available. This means that your default catalog cannot be shared with an external Prime account. In other words, only the custom catalogs that you explicitly create yourself can be shared externally:

- 4. Click the **Share Internally** link available in the left sidebar.

This opens a page where you can modify who has access to the **Default Catalog**. Remember that, by default, all the users (both internal and external) defined on your Prime account have access to the **Default Catalog**. However, because the content in the **Default Catalog** cannot be modified, controlling access to your learning content using Captivate Prime catalogs, usually, involves disabling the default catalog. This is what you will do now using the following steps:

- 5. Click the **Back** link at the top of the left sidebar to return to the main **Catalogs** page of the administrator.

## Catalogs in the learner experience

In this section, you will log in to your Prime account as a learner and inspect how the work done in the previous sections of this chapter impacts the learner experience. As you do so, remember the following:

- You have two custom catalogs enabled on your Prime account.
- One of them is available to all of the internal users, while the other one is available to all of the external users.
- Some courses are included in only one of the two catalogs, others are included in both catalogs, and finally, some courses are not included in either catalog.
- Some of the learners defined in your Prime account have already enrolled in, started, or even completed courses before you created your custom catalogs and disabled the Default Catalog.

With that in mind, let’s head to the learner experience and examine what impact your custom catalogs have on it:

- 1. Log in to your Prime account as *Learner 01*.

Remember that *Learner 01* is one of the internal learners you defined in *Chapter 5, Managing Users.*

- 2. Once you have logged in, click the **Go To Catalog** link of the home page, or the **Catalog** icon of the left sidebar, to open the **Catalog** page of *Learner 01*.
- 3. Confirm that the courses available on the **Catalog** page of *Learner 01* are those included in the **Internal Catalog** that you created earlier.

> **Note**
> If multiple catalogs are available to the logged-in learner, additional filters are available in the left column of the **Catalog** page to allow learners to filter the courses by catalog.

- 4. Click the **My Learning** icon in the left sidebar to open the **My Learning** page of *Learner 01*.

As discussed in *Chapter 7, Reviewing the Learner Experience*, the **My Learning** page lists all the courses the logged-in learner is enrolled in, including the courses that they have already completed. This includes courses that are not part of the **Internal Catalog** but that the learner enrolled in when the **Default Catalog** was still enabled:

- 5. Click the logo in the upper-right corner of the screen, or the **Home** icon of the left sidebar, to return to the learner home page.
- 6. Scroll all the way down the learner home page and note that the available course recommendations (if any) only contain courses that are part of the catalog(s) currently accessible to the logged-in learner.

You will now log out as *Learner 01* and log back in as *Learner 03*. Remember that *Learner 03* is one of the external learners that you defined in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*. Also, remember that external learners must log in to the Prime account using a special login page that is specific to their external profile.

> **Accessing the login page of external learners**
> If you don't remember the URL of the login page of *Learner 03*, return to the **Users | External** page of the administrator, roll your mouse over the external learner profile, and click the *chain* icon that appears to copy the login page URL to your clipboard.

- 7. Click the user icon in the upper-right corner of the screen, and then click the **Sign Out** link to log out as *Learner 01*.

- 8. Log back in as *Learner 03*. Don't forget to use the special login page of the corresponding external learner profile.
- 9. Once logged in, click the **Go To Catalog** link of the home page or the **Catalog** icon of the left sidebar to open the **Catalog** page of *Learner 03*.
- 10. Confirm that the courses available on the **Catalog** page of *Learner 03* are those that are included in the **External Catalog** you created earlier in this chapter.

These experiments demonstrate that *Learner 01* and *Learner 03* do not have access to the same set of courses anymore. This is because the two learners do not have access to the same course catalog(s), so they are only able to view the courses, learning programs, certifications, and job aids included in the catalog(s) they have access to.

This section concludes our overview of the **Catalogs** feature of Captivate Prime. The main takeaway is that you can't decide who has access to which courses at the individual course level, but you can decide which group(s) of users have access to which catalog. It is by placing courses, learning programs, certifications, and job aids in such catalogs that you can control which learners have access to these learning objects. In addition to this, you can share catalogs with other accounts using the **external sharing** feature of Captivate Prime.
