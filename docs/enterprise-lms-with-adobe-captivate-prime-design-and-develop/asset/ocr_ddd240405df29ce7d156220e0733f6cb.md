# Turning L1 feedback on by default

Let's start by enabling L1 feedback by default on your Prime account using the following steps:

+   1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the Settings link located at the end of the Configure section in the left sidebar.
+   3. Then, click the Feedback link located in the Basics section of the left sidebar.
