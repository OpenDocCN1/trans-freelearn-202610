This approach allows you to define exactly what course material falls into Cybersecurity – Level 1 and Cybersecurity – Level 2. It also allows you to track each learner's progress in acquiring the skill. This information allows you to document the effectiveness of the learning (did IT security actually increased after a given number of employees have acquired the Cybersecurity skill?), send reminders to the learners that are yet to acquire the skill, and more.
