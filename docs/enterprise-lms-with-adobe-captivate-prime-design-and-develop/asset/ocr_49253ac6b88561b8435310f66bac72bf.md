# Certification Approvals

Figure 9.6 – Providing L3 feedback as a manager

Now, let’s be honest! We all know that very few people log in to the LMS at the beginning of each workday. That’s why Captivate Prime also sends a reminder email to managers with a direct link to the appropriate L3 feedback form. This is, of course, the manner in which most L3 feedback forms are accessed and submitted.

In the previous few sections, you have reviewed the main principles behind L1 and L3 feedback. Let’s make a quick summary of what you have learned so far:

+   - L1 and L3 feedback are disabled by default on any new Prime account.
+   - Activating L1 and L3 feedback is done at the course instance level. This means that you can have some course instances with feedback enabled and other course instances of the same course with feedback disabled.
+   - By default, the L1 feedback form contains six predefined questions to which you can add up to two custom questions. This allows you to define questions specific to a course instance, such as questions about a specific instructor or a specific venue.
