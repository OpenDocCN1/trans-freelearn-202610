# Creating Masthead Announcements

The Masthead is the big rectangular area that spans across the top of the learner experience. This is the area where you can currently see the message **Let's learn something new today** when logged in as a learner:

![](bbox=[0.115, 0.185, 0.85, 0.335])

Figure 15.5 – The default Masthead of Adobe Captivate Prime

This is, of course, one of the preferred areas to display all kinds of announcements to learners. Typically, you'll use the Masthead to promote new courses, learning programs, certifications, or catalogs, but there's nothing to stop you from using it for other purposes, such as promoting a corporate event, announcing the deadline for company-wide training, or announcing the learner who has accumulated the most gamification points! In addition to its prominent position in the user interface, the Masthead is unique in that it can be used as a call-to-action that redirects the learner to another web page.

In this exercise, you will use the masthead to announce the immediate availability of the internal catalog you created in *Chapter 14, Working with Catalogs and Peer Accounts*, and to provide your internal users with a direct link to this new catalog. To do so, your first task is to grab the link to the internal catalog by following these steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Catalogs** link available in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. Hover your mouse over the internal catalog you created in the previous chapter.
+   4. Click the *chain* icon that appears on the screen to copy the direct link to the corresponding catalog to the clipboard.

**Note**
If **Internal Catalog** is not available in your Prime account, choose any of the available catalogs.
