# Creating Custom Fields

Captivate Prime also allows you to create additional custom fields from scratch. In this section, you will create the *Hobbies* field to allow users to enter their hobbies in their user profile. After all, this can be an important piece of data for skills management, as the skills you acquire from your hobbies can be valuable in the workplace. Let's get started:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as an Administrator.
+   2. From the Administrator home page, click the **Users** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. When on the **Internal Users** page, click the **Active Fields** link located in the **Advanced** section of the left sidebar.
+   4. Type `Hobbies` in the **Type field name and hit Enter** field for **Internal Users**.
+   5. When done, hit the *Enter* key or click the **Add** link.
+   6. Next, click the **Modify Values** link in the **Internal Users** section of the page.
+   7. Open the **Select Field** drop-down list and choose the **Hobbies** fields.
+   8. Inspect the available options, but do not modify anything at this time.
+   9. When you're ready, click the **Done** button at the bottom-right corner of the form.
+   10. Also, click the **Save** button located in the top-right corner of the **Active Fields** page.

Thanks to these steps, you have created yet another custom field. This one is a free text field where users can type anything they want: they are not restricted to a drop-down list of predefined elements. Let's take a look at it.

+   11. Return to the **Internal Users** page by clicking the **Internal** link located in the **Users** section of the left sidebar.
+   12. Click your name in the list to open the details about your user account.
+   13. Click the pencil icon located in the top-right corner of your user's details to open the **Edit User** form.
