# Reporting inappropriate posts

Another aspect of board moderation involves the ability of any learner to report a post that is deemed inappropriate. In such a case, a board moderator must review the report and decide on a course of action.

This is what you will review in this section. First, you will log in to your Prime account as *Manager 01* and make that user report one of the posts that's been published on the **Cybersecurity in the organization** discussion board. Perform the following steps to do so:

+   1. Use another browser or another private window on the same browser to log in to your Prime account as *Manager 01*. Remember that a manager is, first and foremost, a learner, so the learning experience is displayed by default when *Manager 01* logs in.
+   2. Go to the **Social Learning** page of *Manager 01* and switch to the **All Boards** tab.
+   3. Click the three dots icon associated with any of the posts available on the **Cybersecurity in the organization** board.
+   4. In the menu that opens, click the red **report** link. Take some time to read the **Confirmation Required** message and click the **Report** button.

This illustrates how a user can report a post. Notice that *Manager 01* did not have to subscribe to the board or participate in the discussion to be able to report a post.

Now, let's return to the browser window where you are logged in as a board moderator (this can be either *Learner 01* or *Learner 02*) and see how a moderator can follow up on this report.

+   5. Return to a browser window where you are logged in as a board administrator (*Learner 01* or *Learner 02*).
