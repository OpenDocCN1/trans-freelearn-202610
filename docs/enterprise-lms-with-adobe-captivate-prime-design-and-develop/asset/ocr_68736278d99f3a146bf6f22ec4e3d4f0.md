+   - Of course, a blend of these two models is also possible. A portion of the user seats available in a specific Prime account can be purchased directly from that account, while another portion of the available seats is allocated from another Prime account.

Third, there is the branding aspect. Since each Prime account can be branded, peer accounts are very useful for organizations that need to have a completely different brand image with specific logos and colors for each of their individual Prime accounts. This is especially useful for large companies that have multiple brands.
