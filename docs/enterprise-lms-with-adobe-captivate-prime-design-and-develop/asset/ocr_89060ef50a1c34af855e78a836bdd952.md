# Configuring Session Reminders

By default, Captivate Prime sends a reminder to the class participants on the day of the course. As an instructor, you cannot change the content or the appearance of this reminder, but you can decide whether or not Captivate Prime sends this reminder, as well as when and how often this reminder should be sent. Let's take a look at this feature using the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as an instructor to your Captivate Prime account.
+   2. From the instructor home page, click any of the available upcoming sessions. This opens the Session Overview page of the chosen session.
+   3. Click the Reminders link located in the left sidebar of the Session Overview page to open the Reminders page of the chosen session.
