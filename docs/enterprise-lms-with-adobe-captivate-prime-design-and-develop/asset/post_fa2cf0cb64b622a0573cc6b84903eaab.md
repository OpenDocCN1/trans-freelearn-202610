
You should now take the necessary time to log into the email accounts of Instructor 01 and Learner 02 to create their Adobe IDs. This will allow them to access your Captivate Prime account. Make sure you can log in to your Prime account as these two new users before moving on to the next section.

Note that, at this time, the Instructor 01 user is defined as a regular Learner, so when logging in as Instructor 01, you will be automatically redirected to the Learner experience. You will assign the Instructor role to that user in the Managing roles section later in this chapter.

## Creating a self-registration link

In the last two sections, you, the Prime administrator, have created new users either one by one or in batch form by uploading a CSV file. In both cases, creating new users requires action on your part. In this section, you will allow users to register themselves to your Prime account, a technique that requires (almost) no intervention from you.

The idea is to generate a self-registration link and share that link with users who might be interested in joining your Captivate Prime account. Users can then click the link and fill out a form to register themselves on the platform. Let's get started:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an administrator and that you are on the **Internal Users** page.
- 2. Click the **Add** button located in the top-right corner.
- 3. Click the **Self-Registration** option in the menu that opens. This opens the **Add Self-Registration** profile form.
- 4. Enter a profile name in the **Profile Name** field.
- 5. Enter your name in the **Manager's Name** field. It is important to note that the manager must be a user who is already registered on the Prime account. You can choose any learner to be a manager; this user does not have to be an administrator.

Note that the **Profile Name** and **Manager's Name** fields are the only two required pieces of information regarding this form.

- 6. As a best practice, also supply a short **Description** for this self-registration profile.
- 7. You can also supply an optional image using the **Add Image** field. (A sample picture is provided in the download associated with this book. Look for the `branding/o21DevTeam.png` image.)
- 8. When the **Add Self-Registration Profile** form looks as follows, click the **Save** button located at the bottom-right corner of the form:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_184_0.png)

Figure 5.9 – Filling in the Add Self-Registration Profile form

After saving the new self-registration profile, Captivate Prime generates a unique URL that you must share with potential Prime users. This unique URL appears on the screen and is also sent to you in an email, confirming the creation of the new self-registration profile.

You can create as many self-registration profiles as needed (keep an eye on the number of user licenses you have purchased!). For example, each department of the organization can have its own self-registration profile with a different manager assigned to each profile. This allows you to add users to your Prime account without any action from an administrator, all while respecting the hierarchical structure of your organization.

## Using a self-registration profile to auto-register as a Prime learner

Now that a self-registration profile is active, you will switch hats again and put yourself in the shoes of a user who wants to register to your Prime account.

You will use the self-registration link to create *Manager 01*. Later in this chapter, you will define this new user as the manager of other learners. Also, remember that *you* will be defined as the manager of any user using this self-registration link to register to the platform. This will allow you to create a three-level hierarchy where you will be the general manager, while *Manager 01* will manage a small team of users.

Finally, keep in mind that, by default, all internal users must have an Adobe ID. So, one of the steps in the self-registration process is for users to create their own Adobe IDs. Let’s experiment with this by following these steps:

- 1. Open a new private window in your web browser (or another browser altogether).
- 2. Paste the auto-registration URL Captivate Prime generated in the previous section in the address bar. This will take you to the Adobe **Sign in** page. The link is also available in the email you received from Captivate Prime.
- 3. Click the **Create an account** link to create a new Adobe ID for *Manager 01*.
- 4. Fill in the **Create an account** form and click the **Create account** button when you’re done.
- 5. You should be automatically redirected to the Learner experience of your Prime account and logged in as *Manager 01*.

Take a look at the top-right corner of the screen. You should recognize the image that you uploaded while defining the self-registration link in the previous section:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_186_0.png)

After this process, a new user will have been added to your Prime account. For now, this new user is just another learner. ## Administering self-registration links

In this section, you will return to the Administrator application to inspect the options available for managing the new self-registration link. This includes modifying, pausing, resuming, and deleting the self-registration profile. Follow these steps to give it a try:

- 1. Return to the Administrator application on the Internal Users page. Confirm that Manager 01 has been included as a Learner in the list of Internal Users.
- 2. Above the list of users, click the Self-Registration tab, as shown in the following screenshot:

Figure 5.11 – Clicking the Self-Registration tab above the list of users

This takes you to the list of Self-Registration profiles, where you can see how many people have registered to your Prime account using each of these links. For now, only the profile you created earlier in this section should be displayed in the list.

- 3. Hover over the profile you created previously. This will reveal a link icon that you can use to copy the self-registration link to the clipboard.
- 4. Click the profile's name to open the Edit Self-Registration Profile form, where you can modify the profile's Description and image. Note that you cannot modify Profile Name or Manager's Name.
- 5. Click the Cancel button to return to the list of self-registration profiles.
- 6. Click the number that appears in the No. of People column for your registration profile. This will take you to the list of learners who have used this self-registration profile to register to the platform.
- 7. Click the Internal link located in the Users section of the left sidebar to return to the list of Internal Users.
- 8. Now, click the Self-Registration tab situated above the list of users to return to the list of self-registration profiles.
- 9. Use the checkbox in the first column of the list to select the self-registration profile you created earlier.
- 10. Click the Actions button located in the top-right corner of the screen to inspect the available actions.
