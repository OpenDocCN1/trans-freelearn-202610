# Branding your Captivate Prime account

Other important aspects of a company's corporate image are the logo, color theme, fonts, and so on used in all of the company's communications. In this section, you will learn how to upload your logo and modify the color scheme of Captivate Prime LMS:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as an administrator and that you are on the administrator homepage.
+   2. Click the Branding link located in the Configure section at the bottom of the left column.

This takes you to the Branding page of the administrator application. From here, you can upload your logo and modify the color theme of your Prime account. Let's review these two options in more details.

# Uploading your logo

First, you will upload your company logo using the following procedure:

> Note
> If you do not have access to your company logo, you can use the sample logo provided in the files that accompany this book. Look for the branding/sampleLogo.png or branding/sampleLogo_negative.png files.

+   1. Make sure you are on the Branding page of the administrator area.
+   2. Click the Edit link associated with the Logo Styling option.
+   3. Drag and drop your logo image or click the upload area to select the logo image you want to use. For best results, it is recommended to use an image file in .jpg or .png format.
+   4. Choose your favorite Header Style among the three available options (Only Logo Image (default), Logo Image + CompanyName, or Only Company Name).

The header style defines the way your logo and/or your company name is displayed in the top left corner of the Captivate Prime interface.

+   5. Click the Save button located in the bottom right corner of the page to validate.
