# Self-Enrollment by Learners

Since Captivate Prime is a learner-centric LMS, learner self-enrollment is considered the preferred way to enroll for a course. In fact, the new immersive learner home page has been designed as a Netflix-like interface where people can browse the catalog and enroll in courses, similar to browsing Netflix's vast catalog to find the next movie or series you want to watch.

> **Customizing the learner home page**
> Customizing the learner home page, as well as the differences between the classic home page and the immersive layout, were discussed in *Chapter 2, Customize the Look and Feel of Captivate Prime.*

In this section, you will review this self-service learning approach by logging into the platform as *Learner 03* and living through the experience of enrolling in one of the course instances you created earlier in this chapter.

> **Logging in as Learner 03**
> Remember that Learner 03 is the only *external* learner that you defined in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*, so this user must log in to your Captivate Prime account using a custom login page. The URL of this login page is available in the email message that was sent to you when you created the external registration profile used by *Learner 03* to register to your Prime account. If you don't have access to this email message anymore, browse to the **External Users** page of the administrator application and hover over the external registration profile. This will reveal a chain icon so that you can copy the link to your clipboard.

Let's review the default enrollment experience of *Learner 03* by performing the following steps:

+   1. Open another browser or a new private window in your web browser and use it to log in to your Prime account as *Learner 03*.
+   2. Depending on the roles that have been assigned to *Learner 03*, you might not be redirected to the Learner experience by default. In such cases, click the user icon in the top-right corner of the screen to switch to the learner experience.
