## Linking Courses to Skills

At this point, your new course is in a *Draft* state. Before you can publish it, you must link the course to one or more skill(s) by performing the following steps:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an Author.
- 2. From the Author home page, click the courses link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. At the top of the list, click the **Draft** tab to access the list of courses currently in a *draft* state. From this list, you should find the course you started to create in the previous section.
- 4. Hover over the lower area of the course card (where the three little dots are visible) and click the **View Course** link. This action will take you back to the form you used in the previous section to create the course.
- 5. Click the **Settings** link located in the left sidebar. This action will take you to the course's **Settings** page. This is where you can pick the *skills* this course is linked to.
- 6. At the top of the form, open the **Pick a Skill** drop-down list.

- 7. Choose the **Cybersecurity** skill you created earlier in this chapter.
- 8. Open the **Level** drop-down list and choose level **1**.
- 9. Finally, type 30 in the **Credits** field.
- 10. Click the **Save** button located in the top-right corner of the page.

The upper section of the **Settings** page should now look as follows:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|  | Cybersecurity | 1 | 30 | 50 | X |
|  | Add More |  |  |  |  |

With the current settings, your course awards 30 credits toward the *Cybersecurity – Level 1 Skill*. Remember that a learner needs to accumulate 50 credits before achieving *Cybersecurity – Level 1*. In other words, this course is not enough to achieve that specific skill.

This is a very common pattern in Captivate Prime skills management. Imagine that three different courses are linked to the Cybersecurity – Level 1 skill:

- The *Fundamentals of Cybersecurity* course (which is the course you are building in this section) awards 30 credits.

## Customizing a course and making it discoverable

Courses are only useful when users can enroll in them. In *Chapter 6, Enrolling Learners to Courses*, you will discover that, although administrators and managers can enroll learners in courses, Captivate Prime is built around the idea of a *self-service learning platform*, where a Course Catalog is made available to learners so that they can enroll themselves in the courses they are interested in. A Prime Course Catalog can contain thousands of courses, so it is critical to make your course discoverable by the learners that might be interested in taking it.

As authors, there are a few things that you can do to increase your course discoverability within the platform:

- You can provide a **Brief Description** and a **Detailed Overview** of the course. This allows the learner to get a better idea of the course's content and increases the chances that a mere interest will convert into an actual enrollment. Captivate Prime's internal search engine also uses this content to index your course and relate it to the proper keywords.
- You can link your course to tags. Tags were discussed in the *Working with tags* section of *Chapter 3, Uploading Learning Content and Managing the Content Library*.
- You can tell Captivate Prime about the target audience for the course. This increases the chances that the target audience will find the course in the catalog and enroll. This requires users and user groups to be present in your Prime accounts, which will be covered in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*.
- Finally, you can make the course visually appealing by providing a **Banner Image** and a **Cover Image**.

In this section, you will add this additional metadata to the course and provide both a **Cover Image** and a **Banner Image**:

- 1. From the **Author** home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 2. At the top of the list, click the **Draft** tab to access the list of all courses currently in a *draft* state. In this list, you should find the course you started to create earlier in this chapter.
- 3. Hover over the lower area of your course's card (where the three little dots are visible) and click the **View Course** link. This action will take you back to the form you used when you first created the course.
- 4. Provide both a **Brief Description** and a **Detailed Overview** of the course.

**Brief Description** appears on the Course Card, which is visible both in the catalog itself and in the search results page when learners are hunting for courses. It should be very short (maximum 140 characters) and contain the main keywords that relate to your course. Consider it as bait to make a learner click your course rather than the other courses available in the catalog.

**Detailed Overview** is a longer description of your course (maximum 1,500 characters). It appears on the course page once the learner has clicked the course card in the catalog or the search results page. In other words, learners who reach **Detailed Description** have already expressed an interest in taking the course. That's why **Detailed Description** can be made much longer than **Brief Description**. The detailed overview should contain high-level information about the course's content, the learning outcomes, the methodology used in the course, and more.

**Sample descriptions**
To help write a meaningful **Brief Description** and **Detailed Overview**, examples can be found in the `cybersecurity/description_EN.pdf` file in the download provided with this book.

Before hitting the **Save** button, you must upload both a **Cover Image** and a **Banner Image** for the course.

If you're using your own images, the recommended size of the cover image is 300 pixels by 300 pixels, and the recommended size of the banner image is 1,600 pixels by 140 pixels, as stated in the official Captivate Prime user guide at https://helpx.adobe.com/be_en/captivate-prime/system-requirements.html.
