
## 15 Working With Messages and Announcements

In our ultra-connected world, we all have a large number of accounts on a growing number of platforms and networks. From social networks to messaging apps, from email accounts to corporate platforms, we are surrounded by all sorts of online services, both in our personal and professional lives. In this cluttered technological environment, the enterprise LMS can be viewed by learners as yet one more platform to use and engage with. This can lead to technology fatigue from the learners, resulting in less engagement and scattered attention.

Adobe Captivate Prime acknowledges this reality and has developed strategies to bring learners back to the LMS and keep them engaged in their learning process. The features discussed in Chapter 11, Badges and Gamification, and Chapter 12, Enabling and Managing Social Learning, contribute to this strategy, but there is more! Captivate Prime also sends a large number of email messages to users. These messages are designed to keep users up to date on what's going on in the LMS, which helps them come back to the platform and interact with both the learning content available to them and with their peers. Finally, let's face it – most workers in an organization receive their training commands and requirements from their email inbox or messages on their phone. So, in a nutshell, messaging is critical to the success of any LMS deployment.

These messages extend the reach of the LMS to the mailbox of each learner and are, for most users, the starting point of the learner's experience. This is the reason why most organizations take great care in customizing these messages, both in terms of visual design and content.

Captivate Prime also allows you to deliver different types of announcements to specific target audiences within the learner experience. You can use them in a wide variety of situations, such as promoting new learning content, sending various types of reminders, displaying platform announcements, and more.

This is what we will discuss in this chapter, which contains the following main sections:

- Working with announcements
- Configuring the messaging system
- Customizing individual email messages

By the end of this chapter, you will have the necessary tools to take full ownership of your Prime account by customizing the user experience, both on the platform itself and through the messages sent by the LMS.

## Working With Announcements

Let's pretend your organization has just subscribed to a new third-party content library, or has purchased access to some or all of the content available in the new Content Marketplace of Captivate Prime. Thanks to the work done by your developers in collaboration with the content provider's teams, this new library is made available to your learners as a new Captivate Prime catalog. Now is the time to inform your learners of the availability of these new courses, encourage them to check out this new catalog, and enroll in the courses it contains. This is one of the many situations where the **announcements** feature of Captivate Prime comes in handy.

There are three different types of announcements that you can create as a Captivate Prime administrator:

- **Notifications** are displayed as dialog boxes and under the bell icon located in the top-right corner of the learner experience. This is a great option for targeted reminders (such as reminding managers to submit L3 feedback) or platform announcements (such as the upcoming reset of gamification points). L3 Feedback was discussed in *Chapter 9, Configuring and Using Feedback*, while gamification points were discussed in *Chapter 11, Badges and Gamification*).
- You can also display announcements in the **Masthead**, which is the top portion of the learner home page. These announcements are much more prominent in the learning experience and are based on either an image or a video file. They serve as a call to action for the targeted audience. As such, they usually include a link that allows users to learn more about the announcement.
- Finally, **Recommendations** are a type of announcement you can use to manually recommend courses to specific target audiences. These manual course recommendations come in addition to the AI-powered recommendations and were discussed in *Chapter 13, AI-Powered Recommendations for Learners*.

Now, let's explore *notification* and *masthead* announcements, starting with notifications in the next section.

## Creating Notifications

The first type of announcement you'll look at in this chapter is the notification. Notifications are typically used for general platform announcements (such as the winner of a contest, a maintenance downtime, a corporate event, and so on) or targeted reminders (such as reminding managers to submit their L3 feedback or to review enrollment requests from their team members, and so on).

In this example, you will create a notification announcing the upcoming annual reset of the gamification points, as discussed in the *Configuring Advanced Gamification Options* section of *Chapter 11, Badges and Gamification*. Before doing so, remember that you have enabled gamification for internal users only, so you should target your notification at this specific user group only.

It is now time to head back to the administrator of Captivate Prime and create your first notification using the following steps:

1. Log in to your Captivate Prime account as an administrator.
2. From the administrator home page, click the **Announcements** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
   This opens the main **Announcements** page of the administrator.
3. Click the **Add** button located in the top-right corner of the page to open the **Create Announcement** dialog.
4. At the top of the **Create Announcement** dialog, make sure the **Type** drop-down menu is set to **As notification**.
5. Type your notification message in the **Message** field of the form. Note that this is required information.

> **HTML message**
> The **Message** field of the **Create Announcement** form supports HTML. This allows you to enter a snippet of HTML code into the **Message** field rather than simple text. This is very useful for inserting a hyperlink into the message or adding some formatting.

6. Optionally, you can attach an image or a video file to your announcement. For this exercise, you can use the `announcements/notification.png` sample image included in the download associated with this book.
7. At the bottom of the dialog, the **Target** property is the second required element of the form. In this example, the notification is targeted at the **All Internal Users** group.
   Start typing `Internal` in the **Type here** field and click **All Internal Users** when it appears in the list. Note that you can add as many user groups as necessary in the **Target** section of the form.

> **Sending notifications to learners enrolled in a specific course**
> Using the **Trainings** tab of the **Target** section in the **Create Announcement** form, you can target the notification at learners enrolled in specific courses, learning programs, and certifications instead of using user groups.
