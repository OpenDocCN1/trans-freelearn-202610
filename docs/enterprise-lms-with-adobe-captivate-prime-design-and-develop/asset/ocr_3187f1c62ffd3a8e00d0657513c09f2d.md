L1 feedback refers to the first level of Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Learning Evaluation. As a reminder, level 1 is known as the *Reaction* level. It tries to measure the overall learner satisfaction. In Captivate Prime, L1 feedback is a form that each learner must complete at the end of a course or learning program.

L3 feedback refers to the third level of Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Learning Evaluation. This is the *Behavior* level, which attempts to measure whether a student's behavior has changed after taking a course. In Captivate Prime, it is a form that the learner's manager fills in, usually a few days or weeks after the learner has completed a training.

> **Courses and learning programs**
> Keep in mind that although the next few sections discuss L1 and L3 feedback for courses, most of the features discussed can also be applied to learning programs. The specifics of L1 and L3 feedback for learning programs are addressed in the *L1 and L3 feedback for learning programs* section later in this chapter.

By default, L1 and L3 feedback are disabled on any new Prime account, but you can override this setting and enable feedback for each individual course (or learning program) instance. This is what you will examine in the next section.

# Enabling L1 and L3 Feedback for a Course Instance

By default, L1 and L3 feedback are disabled at the account level, because this might be a new approach for many trainers and HR managers who are not too familiar with Kirkpatrick's model or who have never used an LMS that offers such a feedback workflow. If it were enabled by default, there would be a large number of email interactions, which most organizations do not want in the early stages of their Captivate Prime deployment.

Administrators can override this account default by enabling L1 and/or L3 feedback for each individual course. In other words, when creating a new course, feedback is *disabled* by default unless an administrator explicitly enables feedback for that particular course.

To be more exact, feedback is enabled or disabled for each individual *course instance*, not for the course as a whole. It means that you can, for the same course, enable feedback for some instances and leave feedback disabled for other instances. You can also customize L1 and L3 feedback for each individual course instance.
