## 11 Badges and Gamification

People learn best when they are having fun! This is something teachers and instructors have known for thousands of years. There is historical evidence that games and game-based learning were well-known educational ideas in ancient Greece and during the Roman Empire. In addition, the earliest African board games used to teach mathematics, logic, and strategy were designed over 5,000 years ago!

Despite this long and rich history, the term *gamification* first appeared online in 2008 and gained popularity around 2010. A widely accepted definition of gamification is *the use of game-design elements and game principles in non-game contexts.*

This definition is not specific to education. In fact, gamification has been applied to many aspects of our daily lives. After all, airlines allow us to earn miles, brands have loyalty programs and award us with points when we purchase some of their products, many organizations use employee honor rolls to increase engagement and productivity and to recognize accomplishments, we collect points and stamps when we shop and redeem them for gifts of all kinds, and so on.

Regardless of the area of application, game design elements are the basic building blocks of gamification. Some of the typical game design elements used include points, badges, leaderboards, scenario-based training, and avatars. Many of these game design elements are available in Captivate Prime and are used by organizations to increase learner engagement and the retention rate.

In this chapter, we will discuss **badges** and how they are used within Captivate Prime. You will then explore the **gamification** features that you can use to award points to your learners when they interact with learning content on your Prime account. You will start by exploring these from the administrator's perspective, before reviewing how they affect the learner experience.

This chapter contains the following main sections:

- Creating and using badges
- Enabling and configuring gamification
- Gamification from the learner's perspective

By the end of this chapter, you will have reviewed the features that will help you make learning more fun on your Prime account. By applying these techniques, you should see a significant increase in learner engagement and the number of learning activities carried out on your Prime account. This will further help your organization meet its training goals and promote a learning culture among employees.

### Creating and Using Badges

Badges are one of the most commonly used game design elements in online education. They are visual representations of the learner's achievements. They symbolize the learner's merit and help them showcase their skills. They help add credibility to the learner's credentials and are a source of pride.

In Captivate Prime, learners earn badges by achieving skills, completing courses and learning programs, or earning certifications that you define in the LMS. To use badges, you must first define them as such in the system. Then, you need to link those badges to courses, learning programs, skills, and certifications.

In this section, you will learn how to create custom badges using the following steps:

1. Make sure you are logged in to your Prime account as an administrator.
2. From the administrator home page, click the **Badges** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar.

This opens the **Badges** page. As shown in the following screenshot, a handful of badges are already defined on your Prime trial account by default:

3. Click the **Add** button located in the upper-right corner of the screen (see the arrow in the preceding screenshot) to open the **Add Badge** dialog.

### Assigning Badges to Skills

There are several ways learners can acquire badges. One of these is by achieving skills. Creating skills and linking them to courses was discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*. As a reminder, Captivate Prime allows you to define skills and levels within those skills. When you create a course, you can have the course assign points in one or more skill levels. Learners achieve a skill when they reach the required number of points.

You will now return to the custom **Cybersecurity** skill you created in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, and associate a badge with each of the two levels you have defined for this skill. These badges will allow learners to make their newly acquired skills public, thus adding credibility to their achievements:

1. Make sure you are logged in to your Prime account as an administrator.
2. From the administrator home page, click the **Skills** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar to open the **Skills** page.
3. Click the **Cybersecurity** skill you created back in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, to open the **Edit Skill** dialog. Remember that you have defined two levels for this particular skill.
   (If the **Cybersecurity** skill is not available on your Prime account, feel free to use any other skill available. You can also create a custom skill from scratch using the techniques discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*.)
4. If necessary, expand the **Level 1** section located in the lower portion of the **Edit Skill** dialog.
5. Open the **Choose from the following Badges** drop-down menu and select the **Cybersecurity Fighter** badge you defined in the previous section.
6. Repeat the preceding steps to assign the **Cybersecurity Specialist** badge to **Level 2** of the **Cybersecurity** skill.
