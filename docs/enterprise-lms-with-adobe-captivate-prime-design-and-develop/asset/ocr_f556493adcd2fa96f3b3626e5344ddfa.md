+   2. Click the **Record Video** icon located in the middle of the application welcome screen.
+   3. Choose between **Webcam only**, **Screen only**, or **Both**. In this example, click the **Both** icon to review every option available.
+   4. Use the webcam and microphone icons just above the red *record* button to choose the webcam and the microphone you want to use.
+   5. When you're ready, click the red *record* button. After a small countdown, you'll be in recording mode.
+   6. Record your video, keeping in mind that you are recording both your webcam and your screen. When you're done, click the *pause* button to stop the recording.
+   7. Take the necessary time to review the video you just recorded. If you are happy with it, click the **Next** button; otherwise, click the **Discard** link and start over. You can also add to your recording by clicking the **Resume** link.
+   8. After clicking the **Next** button, you will be prompted to save your video. Give it a name and click the **Save** button.
+   9. Now, you can edit your videos in many ways using the tools available in the left column of the Adobe Captivate Prime desktop application.
