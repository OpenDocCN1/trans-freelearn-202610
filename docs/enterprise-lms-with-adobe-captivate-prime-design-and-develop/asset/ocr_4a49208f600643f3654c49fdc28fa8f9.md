Learners take courses because it allows them to acquire new skills or update existing skills. As for organizations, they know that, in a competitive environment, skilled employees are one of the keys to success and profitability. Therefore, for both learners and organizations, it is critical to align courses to skills. This allows learners to choose the courses they want to take. It also helps organizations track the skills acquired by each of their employees to ensure that their workforce stays up to date, and that the skills available in the organization remain relevant to an ever-changing marketplace. Skills may also refer to specific compliance or mandatory training in organizations (such as government entities), where taking courses is considered a function of the position.

In this chapter, you will take your first look at Captivate Prime's Skills Manager by creating a new custom skill. Then, you will assemble content modules into courses and review the wide range of options available for creating various types of courses.

By the end of this chapter, you will have a custom course that's ready for enrollment, and your Prime account will be ready to welcome your first learners. To achieve this goal, this chapter will cover the following topics:

+   - Creating custom skills with Skills Manager
+   - Creating Courses
+   - Adding different types of modules and content to courses
+   - Linking courses to skills
+   - Updating, retiring, and deleting courses

These topics make for a chapter full of great features and exciting discoveries
