
In Captivate Prime, these features are based on a well-known theoretical model named the *Four Levels of Learning Evaluation* that was developed by *Dr. Donald Kirkpatrick* in the *1950s*.

Level 1 is the *Reaction* level. In Captivate Prime, this is a form that each learner fills in whenever a course or learning program is complete. Level 2 is the *Learning* level. In Captivate Prime, these are the scores acquired by learners as they review the modules of the courses they take. It includes the quiz scores of the interactive modules, the grades obtained by submitting assignments, and any other type of activity that can be graded. Level 3 is the *Behavior* level. In Captivate Prime, this is a form that the learner's manager must complete to assess how attending a training has modified the learner's behavior when back in the workplace. Typically, managers would fill in the L3 feedback form 45 days after training completion.

L1 and L3 feedback are disabled by default in Captivate Prime. You can enable them either at the course instance level, the course level, or the account level. Also remember that these feedback features are not limited to courses and also apply to learning programs.

But don't forget that there is a fourth level to Kirkpatrick's Learning Evaluation model. It is the *Results* level and it is where organizations take advantage of the data collected to measure the impact of learning on the organization as a whole. This is the subject of the next chapter, where you will review the extensive reporting capabilities available in Captivate Prime.

## 10 Reporting in Captivate Prime

In the previous chapter, we reviewed L1, L2, and L3 feedback for courses and learning programs. This provided you with important data about the learner behavior in your Prime account. Remember, these feedback features are based on Dr. Donald Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Learning Evaluation model.

Four levels, did you say? That's right! You only covered the first three levels in the previous chapter. Fortunately, this chapter is entirely dedicated to the fourth level of Kirkpatrick's model. As a reminder, the fourth level is the **Results** Level, which aims to measure the result of the training strategy at the organizational level. It is at this stage that the focus shifts from the individual learner to the impact of learning on the organization as a whole.

Captivate Prime's reporting capabilities are designed to help organizations answer questions such as *Are we increasing revenue through training?*, *Are we reducing costs?*, *Are we decreasing risk and increasing safety?*, *Are we increasing the organization's level of compliance?*, and so on. For organizations, such insight is critical to identify areas for improvement, continually refine the organization's L&D strategy, and ultimately make informed business decisions.

Technically speaking, generating reports requires two distinct steps. First, you need to collect data, then you leverage that data to generate reports, charts, and so on that help you make sense out of it.

In Captivate Prime, data collection is largely automatic. You don’t have to do anything to enable or configure any data collection mechanism on the platform. As for data mining, Captivate Prime comes with a handful of predefined **reports**, but you can also create your own **custom reports**, share them with other users and even export the data to another tool (such as Microsoft Excel) for further analysis.

These reports are grouped into **dashboards**. Some of these dashboards come predefined and are available out of the box on every Prime account, but you can also create your own **custom dashboard** where you can arrange custom reports as you see fit.

This chapter begins by exploring the default dashboards available in Captivate Prime and discussing the reports they contain. You will then create your own custom reports and dashboards. Finally, you will examine extra options allowing you to subscribe to reports and discuss the reporting options available to managers. To achieve these objectives, this chapter contains the following main sections:

- Reviewing default reports
- Creating custom reports and dashboards
- Subscribing to reports
- Reporting features for managers

These reporting capabilities are one of Captivate Prime’s key features. They help make Captivate Prime one of the leading enterprise LMSes on the market. So, let’s get started!

## Reviewing the User Activity dashboard

The first link available in the left sidebar while in the **Reports** section takes you to the **User Activity** dashboard, which contains three important reports. These reports are available out of the box in every Prime account. They give you some interesting insight into the user activity happening within your account. Thanks to these reports, you will be able to answer questions such as these:

- How many users are registered on the Prime account and how does that number evolve over time? Remember that Captivate Prime is billed per seat, so keeping an eye on this metric is paramount in mastering your costs.
- How many learners are actually accessing the platform? How many users visit the Prime account every day? How does the number of visits evolve over time? These are important metrics that help you gauge learner engagement with the LMS and the organization's training offering.
- How many learners use the Captivate Prime mobile app to access training content from their mobile devices?
- How long do learners spend learning on Captivate Prime? How does that number evolve? These metrics allow you to answer questions such as are learners spending enough time learning? Is the time spent learning in line with the expectations of the organization? How do promotion campaigns affect the time spent learning? And so on.
