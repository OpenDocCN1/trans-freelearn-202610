# Reviewing the Social learning Options of the Administrator

So far in this chapter, you have reviewed the social learning features of Captivate Prime using, for the most part, the default settings. There are only two general options that you have modified in previous exercises:

+   - You have turned off the social learning feature for external learners.
+   - You have activated the auto-assisted manual curation workflow.

All the other settings are still at their respective defaults.

In this section, you will explore the general social learning options and see how they can be used to tweak the social learning feature of Captivate Prime, so as to meet the specific requirements of your organization. Perform the following steps to start exploring these settings hands-on:

+   1. Log in as an administrator to your Captivate Prime account.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the Social Learning link located in the Manage section of the left sidebar.
+   3. At the top of the page, switch to the Settings tab to access the general settings for social learning in Captivate Prime.

Now that you are at the right place, let's review the options available on this page one by one in the next few sections.

# Using Social Learning Scopes

In the trial Prime account you use to do these exercises, only a few users interact with each other; only a few discussion boards are available, and these are visible to everyone. This is a pretty simple situation to administer, and it works well for small organizations with up to a few hundred learners.

But if you have a larger organization with thousands of users creating a large number of discussion boards and participating in many discussions, you might want to segment social learning into different scopes. This allows you to maintain cohorts of users based on a characteristic of your choosing. You can, for example, do the following:

+   - Create social learning scopes based on language, so that learners can only access the discussion boards created in their own language.
