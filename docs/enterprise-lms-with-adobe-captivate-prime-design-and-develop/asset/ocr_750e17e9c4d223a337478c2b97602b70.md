A common use case for prerequisites is to create a short course that explains the Captivate Prime learner experience and make it a prerequisite for all the other courses on the platform. This ensures that every new user must go through a course on Captivate Prime itself before being able to enroll in any other course. This use case also illustrates the possibility to use the same course as a prerequisite for many other courses.

In this section, you learned how to add prerequisites to your course. In the next section, you will discuss and set the course completion criteria.

# Setting the course completion criteria

Your course is almost ready to be published. There is one last important aspect to discuss before you can finally make it available to learners: the course completion criteria.

In Chapter 3, Uploading Learning Content and Managing the Content Library, you uploaded individual content modules and set both the success and completion criteria of each module. This was an important step in making Captivate Prime able to track learner's progress within the course.

But the courses themselves also have completion and success criteria. This step is also very important as completing a course grants the learner credits toward completing skills, badges, and other objectives. Also, remember that a course can be a prerequisite for other courses. Therefore, marking a course as complete may be a requirement for enrolling in other courses.

Let's take a look at these options by performing the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as an Author and return to the Overview page of the course do you want to update.
+   2. Scroll down the page until you see the Sequencing of Modules and Mandatory Modules sections.
