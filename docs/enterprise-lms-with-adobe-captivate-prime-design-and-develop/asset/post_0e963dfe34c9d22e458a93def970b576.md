## Recommendations Based on Peer Activity

The default learner home page of Captivate Prime contains several widgets whose content is provided by the artificial intelligence engine that is built into the platform. In the previous section, we discussed the **Recommended based on your areas of interest** widget, and you learned how the *Admin Defined* and *Industry Aligned* skills affect its content.

In this section, you will explore the **Recommended based on peer activity** widget and the options available to the administrator that affects the recommendations it produces.

To come up with the recommendation provided by that widget, the artificial intelligence must analyze how you and your peers behave on the Prime account. This includes analyzing the courses that learners have enrolled in, the skills covered by those courses, the L1 and L3 feedback that was received, whether or not new courses published on the platform are successful, current industry trends, and more. All of this data is entered in the artificial intelligence to generate the content of the **Recommended based on peer activity** section of the learner home page.

This complex process is mostly automatic and takes place behind the scenes without any intervention from the administrator. However, there is one critical aspect of this process that the administrator can (and should) interfere with. The AI must know what you mean by "peers."

For example, an organization might decide that all users in a specific department are considered "peers." In such a scenario, the AI analyzes the behavior of users in the marketing department separately from users in the development department when generating the recommendations displayed in the **Recommended based on peer activity** section of the learner home page. Other organizations might decide to split users based on geographical location, seniority, or the number of languages spoken. Indeed, some organizations might even decide to divide their group of learners based on several of these characteristics at the same time.

You will now return to the administrator to discover how you can choose the user characteristics used by the AI to determine peer groups within the users defined on your Prime account:

- 1. Use the browser window in which you are logged in as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Branding** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Scroll down the **Branding** page until you see the **Homepage Experience** section.
- 4. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Homepage Experience** option.
- 5. Open the **Internal** drop-down menu associated with the **Identify Learners as Peers based on these fields** option.

As shown in the following screenshot, the **profile** option should be the only one selected in this drop-down menu:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_506_0.png)

Figure 13.6 – The only selected option of the Internal drop-down menu should be profile

This means that learners with the same *profile* are considered peers by Captivate Prime's artificial intelligence. Remember that each organization is free to decide what *profile* means, but most of the time, profile is a synonym of *position, role, rank, or job description* in Captivate Prime.

Note that you can choose up to five of these fields from the list, which allows each organization to precisely define who should be considered as *peers* by the AI. Also, note that Captivate Prime differentiates between internal and external users when defining peer groups.

> **Important note**
> This only applies to Captivate Prime accounts containing more than 1,000 users. If your account has fewer than 1,000 users, the AI does not have enough data to split your learners into peer groups. In such scenarios, all the users are part of the same peer group.

Once again, this option works well on larger accounts containing several thousand users and courses. It is very difficult to experience the full power of this feature on a trial account containing only a few users and courses. Feel free to experiment further with this feature as your account grows larger, both in terms of the number of users and the number of courses available:

- 6. Click the **Cancel** button in the lower-right corner of the page to close these options without saving any changes.

## Using Manual Recommendations

So far, in this chapter, the recommendation types that you've explored are powered by the built-in AI technology of Captivate Prime. The administrator can certainly influence how recommendations are generated, but the process is, for the most part, entirely automatic. These recommendations serve learners well and deliver on Captivate Prime's promise of being a learner-centric LMS.

But Captivate Prime is also an enterprise LMS, and organizations have a say in which courses the platform should recommend to their employees, partners, and customers. In many cases, it is very useful for organizations to ensure that a carefully selected set of courses are given a prominent place on the learner home page. For example:

- You want your employees to have an opportunity to be trained on a new product before an official product launch.
- You need to train your workforce on a new tool your organization is implementing or on a new version of a business-critical enterprise solution used in your company.
- You want to advertise courses related to skills that are not sufficiently available in your organization.
- Etc.

These are just a few of the many circumstances in which manually recommending courses to a specific target audience within your organization can be very helpful. Hopefully, Captivate Prime has you covered! Let's review this feature, hands-on, in this section using the following steps:

- 1. Use the browser window in which you are logged in as an administrator.
- 2. From the admin home page, click the Announcements link located in the Manage section of the left sidebar to open the Announcements page of the administrator.
