If needed, you can drag the modules up and down to reorder them. After completing the preceding steps, you have provided the only two pieces of information that are required to continue with the creation of this course.

+   9. Click the **Save** button located in the top-right area of the page.

This action takes you to the **Settings** page of this particular course, where one additional piece of information is required before Prime allows you to publish this course. But for now, you will simply return to the course catalog.

+   10. Click the **Back** button located at the top of the left sidebar to return to the **Course Catalog** page of the Author role.

The **Course Catalog** page provides a list of all the training material available to the learners in your Captivate Prime account. Unfortunately, your new course is not available on this list yet. Remember that you still need to supply one piece of information before your course can be published, so at this stage, your course is in a *Draft* state.

In this section, you provided the basic pieces of information needed to create a course: you gave your course and name, and you selected the modules that make up the course. But for Captivate Prime, this is not enough to let you publish the course. In the next section, you will link the course to one or more *skills*. This is the missing piece of information before the course can be made available to learners.

# Linking Courses to Skills

At this point, your new course is in a *Draft* state. Before you can publish it, you must link the course to one or more skill(s) by performing the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged in as an Author.
+   2. From the Author home page, click the courses link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. At the top of the list, click the **Draft** tab to access the list of courses currently in a *draft* state. From this list, you should find the course you started to create in the previous section.
+   4. Hover over the lower area of the course card (where the three little dots are visible) and click the **View Course** link. This action will take you back to the form you used in the previous section to create the course.
+   5. Click the **Settings** link located in the left sidebar. This action will take you to the course's **Settings** page. This is where you can pick the *skills* this course is linked to.
+   6. At the top of the form, open the **Pick a Skill** drop-down list.
