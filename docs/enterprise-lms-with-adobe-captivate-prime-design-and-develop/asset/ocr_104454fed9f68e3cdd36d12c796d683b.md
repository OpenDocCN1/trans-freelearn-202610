+   4. When done, click the blue checkmark located at the bottom of **List of Modules** to save your changes.
+   5. Now, click the **Session** link in the course instance properties to open the **Session Details** panel.

This is where you can provide instance-specific details for all the course activities that require it. This is typically the case for the synchronous live or virtual classroom activities that you must supply details for, such as the start and end date, the instructor, the venue, and the seat limit. However, other types of activities also require instance-specific details:

+   - For the **URL** activity, you can supply an instance-specific URL.
+   - You can also designate instructors for the **File Submission** and **Checklist** activities.
