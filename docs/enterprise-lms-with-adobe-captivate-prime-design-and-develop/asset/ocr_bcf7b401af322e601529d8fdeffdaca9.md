> **Caution**
> We insist on the fact that enabling discussion by default on your Prime account does affect all the courses, *even those that were available before enabling the Discussion Board checkbox*. Make sure that everyone is made aware of the change so that appropriate action can be taken in a timely fashion. To avoid any confusion, this setting is probably something you want to turn on or leave off when you first implement Captivate Prime in your organization.

At the individual course level, if the discussion board is not explicitly enabled for a specific course (see the previous section), the account-wide setting is applied. Since the account-wide setting has been enabled, it is no longer possible to disable the discussion board at the course level. This is probably why most organizations want to keep the account-wide discussion board setting disabled: to allow administrators to enable discussion from course to course based on the wishes of the instructors and administrators in each course.

This concludes your exploration of course discussions in Captivate Prime. This feature attempts to mimic the informal conversations that occur naturally alongside any face-to-face training. It is very simple to implement and use, but its capabilities are limited. Let's quickly recap on what you have learned in this section:

+   - Course discussion is disabled by default at the account level, but administrators can override this general account setting and enable discussions on a course-to-course basis.
+   - When course discussion is enabled for a course, any enrolled learner can post messages. However, there is no built-in mechanism to answer someone else's post or to like/dislike a message.
+   - Users can delete their own messages, but not the messages that have been posted by other users. Administrators can delete any posted message, even if they are not enrolled in the course.
+   - When you enable **Discussion Board** at the account level, this immediately enables the discussion board of every course currently available on your Prime account, with no way of disabling it on a course-to-course basis.
