# Using industry-aligned skills

The current learner experience you reviewed in the previous section works very well when you have a somewhat limited course catalog containing mostly courses defined internally by in-house authors or contractors. However, most large organizations also include third-party off-the-shelf content in the course catalog of their Prime accounts.

There are a lot of learning content providers in the marketplace. Many larger organizations subscribe to third-party learning content libraries in order to provide their workforce access to a wealth of learning resources. Some of the most famous providers include LinkedIn Learning (previously known as Lynda.com), Udemy, Open Classrooms, and Pluralsight.

> **Note**
> The August 2021 update of Captivate Prime introduced the Content Marketplace, which is Adobe's own offering of off-the-shelf content. This feature will be discussed in Chapter 14, Working with Catalogs and Peer Accounts.

Captivate Prime is able to integrate this external content into its own course catalogs either through dedicated integrations (we will discuss that in Chapter 16, Exploring the Integration Admin Role) or through some kind of custom development.

Using this capability, you greatly expand the list of skills covered by the courses available on your Prime account. This is great news for your learners and the organization as a whole, but it makes administering and tracking these skills a lot more complicated!

+   - How do you maintain the list of skills available on your Prime account when most courses come from these external libraries? Not to mention that these libraries are constantly being updated by their respective owners.
+   - How do you map the skills covered by these massive course catalogs to the skills your company and employees need the most?
+   - How do you mine these huge course catalogs to make relevant course recommendations in an ever-changing world where industries and regulations are in constant motion?

These are some of the questions you need to address to ensure that both the learners and the organization get the most out of this massive course offering.
