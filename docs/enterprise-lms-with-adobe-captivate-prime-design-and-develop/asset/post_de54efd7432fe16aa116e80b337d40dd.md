
This report provides information on the trainings of your Prime account that are considered *active*. Active trainings are trainings that are enrolled, viewed in the Fluidic Player, or completed within the given timeframe. By default, the **Active Trainings Report** shows data pertaining to the current month, but this can be changed using the **Date Range** drop-down menu:

- 5. Open the **Date Range** dropdown located above Active Trainings Report.
- 6. Choose any date range available and see how the report is immediately updated.
- 7. Open the **Date Range** drop-down again and select **This Month**, which is the default value.

Note that you can also filter this report by **User Group** using the very same technique as for the reports included in the **User Activity** dashboard discussed earlier.

**Start Ratio** represents the percentage of learners that effectively started the training after enrollment.

The **Learner Feedback** column is the average of all L1 feedback on a scale from 1 to 10. Remember that L1 feedback is provided by the learners at the end of the course.

## Exploring the Learning Summary dashboard

For learning to occur on your Prime account, you need learners on one end and learning content on the other end. The **User Activity** dashboard discussed earlier focused on the *learner* aspect, while the **Training Content** dashboard discussed in the previous section focused on the *learning content* aspect.

In this section, you will explore the **Learning Summary** dashboard, which is the third default dashboard available in Captivate Prime. This third dashboard focuses on the interactions between learners and the learning content. From that perspective, this dashboard contains reports that measure the *liveliness* of your Prime account.

The reports contained on this dashboard allow you to answer questions such as:

- How many enrollments, views, and course completions have taken place on the Prime account? How many learners are actually involved in these statistics?
- What are the skills acquired by the learners in a given period of time? This helps you figure out whether the skills available in the workforce match the needs and targets of the organization.
- How are learners doing with the deadlines? Are they taking their assigned courses on time, so they remain compliant? This is especially useful in organizations using Captivate Prime for compliance training.

Let's now take a look at the **Learning Summary** dashboard and at the reports it contains using the following steps:

- 1. Log into your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Reports** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Next, click **Learning Summary** located in the left sidebar.

This last action takes you to the **Learning Summary** dashboard. From top to bottom, this dashboard contains three main reports: the **Summary** report, the **Skills Status** report, and the **Compliance Dashboard**.

This dashboard is a little different than those discussed in the previous two sections. First, notice that there is no **Export** button visible on this dashboard. There is also the **Showing Data For** drop-down menu in the top-right corner of the dashboard. This drop-down menu affects all three reports displayed on this dashboard:

- 4. Open the **Showing Data For** drop-down menu located in the upper-right corner of the **Learning Summary** dashboard and take some time to examine its content.

The options available in the **Showing Data For** drop-down menu include all *root users* as well as all *external profiles*.
