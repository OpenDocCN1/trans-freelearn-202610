# Customizing the email banner and the email signature

You may have already noticed that all emails sent from Captivate Prime start with the same *banner* and end with the same *signature*. In this section, you will customize both these elements. As you do so, keep in mind that these options affect all the emails that's sent from your Captivate Prime account.

Perform the following steps to customize **Email Banner** and **Email Signature**:

+   1. Make sure you are on the **Settings** tab of the **Email Templates** section of the administrator.
+   2. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Email Banner** option.

From here, you can choose a solid background color or upload a custom banner image. In this exercise, you will upload a banner image that is included in the download associated with this book.

+   3. Select the **Custom Image** radio button.
+   4. Upload the `branding/emailBanner.jpg` image available in the downloads associated with this book.
+   5. When the image is uploaded, switch the **Show Email Text** option to **Yes** to see where the title of the email will appear on the banner image.
+   6. Optionally, you can click the **Preview** icon (the icon with an eye) to have a closer look at your new banner.
+   7. When your screen looks as follows, click the **Save** button to save your changes:

Note that the uploaded image must be either a .jpg or a .png file with a size of 1,240 x 200 pixels.
