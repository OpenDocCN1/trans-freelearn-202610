**LDAP**
**LDAP** stands for **Lightweight Directory Access Protocol**. It is the most widely used industry standard for accessing and maintaining directory services within a computer network. Most enterprise directory software complies with the LDAP standard. The easiest way to see LDAP in action is to look at your global address book in your email systems, which shows a functional view of the LDAP directory.

Since most organizations already have a list of their users in the directory deployed within their IT infrastructure, it makes little sense to redefine these same users again in Adobe Captivate Prime. That's why there are several ways in Captivate Prime to automatically import the users defined in these external directories and to keep this list of users up to date.

# Automated User Skill export

As discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, the concept of *skills* is at the very heart of the entire training strategy of most organizations. Learners take courses to acquire new skills, and organizations provide training opportunities for their workforce to develop and maintain a current and compliant set of skills within the organization.

Many organizations go even further and include skills management in the broader scope of Human Resources Management by providing additional benefits and career opportunities to those individuals that have achieved certain skills.

That is why Captivate Prime allows you to automatically export user skill reports to your preferred Human Resources Management System.

# Exporting report data from Captivate Prime

Data is the new *El Dorado* for a growing number of businesses. Massive datasets are constantly being harvested and are fueling data mining and visualization technologies used to provide organizations with historical, current, and predictive views of their business operations.

Adobe Captivate Prime is only one of many platforms that produce such data, and although it offers extensive reporting capabilities (as discussed in *Chapter 10, Reporting in Captivate Prime*), these are limited to analyzing the data produced within your Prime account.
