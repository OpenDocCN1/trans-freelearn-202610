## Assigning Badges to Courses and Learning Programs

Another way to earn badges is by completing courses and learning programs. To do this, administrators must first link badges to courses and learning programs. This is precisely what you will do in this section.

To be more exact, you will link the badges to course instances and learning program instances. This allows you to have different badges for each course instance (kind of like how each Space Shuttle mission had its own badge).

You will now assign a badge to the *Mastering Photo Editing* learning program you created in *Chapter 6, Enrolling Users to Courses*, using the following steps:

- 1. From the administrator home page, click the **Learning Programs** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 2. Hover over the **Mastering Photo Editing** learning program's card.
  (If the **Mastering Photo Editing** learning program is not available on your Prime account, feel free to use any other learning program or course available to you.)
- 3. Click the **View Learning Program** link to open the learning program **Overview** page.
- 4. Click the **Instances** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
- 5. Click the **Badges** link associated with the instance you want to modify.
  In the case of the **Mastering Photo Editing** learning program, only the default instance is available.
- 6. Toggle the **Enable Badge** switch to on.
- 7. Open the **Choose from the following Badges** drop-down menu and choose the **Photo Editing Master** badge you created earlier in this section.
- 8. When your screen looks as in the following screenshot, click the blue checkmark (identified by the arrow) to validate your changes:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_414_0.png)

From now on, every learner that completes the *Mastering Photo Editing* learning program will be awarded the *Photo Editing Master* badge.

## Assigning badges to certifications

As discussed in the *Creating certifications* section of *Chapter 6, Enrolling Users to Courses*, certifications are similar to learning programs in that they also allow you to group related courses together. What makes them different from learning programs is the fact that they have a time limit to complete and that they can expire after a certain amount of time.

When it comes to badges, Captivate Prime allows you to assign a badge to a certification so that every learner that completes the certification on time is awarded the corresponding badge. Let's review this feature using the following steps:

- 1. From the administrator home page, click the **Certifications** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 2. Hover over the **Certified Prime Learner** certification you created back in *Chapter 6, Enrolling Users to Courses*.
  (If the **Certified Prime Learner** certification does not exist in your Prime account, feel free to use any certification available to you.)
- 3. Click the **View Certification** link to open the **Overview** page of the chosen certification.
- 4. Click the **Edit** button located in the upper-right corner of the screen.
- 5. Scroll down the page until you see the **Badge** option shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_416_0.png)

- 6. Open the **Choose from the following Badges** drop-down menu and choose the **Certified Prime Learner** badge created earlier in the section.
- 7. Finally, click the **Republish** button in the upper-right corner of the screen to validate your changes and make them available in the course catalog.

## Retiring Badges

As the learning content of your Captivate Prime account evolves, so do the badges. In this section, you will learn how to retire and delete old badges using the following steps:

- 1. From the administrator home page, click the **Badges** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar to open the **Badges** page.
- 2. Use the checkboxes in the upper-left corner of each badge to select one or more of the available badges (in this example, make sure you do not select any of the four custom badges created earlier in this chapter).
- 3. Open the **Actions** drop-down menu located in the upper-right corner of the screen.
- 4. Click **Retire** to retire the selected badges.

A *retired* badge can no longer be linked to any skill, course, learning program, or certification, but it remains available in the skills, courses, learning programs, and certifications to which it is already linked. In addition, learners who have been awarded retired badges retain them and can continue to use them as usual.

- 5. Click the **Retired** tab located in the upper-left corner of the page to open the list of retired badges.
- 6. Again, use the checkboxes to select one or more of the retired badges.
- 7. Open the **Actions** dropdown located in the upper-right corner of the screen and inspect the available options.
- 8. In this example, select all the retired badges and republish them to return to the initial situation.

Clicking the **Republish** option puts the selected badges back in the *active* state, so they can be assigned to skills, courses, learning programs, and certifications again.

Clicking the **Delete** button definitively deletes the selected badges from the system.

In this section, you learned how to *retire* and *delete* existing badges. This concludes your exploration of badges in Captivate Prime. As a reminder, badges are a visual representation of a learner's achievement. Learners are awarded badges by acquiring new skills, taking courses and learning programs, and getting certified. By default, Captivate Prime contains a handful of predefined badges but it is very easy to define your own custom badges. You can also *retire* and then *delete* existing badges (including those provided by Captivate Prime out of the box) very easily.

> **Note**
> 
> There is absolutely no obligation to use badges in Captivate Prime. In fact, organizations that are new to badges are not always sure how they will be perceived by their staff, or even whether they will incentivize learners at all. Badges can be something for later on in the Captivate Prime deployment, once learners are up and running with the platform.
