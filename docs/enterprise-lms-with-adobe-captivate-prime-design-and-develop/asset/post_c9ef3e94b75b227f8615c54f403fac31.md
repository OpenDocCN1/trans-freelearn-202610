## Automating User Creation and Updates

In the *Creating internal and external users* section, earlier in this chapter, you reviewed different methods for adding internal and external users to your Captivate Prime account. These methods work well and may be all you need, but they have some serious limitations, especially for larger organizations and multinational corporations.

- First, many organizations already have directories where all the user data Captivate Prime needs is available. Therefore, it makes little sense to duplicate this data in the Captivate Prime account. For many organizations, importing existing data from whatever system is used in the organization is probably a far better approach.
- Creating users is one thing, but maintaining the user list over time is a completely different challenge. Employees are constantly joining and leaving the organization, people change roles, departments, or locations, managers change positions, new teams are created, and old teams are dismantled as the business evolves. Of course, the larger the organization, the bigger the challenge of maintaining an up-to-date user list. This is another instance where duplicating user data makes little sense. Captivate Prime should be able to update its user list whenever the central corporate directory is updated.
- Furthermore, within large corporations, government entities, and other organizations, there's full-time staff who closely manage users and groups, so why not harness their hard work and do justice to their daily commitment to the organization?

As an enterprise LMS, Captivate Prime understands that it needs to fit within your current user management solution. It also understands that it needs to scale with your organization, no matter how large your business might grow.

You will now review how Captivate Prime can be integrated with your existing system to automate user creation and update.
