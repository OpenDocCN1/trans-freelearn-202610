## Exporting the Feedback report

In the previous chapter, we discussed L1 feedback and L3 feedback. As a reminder, L1 feedback is provided by the learner directly upon course completion, while L3 feedback is provided by the learner's manager after the training has ended. In the Retrieving L1, L2, and L3 feedback section of Chapter 9, Configuring and Using Feedback, you saw how you can access this data for particular training.

We will now explore another way of accessing L1 and L3 feedback with the added benefit of being able to consolidate the data of several different courses into a single export:

- 1. From the Excel Reports tab of the Custom Reports section of the administrator, click the Feedback Report link to open the Feedback Report dialog.
- 2. Take some time to examine the available options.
  In this example, choose to download feedback for All Trainings and choose a Feedback Date Range setting of at least one month to have enough data to export.
- 3. Click the Download button when ready. Once again, read the Generating Report Request message and click the OK button.
- 4. When the report is ready, a notification appears under the bell icon in the upper-right corner of the screen. Click the notification to download the Login/Access report in CSV format.
- 5. Open the downloaded CSV file in your favorite spreadsheet application and inspect the available data.

## Subscribing to reports

In the *Creating custom reports* section earlier in this chapter, you saw how to create a wide number of meaningful reports. But these reports are only useful if you visit them from time to time and examine how the figures evolve. Captivate Prime understands that administrators and managers are very busy with the day-to-day management of the organization and do not always have the opportunity to come and review statistics and reports directly on the platform.

That's why you can have Captivate Prime send your preferred reports by email on a regular basis. Note that this is only possible with the custom reports you have created yourself or with the custom reports available on custom dashboards that have been shared with you.

Let's see how you can subscribe to such custom reports using the following steps:

- 1. From the **Custom Reports** section of the administrator, click the **Dashboard Reports** tab to return to the custom reports you created earlier in this chapter.
- 2. Choose the report you want to subscribe to. Keep in mind that you may have additional reports available on custom dashboards. These are also eligible for subscription.
  In this example, I choose the **Learning Time Spent for External User** report created earlier.
- 3. Switch to the **Subscription** tab of the custom reports page.
- 4. Enter the name of the report you want to subscribe to in the **Reports** field. Click the chosen report when it appears in the list.
- 5. Open the **Frequency** drop-down menu and choose the most appropriate option.
- 6. If needed, you can customize the **Subject of the email** in the corresponding field.
- 7. You can also customize the email address(es) the report should be sent to in the **Alternate email** field. If you leave this field empty, the reports will be sent to the email address associated with your Captivate Prime profile.
- 8. Click the blue check icon at the end of the row to validate your new subscription.

As shown in the following screenshot, you should now have one subscription listed on this page.

Keep in mind that such subscriptions are only possible with custom reports that you create yourself or that are present on the dashboards that are shared with you. This is yet another great reason to create custom reports in the first place!

## Reporting Features for Managers

So far, you have experienced the reporting features of Captivate Prime when logged in as an administrator. In this section, you will discover that most of the features discussed in this chapter are also available to managers.

There is one important difference between accessing the reports as an administrator versus accessing the reports as a manager. Administrators have access to all the data collected by Captivate Prime while managers only have access to the data pertaining to their team.

You will now log in to Captivate Prime as Manager 01 and explore the reporting features from a manager's perspective:

- 1. Log into your Captivate Prime account as Manager 01.

Remember that in Captivate Prime, a manager is first and foremost a learner just like any other. This is the reason why Manager 01 is directed to the learning experience by default.

- 2. Open the user menu by clicking the user icon located in the upper-right corner of the screen.
- 3. Click the **Manager** option to switch to the manager experience. When the page reloads, you should be logged in as a manager.

## Summary

In this chapter, you reviewed the extensive reporting capabilities available in Captivate Prime. These features can be seen as the fourth level of Kirkpatrick's *Four Levels of Learning Evaluation* model. This is the **results** level where organizations take advantage of the data collected to measure the impact of learning on the organization as a whole.

A bunch of reports are available by default on every Prime account, but you can also create your own custom reports, export various datasets as CSV files, and subscribe to reports to have Captivate Prime send you the data pertaining to custom reports by email on a regular basis. Reports are organized into dashboards, and you can create your own custom dashboards in addition to the default dashboards available in Captivate Prime. Administrators can even share the custom dashboards they create with the managers present on the Prime account.

These very powerful reporting features contribute to establishing Captivate Prime as one of the best modern enterprise LMSes on the market.
