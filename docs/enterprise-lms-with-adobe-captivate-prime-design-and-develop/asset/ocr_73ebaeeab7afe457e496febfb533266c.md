# Understanding the Course Effectiveness Score

In addition to the star rating discussed in the previous section, Captivate Prime also calculates a course effectiveness score for each course.

The course effectiveness score is automatically calculated by Captivate Prime and is obtained by combining the results from the L1, L2, and L3 feedback. Captivate Prime calculates the effectiveness as a score out of 100 points for each course and learning program available on your Prime account. Let’s take a look at it hands-on using the following steps:

+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar to access the list of courses currently available on your Prime account.
