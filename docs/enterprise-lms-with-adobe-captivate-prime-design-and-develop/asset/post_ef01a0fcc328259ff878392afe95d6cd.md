
## Reviewing the Administrator options

Back in *Chapter 2, Customize the Look and Feel of Captivate Prime*, we discussed the differences between the **Classic** learner experience and the **Immersive** learner experience, and we customized the content that was available on the learner home page. Let's quickly review these options and make sure that you are ready for the exercises of this chapter:

- 1. Use the browser window in which you are logged in as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Branding** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Scroll down the **General** page until you can see the **Homepage Experience** option.
- 4. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Homepage Experience** option. This enables all the options that are located at the bottom of the **General** page, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_495_0.png)

Figure 13.1 – The Homepage Experience section of the Branding page

- 5. Make sure **Homepage Experience** is set to **Immersive**.

As a reminder, the immersive experience was introduced in the December 2020 update. It has been designed as a Netflix-like interface displaying personalized recommendations to each learner. We discussed this in the *Customizing the learner experience* section of *Chapter 2, Customize the Look and Feel of Captivate Prime*.

- 6. Make sure the **Training Type** option is currently set to **Custom**.

This tells Captivate Prime that the course catalog mostly contains custom courses (that is, courses you have defined yourself, such as the **Fundamentals of Cybersecurity** course that you created in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*). This works well for smaller course catalogs containing very few (if any) third-party off-the-shelf contents. Therefore, Captivate Prime's AI-driven recommendation engine only needs to consider the skills linked to your custom courses to generate recommendations. Remember that you have explicitly defined these skills and manually linked them to your courses using the features discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*.

- 7. Make sure the **Enable Learner to Explore Areas of Interest (Skills)** and the **Prompt Learner to choose Areas of Interest (Skills)** options are both set to **Yes**. We will discuss these options further in the *Reviewing the Learner Experience* section later in this chapter.
- 8. When your screen looks similar to *Figure 13.1*, click the **Save** button to save the changes and apply these options to your Captivate Prime account.
- 9. Click the **Learner Homepage** link located at the top of the left sidebar to open the **Learner Homepage Settings** page.

As discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, this is the place where administrators can modify the content of the learner's home page.

- 10. At the top of the page, make sure the **Immersive** switch is turned on.
- 11. Then, click the **Reset to Default** link located in the upper-right corner of the page.
- 12. Scroll through the whole page from top to bottom and review the applied settings. Most of them should be self-explanatory. Make sure you keep them in mind for the next section.
- 13. When ready, click the **Save** button located in the upper-right corner of the page.

These options and switches are now in their correct configurations for the exercises of the next section, where you will review the current learner experience.

## Reviewing the Learner Experience

You will now switch to the browser window in which you are logged in as *Learner 01*. Here, we will quickly review some key areas of the learner experience that are influenced by the options you manipulated in the previous section. Perform the following steps to do so:

- 1. Use the browser window in which you are logged in as *Learner 01*.
- 2. Make sure you refresh your browser window to apply the changes you made in the previous section.
- 3. Scroll down the learner home page, paying special attention to the contents of the **Recommended based on your areas of interest** and **Recommended based on peer activity** sections.

These are the two sections of the learner home page where the effects of the AI-powered recommendation engine are visible. The contents that you can view in these two sections have been carefully selected by the AI and are specific to *Learner 01*.

Remember that, in Captivate Prime, the expression *areas of interest* is a synonym of *skills*, so the contents of the **Recommended based on your areas of interest** section are directly dependent on the skills affecting the *Learner 01* profile. These skills are determined using a variety of methods:

- First, there are the skills related to the courses that *Learner 01* has enrolled in. For example, the **Fundamentals of cybersecurity** course is linked to the **Cybersecurity level 1** skill, as defined in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*. So, when *Learner 01* enrolled in the **Fundamentals of Cybersecurity** course back in *Chapter 6, Enrolling Learners to Courses*, the **Cybersecurity level 1** skill was automatically linked to *Learner 01*'s profile, so any other course related to the same skill is considered by the artificial intelligence when generating the **Recommended based on your areas of interest** section of the learner home page.
- Additionally, remember that administrators can assign skills to individual learners and/or user groups. This allows organizations to promote the skills they want their employees to focus on. These administrator-assigned skills are also considered by the artificial intelligence when generating recommendations.
- Because you have set the **Enable Learner to Explore Areas of Interest (Skills)** option to **Yes** in the administrator application, learners can also handpick their *areas of interest* using the **Skills** section of the learner experience.

Now, let's return to the learner experience of Captivate Prime and quickly review the **Skills** page used by learners to modify their *areas of interest* (that is, the list of skills they are interested in):

- 4. Click the **Skills** icon from the left-hand side column of the learner experience to open the skills page of *Learner 01*.

As shown in the following screenshot, this page lists the *Skills* (aka **Areas of Interest**) linked to *Learner 01*'s profile:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_498_0.png)

Figure 13.2 – The skills related to Learner 01's profile

These are the skills that the AI considers when generating the contents of the **Recommended based on your areas of interest** section of the home page. Remember that *Learner 01* is free to modify this list at any time.

- 5. Use your mouse to hover over any of these skills. This reveals a cross icon that you can use to remove the skill from the list.
- 6. Click the **Add Interest** link to open the **Add to my Areas of Interest** page.

This page lists all the available skills that are not currently part of *Learner 01*'s areas of interest.

*Learner 01* can click the skills they are interested in to select them and then click the **Add** button in the lower-right corner of the page to add them to the list of skills considered by the AI when populating the content of the learner home page.

In the last two sections, you reviewed the current learner experience provided by your Prime trial account and the corresponding administrator options. Additionally, you rediscovered the concept of **Skills** in Captivate Prime and its critical importance when the AI generates the unique home page of each individual learner.

Generating the **Recommended based on your areas of interest** section of the learner home page is an example of a process that takes place automatically behind the scenes but is deeply influenced by the choices made by both administrators and learners.
