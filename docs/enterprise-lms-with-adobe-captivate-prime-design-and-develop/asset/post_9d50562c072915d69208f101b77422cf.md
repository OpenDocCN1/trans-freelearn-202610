
## LDAP
**LDAP** stands for **Lightweight Directory Access Protocol**. It is the most widely used industry standard for accessing and maintaining directory services within a computer network. Most enterprise directory software complies with the LDAP standard. The easiest way to see LDAP in action is to look at your global address book in your email systems, which shows a functional view of the LDAP directory.

Since most organizations already have a list of their users in the directory deployed within their IT infrastructure, it makes little sense to redefine these same users again in Adobe Captivate Prime. That's why there are several ways in Captivate Prime to automatically import the users defined in these external directories and to keep this list of users up to date.

## Automated User Skill export

As discussed in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, the concept of *skills* is at the very heart of the entire training strategy of most organizations. Learners take courses to acquire new skills, and organizations provide training opportunities for their workforce to develop and maintain a current and compliant set of skills within the organization.

Many organizations go even further and include skills management in the broader scope of Human Resources Management by providing additional benefits and career opportunities to those individuals that have achieved certain skills.

That is why Captivate Prime allows you to automatically export user skill reports to your preferred Human Resources Management System.

## Exporting report data from Captivate Prime

Data is the new *El Dorado* for a growing number of businesses. Massive datasets are constantly being harvested and are fueling data mining and visualization technologies used to provide organizations with historical, current, and predictive views of their business operations.

Adobe Captivate Prime is only one of many platforms that produce such data, and although it offers extensive reporting capabilities (as discussed in *Chapter 10, Reporting in Captivate Prime*), these are limited to analyzing the data produced within your Prime account.

The ability to export this data from Adobe Captivate Prime allows it to be integrated with much larger datasets produced by a wide variety of systems within the organization. Organizations can then use data mining and visualization technologies to cross-reference Prime-generated learning data with a host of other data from a variety of sources for integration into their overall business intelligence efforts.

## Data exchange with virtual classroom solutions

Back in *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, you learned that a Captivate Prime course can comprise both **synchronous** and **asynchronous** modules:

- *Asynchronous* modules are training content uploaded to the LMS that learners access individually at their own convenience. Typical asynchronous modules are instructional videos, PDF documents, PowerPoint presentations, and SCORM-compliant interactive learning modules.
- *Synchronous* modules are live or virtual classes where multiple participants are together in the same physical or virtual space. Traditional instructor-led training, which takes place in a classroom or lecture hall, and virtual classes delivered using any type of video conferencing solution are typical examples of synchronous modules.

Synchronous modules are hosted directly on your Prime account using your account's content library, as described in *Chapter 3, Uploading Learning Content and Managing the Content Library*. But Captivate Prime has no built-in solution for conducting virtual classrooms.

In other words, when adding synchronous modules to your courses, you must rely on an external video conferencing system or virtual classroom solution such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Adobe Connect.

Hopefully, Captivate Prime is able to exchange data with these popular virtual classroom solutions. For example, attendance data, interaction data, and completion statuses can be automatically synced between most of these solutions and your Prime account.

## Integration of external content libraries

Captivate Prime can exchange data with virtually any third-party instructional content library, either through a dedicated connector or through custom development using the Captivate Prime API. This allows you to display courses provided by these external libraries in your Prime account and track your learners' interactions with this external content.

## Connecting to cloud services and FTP accounts

Captivate Prime can import and export all kinds of data using Box.com (a popular cloud-based file sharing solution) or a generic FTP connection. This allows you to define custom workflows for data exchange between Captivate Prime and virtually any enterprise solution used in your organization.

> **CSV and FTP**
> 
> **CSV** stands for **Comma-Separated Values**. A CSV file is a delimited text file that uses a separator (usually a comma, a tab, or a semicolon) to separate each value. The CSV file format is a very popular generic solution for exchanging data between all kinds of systems. **FTP** stands for **File Transfer Protocol**. It is a standard communication protocol used for transferring files between computers.

One such workflow was discussed in detail in *Chapter 5, Managing Users*, to automatically import user data into Captivate Prime using CSV files uploaded to an FTP server.

In this section, you have had a high-level overview of the main types of data that can be exchanged between Captivate Prime and other systems used in the organization. In essence, these data exchanges can be categorized as follows:

- **User data**: This allows Captivate Prime to retrieve users defined in other systems.
- **Reporting data**: This allows you to export reporting data from Captivate Prime for further analysis with dedicated data mining and visualization tools.
- **Learning activity data**: This allows you to use external virtual classroom solutions and learning content libraries and synchronize completion statuses, quiz results, and other data with Captivate Prime.

In the next section, you will learn how the various connectors built into Captivate Prime help you implement these data exchanges.

## Exploring the Available Connectors

Now that you know a bit more about the main types of data that can be exchanged to and from Adobe Captivate Prime, let's look at how to implement them in your Prime account. These data exchanges can be done either through dedicated connectors available out of the box in Adobe Captivate Prime or through the Captivate Prime API, which requires some custom development.

In this section, you will log in to your Prime account as an integration admin and explore the connectors available out of the box in Adobe Captivate Prime. Use the following steps to start your exploration of the built-in connectors available in Prime:

- 1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator (or as any other user that has the Integration Admin role assigned).
- 2. Click the user icon located in the upper-right corner of the screen to reveal the list of roles assigned to the currently logged-in user.

- 5. Hover your mouse over the Salesforce connector and click the Getting Started link that appears on the screen.
