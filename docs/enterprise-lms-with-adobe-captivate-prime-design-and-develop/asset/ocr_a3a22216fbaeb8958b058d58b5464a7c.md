+   12. Click the **Catalog** tab located in the top-left area of the page.
+   13. Search for the **Learner Experience in Adobe Captivate Prime** course (this is one of the courses that is automatically added to your course catalog when creating a Prime trial account).
+   14. When spotted, hover over the lower area of the course card and click the **Add** link.
+       You must repeat this process for all the courses you want to add to this certification. In this example, though, the **Learning Experience in Adobe Captivate Prime** course is the only one you need.
+   15. Return to the **Curriculum** tab located in the top-left area of the screen.
+   16. Confirm that the chosen course is the only one in the certification and take some time to inspect the available options. Notice the **Completion Requirements** drop-down list located above the list of courses.
+   17. When you're ready, click the **Publish** button located in the top-right corner of the page.

Just like for courses and learning programs, clicking the **Publish** button is what makes the certification officially part of your course catalog. From now on, the certification is available in the course catalog and is open for enrollment.

> **Note**
> You must provide at least one course before you can publish an *internal* certification. *External* certifications can be published without mentioning any course.

The options for maintaining certifications are mostly identical to those used for maintaining learning programs and courses and will not be discussed further here.

After reading these few sections, you have seen that courses are *not* the only thing learners can enroll in:

+   - First, you discovered *course instances* and the fact that learners enroll in course instances, not courses, per se.
+   - You also grouped several courses into a *learning program*. Learners who enroll in such a learning program enroll in all courses that are part of the learning program at once.
+   - Finally, you created a certification. Certifications are similar to learning programs, but they add a time limit for completion, as well as the ability to create recurring certifications with automatic re-enrollment of learners.
