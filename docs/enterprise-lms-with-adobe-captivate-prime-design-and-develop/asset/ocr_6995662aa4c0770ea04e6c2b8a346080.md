# Summary

With this chapter, you have made Captivate Prime your own by customizing the name of your account and the URL at which it is accessible. You have also customized the look and feel of your account by uploading your company logo and by making the platform use the colors of your brand identity. These are critical aspects in making Captivate Prime part of the overall digital experience your organization aims to provide to its employees, partners, suppliers, customers, and so on.

You have also discussed other possible customizations that you will review throughout the various chapters of this book. Finally, you discovered that Adobe Captivate Prime can be used as a headless LMS. This allows you to decouple the learning experience from the Captivate Prime backend, which makes virtually any customization possible.

You are now one step closer to providing a highly engaging experience to your learners. That being said, there is still work to be done before you can welcome your first learners into your new Prime account. Indeed, your account is still completely empty. It lacks both the custom course content that you shall provide to your learners, and the learners themselves!

You will solve one of these two issues in the next two chapters, in which you will learn how to upload custom learning modules to the platform and how to assemble these modules into courses.
