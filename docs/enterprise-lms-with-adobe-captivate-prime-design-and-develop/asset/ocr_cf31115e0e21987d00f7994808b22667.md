+   3. Once on the **Branding** page, click the **Learner Homepage link** in the left sidebar to open the **Learner Homepage Settings** page.
+   4. At the top of the **Learner Homepage Settings** page, toggle the **Immersive** switch on.

Toggling this switch on displays the options for the *Immersive* experience. Keep in mind that these options currently have no influence on the experience you provide to your learners as they are still using the old *Classic* experience.

A preview of the *Immersive* experience is displayed on the right side of the page. This preview should be similar to the learner homepage you reviewed in the previous section. The available options are displayed in the left column.

+   5. Take some time to inspect the available options. Most of them should be self-explanatory.

Note that some sections of the homepage (**Recommended by <your account name>** and **Browse by Catalog**) are not enabled by default. This is because these sections need some additional input from the administrator before displaying relevant content. You will review these options in more detail in *Chapter 13, AI-Powered Recommendations for Learners.*

+   6. Use the preview on the right to rearrange the sections of the *Immersive* homepage using simple drag and drop actions.

Similar to what you did when customizing the *Classic* experience, you can reorganize the sections of the *Immersive* experience using simple drag and drop actions. Note that some sections (such as the **Masthead**, the **My Learning** section and the **Footer**) cannot be rearranged in such a way.

+   7. Rearrange the *Immersive* homepage as you see fit.
+   8. When done, click the **Save** button located in the top right corner of the screen.
+   9. After Prime displays a quick **Settings Saved** message, return to the browser tab/window in which the *Immersive* experience is displayed (use the following link if that tab/window is not open anymore: https://captivateprime.adobe.com/app/learner?learnerLayout=IMMERSIVE).
+   10. Refresh the page and confirm that it conforms to the new settings you established in the administrative area.
