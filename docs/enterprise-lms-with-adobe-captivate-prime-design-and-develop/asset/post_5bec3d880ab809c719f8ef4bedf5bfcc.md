## Adding an instructor-led activity to a course

So far, the activity modules you have added to your course are all self-paced learning activities. These self-paced activities (also known as *asynchronous* activities) have been uploaded to the Content Library before they could be used to build courses. There's nothing wrong with that! Many excellent online courses are simple collections of asynchronous self-paced learning modules.

That being said, you can also add *synchronous* learning activities to a Captivate Prime course. *Synchronous* learning activities are instructor-led training delivered live, either in an actual classroom or lecture hall, or using a virtual classroom environment such as Adobe Connect, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams.

This capability allows Captivate Prime to address the following use cases:

- You can create courses that include only *synchronous* learning activities (live or virtual). In *Chapter 8, Exploring the Instructor Role*, you will learn that Captivate Prime has features to help you manage class attendance, waitlists, reminders, skills acquired during live or virtual classes, and more.
- You can create courses that include a blend of self-paced asynchronous activities and synchronous live or virtual classes. For example, you can schedule a live class after learners have reviewed several self-paced modules that are then considered prerequisites to the live class. This **blended learning** approach is very popular with learners, trainers, and Human Resources because it allows you to combine the benefits of both asynchronous and synchronous learning approaches.

This is another illustration of the wide range of tools available to instructional designers to create engaging and effective learning experiences.

Let's add a *synchronous* learning activity to the course you are currently building:

- 1. Make sure you are logged in as an Author.
- 2. From the Author home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Click the **Draft** tab to access the list of courses currently in the *Draft* state.

4. Hover over the bottom part of the Course Card and click the **View Course** link.

This action takes you back to the course **Overview** page, where you can define the main properties of the course, including the modules that make up its content.

5. Scroll down the page until you see the **Modules** section. The list of modules that are already part of this course should be visible in the **Content** section.

6. Click the **Add Modules** link located at the top of the **Content** section.

7. This time, choose the **Classroom Module** option to add a live instructor-led class to your course.

8. Fill in the form with the required information, as shown in the following screenshot.

9. When you're ready, click the **Done** button, located in the lower-right corner of the form.

10. Finally, click the **Save** button in the upper-right corner of the page to save the changes to the course:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_142_0.png)

Figure 4.9 – Adding a Classroom Module to your course

One of the main differences between a self-paced *asynchronous* module and a *synchronous* module (live or virtual) is the fact that a *synchronous* module is time-dependent:
**Classroom Module**, which you just created, has a **Start Date** and **Time**, as well as an **End Date** and **Time.**

This raises the question of the learner's enrollment. What happens when a learner enrolls in the course *after* the start/end date of the synchronous course activities? This is the purpose of course **instances**, which you will study in *Chapter 6, Enrolling Learners to Courses.*

The **Seat Limit** field is important for class registration and waiting list management.
In this example, make sure you choose a somewhat low seat limit (5, in the preceding screenshot).

Also, notice the **Instructor** field. You probably tried to enter your name in the **Instructor** field when creating the activity. Unfortunately, by default, Prime won't let you do this! This is because you are not registered as a potential instructor within the system. Assigning roles to users will be covered in *Chapter 5, Managing Users.* Until then, you can safely leave this field empty.

Finally, the **Do you want to add a quiz score for this Module** checkbox allows you to decide if a grade is awarded to learners attending this live class or not. Assigning grades to the class attendees is one of the responsibilities devoted to the instructor. You will explore the instructor role in *Chapter 8, Exploring the Instructor Role.* Until then, make sure you leave this box deselected.

> **Authors versus instructors**
> Coming from the Education industry, it took me a while to understand the reason for distinguishing authors from instructors. After all, in most schools and universities, a teacher is the person that both *creates* a course and *teaches* it. But Captivate Prime is an enterprise LMS, and what seems obvious in Education sometimes becomes much less so in corporate environments.
> In large organizations, a single L&D department may be responsible for *designing* courses that are delivered all over the world in various branches to a wide variety of learners. In such a case, the *Author* of the course is the L&D department of the organization, while the *Instructors* are the individuals responsible for delivering that training in regional offices. Of course, the same person can be both an Author *and* an Instructor, but this is not always the case.

## Adding a virtual class module

*Synchronous* activities can be either live or virtual. In the previous section, you added a live instructor-led module to your course. The process of adding a virtual class is very similar, but with one key difference. This is what you will explore in this section. Let's get started:

- 1. Return to the **Overview** page of your course. This is where you can edit basic course information, such as **Course Name**, **Brief Description**, **Content**, and more.
- 2. Scroll down the page until you see the **Modules** section.
- 3. Click the **Add Modules** link located at the top of the **Content** section.
- 4. Click the **Virtual Classroom Module** option to open the **Virtual Classroom** form.
- 5. Take some time to review the content of the **Virtual Classroom** form.
- 6. When done, click the **Cancel** button located in the lower-right corner of the form to return to the course **Overview** page, without actually adding any modules.

For the most part, the **Virtual Classroom** form is the same as the **Classroom Module** form you completed in the previous section. This should not come as a surprise since a virtual class module is also *time-dependent* (it has a start and end date/time).

The only difference is the **Details** section, located in the middle of the form, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](img/5ceaefa2a9a737206c59f0b54e1a07d0_144_0.png)

Figure 4.10 – The Details section of the Virtual Classroom form

This is where you can specify a **Conference URL**. This URL can redirect the user to *any* virtual class or video conferencing solution available (even if the chosen solution is not from Adobe). For example, if your organization uses Zoom, simply schedule the class in Zoom and copy/paste the Zoom link to the class here.
