# Sharing with the Adobe Captivate Prime Desktop App

In addition to sharing text-based content, web pages, and files, you can also share screenshots, voice recordings, and short videos using Captivate Prime's discussion boards. For this type of content, sharing is the easy part. But first, you must create the content, and that's much harder.

This is the reason why the Prime engineering team has developed the Adobe Captivate Prime desktop application. This application allows you to easily create screenshots, voice recordings, and even small videos that you can upload to one of Captivate Prime's discussion boards in just a few clicks!

> Captivate Prime desktop application availability
> At the time of writing this book, the Adobe Captivate desktop application is available for both Windows and Mac computers (up to macOS 10.15 – macOS Big Sur (version 11) is not supported yet).

This is what you will explore in this section, but before that, you must download and install the Adobe Captivate Prime desktop application by performing the following steps:

+   1. Use the browser window where you are logged in as Learner 01. Go to the Social Learning page, and then to the Cybersecurity in the organization discussion board.
+   2. Click the New Post button at the top of the page.

Take some time to inspect the icons visible just above the Write or paste something here... field. They represent the different content types that can be shared on the discussion boards of Captivate Prime, as shown here:
