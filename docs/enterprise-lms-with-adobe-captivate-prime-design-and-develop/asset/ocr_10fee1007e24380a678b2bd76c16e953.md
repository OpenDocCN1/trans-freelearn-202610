# Discovering the fluidic player

The **fluidic player** is a key component and a landmark feature of Adobe Captivate Prime. What makes it unique is its ability to deliver a wide variety of learning content. This allows learners to remain in the same learning environment regardless of the reviewed module being a video, a document, a SCORM package, or any other content supported by Captivate Prime.
