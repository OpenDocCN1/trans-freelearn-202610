There are three actions available in this list:

+   - The **Delete** action deletes this self-registration profile. Be very careful when using this action as it also deletes all the users that have used these self-registration profiles to register themselves to your Prime account.
+   - The **Pause** action allows you to temporarily disable this particular registration profile. This prevents additional users from registering using that link, but it does *not* remove or disable users already registered. That's why the **Pause** action is usually preferred to the **Delete** action described previously.
+   - The **Resume** action restores the possibility to self-register using that link.

Thanks to these options, you, the Prime administrator, have all the necessary tools you need to create and manage internal users.

In the next section, you will learn how to add external users to your Prime account.

# Creating external users

So far in this chapter, you have created internal users. Internal users are individuals who are part of your organization. A typical internal user is an employee of your organization.

But you may also need to grant access to your Prime account to people outside the organization. This feature allows you to meet the following use cases:

+   - Your organization sells training, and you need to provide access to customers who have purchased training from you.
+   - You need to provide training to external partners, suppliers, franchisees, or contractors.
+   - You must provide Instructor access to an external provider that your organization has contracted to deliver live courses (classroom or virtual).
+   - Your organization has outsourced the creation of training materials to external eLearning agencies, and you want to provide Author access to the people working in these agencies so that they can upload the content they create directly to your Prime account.
+   - Etc.

Prime users who are not part of your organization are considered external users. They enjoy the same experience as internal users but with a few nuances:

+   - By default, external users do not have access to Gamification (you will explore gamification in *Chapter 11, Badges and Gamification*).
