It is also important to know that the Prime administrators have the same abilities as the board owner: they can delete any post and they can edit (and even delete) the board itself. This is very important when, for example, the owner of a board leaves the organization. In such a case, any administrator can take over the board's moderation, designate new moderators, or even completely delete the board.
