# Modifying the Content of Email Messages

In the *Configuring the Messaging System* section, earlier in this chapter, you modified **Email Banner** and **Email Signature** of all the messages sent by Captivate Prime. It is important to remember that these modifications affect all the messages in the same way.

In this section, you will learn how to modify the content of each email message. You will review this process using one of the messages available in the main **Email Templates** section of the Captivate Prime administrator, but the process is the same for every other email template available in the various sections of the **Captivate Prime** administrator.

In this example, you will customize the **Course – Enrolled by Admin/Manager** message at the platform level, so this change will affect all the courses that are available on the platform by default (unless you also modify the same message at the course level, in which case the course-level message takes precedence). When you're done, you'll enroll *Learner 02* in any of the courses available on the Prime account to trigger your customized email message being sent by Captivate Prime. Finally, you will log in to the mailbox of *Learner 02* and review your customized email message.

Let's start by customizing the email template by performing the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator home page, click the **Email Templates** link located in the **Configure** section of the left sidebar.
+   3. Click the **Learning Activity** tab.
+   4. Finally, click the **Course – Enrolled by Admin/Manager** message.
