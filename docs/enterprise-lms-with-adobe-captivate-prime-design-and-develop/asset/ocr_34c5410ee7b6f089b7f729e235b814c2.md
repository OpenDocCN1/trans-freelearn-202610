# Sharing a voice recording

Sometimes, a short voice message is faster and easier than a text-based message. This is why the Adobe Captivate Prime desktop application lets you easily capture and share a voice recording. This is what you will explore in this section by performing the following steps:

+   1. Open the Captivate Prime desktop application that is installed on your computer and make sure you are logged in as Learner 01.
+   2. Click the **Record Audio** icon located in the middle of the application's welcome screen.
