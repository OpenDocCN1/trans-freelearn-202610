# Adding content to catalogs

A catalog is a collection of learning objects. In the previous section, you created two custom catalogs in your Prime account. You will now add content to these course catalogs using the following steps:

+   1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
+   2. From the administrator homepage, click the **Catalogs** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
