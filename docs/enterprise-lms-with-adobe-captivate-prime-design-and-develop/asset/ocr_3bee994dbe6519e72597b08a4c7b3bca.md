**Brief Description** appears on the Course Card, which is visible both in the catalog itself and in the search results page when learners are hunting for courses. It should be very short (maximum 140 characters) and contain the main keywords that relate to your course. Consider it as bait to make a learner click your course rather than the other courses available in the catalog.

**Detailed Overview** is a longer description of your course (maximum 1,500 characters). It appears on the course page once the learner has clicked the course card in the catalog or the search results page. In other words, learners who reach **Detailed Description** have already expressed an interest in taking the course. That's why **Detailed Description** can be made much longer than **Brief Description**. The detailed overview should contain high-level information about the course's content, the learning outcomes, the methodology used in the course, and more.
