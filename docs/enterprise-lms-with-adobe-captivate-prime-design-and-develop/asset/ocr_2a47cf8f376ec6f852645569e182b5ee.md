In this section, you will log in to your Prime account as an integration admin and explore the connectors available out of the box in Adobe Captivate Prime. Use the following steps to start your exploration of the built-in connectors available in Prime:

+   1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator (or as any other user that has the Integration Admin role assigned).
+   2. Click the user icon located in the upper-right corner of the screen to reveal the list of roles assigned to the currently logged-in user.
+   3. Click the Integration Admin role, as shown in the following screenshot:

![](bbox=[0.31, 0.295, 0.655, 0.72])

Figure 16.1 – Clicking the Integration Admin role

4. When the page reloads, you should be connected as an Integration Admin.
