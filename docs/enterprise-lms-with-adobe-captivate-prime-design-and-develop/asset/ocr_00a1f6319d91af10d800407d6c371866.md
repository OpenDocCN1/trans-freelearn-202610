In this chapter, you will log in to your Prime account as an instructor and review the tools and features that enable instructors to carry out their assigned tasks. To achieve this goal, this chapter takes you through the following sections:

+   - Managing instructor-led (face-to-face and virtual) classes
+   - Reviewing file submission assignments
+   - Filling out checklist activities

The role of the instructor is very important to the day-to-day operations of your Captivate Prime account and to the quality of the learner experience, especially in an era where face-to-face and distance learning are bound to become increasingly blended. With Captivate Prime, trainers have all the tools they need to build the blended courses that are becoming increasingly popular in more and more organizations. So, let's dive right into the instructor role!

# Technical requirements

In order to perform the exercises described in this chapter, you need to meet the following requirements:

+   - You must have instructor access to a working Captivate Prime account, which can be a trial account.
+   - The instructor account must be defined as the instructor for at least one (live or virtual) class activity, one file submission activity, and one checklist activity.
+   - Students must be registered for the instructor-led class and you must have at least one additional student on the waitlist.
+   - At least one file should have been submitted for the file submission activity.

If you have completed the exercises in the previous chapters, you are sure to meet the above requirements! If not, you can use your own course content and users to complete the exercises in this chapter. However, the instructions are based on the exercises from the previous chapters, so feel free to adapt them to your personal situation.
