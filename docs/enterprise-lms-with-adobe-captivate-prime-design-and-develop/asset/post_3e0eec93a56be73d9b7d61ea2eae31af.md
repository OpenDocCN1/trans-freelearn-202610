## Exporting Excel Reports

Although all the reports you have reviewed so far in this chapter can be exported as CSV files and opened in any spreadsheet application, they are primarily displayed within Captivate Prime as charts. But that's not all Captivate Prime can do for you when it comes to reporting. In fact, there is a full set of additional reports that cannot be displayed as charts in Captivate Prime, but that you can export as CSV files from the platform for further analysis in any spreadsheet application or for archival purposes. This is what we will explore in this section.

Once again, there are so many reports and variations available that we will not be able to cover them all in this book. That's why we will only concentrate on three of the most popular Excel reports among the Prime customers and partners we are in touch with.

Use the following steps to access the Excel reports available in Captivate Prime:

- 1. Make sure you are logged into your Prime account as an administrator.
- 2. From the administrator home page, click the **Reports** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
- 3. Then, click the **Custom Reports** link on the left sidebar and go to the **Excel Reports** tab.

This takes you to the long list of Excel reports available in Captivate Prime.

**Excel or not Excel...**
... that's the question! Although Captivate Prime calls them *Excel* reports, all exports are done in CSV format. Keep in mind that a CSV file can be opened in many applications, not just spreadsheets, and if you use a spreadsheet application, not just Microsoft Excel. Excel is simply the most popular spreadsheet software among Prime customers and that's probably why it's called **Excel Reports** in Prime. Feel free to try this feature with whatever spreadsheet application you are using to take advantage of the unique charting and data analysis features present in your specific software package.

You will now export three of the available Excel reports starting with the **Learner Transcripts** report in the next section.

## Exporting the Login/Access report

You will now explore the Login/Access report available on the Excel Reports tab of the Custom Reports section of Captivate Prime. This report contains detailed information about user logins and access to your Captivate Prime account. It is used by organizations to answer questions such as these:

- - Who are the most active users on the platform?
- - When do users typically connect to the Prime account?
- - What is the profile, location, department, and so on of the most active users?
- - Is there a correlation between user activity and their profile, manager, location, and so on?

Use the following steps to generate and explore the Login/Access report:

- 1. From the Excel Reports tab of the Custom Reports section of the administrator, click the Login/Access report link to open the Login/Access report dialog.
- 2. Open the Select Range drop-down menu and explore the available options.
  In this example, choose Last One Month in order to have enough data on the generated report.
- 3. Read the Generating Report Request message and click the OK button.
- 4. When the report is ready, a notification appears under the bell icon in the upper-right corner of the screen. Click the notification to download the Login/Access report in CSV format.
