The last remaining field is the **Tags** field at the bottom of the form. At this time, you should not be able to add any tags as these must be defined beforehand by an Administrator.

+   5. Click the **Save** button, located in the top-right corner of the screen.

+   6. Click your new module when Captivate Prime has finished processing it.

Processing times vary based on various factors, such as the file type, size, and complexity.
