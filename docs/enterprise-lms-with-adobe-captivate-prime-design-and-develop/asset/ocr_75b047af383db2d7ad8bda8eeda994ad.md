In the preceding screenshot, you can see that this particular session has a **Seat Limit** of **2** and that both seats are already **Filled**. You can also see that **Learner 02** is currently the only learner on the waitlist.

+   4. Use the checkbox to select **Learner 02** in the list.
+   5. Click the **Actions** button located in the upper-right corner of the screen and take some time to review the available actions.

The **Send Email to All** action is the only action available when no learner is selected.

The **Unenroll Learners** action removes the selected learner(s) from the waitlist without enrolling them for the course. On the contrary, the **Confirm Learners** action moves the selected learner(s) from the waitlist to the list of confirmed learners. In both cases, Captivate Prime automatically sends an email notification to the affected learners.

+   6. In this example, click the **Confirm Learners** action to move **Learner 02** from the waitlist to the list of confirmed learners.
+   7. Click **Yes** to confirm your intention. When the page reloads, **Learner 02** should no longer be on the waitlist.
+   8. Click the **Learners** link located in the left sidebar.

When the **Learners** page re-opens, make sure that *Learner 02* has been added to the list of confirmed learners. This illustrates the ability to allocate a seat to someone on the waitlist even if the **Seat Limit** has been reached. In other words, instructors can override the **Seat Limit** of a session.

This is one of the two ways a learner can be cleared from the waitlist and added to the list of confirmed learners. The other way is to unenroll a learner that is currently on the confirmed learners list. In such cases, the next learner on the waitlist is *automatically* confirmed for the session.

# Uploading resources to a session

Instructors can also upload additional resources for a session. These resources can include educational material, as well as any document that facilitates the practical organization of the event, such as a map, a parking reservation form, the schedule for the day, and so on.
