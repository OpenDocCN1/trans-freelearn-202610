# Reporting in
Captivate Prime

In the previous chapter, we reviewed L1, L2, and L3 feedback for courses and learning programs. This provided you with important data about the learner behavior in your Prime account. Remember, these feedback features are based on Dr. Donald Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Learning Evaluation model.

Four levels, did you say? That's right! You only covered the first three levels in the previous chapter. Fortunately, this chapter is entirely dedicated to the fourth level of Kirkpatrick's model. As a reminder, the fourth level is the **Results** Level, which aims to measure the result of the training strategy at the organizational level. It is at this stage that the focus shifts from the individual learner to the impact of learning on the organization as a whole.

Captivate Prime's reporting capabilities are designed to help organizations answer questions such as *Are we increasing revenue through training?*, *Are we reducing costs?*, *Are we decreasing risk and increasing safety?*, *Are we increasing the organization's level of compliance?*, and so on. For organizations, such insight is critical to identify areas for improvement, continually refine the organization's L&D strategy, and ultimately make informed business decisions.

Technically speaking, generating reports requires two distinct steps. First, you need to collect data, then you leverage that data to generate reports, charts, and so on that help you make sense out of it.
