+   9. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Homepage Experience** option.
+   10. Switch the **Homepage Experience** to **Immersive**.
+   11. Click the **Save** button located in the bottom right corner of the page.

Note that this last action modifies the options available further down the page. You will review these options in *Chapter 13, AI-Powered Recommendations for Learners.*

This last action is what makes the switch to the *Immersive* experience effective for all learners using your Prime account.

> **Important**
> For the remaining examples of this book, we assume that the default *Immersive* experience is enabled in your Prime account.

# Other customizations

In this chapter, you have reviewed the main branding and customization options available in your Prime account. That being said, there are lots of other aspects of the system that can be customized. You will review them throughout the chapters of this book. Nevertheless, let's make a quick list of the other main possible customizations here:

+   - **Badges**: In *Chapter 11, Badges and Gamification*, you will learn about badges. Captivate Prime comes with a handful of badges ready to go, but you can define your own custom badges in addition to those available out of the box in Prime.
+   - **Course cards**: In *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, you will be able to customize the look and feel of each course card by uploading a set of unique cover and banner images for each individual course.
+   - **Email templates**: In *Chapter 15, Working With Messages and Announcements*, you will customize both the look and feel and the content of a wide range of email messages that the Prime platform sends to your users.

These customizations come in addition to those already discussed in this chapter. This makes Captivate Prime a highly customizable cloud-based LMS that you can use to provide your learners with an enterprise-class experience that fits your company's brand identity.
