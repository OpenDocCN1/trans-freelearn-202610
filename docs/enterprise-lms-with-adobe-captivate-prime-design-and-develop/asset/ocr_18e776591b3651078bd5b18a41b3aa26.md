# Retiring course instances

When a course has multiple course instances, learners can choose which instance they want to enroll in. This will quickly reveal one of the greatest mysteries that all event organizers inevitably face: some courses instances will be in high demand, and others not at all. This is especially problematic if the course contains live class activities with limited seating. Waiting lists can quickly fill up, which may lead to a logistical nightmare and unhappy learners.

This is one of the circumstances in which retiring a course instance is very useful. A retired instance no longer appears in the course catalog, making it impossible to accept new enrollments. At the same time, learners who are already enrolled keep their status and take the course as if nothing had happened to the course instance they are enrolled in!

Use the following steps to retire a course instance:

+   1. From the administrator home page, click the **Courses** link located in the **Learning** section of the left sidebar.
+   2. Hover over the card of the course you created *Chapter 4, Creating Skills and Courses*, and click the **View Course** link to open the course's page.
+   3. Click the **Instances** link located at the top of the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
+   4. Click the down arrow located in the top-right corner of the course instance you wish to retire.
