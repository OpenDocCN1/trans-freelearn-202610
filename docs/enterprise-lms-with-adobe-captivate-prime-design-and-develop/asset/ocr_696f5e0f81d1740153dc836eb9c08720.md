In this section, you have examined how learners provide L1 feedback after completing a course. But don't forget that you have also enabled L3 feedback for this course. L3 feedback is provided by the learner's manager and will be discussed in the next section.

# Providing L3 Feedback as a Manager

L3 feedback corresponds to the third level of Kirkpatrick's Four Levels model.
As a reminder, this is the Behavior level, which aims to measure whether the training leads to a change in learner's behavior once they return to their workplace.

In Captivate Prime, managers are responsible for submitting L3 feedback. After all, who better to measure a change in behavior after training than the employee's direct manager? Typically, L3 feedback should be submitted a few weeks after an employee has completed a training.
