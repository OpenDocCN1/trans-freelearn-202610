# Enabling Social Learning

The first decision you must make as an administrator is whether to enable or disable social learning in your Prime account. Like any social network, Captivate Prime's social learning feature involves direct interactions between individual learners. This means that learners have access to each other's profiles, which raises privacy concerns for many organizations. This is especially true if your organization uses Captivate Prime to deliver training to mostly external users.

> **Internal and external users**
> The differences between internal and external users were discussed in *Chapter 5, Managing Users.* In a nutshell, internal users are employees of your organization, while external users are employees of other organizations (such as customers, suppliers, partners, franchisees, and so on).

Some organizations have rules about using social media during business hours. Some Prime customers decide to not use social learning because it might send a conflicting message to their staff.

In this section, you will start by disabling social learning completely and see how it affects the learner experience. Then, you will re-enable social learning, but for internal users only.
