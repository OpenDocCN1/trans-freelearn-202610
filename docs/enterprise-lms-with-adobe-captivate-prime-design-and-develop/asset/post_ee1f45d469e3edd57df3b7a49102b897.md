## Content curation

In the previous section, you discussed content moderation. Moderators do not have to be experts in the subject being discussed; their only responsibility is to make sure that the discussion remains courteous.

**Content curation**, on the other hand, is there to guarantee the quality of the content being posted on discussion boards. As such, content curation requires that posted content be analyzed for accuracy, relevance, and whether it's up to date. That is why content curation is the responsibility of individuals considered experts in the topic being discussed.

Remember that learners receive points each time they participate in a social learning activity on the Prime account. These points are used to identify **Subject Matter Experts** (**SMEs**) in the various boards available on the Prime account. These SMEs are in charge of manual content curation in the discussion forum(s) they are experts of.

These are the features that you will explore in this section. But before you start your exploration, it is necessary to activate content curation, which is disabled by default on any new Prime account.

### Activating content curation

By default, no content curation is conducted on any new Prime account. This means that the quality of the content that's posted on the various discussion boards is not guaranteed, which can lead to poor quality content being distributed to a potentially large number of learners.

In this section, you will return to the administrator application and review the options that allow you to enable content curation on your Prime account. Perform the following steps:

1. Log in to your Prime account as an administrator.
2. From the administrator home page, click the **Social Learning** link located in the **Manage** section of the left sidebar.
3. Switch to the **Settings** tab by clicking the **Settings** link at the top of the page.
4. Click the **Edit** link associated with the **Content Curation** option.

As shown in the following screenshot, **No Curation** is the default value:

Take some time to read the descriptions associated with the three values:
