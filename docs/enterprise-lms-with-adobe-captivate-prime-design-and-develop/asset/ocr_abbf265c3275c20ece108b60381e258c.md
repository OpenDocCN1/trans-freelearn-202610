To provide L3 feedback after a team member has completed a training, managers must perform the following steps:

+   1. Log in to your Captivate Prime account as a manager.
+   2. Use the user icon in the upper-right corner of the screen to switch to the manager role. As a matter of fact, providing L3 feedback is one of the special permissions of the manager role, so managers must explicitly switch to that role in order to have access to the L3 feedback form.
+   3. From the manager home page, click the **Notifications** link located in the **View** section of the left sidebar.
+   4. Once on the **Notifications** page, click the **Pending Tasks** tab.
