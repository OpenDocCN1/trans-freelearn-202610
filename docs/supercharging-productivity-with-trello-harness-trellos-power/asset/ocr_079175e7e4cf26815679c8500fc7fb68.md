# Close board

If you ever want to delete a board, you can close it. Even after you close it,
you can undo it if you change your mind! Simply click **Reopen board**. You
can also find your closed boards from your **Boards** page (to get there, click
the Trello logo in the top-left corner from anywhere in Trello). From there,
scroll to the bottom of the page, then click the **View all closed boards**
button.

# Lists

The second major component of your Trello board is your **lists**. Lists are
how you organize sticky notes inside the board. While there are a million
strategies for how you can use lists, they’re often used as stages of your
workflow.

In many cases, it’s as simple as **To Do**, **Doing**, and **Done**.
