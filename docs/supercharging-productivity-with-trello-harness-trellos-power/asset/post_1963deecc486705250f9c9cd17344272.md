## Reports

You’ll use reports to display a high-level overview of your projects across one or more boards. It’s helpful for showing things like how much work is getting done or seeing if you’re allocating tasks evenly between teammates. You can also see how long work takes, identify slow-moving cards and track other trends on your boards. With only a little bit of effort, you can use it to create custom reports like this.

Figure 14.7 – Creating custom reports

Let’s go through an example of how to make a simple report showing how much work is getting done and a few different charts we can create to look at that.

Head back to the home page of Blue Cat by clicking the **Home** icon in the top left. Click the button on the right-side panel that says **Create a Report**. You’ll be asked to name your report. It is helpful at this point to think about when you would use the report and name it after that, that is, **Monday morning standup** or **Monthly board review**. So if you can think of a sensible name then pick that, or feel free to call it **Test report** if you prefer. Now, click **Save**.

Next, you will need to click **Create chart**. You’ll now see various types of charts that you can choose from. This book isn’t about charts and task reporting overall, just how to do those things in Trello, so I won’t go into much detail here, but you can always ask the Blue Cat reports team or post in the Atlassian Community if you need some help figuring out which chart to use.

## Chart Type

What type of chart would you like to add to your Report?

### Lists and Counts

**List**  
Show a list of cards or checklist items in your Report

**Count, sum or average**  
A simple count of items or the sum/average of a custom field value

### Pie and Bar Charts

**Pie Chart**  
Show a breakdown of data by member, label, age in list etc

**Bar Chart**  
Show a breakdown of cards grouped by member, label, age in list etc

**Stacked Bar Chart**  
Bar chart grouped by two dimensions

### Date Charts

**Historical Chart**  
Show a line chart of number of cards over time

**Date chart**  
Show a line chart of cards against their due or other date fields

### Time Charts

**Time To Complete Chart**  
See how long cards take to complete, your whole process or a single step

Figure 14.8 – Chart type options

I’m going to choose a **Historical chart**, because I want to show how much work is being completed over time. If you pick the wrong chart, you can click the **Back** button to change or you can edit it later.

For instance, you can specify **Open Cards** or **Completed Cards**. Then you’ll pick which board(s) you want to look for cards on.

Here we will pick **Completed Cards** as that is what we want to show in this chart. Next, we select the list our cards are moved to once complete, this is how Blue Cat Reports knows a card is completed and when it was completed for your reports.
