If you add an image to a card, Trello automatically sets it as the card cover. This means that a thumbnail of the image will show up at the top of the card, both when the card is open and when the card is closed and viewed on the board. To disable the image from previewing, select **Remove cover** from the image in the **Attachments** section inside the card.
