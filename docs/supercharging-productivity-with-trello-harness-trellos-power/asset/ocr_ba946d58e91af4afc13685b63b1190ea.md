# # Setting a location on a card

To add a location to a card, open a card and click **Location** from the right
side of the card underneath the **Add to card** section. In the search box that
appears, start typing the name or address of a location. Google Maps shows
results based on what you type. Upon selecting the most relevant one, a
preview of your address will appear on the card.
