Click **hours** in the second row, and you can select another cadence. Change it to **weeks**, and now your card will be due one week (or seven days) from when it was created. Click the + button at the end of the row in the modal.

Finally, don’t forget to click the + button at the very end of the row to add this action to the workflow!

Almost done, but there’s one more step. Scroll up to confirm the rule looks the way you expect.
