# # Voting on a card

On every card on your Trello board, you’ll notice a new button in the **Actions** section at the bottom right, just to the right of the **Activity** section.

![](bbox=[0.125, 0.296, 0.867, 0.77])

Click the **Vote** button and notice a checkmark appears to show that you’ve voted for the card.
