Don’t see it right away? Check on the right side below the **Add to card** and
**Power-Ups** sections. Click the button and watch the members and due date
be removed, and the card moves to the list specified in our rule.

A handy notification appears in the bottom right to let you know what is
happening when you press the button. This is helpful for teammates, or
even a refresher for yourself if you can’t remember what you told a button
to do.

# # Board buttons

Board buttons are used to trigger automation from the board level, not a
specific card level. I find this particularly helpful for sorting lists or creating
templated cards for requests.

You’ll find these buttons in the top toolbar of your Trello board, alongside
the **Automation** and **Power-Ups** buttons.
