# # Creating an approval

Every Power-Up is a little bit different, but to use this one, head to any card on your Trello board. Look in the **Power-Up** section just below the **Add to card** section on the right side of any card.

I’m going to pick a card in my Review list on my board, but you can choose from any card or list you like:

![](bbox=[0.195, 0.296, 0.795, 0.652])

Figure 12.3 – Approvals button on cards

Click on the **Approvals** button, and a modal invites you to start adding approvals. You’ll see any existing approvals, but since we’re new, it’s empty. Click the + button at the bottom.
