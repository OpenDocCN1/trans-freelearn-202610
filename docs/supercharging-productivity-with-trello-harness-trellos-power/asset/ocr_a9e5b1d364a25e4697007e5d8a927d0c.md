To add the List Limits Power-Up, head to the Power-Up directory and
search for List Limits in the top left. Choose the first option that appears
and click **Add**.

Figure 11.12 – List Limits Power-Up card

Once the button changes to, say, **Settings**, exit out of the Power-Up modal
by pressing the *Esc* key on your keyboard or clicking the **X** icon in the top-
right corner of your screen. This takes you back to your board.
