# # Choosing what syncs

If this feels like a lot, just keep in mind that for most cases, you can just click through these settings as the default is often what you want! The reason we’re going through these step by step is to show you the options you have available in case you want to customize and do something specific.

In this step, you can filter which cards (or issues, pages, or other items depending on the apps you connected) are synced. You might choose to do this if you only want to sync your own cards to another board or tool, instead of all cards.

To do this, you’d click **Add a new rule** to specify that you only want to sync tasks from another project where you’re the owner.
