with automation, there is such a thing as too much of a good thing. Having
dozens of Power-Ups on your board will slow down performance and add
distractions, preventing folks from effectively using your board.

Instead, I recommend looking around to see the options available and then
thinking about what makes the most sense in your workflow.

The sections on the left side of the directory (see _Figure 11.3_) will also help
you narrow down what Power-Ups might be most useful to you. For
instance, you can see which ones are featured and most common, or you
can sort by specific use cases or industries.
