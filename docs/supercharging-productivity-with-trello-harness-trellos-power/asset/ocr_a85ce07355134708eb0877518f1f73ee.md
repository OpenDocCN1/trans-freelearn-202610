Figure 14.28 – Sample charts built by default in Placker

You can see the status of your cards, and even check their progress based on
their end date! This quickly shows whether your team is effective at
completing tasks (and on time), or whether you might be starting a lot of
things and finishing a few.

There’s even a pivot table showing each member’s cards aggregated by
status across multiple boards, helpful for being able to quickly see how a
team is progressing through tasks for the period.
