# Testing a form

There’s one more thing to do before sharing your form with the world! If
you’ve read the previous chapters, you’ll know I’m a big proponent of
testing as we go. Let’s test out our form.

Go to the share link and fill out the form. After submitting, return to your
Trello board and confirm that you have a new card in the list you expected,
and that all the information is mapped to the fields you wanted.
