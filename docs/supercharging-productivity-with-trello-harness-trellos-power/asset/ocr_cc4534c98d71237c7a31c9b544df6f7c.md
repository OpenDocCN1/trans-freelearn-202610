# # Part 3 – Power Up Your Boards

In the final part, we’ll discuss add-ons for your Trello boards called Power-Ups. You’ll learn about some of the most common ones that cover a variety of use cases, and we’ll help you make your boards work as effectively as possible. We’ll even discuss reporting Power-Ups to help you make sense of the data in your Trello boards so that you have everything you need to be successful!
