# Figure 1.24 – Adding a card to a list

Make a card that says `Add cards to my Trello board`. Press `Enter` to save it. And voilà! It will appear on your list!

# Copy

The next action in the list menu is to copy a list. This does just what it sounds like – it duplicates the list and adds it right next to the current list, including any cards in that list. Go ahead and try it out! Click **Copy** to see another list appear with the card you just created.

# Move

Lists also have a **Move** option. You can drag a list to move it to another place within the board, or you can use this action to move it. You can even move the list to another board entirely! You might want to do this when you’re figuring out how to organize your board, or after you’ve completed a project.

# Watch

You can also watch a list, similar to watching a board.

This sends notifications about anything that happens to any card in this list. It’s perfect if you’re managing a particular stage of a workflow.
