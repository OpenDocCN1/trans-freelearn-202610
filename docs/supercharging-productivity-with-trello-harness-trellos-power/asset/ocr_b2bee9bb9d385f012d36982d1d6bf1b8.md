# # Cards represent objectives

On this board, cards *can* represent tasks, or they can be larger initiatives. Since this type of board is often used for larger products that require teams to collaborate, there are likely a *lot* of tasks that need to be done, and listing them all could clutter the board and make it difficult to read.

Another way to organize the cards is by objective. Consider a product launch, for instance. The marketing team might have several objectives, such as creating a landing page, writing a blog post, creating ads, and organizing influencers.
