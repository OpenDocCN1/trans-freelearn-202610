This automation helps you see the most popular cards and will sort them automatically after the data (number of votes on a card) changes.

That’s a lot of things you can trigger based on card changes. Hopefully, by this point, you’re starting to think of some activities that are part of your workflow that you might incorporate as triggers, but we’re only getting started. Now it’s time to talk about dates.
