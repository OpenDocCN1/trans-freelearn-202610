Not only can you pick cards that meet certain criteria, but you can hide cards that also meet certain criteria. For instance, I can show all cards except ones that have a due date more than a week away, or except ones that don’t have any label. Just start typing and you’ll be able to find the attribute you want and create a filter. You can also quickly filter to just see your own cards (there’s even a button for this at the top of the board, before you click into the filter).

# # Default attributes for reporting

When you open a card from the Placker modal, you’ll see a lot more fields than you get in Trello. This is helpful when you need more advanced reporting because you can assign many attributes to a card, such as planned and actual dates, estimated budget, progress on a card, priority, and more!
