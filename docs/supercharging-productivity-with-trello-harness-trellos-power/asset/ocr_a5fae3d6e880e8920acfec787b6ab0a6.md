You can also filter by other criteria, such as syncing only cards created at a certain time or with certain labels or attributes.

By default, your flow will only sync cards from the current date onward and will not apply retroactively to previously created cards. If you want it to apply to existing cards, you’ll need to click the trash can icon to delete the default creation date filter.
