**Important Note**
An external data transfer of a significant magnitude (in the order of GB) should be flagged by efficient network monitoring tools. However, such transfer could appear standard for organizations where clients download large files, such as gaming software companies. For example, customers download games of over 15 GB once-off on **EASport** servers worldwide. An *obfuscated* data transfer of 5 GB *can* escape security.
