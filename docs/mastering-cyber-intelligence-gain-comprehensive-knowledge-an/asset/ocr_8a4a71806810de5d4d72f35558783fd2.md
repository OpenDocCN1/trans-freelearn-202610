# 11
Usable Security:
Threat Intelligence
as Part of
the Process

The adoption of the **Development and Operations** (**DevOps**) methodology has proven efficient and flexible to organizations compared to other development and operations practices. DevOps, whose function is mainly delivering quality, high-end solutions using efficient development cycles, owes its success to the cross-functional collaboration and merging of the development and IT operations. The challenges faced by security professionals to collaborate with the DevOps team have prompted the need to shift to **Development, Security, and Operations** (**DevSecOps**), integrating security from the start of development tasks.
