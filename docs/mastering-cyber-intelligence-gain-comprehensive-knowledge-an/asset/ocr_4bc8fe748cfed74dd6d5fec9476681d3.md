# Summary

Observing standards facilitates the integration of threat intelligence, the distribution of results, and interaction with the intelligence community, and it also guarantees the development of a sound foundation and process. Intelligence tradecraft standards have never been contradictory to each other, but instead, they are complementary to each other by design. Therefore, it is important for an analyst to know them (at least the most popular ones) and what they address in the intelligence stack.
