The security design can be divided into a four-layer stack: the physical layer, the logical security layer, the security management layer, and the strategic layer, in charge of risks, policies, compliance, and risks. The primary goal is to protect the organization's assets and sensitive resources. Hence, threat analysis must be highly considered. The logical layer shows the organization's assets (application, data, endpoints, personal information, and so on).
