+   7. **Type (optional)**: Allows your users to specify a specific volume backend previously configured and defined by your OpenStack infrastructure engineers. An example would be a group of Cinder storage servers/nodes that only have SSD drives. Another example could be a group of Cinder storage nodes that have traditional SATA drives. This gives your OpenStack users the power of choice.
+   8. **Size (required)**: Size of the volume in gigabytes. Enter 1.
+   9. **Availability Zone (optional)**: Similar to Nova availability zones, Cinder availability zones are defined during installation and give you a common method for segregating cinder storage nodes. By default, all Cinder storage nodes belong to the Nova availability zone.
+   10. Select **Create Volume**.
