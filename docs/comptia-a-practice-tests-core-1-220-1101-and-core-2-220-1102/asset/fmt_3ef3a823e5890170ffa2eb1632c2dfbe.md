## 5.6 Given a scenario, troubleshoot and resolve printer issues

1. How can a duplex printer help reduce excessive paper usage?

    1. It automatically prints on both sides of the paper
    2. It prints at a slower speed, conserving paper
    3. It uses recycled paper for printing
    4. It has a larger paper tray, reducing the need for frequent refills

2. What could be the potential cause of printed text not sticking to the paper?

    1. The printer is using low-quality ink cartridges
    2. The paper being used is not compatible with the printer
    3. The printer’s fuser unit is not functioning properly
    4. The printer’s temperature settings are too low

3. What could be the potential cause of a new printer with a duplex unit only being able to print on one side at a time?

    1. The printer’s firmware requires an update
    2. The printer’s duplex unit is not properly set up
    3. The printer’s paper tray is not properly aligned
    4. The printer’s ink cartridges are low on ink

4. What could be the potential cause of print quality issues, including marks on the printing paper? (Choose two)

    1. The printer’s firmware requires an update
    2. The printer’s drum unit is damaged
    3. The printer has dirty feed rollers
    4. The printer’s ink cartridges are low on ink

5. When paper fails to feed properly in a printer, what is the most likely cause?

    1. The paper tray is empty
    2. The paper is too thick for the printer
    3. The printer driver needs to be updated
    4. The ink cartridges are low on ink

6. What is the most likely cause of a paper jam in a printer?

    1. The paper tray is empty
    2. The paper is wrinkled or folded
    3. There are obstructions in the paper path
    4. The ink cartridges are low on ink

7. To minimize paper waste and enhance print job control, what measures can be implemented?

    1. Load a large stack of paper into the tray
    2. Print double-sided documents whenever possible
    3. Require a login for print jobs
    4. Avoid using paper trays and feed paper manually

    1. Causes overheating and damage to the print head; implement adequate ventilation and cooling solutions
    2. Results in increased ink consumption for thermal printing; adjust printer settings for optimized ink usage
    3. Causes blank spots or missing text on thermal paper; clean the print head
    4. Leads to decreased print speed and slower output; upgrade the thermal printer to a higher-performance model

    1. Overheating caused by a dirty air filter
    2. Insufficient power supply to the projector
    3. A malfunctioning lamp in the projector

     4. Outdated printer driver software

     1. An inkjet printer

     4. A dot matrix printer

12. Why does a dot matrix printer produce documents with missing words?

     1. Worn ribbon in the printer

     4. Outdated printer driver software
