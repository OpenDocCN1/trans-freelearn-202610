# Catalog and Shopping Cart Price Rules

![](bbox=[0.105, 0.106, 0.895, 0.528])

+   - **Actions:**
+     - **Apply:** This will be the action that will be applied to all matching products, based on the rule information and conditions that form the previous fields. The available actions are:
+       - **Apply as percentage of original:** The discount is calculated as a percentage of the product price. If the product price is $200 and the discount is 10%, then the final price with discount will be $190.
+       - **Apply as fixed amount:** The discount will be a fixed amount. If the product price is $200 and the discount is 10%, then the final price with discount will be $190.
