Now that you have the credentials, copy and paste that information in the PayPal Express Checkout form in Magento.

In addition to that, you will see the basic and advanced settings to configure at the end of the PayPal Express Checkout section:

You will see the following fields for the Basic Settings form:

+   - **Title**: You can customize the title that will be displayed for this payment method in the Storefront.
+   - **Sort Order**: You can set the specific order in the list of payment methods on the Storefront, 0 being the payment method that would be displayed at the top of the list.
+   - **Payment Action**: You can choose between the following actions for the purchase:
+     - **Authorization**: In this action, the purchase is approved but the payment should be authorized by the merchant to charge the customer. The merchant is responsible for invoicing and then fulfilling the order.
