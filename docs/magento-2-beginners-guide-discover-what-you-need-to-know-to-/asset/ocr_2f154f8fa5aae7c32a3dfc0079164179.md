+   - **Cross-Sell Products:** Cross-sells are displayed to the customer in the shopping cart page. A cross-sell can be seen in the supermarket waiting line, right before placing the order. At that time, you will see products that the supermarket places in that specific location to try to convince you to buy more. Just like the supermarket, the cross-sell products in Magento are intended to convince the customer to buy more products before placing the order.
