The **Create PanelGrid Wizard** gets started. Click on **Next**. In the **PanelGrid Options** window select the **Panel Grid from Managed Bean or Expression** radio button. Click on **Bind** to bind the panel grid with a managed bean.

![](bbox=[0.22, 0.2, 0.78, 0.49])

In the **Expression Builder** window, select the **JSF Managed Beans | PanelGridMBean** node and add the **PanelGridMBean** to the **Expression** field with the > button.

![](bbox=[0.22, 0.57, 0.78, 0.83])
