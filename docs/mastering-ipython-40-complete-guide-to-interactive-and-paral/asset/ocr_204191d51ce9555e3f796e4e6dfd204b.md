*This paper analyzes the feasibility and strategic path of international e-commerce for Chinese SMEs under the Belt and Road Initiative. Based on the current situation and existing problems, the paper proposes a framework of cooperative e-commerce platform and discusses the key factors for success.*

**Keywords:** Belt and Road Initiative; SMEs; international e-commerce; feasibility study; cooperative platform

# ## 1. Introduction

With the continuous advancement of the Belt and Road Initiative, Chinese small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) are facing new opportunities for international market expansion. However, due to limited resources and lack of experience, these enterprises often encounter difficulties in cross-border e-commerce. This study aims to explore the feasibility of establishing an international e-commerce platform tailored to the needs of Chinese SMEs.

As an emerging business model, international e-commerce can help SMEs overcome geographical barriers, reduce transaction costs, and improve market access. The development of the Belt and Road countries provides a favorable policy environment and market demand for such platforms.

# ## 2. Literature Review

Existing studies have extensively examined e-commerce adoption by SMEs, the impact of the Belt and Road Initiative on trade, and the design of cross-border platforms. However, few studies focus on the combination of these two topics. This paper fills this gap by investigating both the macro-level policy support and the micro-level enterprise needs.

+   - Macro-level: policy coordination, infrastructure connectivity, trade facilitation
+   - Micro-level: SME capabilities, technology adoption, logistics integration
+   - Platform-level: business model, service ecosystem, risk management

# ## 3. Feasibility Analysis

This section evaluates the feasibility of the proposed platform from the perspectives of policy, market, technology, and capital. The results indicate that although challenges exist, the overall environment is favorable for the establishment of an international e-commerce platform under the Belt and Road context.
