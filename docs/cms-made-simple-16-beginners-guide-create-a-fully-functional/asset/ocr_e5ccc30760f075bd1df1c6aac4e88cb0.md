# Using Third-party Modules

# Adding department as a pulldown field

Another type of field that you can easily add to the form is a drop-down field, which is called **pulldown** in CMS Made Simple. Click on the **Add New Field** link, and select **Pulldown** as the **Field Type**. Enter the fields as shown in the following screenshot:

![](bbox=[0.229, 0.247, 0.773, 0.655])

Just as in radio buttons, here you can differentiate between what the visitor sees while filling in the form (**Option Name**) and the value of the field sent to you (**Value Submitted**).

> 💡 Do not forget to update the **Email** and **Submission** templates after adding the field to the form.
