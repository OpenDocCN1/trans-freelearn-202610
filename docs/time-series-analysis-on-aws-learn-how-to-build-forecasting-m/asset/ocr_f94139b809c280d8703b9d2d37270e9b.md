6. You can let your scheduler run thrice (as the generator from *step 5* produces enough inference data for 15 minutes) before stopping it (this will stop the incurring cost of the scheduler). To do this, on the scheduler dashboard, you can just click on the **Stop** button, located at the top right of this section.
