+   3. Resample the energy dataframe to a one-day granularity (from the raw 30 minutes available). The related dataset that we will use later has a one-hour granularity and Amazon Forecast requires that both datasets have the same underlying frequency. We will also resample the related dataset to one-day granularity, to ensure that the energy consumption data frequency will match the London weather data.

+   4. Limit the scope of our analysis to a time ranging from 2012-07-01 to 2013-06-30 (one full year of data): this will limit the training time and any processing or visualization you want to run on the dataset. This will also leave us with a whole section of the data ranging from 2013-07-01 to the end of the available time (2014-02-28) to use as a test dataset our models will have never seen.
