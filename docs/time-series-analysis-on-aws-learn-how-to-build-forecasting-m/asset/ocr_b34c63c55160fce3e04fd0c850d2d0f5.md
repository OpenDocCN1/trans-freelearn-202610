+   5. In the last section (**Select your use case**), select **Allows Amazon Forecast to access data in S3**.

+   6. Click on the **Next** button until you reach the last step (**Review**): provide a name and a description for your role (I called it `AmazonForecastEnergyConsumption`).

+   7. Click on **Create role**: your role is created and you are presented with a summary of your role. At the top of this screen, you will see a role ARN (**Amazon Resource Name**) field: copy this ARN and paste it somewhere handy. We will need it when we ingest our data into the service.
