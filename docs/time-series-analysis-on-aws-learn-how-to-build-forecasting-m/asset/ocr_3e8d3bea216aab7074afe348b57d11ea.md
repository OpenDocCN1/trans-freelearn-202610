# Solutions deployment

To deploy this solution in your AWS account, you can follow the steps given here:

+   1. Navigate to the solution home page at `https://aws.amazon.com/solutions/implementations/improving-forecast-accuracy-with-machine-learning/` and scroll down to the middle of the page to click on the **Launch in the AWS Console** button.

![](bbox=[0.117, 0.246, 0.846, 0.649])

Figure 7.10 – AWS Solutions home page

+   2. You will be brought to the CloudFormation service home page. If you are not logged into your account, you will have to sign in before you're redirected to CloudFormation.
