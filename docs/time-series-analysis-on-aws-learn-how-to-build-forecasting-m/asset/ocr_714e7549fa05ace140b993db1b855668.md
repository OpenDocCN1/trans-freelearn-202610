**Important Note**
When you stop a training job, the predictor is not deleted: you can still preview its metadata. In addition, you cannot resume a stopped job.

Additionally, you have the option to upgrade a standard predictor to an AutoPredictor (as you can see in the **AutoPredictor** column).
