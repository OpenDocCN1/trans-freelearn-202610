When configuring the ingestion, the job details you must define are as follows:

+   - **S3 location**: The S3 location of the training data where the time series are located. In *step 5* of the *Uploading your dataset to Amazon S3* section, you copied the S3 URI of your training data. Paste it into this field.
+   - In the **IAM role** field, select **Enter a custom IAM role ARN** and paste the ARN of the AmazonForecastEnergyConsumption role that you created earlier in the IAM service. The format of the ARN should be `arn:aws:iam:::<ACCOUNT-NUMBER>:role/LookoutEquipmentIndustrialPumpDataAccess`, where `<ACCOUNT-NUMBER>` needs to be replaced with your AWS account number.
