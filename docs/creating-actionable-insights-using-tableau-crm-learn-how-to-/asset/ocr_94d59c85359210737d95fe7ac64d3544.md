# A good dashboard tells a story with data

Well-designed analytics tell a story with data. They will narrate a story simply, clearly, and concisely, resulting in a clear and actionable business strategy. If a user reviews your dashboard and is unable to understand and action a data story being told, then it is high time to redesign your dashboard!
