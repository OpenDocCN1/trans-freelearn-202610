# Why, when, and how to use complex formula transformations

In preparing your datasets for analysis, visualization, or modeling, you will need to incorporate logic and calculations that reflect business rules and requirements, and this often becomes complex. In that case, buckets and other simple functions are no longer up to the task, and you need to employ complex formula transformations.
