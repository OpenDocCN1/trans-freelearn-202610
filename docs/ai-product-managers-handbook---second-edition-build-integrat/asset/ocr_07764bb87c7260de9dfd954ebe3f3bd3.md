# 3. Error Analysis

After aligning them, we analyze the semantic errors produced by our translation model because these types of errors are critical in translation quality.

It is observed from the error analysis that semantic errors (involving SENSE, DISCOURSE, and PRAGMATICS levels) are the most prevalent and persistent.

We analyzed each sentence with semantic errors and categorized them in terms of MTS errors (Vilar et al., 2006), a well-known taxonomy for machine translation errors. Specifically, each sentence, we first detect and classify the semantic errors at word, phrase, and sentence levels.

| --- | --- | --- |

| DISCOURSE (Collocation) | EN: ... get 2 silver medals... | Improper word selection... |
| PRAGMATICS (Extra words) | EN: The police are still looking for... | The extra words inserted... |
