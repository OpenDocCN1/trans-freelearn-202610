
The individual tools will change, but the habit you have practiced here will last: looking at a feature as a whole system. Where does the data come from? Where is it stored? Who is allowed to change it? What happens when something fails? Once you can answer those questions, the code becomes easier to write, review, and fix.

Most importantly, you've practiced a way of learning that will continue to serve you as tools evolve. The specific frameworks and models will change. What will last is your ability to understand a problem, break it into pieces, and use AI without letting it do your thinking for you.

Finishing the book is not the end. It is the point where you have enough pieces to start building something that is yours.
